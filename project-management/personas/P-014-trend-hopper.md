## Bio
Emma discovers new mobile games through TikTok trends and social media hype. She downloads viral games to understand what everyone's talking about, plays intensely for 2-4 weeks, then moves on to whatever's trending next. She identifies through being "current" with gaming trends but rarely invests deeply. She's never spent money on any game and views payment as the line between casual exploration and commitment she doesn't want.

## Goals
- Stay culturally current with trending mobile games
- Understand gaming references in social media conversations
- Have novelty entertainment without long-term investment

## Frustrations
- Games that pressure her into spending before she's decided if she likes them
- Long tutorial sequences when she just wants to see what the game is
- Being locked out of content after deciding she wants to move on

## Behaviors
- Plays 2-3 hours daily when a game is new, then drops to 0
- Deletes games within 2-4 weeks
- Never spends money — payment signals unwanted commitment
- Follows multiple games concurrently in early exploration phase

## Job to Be Done
> "When I see a game trending socially, I want immediate access to understand the experience without commitment barriers, so I can participate in cultural conversations."

## Relationship to Product
Emma is a high-churn, low-commitment user who contributes to early viral growth but doesn't sustain engagement. She requires low-barrier entry with no pressure to spend, and she abandons games that demand commitment before she's decided to stay.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Emma downloads a trending game and exits when a paywall appears on day 3
- **Scenario 2:** Emma has played a viral game for two weeks and sees ads for a "limited time offer" she ignores because she's already planning to delete it
