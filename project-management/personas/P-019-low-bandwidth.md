## Bio
Samuel lives in a rural area of Kenya with limited internet access — his data plan caps at 2MB per day, and connection is unreliable. He's a lifelong gamer who has adapted to bandwidth constraints through strategic offline gameplay. He only plays games with reliable offline modes, downloading content during rare visits to town with WiFi. He spends sparingly — typically $5-10 monthly on games that respect his constraints.

## Goals
- Continue gaming hobby despite low-bandwidth environment
- Find games with predictable offline-download requirements
- Minimize mobile data usage while maintaining entertainment

## Frustrations
- Games advertising "offline" but requiring online for core features
- Unannounced updates consuming his entire month's data budget
- Developers ignoring low-bandwidth markets in game design

## Behaviors
- Downloads game content only when visiting town with WiFi
- Plays exclusively offline-capable games
- Spends $5-10 monthly on games respecting bandwidth
- Advises local mobile provider about data-friendly gaming options

## Job to Be Done
> "When I want to continue gaming in my low-bandwidth environment, I need games with reliable offline modes and predictable download needs, so I can plan around my data constraints."

## Relationship to Product
Samuel represents an underserved global market of low-bandwidth users. He's a technical user who can provide detailed feedback on data optimization and offline reliability. He spends loyally on games that work in his environment and becomes an advocate within his local community.

## Scenarios
