## Bio
Eleanor retired last year and now has time to nurture her lifelong love of strategy games. She played Age of Empires and Civilization on PC for decades, but switched to mobile for convenience. She's deeply loyal to games that respect her intelligence and avoid gambling mechanics.

## Goals
- Find challenging strategy games that reward patience and planning
- Support indie developers who make thoughtful games over predatory ones
- Stay mentally sharp through complex game systems

## Frustrations
- Pay-to-win mechanics that diminish strategic depth
- Mobile games designed for quick dopamine hits rather than long-term planning
- Games that shut down after she invests months of time

## Behaviors
- **Gaming**: Plays 2-3 hours/day, split into morning and evening sessions. Very consistent across all days.
- **Spending**: Fixed budget of $10/month for premium ad-free purchases and content upgrades. Never spends on consumables or gacha.
- **Genre preferences**: Strategy games, 4X, tower defense, puzzle games with depth.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I settle into my morning coffee, I want to engage with systems I can master over months, so I can feel continuous intellectual growth."

## Relationship to Product
Eleanor stays when games offer depth, fair progression, and one-time purchase options. She quickly abandons games with energy systems, timers, or aggressive monetization—she sees through predatory design.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** New game discovery — Downloads a promising strategy game, plays through tutorial, immediately notices paywalls that block mid-game progression, deletes and leaves a review.
- **Scenario 2:** Favorite game — After 6 months of play, developer announces server shutdown, leaving 200+ hours of progress unrecoverable.
