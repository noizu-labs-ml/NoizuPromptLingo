# P-004: James Morrison

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 45 |
| Occupation | CTO at a Series B startup, Married, 2 kids |
| Location | London, UK |
| Device | iPhone 15 Pro Max |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
James runs a high-pressure tech company and uses idle games as his primary stress outlet. He doesn't have time to think deeply—he wants to watch numbers go up while his brain unwinds. His disposable income makes him a whale, but he pays for peace and progression, not competition.

## Goals
- Maintain steady progression without grinding or complex mechanics
- Unlock all characters and upgrades as quickly as possible
- Have a game that rewards him for showing up, even just for 5 minutes

## Frustrations
- Games that require deep strategy or active management (he wants passive)
- FOMO events that pressure him to login at specific times
- Progress caps that prevent him from advancing even when he pays

## Behaviors
- **Gaming**: Plays 15-30 minutes/day, mostly during commute and meetings. Checks in throughout the day for dailies.
- **Spending**: Spends $50-200/month. Large purchases ($99.99 bundles) during sales. Total lifetime spend on his main game: $3,000+.
- **Genre preferences**: Idle games, clicker/idle RPG hybrids, incremental games.

## Job to Be Done
> "When my brain is fried from 10 hours of crisis management, I want to open a game where I make progress without thinking, so I can feel small, satisfying wins."

## Relationship to Product
James stays when the game respects his limited time and lets him pay for convenience without feeling exploited. He leaves when games introduce forced social pressure or complex systems that require more mental bandwidth.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Airport layover — Spends $99.99 on a progress-busting bundle to unlock a new idle hero and 100x rewards for 24 hours.
- **Scenario 2:** Late night — Opens game at 11 PM, excited to spend money on a daily deal, but hits a progress cap and has nothing to upgrade despite his VIP status.
