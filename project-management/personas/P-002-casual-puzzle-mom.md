# P-002: Sarah Chen

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 35 |
| Occupation | Marketing Manager, Mother of 2 |
| Location | San Francisco, CA, USA |
| Device | iPhone 14 |
| Tech comfort | medium |

## Bio
Sarah is a busy marketing manager and mother of two young children (ages 5 and 8). She puzzles during nap time, soccer practice, and evening unwinding. Her guilty pleasure is gacha pulls—it's her only "luxury" spending after years of budgeting for her family's needs.

## Goals
- Complete daily levels and events for achievement satisfaction
- Collect characters she finds aesthetically appealing (especially cute designs)
- Maintain a streak without spending more than her $15/month entertainment budget

## Frustrations
- Energy systems that gate progress exactly when she has time to play
- Gacha rates that make her feel like she got nothing for her money
- Aggressive monetization when she just wants to relax

## Behaviors
- **Gaming**: Plays in 15-20 minute bursts, 4-5 times/day. Total: 1-2 hours daily.
- **Spending**: Spends $10-15/month on gacha pulls during special events. Has spent $50 once for a "must-have" character bundle.
- **Genre preferences**: Puzzle games, match-3 with collection mechanics, gacha RPGs.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I have a few minutes between family duties, I want to lose myself in simple, satisfying puzzles, so I can feel progress without mental load."

## Relationship to Product
Sarah stays when the game respects her time (generous rewards, no forced ads) and when gacha feels fair (pity systems, fair rates). She leaves when timers prevent her from using her limited time effectively.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Lunch break — Has 8 gacha pulls saved up, excited for the new character banner, gets 0/50 featured character.
- **Scenario 2:** Bedtime — Wants to play 3 levels before sleep, but out of energy and has to either wait 2 hours or watch 6 ads to continue.
