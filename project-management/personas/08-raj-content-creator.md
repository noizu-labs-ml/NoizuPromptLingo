# Persona 08: Raj — The Accessible Gaming Content Creator

## Demographics
- **Age:** 30
- **Location:** Seattle, WA
- **Occupation:** Full-time content creator (YouTube: 45K subscribers, Twitch: 2K followers)
- **Vision:** Fully sighted
- **Niche:** Covers accessible gaming, assistive technology reviews, and disability tech

## Technology Profile
- **Setup:** Streaming PC, capture card, multi-cam, screen reader output routed to stream audio
- **Tools:** OBS, screen reader audio passthrough for demonstrating accessible UX
- **Testing:** Keeps NVDA and VoiceOver configured for live demos

## Goals
- Find genuinely innovative accessible games to feature (his audience is hungry for them)
- Create content that bridges the blind and sighted gaming communities
- Demonstrate to game developers what "accessible by design" looks like in practice
- Grow his channel by covering a game with a compelling long-term narrative arc

## Frustrations
- Most "accessible game" announcements are vaporware or shallow demos
- Hard to make compelling video content from audio-only games
- Mainstream gaming press ignores accessible games entirely
- Game developers won't respond to interview requests about their accessibility work

## Behaviors
- Contacts developers pre-launch for preview access and interviews
- Creates "first 30 minutes" videos comparing screen reader vs. visual experience
- Does deep-dive technical breakdowns of ARIA implementations
- His audience trusts his recommendations — a positive review drives measurable installs
- Cross-posts between gaming, accessibility, and tech communities

## Relationship to Blade of Eternity
Raj is a distribution channel. His content reaches both blind gamers looking for games and sighted developers looking for accessibility examples. The game's dark editorial visual style actually looks good on stream (unlike audio-only games), and the screen reader output can be mixed into stream audio as a second narrative voice — unique content that no other game provides.

## Key Scenario
Raj streams a 2-hour first-look. He plays the first hour visually, showing the editorial typography and dark UI. The second hour, he turns on NVDA and plays blind, narrating his experience. Viewers hear both the stream audio and the screen reader — the ARIA architecture makes it compelling radio. He ends with "this is the most impressive accessible game I've seen."

## Success Metric
Raj's Blade of Eternity video exceeds his channel average views by 2x, and the dev team grants an interview.
