# Persona: Sarah Mitchell — Busy Professional

## Profile
- **Age:** 38
- **Role:** M&A Lawyer, mid-size firm
- **Environment:** MacBook Air, office + commute, reads 3–5 large documents/week
- **Tech Comfort:** Medium — uses standard Office tools, not a power user

## Goals
- Extract key clauses and defined terms from 200-page contracts quickly
- Verify that a term is used consistently throughout a document
- Listen to documents during commute and ask targeted questions
- Generate a quick "what are the key obligations for Party A?" summary

## Frustrations
- Ctrl+F is insufficient — she needs semantic search ("find all payment obligations")
- Can't delegate "first pass" reading to AI in a way she trusts
- Defined terms are buried; building a glossary is manual and slow
- No way to ask "is this clause standard?" while reading on the train

## Behaviors
- Needs confidence in citations — will not accept unsourced AI answers
- Heavy user of the outline/TOC to jump between sections
- Often reads with document in split-screen with email
- Wants quick summaries but verifies the source before relying on them

## Key Scenarios
1. Imports a 300-page acquisition agreement; asks "extract all defined terms and their definitions" — app produces a glossary with page citations
2. Voice asks "what are the termination conditions?" while commuting — gets answer with "see Section 9.2, page 47"
3. Asks "does Section 12 contradict Section 4 on liability caps?" — app surfaces both passages side by side

## Acceptance Criteria
- All Q&A answers include page + section citations
- Defined term extraction with 95%+ accuracy for standard legal formatting
- Answers must quote directly, not paraphrase, for legal use
