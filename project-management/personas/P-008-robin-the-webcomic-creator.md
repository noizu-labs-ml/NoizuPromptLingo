# Robin Tremblay — The Webcomic Creator

## Demographics

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Age** | 24-32 |
| **Role** | Webcomic artist and writer |
| **Technical Level** | Novice |
| **Industry** | Independent comics / Webtoon |
| **Location** | Montreal, Canada |

## Bio

Robin has been publishing a fantasy webcomic on Webtoon for three years — 500+ pages across 180 episodes. The worldbuilding has grown organically and Robin frequently gets reader comments pointing out contradictions between early and recent episodes. Robin wants to retroactively organize the world's lore and share a "codex" page with readers, but doesn't have time to manually catalog 500 pages of implied worldbuilding.

## Goals

1. Extract and organize the worldbuilding implied across 500+ pages of comics into structured entries
2. Publish a reader-facing "world codex" that enhances the reading experience without spoilers
3. Plan future story arcs with awareness of all established lore

## Frustrations

1. The worldbuilding is scattered across dialogue, narration boxes, and visual details — not written down anywhere
2. Readers have built fan wikis that are more complete than Robin's own notes
3. Can't share lore with readers without spoiler risk — needs granular visibility controls

## Behaviors

- Publishes one episode per week, scripts 2-3 episodes ahead
- Takes notes on paper during brainstorming, rarely digitizes them
- Reads reader comments and fan theories daily
- Uses Clip Studio Paint for art, Google Docs for scripts, nothing for lore management

## Job to Be Done

> "When my readers know my world's lore better than I do because they built a fan wiki, I want to import my scripts and extract a structured knowledge base from them, so I can be the authority on my own creation."

## Relationship to Product

Robin would discover Knowledge Base through webcomic creator communities or a recommendation from another comic artist. The import/parsing feature is critical — Robin can't manually enter 500 pages of lore. The player-facing/reader-facing view for sharing curated entries without spoilers is a differentiator. Robin would churn if the import process doesn't handle script/dialogue format well.

## Scenarios

1. **Script import and extraction** — Robin pastes the scripts from 180 episodes into Knowledge Base. The system extracts characters, locations, factions, and events, presenting them for review. Robin spends a weekend curating and promoting entries to canon.
2. **Spoiler-safe codex sharing** — Robin marks 40 entries as "public" and shares a read-only codex link with readers. As new episodes reveal information, Robin promotes more entries to the public codex.
