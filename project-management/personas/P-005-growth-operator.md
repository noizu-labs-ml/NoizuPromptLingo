# Renee Okafor — The Growth Operator

## Demographics

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Age** | 33 |
| **Role** | Growth Marketing Lead |
| **Technical Level** | Intermediate |
| **Industry** | Consumer subscription apps |
| **Location** | Lisbon, Portugal |

## Bio

Renee runs paid acquisition and content for a small portfolio of products. She's not an engineer and rarely touches tickets or code review — her corner of the platform is campaigns, ad copy, and landing pages, generated with LLM assistance and reviewed by her before anything goes live.

## Goals

1. Generate ad copy variants and landing pages fast enough to keep up with weekly campaign cycles.
2. Keep an approve/reject trail on generated copy so nothing ships without her sign-off.
3. Pull competitor and keyword research without asking an engineer to run a script.

## Frustrations

1. Marketing tooling that's bolted onto an engineering product as an afterthought, with engineering-first UX.
2. No clear record of which ad copy variant was actually approved versus just generated.
3. Losing track of which landing page belongs to which domain when running several campaigns at once.

## Behaviors

- Reviews a batch of generated ad copy each morning, approving or rejecting individually.
- Keeps a running list of competitors and tracked keywords per campaign.
- Hands off approved landing pages to a developer only for final deploy, not for content edits.

## Job to Be Done

> "When I'm running a new campaign, I want to generate, review, and approve ad copy and landing pages in one place, so I don't have to shuttle content between a doc, a design tool, and an engineer."

## Relationship to Product

Renee doesn't care that this is "the same platform engineering uses" — she cares that campaigns, ad groups, and landing pages live in one screen with a clear approve/reject state. She'd churn back to spreadsheets and a separate copywriting tool if the creative suite required understanding tickets, boards, or MCP concepts to get basic work done.

## Scenarios

1. **Campaign kickoff** — Renee creates a campaign, adds two ad groups, and generates five ad-copy variants per group for review.
2. **Approval pass** — Renee reviews generated ad copy, approves three, rejects two with a reason, and regenerates against the same brief.
3. **Competitive check** — Renee pulls a competitor and keyword report before finalizing a landing page's headline.
