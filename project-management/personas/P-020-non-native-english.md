# P-020: Yuki Sato

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 31 |
| Occupation | Graphic designer |
| Location | Tokyo, Japan |
| Device | iPhone 15 |
| Tech comfort | Medium |

## Bio
Yuki has intermediate English proficiency but struggles with complex gaming terminology, cultural references, and fast-paced UI text. She loves games but constantly feels at disadvantage in games designed around native English speakers. She actively seeks games with Japanese localization and will pay premium for native language support — her monthly gaming budget of $30-60 is allocated to games with quality Japanese translation or no language dependency.

## Goals
- Play games at her full skill level without language disadvantages
- Support games that invest in her language market
- Avoid frustration from misunderstandings of game mechanics

## Frustrations
- Machine translations that miss context and nuance
- Time-limited events explained in complex English she can't read quickly
- Cultural references she doesn't understand affecting game progression

## Behaviors
- Prioritizes games with Japanese localization
- Spends $30-60 monthly on games respecting language needs
- Abandons games with unplayable English-only content
- Recommends language-inclusive games to Japanese gaming communities

## Job to Be Done
> "When I want to enjoy a new game, I need accurate Japanese localization with culturally appropriate translations, so I can focus on gameplay strategy rather than language decoding."

## Relationship to Product
Yuki represents the localization opportunity gap — she's an enthusiastic spender when her language is respected. She advocates for both technical translation and cultural adaptation, rejecting both no-translation and low-effort machine translation approaches.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Yuki downloads a new game excitedly but deletes it within hours when the Japanese localization is revealed as poor machine translation
- **Scenario 2:** Yuki pays $30 for premium currency in a game with excellent native localization, citing this as worth supporting
