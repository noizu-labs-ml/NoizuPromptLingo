# Maya Jackson — Content Creator ("No-Code Dreamer")

**Type:** Tertiary
**Segment:** Non-Technical — Creator Economy
**Pipeline entry:** Phase 1 (Sketch)
**Likely exit:** Phase 4 (Publish) — needs the full pipeline because she can't code at all

---

## Demographics

- **Age:** 26
- **Role:** YouTuber and course creator with 45K subscribers
- **Location:** Atlanta, GA
- **Income:** $65K from ads, sponsorships, and course sales
- **Tech comfort:** Low — uses Notion, Canva, Gumroad, Carrd; has never written code

## Quote

> "I have the audience. I have the idea. I just can't build the thing."

## Goals

1. Build a custom membership platform instead of paying Patreon 10%
2. Create a tool her audience actually asks for (recipe organizer, habit tracker, etc.)
3. Add "app creator" to her brand without learning to code
4. Monetize app ideas directly instead of only through content

## Frustrations

- No-code tools (Bubble, Softr) hit walls when she needs custom logic
- Hiring developers is scary — she doesn't know how to evaluate quality or estimate cost
- Wants to own her product, not rent features from a platform that takes a cut
- Technical tutorials assume coding knowledge she doesn't have and doesn't want

## Behaviors

- Builds in public — would share the creation process as content
- Evaluates tools by how quickly she can show something to her audience
- Price-sensitive on tools but willing to invest if the product generates revenue
- Community-driven — asks her audience what to build and iterates on feedback

## Scenario

Maya's audience keeps asking for a meal planning app with grocery list generation. She opens noizu.ink and describes it conversationally: "People pick recipes for the week, it makes a shopping list, and tracks what's in their pantry." The Sketch phase structures this into personas (busy parent, fitness meal-prepper, budget shopper) and 10 stories. She approves the PRD, picks a consumer playful style, and moves through Draft. The mockups look like a real app. She screenshots them for a YouTube video: "I'm building an app for you." She enters Ink, watches agents build it, and deploys. She shares the URL with her audience. 200 signups in 48 hours.

## Feature Priorities

| Must Have | Nice to Have | Don't Care |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| Zero-code-knowledge required | Audience landing page generator | GitHub export |
| Plain English the whole way | Revenue/payment integration | CLI tools |
| One-click deploy | Custom domain connection | Agent dashboard details |
| Mobile-responsive output | Analytics dashboard | API access |

## Tier Fit

**Launch ($99/mo)** — needs the full pipeline and has no alternative. The tool either does everything or she can't use it. High willingness to pay if the output generates revenue. Potential power user for "build in public" content that markets noizu.ink organically.
