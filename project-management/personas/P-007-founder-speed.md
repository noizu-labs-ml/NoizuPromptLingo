## Bio

Zoya has started six software companies — three exited, two failed, her current venture is in that crucial pre-traction phase. Her superpower is speed — she moves from idea to shipped MVP in days, not months. AI is her latest force multiplier. She uses agents for competitive research, UI text generation, customer support workflows, and even architecture advice. Her problem is maintaining a library of working agent configs and prompts that she can reuse across projects. She doesn't have time to build infrastructure; she needs ready-made patterns she can plug in and ship.

## Goals

1. Ship faster by reusing proven prompt and agent patterns across projects
2. Discover agent configurations that solve common startup problems (market research, customer interviews, UI copy)
3. Validate ideas faster by testing with agents before hiring

## Frustrations

1. Rebuilding prompts from scratch for every new venture — can't maintain a personal library
2. Worrying about whether prompts are actually good or just "seem" good
3. Can't easily test agents in realistic conversational contexts to validate business assumptions

## Behaviors

- Moves between tools rapidly (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity — whatever gets the job done)
- Writes everything in Notion but organization is chaotic
- Prioritizes speed over polish — "done is better than perfect"
- Frequently asks her network "how are you using AI for X?"

## Job to Be Done

> "When I'm validating a startup idea, I want to find working agent configs and prompts that I can deploy immediately, so I can test hypotheses before committing resources."

## Relationship to Product

Zoya is the high-churn but high-value user when engaged. She'll dip in for specific Resources, deploy them, and disappear until the next venture. Features that matter most: quick search/filter (fast discovery), copy-paste-ready configs, reputation signals to avoid bad prompts, and the ability to fork and iterate Resources. She'll churn if discovery is slow or if Resources require significant modification to work. Her engagement patterns will be sporadic but high-impact when they happen.

## Scenarios

1. **Idea Validation Sprint** — Zoya is exploring an AI-powered legal assistant idea. She searches TheRobotLives for "legal research" and "contract review" prompts, finds two with high reputation, adapts them for her use case within hours, and uses them to validate assumptions without hiring a lawyer yet.

2. **Agent-Assisted Research** — Zoya discovers a "Competitive Intelligence" agent in a "Startup Toolkit" Space. She @-mentions it in a Thread asking about competitors in her target vertical, the agent responds with structured analysis, and Zoya has competitive intel in minutes instead of weeks.

3. **Cross-Project Reuse** — A year later, Zoya is building her next venture. She remembers finding a killer "customer interview guide" prompt. She searches her own forked Resources on TheRobotLives, retrieves it without re-engineering, and saves a week of upfront work.
