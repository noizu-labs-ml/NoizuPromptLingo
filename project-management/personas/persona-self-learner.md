# Persona: Robert "Bob" Hendricks — Self-Learner

## Profile
- **Age:** 58
- **Role:** Retired Engineer, avid reader (history, science, self-help)
- **Environment:** iMac at home desk, sometimes iPad; reads 2–4 books/month as PDFs
- **Tech Comfort:** Low-Medium — comfortable with Apple products, dislikes complexity

## Goals
- Listen to non-fiction books like an audiobook, with the visual page as reference
- Ask "what did they mean by that?" when something doesn't land
- Keep his place across reading sessions without thinking about it
- Occasionally look things up without leaving the app

## Frustrations
- Commercial audiobooks don't exist for many PDF-format books he owns
- Can't ask questions of an audiobook — passive listening, no interaction
- Loses his place when life interrupts; resuming is clunky in standard readers
- Font sizes on PDFs are sometimes too small; wants to read along on his own terms

## Behaviors
- Listens for 45–60 minute sessions, then takes a break
- Occasional replays ("I didn't catch that")
- Wants zero-friction setup — import and play, no configuration
- Not interested in advanced features; wants the simple path to work perfectly

## Key Scenarios
1. Imports a 400-page history PDF; starts playing from the beginning; reading highlights current sentence on screen
2. Says "go back" when he missed something; app replays the last two sentences
3. Asks "who was Napoleon's chief of staff?" — gets an answer sourced from the book, not Wikipedia

## Acceptance Criteria
- Resume-from-last-position is automatic, zero configuration
- "Go back" / "replay that" works reliably for recent sentences
- Simple UI — no visible complexity unless requested
- Import-to-playing in under 60 seconds for a basic PDF (indexing continues in background)
