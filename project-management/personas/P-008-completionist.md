## Bio
David brings engineer precision to gaming—he treats every game as a completion project. He spreads his playtime across 5-7 active games, rotating through dailies to maintain 100% achievement rates. He uses spreadsheets to track progress and has never dropped a game below 90% completion.

## Goals
- Achieve 100% completion or platinum status in every game he plays
- Maintain multiple games at "mastered" status simultaneously
- Find games with fair achievement systems (not P2W or impossible grinds)

## Frustrations
- Bugged achievements that make 100% impossible
- Time-limited achievements that require unrealistic play schedules
- Hidden achievements that rely on RNG rather than skill

## Behaviors
- **Gaming**: Plays 1-2 hours/day spread across 5 games. Never spends more than 30 minutes in one session.
- **Spending**: Spends $20-40/month on time-savers (resource packs, xp boosts) specifically for grinding achievements. One-time purchases for DLC that adds achievements.
- **Genre preferences**: RPGs, Metroidvanias, puzzle games—anything with achievement systems.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I approach a new game, I want to master every system and collect every achievement, so I can feel the satisfaction of complete understanding before moving on."

## Relationship to Product
David stays for fair, achievable completion requirements and regular achievement additions. He abandons games when achievements become impossible or when developers stop updating them.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Achievement grind — Needs 5 more boss kills for a rare achievement, buys a $9.99 VIP pass to unlock auto-battle so he can grind while working.
- **Scenario 2:** Completion check — Reaches 99.9% (521/522 achievements), discovers the last achievement is bugged and unobtainable, posts frustrated review.
