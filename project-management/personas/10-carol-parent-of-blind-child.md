# Persona 10: Carol — The Parent Seeking Inclusive Gaming

## Demographics
- **Age:** 42
- **Location:** suburban Chicago, IL
- **Occupation:** Occupational therapist
- **Vision:** Fully sighted
- **Family:** Mother of two — son (12, sighted) and daughter (14, blind from birth)

## Technology Profile
- **Devices:** Family iPad, daughter's iPhone with VoiceOver, shared Windows desktop
- **Familiarity:** Comfortable with technology, understands assistive tech basics from her OT work
- **Daughter's setup:** VoiceOver power user, refreshable braille display for school, stereo headphones for gaming

## Goals
- Find a game her blind daughter and sighted son can play together on equal footing
- Ensure the game is age-appropriate but not condescending — her daughter is 14 and sharp
- Give her daughter a social gaming experience she can discuss with school friends
- Evaluate the game's safety (chat moderation, no predatory monetization) before letting her kids play

## Frustrations
- Every "accessible game" recommendation is either for adults or for young children
- Her son won't play audio-only games — "it's boring, there's nothing to see"
- Mobile games marketed as accessible have aggressive microtransactions
- She can't evaluate whether a game is truly accessible without installing a screen reader herself
- School gaming conversations exclude her daughter because she can't play what everyone else plays

## Behaviors
- Researches games before allowing her children to play — reads reviews, tests herself
- Checks for content moderation tools, chat filtering, and reporting mechanisms
- Prefers games with no real-money advantage mechanics
- Watches her daughter play the first session to verify the experience works
- Active in parent groups for blind/VI children (NFB parents division, Facebook groups)

## Relationship to Blade of Eternity
Carol is the gatekeeper for an underserved demographic: blind teenagers. She validates safety, onboarding clarity, and the cross-ability play thesis. Her ideal outcome is her blind daughter and sighted son in the same clan, having a shared experience that neither could get from any other game. She'll also evaluate whether game violence and social features are appropriate for a 14-year-old.

## Key Scenario
Carol finds Blade of Eternity through Raj's YouTube video (Persona 08). She plays through character creation on her own desktop first, then tests it with VoiceOver on her daughter's iPhone. Satisfied, she sets up accounts for both kids. Two weeks later, her son and daughter are in the same clan, and her daughter is teaching her brother keyboard shortcuts.

## Success Metric
Both children play actively for 3+ months, Carol recommends the game in her parent support groups, and she never encounters a safety concern.
