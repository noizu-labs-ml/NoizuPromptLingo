# Persona 03: The Creator-Spectator

**Name:** Jordan Reeves
**Age:** 31
**Location:** Portland, OR
**Occupation:** UX designer at a mid-size tech company
**Device:** iPhone 14, iPad Pro
**Income:** $110K/year

---

## Profile

Jordan doesn't play competitive games anymore — they burned out on League of Legends years ago. But they're fascinated by AI, game design, and emergent systems. They follow AI research on Twitter/X, watch GDC talks on YouTube, and have strong opinions about game UIs. They'll engage with AI Fighter primarily through the community and content layer: watching replays, studying builds, theorycrafting on Reddit, and creating "build guide" content.

Jordan's engagement model is closer to a sports fan than a player. They want to watch, analyze, and discuss — not grind ranked. If the spectator experience is rich enough, they'll be deeply engaged without ever climbing past Silver.

---

## Goals

- Watch high-level fights and understand the strategies behind them
- Create content: build guides, replay analysis posts, tier lists
- Participate in community discussions about meta, balance, and game design
- Appreciate the graph editor as a design artifact — the aesthetics of the neural pathway motif matter to them

## Frustrations

- Games that only reward direct competitive play and ignore the community/spectator experience
- Poor replay tools that don't let you scrub, slow-mo, or overlay data
- Communities that are hostile to non-competitive participants
- Ugly UIs (as a UX designer, bad design is physically painful)

## Behaviors

- Plays 1-2 casual sessions per day, mostly in Training Gym experimenting with builds
- Watches 5-10 replays per day from the Replay Theater / community highlights
- Posts 2-3 times per week on Reddit/Discord with analysis or build recommendations
- Will spend on cosmetics that look good in screenshots/replays (arena themes, victory animations)
- Follows top players and studies their builds when shared

---

## Key Scenarios

1. **First session:** Spends 20 minutes in the Fighter Studio appreciating the UI. Takes screenshots of the graph editor. Plays one tutorial battle. Immediately goes to the Lab to browse community builds
2. **Week 2:** Has written a Reddit post titled "I analyzed the top 50 Gold-tier fighters — here are the 3 node patterns everyone uses." Barely played ranked (still Silver)
3. **Month 2:** Running a small Discord community of "build theorycrafters." Hosts weekly replay review sessions. Has proposed balance changes to the devs based on data analysis from community replays

---

## Design Implications

- Replay Theater needs to be a first-class feature, not an afterthought — curated highlights, search by fighter archetype, "fight of the week" spotlights
- Build sharing must include rich metadata: win rate, common matchups, graph complexity score
- The spectator/community experience needs to be accessible without high ranked play — no "reach Diamond to unlock Replay Theater"
- The visual design of the graph editor and battle replays must be screenshot-worthy — this persona is the unpaid marketing department
- Tournament spectator mode (future) should support commentary/annotation tools
