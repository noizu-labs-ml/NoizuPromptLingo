# Bertrand Achille — The Community Librarian

## Demographics
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Age** | 37 |
| **Role** | Technical writer + knowledge management consultant |
| **Technical Level** | Intermediate-high |
| **Industry** | Knowledge Management / Consulting |
| **Location** | Montreal, Canada |

## Bio
Bertrand has been collecting links professionally and personally for 20 years. He has maintained a personal wiki, a public Notion knowledge base, and currently publishes a well-regarded newsletter about the craft of knowledge work. He is the kind of person who submits 10 sites in a single evening because he's been hoarding a mental queue of "things that deserve more visibility." He thinks of gotta.cc as the tool that can finally make his link-collecting legible to others.

## Goals
1. Submit the backlog of quality sites he's found over years of browsing and have them properly scored and categorized
2. Build public collections organized by theme that function as living reading lists for his newsletter subscribers
3. Be recognized as a high-quality contributor — he wants his curator profile to carry credibility

## Frustrations
1. Sites he submits get stuck in a queue with no status visibility — he can't tell if they were rejected or just delayed
2. Category taxonomy doesn't match how he thinks about knowledge — some of his best finds don't fit neatly into a single category
3. There's no way to see which of his submissions drove meaningful traffic or engagement on the platform

## Behaviors
- Submits in bursts — 10-20 sites on a focused curation evening
- Writes thoughtful submission notes that provide context the AI might not have
- Creates thematic collections ("Sites for thinking about climate adaptation," "Best independent math education") and promotes them in his newsletter
- Upvotes sites from other curators he respects; flags obvious spam quickly
- Monitors his curator profile's public stats and is motivated by visible contribution metrics

## Job to Be Done
> "I've spent years finding the good stuff — I want a platform that makes my curation visible, shareable, and actually useful to other people who care about quality."

## Relationship to Product
Bertrand is a Creator tier subscriber ($10/mo) and one of the most valuable community members. His submissions improve the directory's coverage; his collections drive traffic to underrepresented sites. He is the persona most likely to become an informal ambassador — writing about the platform, recommending it, and bringing his newsletter audience onto it. He is also the persona most likely to churn loudly if submission UX is poor or if his contributions feel invisible.

## Scenarios
1. **Sunday Submission Evening** — Bertrand spends Sunday evening going through two weeks of saved tabs. He submits 15 sites to gotta.cc with notes, categorizes each, and checks back Monday morning to see which have been scored. He's pleased when three land in the top 20 of their categories.
2. **Newsletter Collection Drop** — Bertrand publishes his newsletter with a link to a gotta.cc collection he built: "12 sites about the science of attention." Three hundred of his subscribers click through. He watches the collection's view count tick up in his Creator dashboard and feels validated.
