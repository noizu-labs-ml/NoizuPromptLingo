# P-012: Jessica Lee

| Attribute | Value |

## Bio
Jessica follows a tight-knit friend group of 6 through mobile gaming. She doesn't consider herself a gamer — she plays because her college friends do, logging in 2-3 evenings per week for group sessions. Her enjoyment comes from the social connection more than the gameplay itself. She'll spend reluctantly but will match group spending to keep up.

## Goals
- Maintain her social group through weekly gaming sessions
- Stay relevant in her friend group by participating
- Have predictable low-stakes social entertainment

## Frustrations
- Finding times to coordinate with all 6 friends
- Falling behind when she misses a session
- Being pressured to spend when she doesn't want to

## Behaviors
- Plays 2-3 evenings per week with friend group only
- Spends $0-30 monthly, mostly to match group purchases
- Prefers the same game for months while friend group is engaged
- Won't play solo — gaming is purely social

## Job to Be Done
> "When my friends want to game together, I want easy drop-in coordination and fair progression that doesn't penalize missed sessions, so I can maintain the social connection without stress."

## Relationship to Product
Jessica is social-first — the game is a vehicle for group connection. She follows her friends between games and doesn't have solo interest. She resents mechanics that pressure her into more time or spending than her social use case requires.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Jessica joins a friend group session and realizes she's falling behind on gear because she missed the previous week
- **Scenario 2:** Jessica spends $10 on a battle pass after her friends all pressure her to join them for the season
