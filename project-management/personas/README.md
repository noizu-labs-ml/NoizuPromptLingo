## Audience Segments

**Core (personas 01, 02, 05):** The primary player base. Tinkerers drive depth, Casuals drive volume, Grinders drive competitive legitimacy.

**Community (personas 03, 09):** Don't play the most but generate disproportionate awareness. Creator-Spectators produce analysis content; Streamers produce entertainment content.

**Adjacent Markets (personas 04, 06, 10):** Non-traditional gaming audiences reached through AI Fighter's unique positioning. Educators use it as a teaching tool, Researchers validate its AI model, Data Artists use its visual language.

**Underserved (personas 07, 08):** Players traditional mobile games often fail. Dad Gamers need async + short sessions. Accessibility Advocates need assistive tech support. Both are high-loyalty if served well.
