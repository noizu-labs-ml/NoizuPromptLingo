## Bio
Robert is a burnt-out accountant using mindless mobile puzzle games to decompress after 12-hour workdays. He plays in 10-15 minute sessions during evening downtime, specifically choosing games that require no thinking. He views games as a meditation break from his mental work and has spent money only once in three years on a game he played for nine months.

## Goals
- Disconnect from work mental patterns without stimulation
- Have reliable low-effort entertainment available at bedtime
- Avoid any games adding stress or decisions

## Frustrations
- High-intensity games that energize rather than relax him
- Games with timers or pressure that create work-like stress
- Ads and pop-ups interrupting his relaxation state

## Behaviors
- Plays 10-15 minutes nightly before sleep
- Only plays mindless puzzles and matching games — nothing competitive
- Has spent $5 total in three years on one game
- Switches games rarely — prefers long-term favorites

## Job to Be Done
> "When I'm mentally exhausted after a long workday, I want mindless repetitive gameplay with no pressure, so I can zone out and prepare for sleep without more thinking."

## Relationship to Product
Robert is a low-engagement, low-stress user who wants games that disappear as thoughtless entertainment. He pays rarely but loyally to long-term favorites. He abandons games with pressure mechanics or intrusive monetization.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Robert opens a puzzle game and immediately exits when he sees a countdown timer
- **Scenario 2:** Robert makes his first in-game purchase after nine months of daily play to remove ads from a relaxation game
