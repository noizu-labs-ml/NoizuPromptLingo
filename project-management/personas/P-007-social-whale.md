## Bio
Priya built her following on aesthetics and trends. She treats mobile gaming as another brand extension—her in-game collections must match her real-world style. She's highly visible in gaming communities and uses her game profiles as social proof. Her followers look to her for both fashion and game recommendations.

## Goals
- Curate impressive in-game collections that showcase her aesthetic
- Maintain a large in-game friend list and guild presence
- Convert gaming content into cross-platform social engagement

## Frustrations
- Limited customization that doesn't match trending aesthetics
- Games without strong social features (profile showcases, guild housing, etc.)
- Content gates that prevent her from accessing everything immediately

## Behaviors
- **Gaming**: Plays 4-5 hours/day but more passively—lots of chatting, browsing collections, and showing off rather than active gameplay.
- **Spending**: Spends $100-500/month. Luxury bundles, limited editions, and status items. Has spent $2,000+ on a single game's cosmetics over its lifetime.
- **Genre preferences**: Social simulation, fashion games, multiplayer with strong customization, pet collection games.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I engage with a game, I want to build and display collections that reflect my personal brand, so I can extend my aesthetic identity across platforms."

## Relationship to Product
Priya stays when games offer rich customization, social showcase features, and frequent cosmetic releases. She leaves when trends stagnate or when the community becomes toxic.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Influencer collab — Game offers a "Priya x Game" limited bundle ($79.99), she buys immediately and posts stories, driving sales for the developer.
- **Scenario 2:** Community drama — Guildmate accuses her of buying her way to top ranks, leading to a 3-hour Discord debate and burnout.
