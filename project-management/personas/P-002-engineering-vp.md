## Bio

Priya leads a 40-person engineering organization at a Series B company scaling from 500k to 5M monthly active users. She has strong management instincts but the platform is showing cracks — P99 latencies are spiking, the codebase has accumulated years of tech debt, and her senior engineers are too deep in feature delivery to step back and address foundational issues. She needs a trusted external expert who can engage at the principal engineer level without the political overhead of a full-time hire.

## Goals

1. Identify and remediate the top 3 architectural bottlenecks causing production incidents
2. Establish quality gates and automated testing coverage before the Q3 release push
3. Get an independent code and security audit to satisfy enterprise customer compliance requirements

## Frustrations

1. Internal engineers are too close to the codebase to see its systemic problems objectively
2. Previous "audits" from Big 4 consulting firms produced 200-page reports with no actionable priorities
3. Hiring a full-time Principal Engineer takes 6 months; she needs expertise now

## Behaviors

- Evaluates vendors through LinkedIn, GitHub profiles, and published technical writing
- Requires a Statement of Work with defined deliverables before any budget approval
- Shares shortlisted vendors with her CTO for final sign-off
- Values async communication and well-structured written deliverables over calls

## Job to Be Done

> "When my platform is showing signs of strain as we scale and my team is too busy shipping to fix the foundation, I want an expert who can embed for 4–8 weeks and produce specific, prioritized fixes with my team, so I can get back to sustainable velocity without a major incident."

Finds noizu.com through a peer recommendation in an Engineering Leaders Slack or via LinkedIn.
