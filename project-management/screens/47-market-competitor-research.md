## Description

Research workspace (route inferred from backend/MCP domain — exact frontend location not confirmed) for tracking competitors within a market segment and researching/tracking keywords to inform campaign strategy.

## Key Components

- **Competitor Tracking Table** — tracked competitors per market segment (US-034)
- **Market Segment Selector** — scopes the research view to a segment (US-034)
- **Keyword Research Panel** — keyword discovery and metrics (US-035)
- **Tracked Keyword List** — keywords saved for ongoing tracking (US-035)

## Interactions

- User selects a segment via the Market Segment Selector and adds entries to the Competitor Tracking Table (US-034)
- User searches the Keyword Research Panel and saves results to the Tracked Keyword List (US-035)
