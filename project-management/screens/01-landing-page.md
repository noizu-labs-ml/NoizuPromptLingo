| Field | Value |

## Description

Public-facing marketing page that demonstrates the product's 4-phase pipeline, showcases social proof, presents pricing tiers, and drives signup conversions. First impression for all new users.

## Key Components

- **Hero Animation** — Looping 4-phase pipeline demo (<30s cycle) with phase labels and skip/pause controls (INK-081)
- **Social Proof Gallery** — "Built with noizu.ink" project cards showing name, screenshot, phases used, and time-to-ship (INK-082)
- **Pricing Table** — 4-tier comparison (Free/Pro/Builder/Launch) with feature rows, annual toggle, and recommended tier highlight (INK-083)
- **Primary CTA** — Above-fold "Start your first project — free" button with no-credit-card messaging (INK-084)

## Interactions

- Hero animation loops automatically with pause/skip controls
- Social proof gallery scrolls horizontally on mobile
- Pricing toggle switches monthly/annual billing
- CTA button routes to signup or directly to Sketch wizard if logged in

## Navigation

- Accessible from: Direct URL, search engines, marketing links
- Links to: Signup, Login, Pricing section anchor
