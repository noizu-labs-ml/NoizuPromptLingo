# US-007: Choose Specific Sparring Partners in the Training Gym

As a **Tinkerer**, I want to select named sparring partners (including community builds) in the Training Gym so that I can train against styles that mirror my expected ranked opponents.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The Training Gym offers a sparring partner browser with filters: archetype, rank tier, win rate, and node complexity
- Community builds marked as public appear in the browser and can be favorited for quick access
- Up to 5 sparring partners can be queued for a training session; they rotate automatically between rounds
- Training results against each partner are tracked separately so the user can see matchup-specific improvement

**Priority:** Should
