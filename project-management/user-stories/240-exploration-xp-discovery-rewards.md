# US-240: Exploration XP and Discovery Rewards

**Persona:** Elena — Blind teenager, VoiceOver+iOS, social focused
**Priority:** P1
**Epic:** World Depth & Exploration

## Story
As Elena, I want to earn meaningful XP and rewards for exploring, finding secrets, and reaching milestones so that thorough exploration is rewarded on the same tier as combat and quests, and I have reasons to go places just to see what's there.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Exploration XP awarded for: first visit to a new location (scales with zone rarity/danger), discovery of a hidden area (US-223), completion of a zone's discovery checklist, and reaching geographic milestones (highest peak, deepest dungeon floor, furthest ocean point)
- [ ] Discovery reward announced immediately via polite live region after the discovery narration: "Exploration reward: 250 XP, 'First Footstep' achievement unlocked" — separate from and after the discovery narration itself
- [ ] Zone discovery checklist accessible in exploration journal: "Thornwood: Rooms visited 8/12, Secrets found 1/3, NPCs met 4/6, Lore collected 2/4" — completionist layer without requiring completion
- [ ] Milestone rewards scale with significance: first dungeon explored (minor reward), all dungeons in a region explored (regional achievement + unique item), all regions explored (Worldwalker title + cosmetic)
- [ ] Exploration achievements listed in journal achievement section with completion status; achievements shareable to party chat: "Elena unlocked 'Worldwalker' — she has explored every region!"
- [ ] Discovery streaks: visiting a new location each day of real-time play earns a streak bonus multiplied against all exploration XP earned that day; streak displayed in daily summary
- [ ] Passive exploration rewards: thorough examination of rooms and objects occasionally yields extra XP: "Your careful examination reveals more than expected (+50 XP)" — rewards attentive play without making examination mandatory
- [ ] Exploration contribution to party visible in group context: if Elena finds a hidden room that benefits the whole party, the party receives a scaled exploration bonus

## Notes
Elena's motivation is partly social (sharing discoveries with friends) and partly the satisfaction of completionism within a bounded space. The zone discovery checklist in the journal gives her a structured goal without making completion mandatory: seeing "Secrets found 1/3" tells her there are 2 more secrets to find without telling her where they are. The discovery streak for daily logins is a gentle engagement hook appropriate for a teenager's play pattern: she's incentivized to explore somewhere new each day rather than farming a familiar route. The party exploration bonus when Elena finds a hidden room creates a social moment — her curiosity benefits her friends, which reinforces her exploratory behavior. Geographic milestone rewards (highest peak, deepest floor) are the open-world achievement Elena will pursue obsessively and tweet about ("I found the bottom of the dungeon, level 47, it took me 3 hours"). Passive examination rewards ("your careful examination reveals more than expected") is the game gently telling Elena that reading descriptions carefully is mechanically rewarded, not just narratively satisfying. The achievement share to party chat is Elena's announcement mechanism — the game makes her exploration social by giving her a one-button way to tell her friends about it.
