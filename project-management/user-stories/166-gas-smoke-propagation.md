# US-166: Gas & Smoke Propagation

**Persona:** Dave — MUD veteran sysadmin (45, sighted, deep systems)
**Priority:** P1
**Epic:** Mutable World & Environment

## Story
As Dave, I want gas and smoke to behave according to physical density — smoke rising and spreading through upper passages, heavy gas sinking and pooling in low areas — so that chemical and environmental hazards require genuine tactical thinking about room topology, ventilation, and elevation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Gas and smoke entities carry density relative to air: light (smoke, steam — rises, spreads upward through connected passages), neutral (equivalent to air — spreads equally), heavy (chlorine gas, alchemical vapors — sinks, pools in depressions and low passages)
- [ ] Gas propagation model: each tick, gas volume spreads from source room to connected rooms based on passage openness, wind direction/speed, and gas density vs. room elevation differential
- [ ] Concentration is tracked per room: `%GasState{type: atom, concentration: 0.0..1.0, density: atom, source_room: room_id}` — concentration decreases with distance from source and increases near source if continuously emitted
- [ ] Gas effects activate at concentration thresholds: low (irritation, perception penalty), moderate (coughing, stamina drain, narrative warning), high (choking, stat penalties, possible unconsciousness), lethal (ongoing health damage, rapid incapacitation)
- [ ] Ventilation clears gas: open windows, wind-facing passages, mechanical ventilation (bellows, fans) accelerate dispersal; sealed rooms concentrate gas; airflow direction matters
- [ ] Gas narration emphasizes smell and physical sensation: "A sharp, mineral smell — like struck flint — fills your lungs. Your eyes water. The air tastes wrong." (gas onset); "You struggle to draw breath against the thickening smoke. The ceiling is already invisible." (high smoke concentration)
- [ ] ARIA alert-level announcements trigger when gas reaches warning and dangerous thresholds; assertive announcement at lethal concentration with escape direction if available
- [ ] Fire smoke is automatically generated by the fire system (US-153) and fed into the gas propagation system; alchemical gas sources (broken vials, ruptured pipes, creature abilities) integrate through the same system

## Notes
Dave's systems perspective leads him to think about gas warfare: flooding a room with heavy gas through a ceiling vent while enemies occupy it, using smoke to screen a retreat, deploying alchemical clouds as area denial. The gas system must support these tactical applications while maintaining physical plausibility.

The density mechanic is the key physical insight: heavy gas (chlorine analogs) sinks to the floor and pools in basements, pits, and low passages — it doesn't climb stairs or fill tall rooms evenly. Light gas (smoke) does the opposite: it rises, collects at ceiling level, and spreads through any upward passage. Neutral gas diffuses everywhere. The topology of a room (elevation, ceiling height, passage positions) determines where gas accumulates.

Ventilation as a countermeasure creates interesting options: a player who knows heavy gas is being pumped into a dungeon level can open lower passages to drain it or create airflow to disperse it. The gas system should reward players who understand and manipulate the ventilation topology of an area.

For accessibility: gas narration should never rely on visual color cues ("the air turns yellowish-green"). Instead, rely on smell (each gas type has a characteristic smell description), physical sensation (burning eyes, throat tightening, difficulty breathing), and taste (metallic, acrid, sweet). These modalities are equally available to all players regardless of visual ability.
