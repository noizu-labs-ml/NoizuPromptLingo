# US-061: Async Battle Resolution

**As a Dad Gamer,** I want my battles to resolve automatically while I'm away so that I never lose a match or a turn because I had to put the phone down.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- All PvP battles are submitted as challenges and resolved server-side within 24 hours
- The player is never required to be online during resolution
- A push notification (opt-in) is sent when results are ready
- Closing the app mid-submission does not cancel or corrupt the battle entry

**Priority:** Must
