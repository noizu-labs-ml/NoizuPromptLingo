# US-079: Content Moderation Queue for Community Cosmetics

**As a Dad Gamer,** I want user-submitted fighter names and cosmetics to pass through moderation before appearing in my client so that my child and I are not exposed to inappropriate content.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- User-submitted fighter names are reviewed (automated + human queue) before appearing in any opponent list
- Cosmetic assets submitted via community programs are tagged with a content rating before release
- Reported content is hidden from the reporting user's client within 60 seconds of submission
- Moderation status is visible to submitters (e.g., "Pending review," "Approved," "Rejected — policy reason")

**Priority:** Should
