# US-086: Early Access Preview for Creators

As a **Streamer**, I want access to new seasonal content (arenas, mechanics, fighters) at least one week before public release, so that I can produce launch-day content and establish myself as the go-to source for patch coverage.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Verified creators in the affiliate program receive a separate "preview" build or content flag
- Preview content is clearly marked as embargoed with a visible lift date
- Creator NDA acknowledgment flow built into the early access unlock
- Preview fighters and arenas are isolated from the live ranked ladder to prevent balance leaks

**Priority:** Could
