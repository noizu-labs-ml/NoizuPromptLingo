# US-092: Category Sponsorship Placement and Management

## User Story

**As a** Category Sponsor (P-006),
**I want to** purchase a sponsored placement at the top of a specific category page,
**So that** my brand is associated with the quality sites in that category and visible to a targeted, engaged audience.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I am a verified business account, when I navigate to the Sponsorship page, then I see a list of available categories with their monthly visitor counts and current sponsorship status (available / taken)
- [ ] Given I select an available category and choose a duration (1, 3, or 6 months), when I complete checkout, then my sponsorship is activated and a labeled "Sponsored by {Brand}" banner appears at the top of that category page
- [ ] Given my sponsorship is active, when I view my sponsor dashboard, then I see impression counts, click counts, and click-through rate for my sponsored placement updated daily
- [ ] Given my sponsorship is displayed on a category page, when a non-Supporter visitor views the page, then the sponsor banner is visible; when a Supporter views the page, then the banner is hidden per their tier benefit
- [ ] Given my sponsorship contract expires in 7 days, when the system detects the upcoming expiry, then I receive an email with renewal options and a one-click renewal link
- [ ] Given I want to update my sponsor creative (logo, tagline, URL), when I submit new creative in the sponsor dashboard, then changes are reviewed and applied within 48 hours

## Notes

Category sponsorships must remain editorially neutral — sponsored placements cannot influence the quality scores or ordering of listings within the category. The Supporter ad-free benefit (US-090) must suppress sponsor banners for paying subscribers, which means sponsor impression counts will undercount vs. raw page views. Price this expectation into the sponsor-facing metrics.
