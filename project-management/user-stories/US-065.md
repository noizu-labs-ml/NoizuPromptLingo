# US-065: Frequency-Aware Matchmaking

**As a Dad Gamer,** I want the ranked matchmaking to pair me with opponents who play at a similar pace so that I'm not matched against someone who submits five battles a day while I submit one.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Matchmaking considers rolling 14-day battle frequency as a secondary sort after MMR
- Players in the lowest activity tier (fewer than 3 battles/week) are pooled together before cross-tier matching
- Match frequency preference is surfaced as an optional setting ("Casual" / "Active" / "Any")
- Matchmaking still resolves a match within 48 hours even if ideal frequency peers are unavailable

**Priority:** Should
