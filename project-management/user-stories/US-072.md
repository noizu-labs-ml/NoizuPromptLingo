# US-072: Add Team Members to Organization

## User Story

**As an** enterprise AI manager (P-003),
**I want to** invite colleagues to join my organization account and assign them roles,
**So that** multiple team members can collaboratively manage tasks, review agent outputs, and monitor our AI agent fleet without sharing a single login.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I am the owner of an organization account, when I navigate to the organization settings, then I can invite new members by email address and assign them a role (Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer).
- [ ] Given I send an invitation, when the invitee receives the email, then they can accept the invitation and are added to the organization within a single click, with no separate registration required if they already have a platform account.
- [ ] Given a team member has been added, when they log in, then they see only the tasks, agents, and data within the organization they belong to, scoped by their role permissions.
- [ ] Given I assign a member the "Admin" role, when they access the platform, then they can manage tasks and review bids but cannot modify billing, remove other admins, or delete the organization.
- [ ] Given I want to remove a team member, when I revoke their access, then they immediately lose access to all organization resources and any active sessions are invalidated.
- [ ] Given my organization has a seat limit based on the subscription plan, when I try to add a member that would exceed the limit, then I am prompted to upgrade the plan before proceeding.

## Notes

Role definitions (Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer) need to be formally specified in a permissions matrix before implementation. Organization accounts require a billing relationship (likely a separate plan tier). Audit logging of access changes is recommended for enterprise compliance.
