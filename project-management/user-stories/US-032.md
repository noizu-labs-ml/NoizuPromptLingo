# US-032: Lesson Plan Template Library

**As an Educator,** I want to browse and clone a library of pre-built lesson plan templates — each with a suggested fighter build, learning objectives, and a structured assignment prompt — so that I can integrate AI Fighter into my CS curriculum without building from scratch.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- At least 5 lesson plan templates are available at launch covering: intro to neural nets, perception vs. decision layers, training vs. overfitting, build optimization, and tournament design
- Each template is downloadable as a PDF and as an editable Google Doc (linked from the Education portal)
- Templates include a recommended student fighter build (JSON) that can be pre-loaded into a classroom sandbox
- Educators can submit their own templates for review; approved submissions are credited to the author

**Priority:** Should
