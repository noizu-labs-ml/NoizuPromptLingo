# US-018: Complete a Daily Challenge to Earn Bonus Resources

As a **Curious Casual**, I want a daily training challenge that gives me a fun goal and bonus rewards so that I have a reason to open the app every day even when I don't have time for ranked play.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- A new challenge is available every day at midnight UTC and expires after 24 hours
- Challenges are short and achievable in under 10 minutes (e.g., "Win a training round using only Evasion nodes", "Reach 5 combo hits in a single round")
- Completing a challenge awards cosmetic currency; streak bonuses apply for 3-, 7-, and 30-day streaks
- Challenge progress persists across app restarts and syncs across devices

**Priority:** Should
