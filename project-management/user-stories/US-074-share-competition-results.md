# US-074: Share Competition Results

## User Story

**As a** indie lifestyle blogger (P-001),
**I want to** share my competition ranking and result when a competition ends,
**So that** I can celebrate my placement publicly and drive interest in BloggersCompete from my audience.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a competition I entered has ended, when I view the competition results page, then I see a prominent "Share your result" CTA with my final rank and score displayed
- [ ] Given I click "Share your result," when the share sheet opens, then I see options: Copy link, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and download as image
- [ ] Given I share to Twitter/X, when the tweet is pre-composed, then it includes my rank (e.g., "🏆 I finished #3 in the [Competition Name] on @BloggersCompete!"), my overall score, and a link to the competition leaderboard
- [ ] Given I click "Download as image," when the image generates, then I receive a 1080×1080px branded graphic showing: competition name, my rank badge, my blog name, final score, and the BloggersCompete logo
- [ ] Given I did not place in the top 3, when I view the share CTA, then the share copy is encouraging (e.g., "I competed in [Name] and scored [X]!") rather than implying a loss
- [ ] Given the competition leaderboard page has OG meta tags, when the link is shared, then a rich preview shows the competition name, top 3 winners, and the total number of participants

## Notes

The downloadable 1080×1080 graphic targets Instagram Stories and LinkedIn visual posts — high-value organic reach channels. Encourage participation in next competition via share copy. Positive framing for all placements is important for retention. See US-073 for profile sharing, US-075 for competition discussions.
