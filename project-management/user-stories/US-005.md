# US-005: Reach Top Rank Without Spending Money

As a **Tinkerer**, I want a published free-tier parity guarantee confirming that no paid item improves fighter performance so that I can compete at the highest rank without feeling disadvantaged.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- A "Fair Play" badge is displayed on the ranked arena lobby confirming P2W items are absent
- All node types, training modes, and arena slots are accessible to free accounts
- Paid items are limited to cosmetics (skins, idle animations, UI themes) with zero effect on graph execution or training speed
- The patch notes explicitly flag any change that touches monetization balance and explain its fairness impact

**Priority:** Must
