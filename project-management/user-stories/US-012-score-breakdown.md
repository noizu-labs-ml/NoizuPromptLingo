# US-012: View Score Dimension Breakdown

## User Story

**As an** Indie Web Developer (P-002),
**I want to** see how a site scores across all five quality dimensions (originality, depth, freshness, human authorship, design quality),
**So that** I understand why a site ranks where it does and can trust the curation logic.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I am viewing a site card or site detail page, when I click/tap on the composite score, then a breakdown panel shows individual scores for each of the 5 dimensions with short labels.
- [ ] Given I view the score breakdown, when it renders, then each dimension score is displayed as both a numeric value (0–100) and a visual bar or indicator.
- [ ] Given I hover over a dimension label, when the tooltip appears, then it shows a 1-sentence explanation of what that dimension measures.

## Notes

Score transparency is a trust-building mechanism. Users should never feel scores are a black box. The 5 dimensions are: Originality, Depth, Freshness, Human Authorship, Design Quality. Connects to US-013 (editorial summaries) and US-017 (sorting by score).
