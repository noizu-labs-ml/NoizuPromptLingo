# US-062: Crafting Quality Variance and Skill Expression

**Persona:** Marcus — Blind Power Gamer
**Priority:** P1
**Epic:** Crafting System

## Story
As Marcus, I want crafted items to have quality variance (common through masterwork) tied to my skill level and material grade so that investing in crafting feels meaningfully rewarded and creates a real economy around high-quality goods.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Craft outcomes announce quality tier immediately: "You finish the blade. Quality: Exceptional. [+12% damage, +8% durability]"
- [ ] Quality is influenced by: crafter skill rank, material grade, tools used, and a random variance component
- [ ] Screen reader receives a single clean summary line — not a wall of stats mid-craft
- [ ] Players can examine any crafted item and hear its full provenance: creator, quality tier, material composition, creation date
- [ ] Masterwork items (top 5% roll) receive a named designation and are permanently attributed to the crafter
- [ ] Crafting skill rank is visible and announces progression milestones (e.g., "Your Smithing rank advances to Journeyman.")

## Notes
Quality variance must not feel arbitrary. The system should communicate clearly what factors influenced the roll so players understand what to optimize. Blind players relying on screen readers should never have to hunt through menus to find quality — it should be in the first line of item description.
