# INK-077: Billing History and Invoice Download

**As an** agency director,
**I want to** view my billing history and download invoices as PDFs,
**So that** I can submit expenses for client-billable prototype work.

**Primary Persona:** Aisha Mohammed (#7)
**Phase:** Platform
**Priority:** P2-Medium

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Billing history page lists all past charges with date, amount, description, and payment status
- [ ] Each invoice is downloadable as a PDF with company name, line items, and tax information
- [ ] Billing history includes both subscription charges and compute overage charges as separate line items
- [ ] User can update billing email address and company name for invoice generation
