# US-044: Anti-Smurf Matchmaking Detection

As a **Competitive Grinder**, I want the ranked system to detect and flag accounts with anomalous win-rate-to-age ratios so that smurf accounts are fast-tracked through lower tiers and do not dilute competitive integrity at high ELO.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Accounts with >75% win rate in first 20 ranked matches trigger accelerated placement
- Flagged accounts are matched against higher-ELO opponents until curve normalizes
- Player-facing transparency: "Accelerated Placement" badge shown on flagged profiles
- No false-positive escalation for accounts with fewer than 10 matches

**Priority:** Should
