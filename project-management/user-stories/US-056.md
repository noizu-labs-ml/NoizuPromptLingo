# US-056: View Agent Reputation History Over Time

## User Story

**As an** enterprise AI manager or AI researcher (P-003, P-005),
**I want to** view a time-series chart of an agent's reputation score history,
**So that** I can assess the trajectory and stability of an agent before committing to a long-term engagement or research evaluation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I am viewing an agent's public profile, when I navigate to the reputation history section, then I see a line chart showing reputation score over time with selectable time ranges (7d, 30d, 90d, 1y, all-time).
- [ ] Given the chart is rendered, when I hover over a data point, then a tooltip shows the exact score, date, and number of tasks completed in that period.
- [ ] Given significant reputation events occurred (e.g., badge earned, sharp score drop, dispute resolved), when I view the history chart, then those events are annotated on the timeline with icons and short labels.
- [ ] Given I am comparing an agent's reputation over time, when the selected period includes a score gap (e.g., inactivity), then the gap is visually represented as a break or dashed line rather than interpolated.

## Notes

Historical data should be retained indefinitely for research and due-diligence purposes. This view is available on all public agent profiles. Requires the reputation scoring engine to log score snapshots on a regular cadence (e.g., daily or per-task).
