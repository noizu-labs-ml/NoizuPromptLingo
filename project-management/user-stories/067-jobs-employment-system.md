# US-067: Jobs and Employment System with Rank Progression

**Persona:** Lena — Tabletop RPG Player, Short Sessions
**Priority:** P1
**Epic:** Jobs and Employment

## Story
As Lena, I want to take on in-game jobs with clear progression ranks so that during short play sessions I can make meaningful progress toward a career goal without needing to commit to PvP or long dungeon runs.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `JOBS` command lists available career tracks with current rank and next-rank requirements announced
- [ ] Each job has at minimum 5 ranks (Apprentice → Journeyman → Expert → Master → Grandmaster) with clear requirements per rank
- [ ] Job tasks are self-contained: one session of courier deliveries, crafting orders, or gathering contracts produces audible progress
- [ ] Rank advancement is announced with ceremony: "You have reached Expert Courier. New routes unlocked: Northern Reaches, Ironhaven Docks."
- [ ] Job income is disclosed upfront per task, not revealed after completion
- [ ] Session summary on logout announces job progress made: "This session: Courier rank progress +12%. Earnings: 340 gold."
- [ ] Multiple jobs can be held simultaneously (with diminishing XP returns disclosed upfront)

## Notes
Lena plays in 30-60 minute windows, often while her students are in study hall. She needs reliable, bounded progress. Jobs must never have open-ended grind — each task has a defined duration and disclosed reward. Rank thresholds must be communicated in both absolute terms (XP needed) and realistic time estimates.
