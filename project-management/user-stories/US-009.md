# US-009: Browse and Deploy Competitive Starter Templates

As a **Curious Casual**, I want a curated template gallery with beginner-friendly, competitive-viable fighter builds so that I can get into ranked play immediately without building from scratch.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The template gallery features at least 6 beginner templates tagged by playstyle (Aggressive, Defensive, Balanced, Evasive)
- Each template shows its current ranked win rate updated weekly so users can pick meta-viable options
- Deploying a template requires one tap and triggers a brief explanation of the build's strategy
- Templates can be customized after deployment; a "fork from template" button creates a user-owned copy in their Fighter Studio

**Priority:** Must
