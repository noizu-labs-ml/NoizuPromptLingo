# US-043: Fast Async Match Resolution with Notification

As a **Competitive Grinder**, I want ranked battles to resolve within 60 seconds of submission and push a notification with the result, so that I can queue continuously during content sessions without stalling between matches.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Server-side battle resolution completes in under 60 seconds from both submissions
- Push notification includes match result, ELO delta, and opponent name
- Notification deep-links directly to the battle replay
- Queue can be re-entered immediately after result is received

**Priority:** Must
