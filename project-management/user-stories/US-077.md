# US-077: No-FOMO Tournament Structure

**As a Dad Gamer,** I want ranked seasons to last at least four weeks and require no minimum daily participation so that I can compete meaningfully even with a 3-battles-per-week pace.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Ranked seasons are a minimum of 28 days in length
- Season rewards are gated on total battle count (e.g., 10 battles), not win-rate within a time window
- Progress toward season rewards is visible at all times without navigating to a separate event screen
- End-of-season notifications are sent 72 hours and 24 hours before close (opt-in)

**Priority:** Should
