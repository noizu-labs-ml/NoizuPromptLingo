# US-217: Mounted Combat

**Persona:** Tyler — MMO refugee, sighted, growth/clans focused
**Priority:** P2
**Epic:** Advanced Combat & Tactics

## Story
As Tyler, I want to fight from horseback with genuine mechanical advantages and risks so that mounts feel like a tactical choice in appropriate terrain rather than a fast-travel vehicle that disappears when fighting starts.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Mounts have their own stat block (speed, HP, stability, combat training level) accessible via SR; only combat-trained mounts available in mounted combat
- [ ] Mounted combat grants height advantage (inheriting US-206 high ground bonus), charge bonus on first attack if approaching at speed, and increased movement range per round
- [ ] Mount is a valid target for enemies; mount HP tracked separately in status channel: "Mount: Warhorse (68/80 HP)" — death of mount triggers dismount with fall damage narrated
- [ ] Dismounting available as a free action outside combat; during combat costs bonus action; forced dismount (mount death) triggers agility check to land safely
- [ ] Mounted player narration integrates mount: "Your warhorse surges forward — you drive your lance into the knight's chest from above"
- [ ] Terrain restrictions: mounts cannot enter buildings, narrow passages, or climb stairs; system announces restriction: "Your horse cannot follow you inside — you'll need to dismount"
- [ ] Charge mechanic: moving 30+ feet in a straight line before attack triggers Charge bonus; available once per combat; announced when conditions met: "Charge distance achieved — Charge Strike available this round"
- [ ] Mounted combat accessible without visual reference: all positional advantages, mount status, and terrain restrictions announced via SR

## Notes
Tyler wants the whole MMO package — mounts included. The P2 priority is appropriate as mounted combat requires solid positioning (US-206) and terrain systems first. The mount-as-target mechanic adds strategic depth: in large battles, killing the enemy commander's horse is a valid tactic, and Tyler should experience the panic of his warhorse going down mid-charge. The terrain restriction system must be proactive, not reactive — announcing the restriction before the player attempts to enter, not after a failed action. Charge mechanics reward the kind of tactical setup Tyler enjoys: positioning his cavalry at a distance and timing the charge. The dismount agility check creates a consistent risk model: falling off a dead warhorse at speed is dangerous, but a practiced rider can tuck and roll. Narration must always include the mount as an active participant — mounted combat should feel like a partnership, not just a stat buff. Mount status in the status channel alongside player HP gives Tyler a single place to track both.
