# US-062: AI Improvement Suggestions

## User Story

**As a** indie lifestyle blogger (P-001),
**I want to** receive specific AI-generated suggestions for improving my scores,
**So that** I know exactly what actions to take rather than guessing why my scores are low.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given my blog has been scored, when I view /dashboard/analytics, then I see an "Improvement Insights" panel with at least 3 AI-generated suggestions
- [ ] Given the suggestion panel renders, when I view a suggestion, then each suggestion includes: the target dimension, the specific issue identified, and a concrete actionable recommendation
- [ ] Given I have multiple dimensions with low scores, when suggestions are generated, then they are prioritized by estimated score impact (highest impact first)
- [ ] Given I am a Free tier user, when the suggestion panel renders, then I see 1 suggestion for my lowest-scoring dimension; Pro users see all suggestions for all dimensions
- [ ] Given a suggestion is shown, when I click "Mark as done," then the suggestion is archived and I am prompted to trigger a re-score to verify improvement
- [ ] Given my blog is re-scored after acting on a suggestion, when I return to the analytics page, then the suggestion panel refreshes with updated insights based on the new scores

## Notes

Suggestions are generated by the AI scoring engine at the time of scoring, not on-demand, to control LLM costs. Free tier gate (1 suggestion vs. all) is a key Pro upgrade driver. Suggestion content must be specific to the blog's actual content, not generic tips. See US-064 for analytics export.
