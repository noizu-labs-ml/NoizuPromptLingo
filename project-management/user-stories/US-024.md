# US-024: Replay Theater — Curated Community Feed

**As a Creator-Spectator,** I want to browse a curated Replay Theater feed sorted by votes, recency, and featured status so that I can discover high-quality matches worth analyzing and sharing.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Theater feed defaults to "Top This Week" with sort options: Top, New, Featured
- Each replay card shows fighter thumbnails, archetypes, match duration, and view count
- Players can upvote replays directly from the feed card without opening the replay
- Featured slot is editable by moderators and surfaced prominently at the top of the feed

**Priority:** Should
