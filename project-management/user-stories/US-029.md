# US-029: Student Account COPPA Safeguards

**As an Educator,** I want to onboard students under 13 through a parental/educator consent flow so that the game is legally compliant for classroom use and I can reassure administrators that student data is protected.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Accounts flagged as under-13 require educator or parental consent before activation
- Under-13 accounts cannot access community chat, public Laboratory, or external share links
- Personal data for under-13 accounts is stored in a separate data silo with restricted retention (90 days after account closure)
- Educator receives email confirmation when each student account is successfully consented and activated

**Priority:** Must
