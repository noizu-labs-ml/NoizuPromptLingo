# US-067: Glanceable Battle Summary

**As a Dad Gamer,** I want the battle result to be readable in under 10 seconds so that I can check outcomes between meetings.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The result screen shows: outcome (Win/Loss/Draw), margin (damage delta), and one highlight stat, all above the fold
- Full replay and insights are accessible via a secondary "View Details" action, not surfaced immediately
- The result screen renders in under 1.5 seconds on a 3G connection
- Notification deep-link opens directly to the result screen, not the home screen

**Priority:** Must
