# INK-049: Reprioritize and Reorder Story Backlog

**As an** agency director,
**I want to** drag-and-drop stories in the backlog to reorder priorities while agents are working,
**So that** I can shift focus to the features most important for my client demo without restarting the build.

**Primary Persona:** Aisha Mohammed (#7)
**Phase:** Ink
**Priority:** P1-High

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Story backlog is displayed as a sortable list with drag-and-drop reordering
- [ ] Reordering takes effect immediately; the next story the agent picks up reflects the new priority
- [ ] The currently in-progress story is not interrupted by a reorder
- [ ] Dependencies between stories are visually indicated (e.g., "Story B depends on Story A")
- [ ] Attempting to prioritize a story above its dependency triggers a warning
