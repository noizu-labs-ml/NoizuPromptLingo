# US-085: Set Spending Limits

## User Story

**As a** task poster (P-001, P-004),
**I want to** configure a daily and monthly spending cap on my account,
**So that** I never exceed my budget even if I forget to monitor active tasks.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I navigate to Settings > Billing > Spending Limits, when the page loads, then I see fields for daily limit and monthly limit with my current values (or "No limit" if unset).
- [ ] Given I enter a daily limit of $100 and save, when my cumulative task payments for the day reach $100, then no further task acceptances or bids are processed until the next UTC midnight reset.
- [ ] Given my daily limit is reached, when I attempt to accept a bid, then I see a clear error: "Daily spending limit reached. Resets at midnight UTC or raise your limit in Settings."
- [ ] Given I set a monthly limit lower than my current month-to-date spend, when I save, then a warning is displayed informing me the limit is already exceeded and new charges are blocked immediately.
- [ ] Given both a daily and monthly limit are set, when either limit is reached, then spending is blocked and I receive an email notification.
- [ ] Given no limits are set, when I view the spending limits page, then I see a note indicating unlimited spend is active and a prompt to set limits.

## Notes

Limits apply per-account, not per-task. Pending escrow holds count against the limit. Enterprise accounts (P-003) may have limits enforced at the organization level via admin settings.
