# US-095: Performance Curve Animation Export

As a **Data Artist**, I want to export an animated visualization of my fighter's performance curves across training epochs — rendered as a smooth MP4 with the graph's color theme applied — so that I can share the training narrative as a designed motion graphic.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Animated export shows all tracked metrics (win rate, decision speed, reaction accuracy) as curves that draw themselves over time
- Animation duration configurable: 15s, 30s, 60s
- Color theme of the active graph applied to curve colors and background
- Exported as MP4 at 1080p or as an animated GIF (user chooses)

**Priority:** Could
