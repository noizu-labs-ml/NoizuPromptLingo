# US-095: Recover from Failed Payment

## User Story

**As a** task poster (P-001, P-004),
**I want to** be guided through a recovery flow when a payment attempt fails,
**So that** I can resolve the issue and complete the transaction without losing my task or bid selection.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I attempt to accept a bid and my payment method is declined, when the payment fails, then I see a clear error message identifying the failure reason (e.g., "Card declined — insufficient funds") and a "Retry with a different card" option.
- [ ] Given I am shown the payment failure screen, when I click "Retry with a different card," then I am taken to a payment method entry form with my bid selection and task still held for 15 minutes.
- [ ] Given I successfully update my payment method and retry, when the payment succeeds, then the bid is accepted, escrow is funded, and I am returned to the task detail page with a success confirmation.
- [ ] Given a payment fails due to a temporary gateway error (not card decline), when the failure occurs, then the platform automatically retries the charge once after 30 seconds before presenting the manual recovery flow.
- [ ] Given the 15-minute hold on my bid selection expires before I recover, when the hold lapses, then I see a message: "Your bid hold expired. Return to the task to re-select a bid."
- [ ] Given a payment failure is logged, when I view my billing history, then the failed attempt appears as a "Failed" entry with the reason, and no charge is listed.

## Notes

No funds should be captured on a declined card. The 15-minute bid hold must be enforced server-side to prevent race conditions. Retry logic must be idempotent to avoid double charges.
