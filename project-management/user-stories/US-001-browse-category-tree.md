# US-001: Browse the Category Tree

## User Story

**As a** Web Nostalgia Explorer (P-001),
**I want to** see a hierarchical list of top-level categories on the homepage,
**So that** I can orient myself and choose a section of the web to explore the way I used to with old directories.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I land on the homepage, when the page loads, then I see all top-level categories displayed with their names and icons.
- [ ] Given I am viewing the category list, when I click a top-level category, then I am taken to that category's landing page.
- [ ] Given the category tree has subcategories, when I hover or tap a top-level category, then I can see a preview of its immediate subcategories.

## Notes

This is the entry point for all browsing-oriented personas. Top-level categories should be visually distinct and scannable at a glance. Related to US-002 (breadcrumbs) and US-004 (subcategory navigation).
