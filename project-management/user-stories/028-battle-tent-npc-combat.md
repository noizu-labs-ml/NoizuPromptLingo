# US-028: Battle Tent NPC Combat — Scripted Encounter Accessibility

**Persona:** Tyler — Sighted MMO refugee, wants player agency
**Priority:** P1
**Epic:** Combat — PvE

## Story
As Tyler, I want Battle Tent NPC opponents to have distinct combat personalities communicated through their action narration so that fights feel strategically varied and I can learn each opponent's patterns through repeated encounters.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Each Battle Tent NPC has a named combat style tag (e.g., "Aggressive," "Counter-fighter," "Attrition") surfaced in the pre-fight info panel
- [ ] NPC action narration reflects personality: an Aggressive NPC "surges forward, pressing the advantage before you can set your footing"; a Counter-fighter "holds ground, weight balanced, eyes tracking your weapon hand"
- [ ] Pattern hints embedded in narration after 3+ losses to same NPC: "Valdris always opens with a feint to your left — you've seen it before."
- [ ] NPC difficulty rating (1–5) announced before match with prose descriptor ("a formidable opponent")
- [ ] Victory and defeat scenes unique per NPC, not generic templates
- [ ] Rematch option immediately accessible after defeat without full menu traversal
- [ ] NPC win/loss record against the player accessible in pre-fight info
- [ ] All NPC dialogue and taunts compatible with screen reader — no image-only content

## Notes
Tyler is sighted but has abandoned visual MMOs — he values prose and agency. The Battle Tent is a sandbox for testing combat skills against curated opponents. NPC personality should make mechanical sense (Aggressive NPCs deal more damage but leave openings; Counter-fighters punish skill spam). Document the personality system so designers can add new NPCs without engineering support.
