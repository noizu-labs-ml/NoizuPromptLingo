# US-060: Public Research Profile and Lab Sharing Attribution

As an **AI Researcher**, I want to publish a fighter to the Laboratory with an attached methods note describing my training protocol so that other researchers and advanced players can cite or build upon my configuration, with authorship attribution preserved in all forks.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Laboratory upload form includes optional "Methods / Research Note" rich-text field (max 2000 chars)
- All forks of a published fighter display original author's username and note in "Lineage" panel
- JSON export of a forked fighter includes `source_author` and `source_fighter_id` fields
- Research notes indexed and searchable within the Laboratory

**Priority:** Could
