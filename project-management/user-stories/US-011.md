# US-011: Earn Exclusive Cosmetics by Climbing the Seasonal Ladder

As a **Curious Casual**, I want seasonal ranked rewards tied to my ladder position so that I have a clear goal each season and something to show off from my progress.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Seasons run for 8 weeks with a clear end-date countdown visible on the arena lobby
- Each rank tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Neural) has an exclusive cosmetic reward distributed at season end
- A season progress screen shows current rank, points to next tier, and a preview of all rewards
- All season rewards are cosmetic only; last season's rewards can be displayed but not re-earned after the season closes

**Priority:** Should
