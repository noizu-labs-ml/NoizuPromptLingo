# US-056: Seasonal Festivals as World Events

**Persona:** Carol — Parent of Blind Daughter (14) and Sighted Son (12)
**Priority:** P2
**Epic:** World Events & Emergence

## Story
As Carol, I want seasonal festivals to create shared, accessible world events that my daughter and son can experience together — blind and sighted — with age-appropriate content that parents can feel comfortable with.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] At least two seasonal festivals are defined in the world calendar (e.g., the Harvest Conclave in Rune, the Night of Veils in Mordoon)
- [ ] Festival events modify room descriptions, available NPCs, special items, and temporary quest hooks for their duration
- [ ] Festival content is accessible to all players regardless of visual ability — no festival mechanic requires visual UI
- [ ] Festival announcements appear via `news` and as in-world NPC chatter in the days before the event
- [ ] Temporary festival quests are appropriate for all age ranges: no graphic violence, no adult themes
- [ ] Festival mechanics include cooperative elements (players working together toward a shared goal)
- [ ] `calendar` command shows upcoming events and current festival status
- [ ] Festival content is pre-authored (not AI-generated) to ensure quality and appropriateness review

## Notes
- Carol's household: one blind 14-year-old, one sighted 12-year-old; both play together — shared experience is the design goal
- Age-appropriateness: dark fantasy tone is acceptable (this is a world with undead); graphic gore or adult content is not
- Cooperative festival mechanics help sighted and blind players work together rather than compete
- Festival duration: minimum 72 real hours to accommodate part-time players
- The Night of Veils in Mordoon connects to the "Night at Mordoon" Twine heritage — it should feel like a living version of that story
