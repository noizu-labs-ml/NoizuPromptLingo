# Violet Chronicle

**Narrative RPG / Puzzle Adventure / Interactive Fiction**

## Core Loop
Explore crystalline palaces solving temporal puzzles, then make narrative choices that ripple across time loops affecting both story branches and puzzle solutions.

## Features
- **Branching Time System**: Every major decision creates parallel timelines that you can switch between later in the story - solve puzzles differently by consulting your alternate self's experience
- **Crystalline Palace Puzzles**: Environmental puzzles that require timing-based solutions from multiple time periods - a locked door in the present requires a key you hid in the past
- **Narrative Memory**: The game remembers every choice you made across all timelines and references them in dialogue and environmental storytelling, creating a personal chronicle of your multiverse journey
- **Violet Orb Companion**: A sentient artifact that offers cryptic hints about puzzles and story, evolving its personality based on your moral choices across timelines
- **Leviathan Integration**: Boss encounters that are narrative choices disguised as combat - your approach to each leviathan determines which timeline fragment joins your consciousness

## Why This Is Awesome
This game revolutionizes storytelling by making time travel more than a plot device - it's the core mechanic that shapes both narrative AND gameplay:
- **Flow State Players**: The seamless interweaving of temporal puzzles and environmental exploration creates a meditative flow where time manipulation becomes instinctual rather than mechanical
- **Emergent Sandbox Gamers**: The branching timeline system creates emergent narrative paths that feel organic rather than predetermined - you'll discover story combinations the designers never explicitly scripted
- **Story-Driven Explorers**: A compelling mystery about the nature of time, identity, and the crystalline palaces that rewards curiosity and thoroughness - every timeline fragment reveals new lore and character development

The game's innovation lies in its commitment to narrative immersion where time travel feels meaningful rather than exploitative. You're not grinding for optimal outcomes - you're genuinely experiencing multiple versions of the same character's journey, and the game honors that by making every timeline matter. The violet orb creates personal attachment to your decisions while the leviathan encounters turn abstract moral choices into visceral, playable moments.
