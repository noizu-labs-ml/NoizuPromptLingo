# Sapphire Drake's Basin

**Action RPG / Metroidvania**

## Core Loop
Explore a vast interconnected underwater cave network as a geomancer who channels the crystalline power of ancient drake veins embedded in the stone. Each new ability unlocks previously inaccessible zones, while boss encounters against colossal aquatic drakes demand mastery of both terrain manipulation and fluid combat. Collect sapphire shards to deepen your connection to the basin's living geology, reshaping passages and summoning stone barriers at will.

## Features
- **Tectonic Dash**: Burst through fragile rock walls and create stone platforms mid-air by crystallizing minerals in the water around you
- **Drake Vein Forging**: Absorb elemental energy from defeated drakes to reforge your geomantic staff with new attack patterns and traversal abilities
- **Basin Mapping**: Chart the procedurally-shifted cave layout each run as tectonic events reshape corridors, secret chambers, and boss lairs

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will lose themselves in the rhythm of fluid underwater traversal, chaining tectonic dashes through crystalline corridors where every movement feels like surfing on liquid stone. **Challenge Seeker** players will find their fix in the punishing drake boss encounters that require reading geological tells and exploiting terrain weaknesses. **Story-Driven Explorer** players will uncover the tragic history of a civilization that tried to harness drake power and paid the price, told through environmental murals carved into living rock.
