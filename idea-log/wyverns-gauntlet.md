# Wyvern's Gauntlet

**Competitive Auto Battler / PvP**

## Core Loop
Draft a team of mythical creatures from a shared pool each match, position them on a hexagonal arena, and watch them battle in real-time with physics-driven combat where positioning and synergies determine the winner. Between rounds, evolve your creatures by combining duplicates, equip them with elemental relics, and adapt your formation to counter opponents in a last-team-standing tournament.

## Features
- **Physics-Driven Combat**: Creatures don't just exchange stat-driven attacks -- a roc's dive bomb creates wind currents that push lighter enemies off high ground, a hydra's heads independently target and can block projectiles aimed at allies, and a basilisk's gaze creates a literal cone of petrification
- **Terrain Draft System**: Before each match, both players vote on arena terrain from a rotating pool -- mountain peaks favor fliers, dense forests benefit ambush predators, open plains empower charging beasts -- creating strategic layering before combat even begins
- **Evolution Chains**: Every creature has 3 evolution tiers unlocked by combining duplicates, with branching paths at tier 2 -- your phoenix can evolve into either a blazing fire-support or a tanky reborn-phoenix that revives once per battle
- **Weekly Mutation Events**: Every week, a random mutation alters all creatures globally -- "Gravity Shift" makes all units fall slowly, "Primal Rage" doubles attack speed at low health -- keeping the meta from stagnating

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** competitive players will find deep strategic satisfaction in the draft phase where reading opponents, pivot-drafting, and understanding the 200+ creature synergy combinations separates good players from great ones, with ranked seasons and tournaments feeding the competitive fire.

**Emergent Sandbox** fans will discover that the physics-driven combat constantly produces unexpected interactions -- a minotaur's charge sends a displaced griffin into a basilisk's petrification cone, which blocks a wyvern's fireball, creating emergent narrative moments unique to each match.

**Flow State** gamers will fall into the hypnotic rhythm of draft-position-watch-adapt, where each round is a compact strategic loop that builds toward climactic final showdowns where every evolution choice and positioning decision is tested simultaneously.
