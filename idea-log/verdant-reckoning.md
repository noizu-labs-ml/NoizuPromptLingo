# Verdant Reckoning

**Action RPG / Botanical Soulslike**

## Core Loop

You play as a reborn druid wielding a cursed scimitar carved from the Heartwood, fighting through a fractal, ever-regrowing fortress garden. Every enemy you fell drops seeds that you plant mid-combat to grow weapons, barriers, and traps in real time. The fortress heals and reshapes itself between deaths -- rooms overgrow, corridors seal with thorns, new bloom-chambers open. Progression comes from cross-pollinating seed traits to breed increasingly exotic combat flora, while pushing deeper into the fortress to confront the serpentine guardian that poisons the root network at the world's core.

## Features

- **Living Arsenal**: Plant seeds mid-fight to grow vine-whips, thorn-barriers, spore-mines, and petal-shields. Each weapon has a growth timer -- plant early, harvest at peak power.
- **Ecosystem Crossbreeding**: Combine traits from harvested seeds in your greenhouse hub. Cross a frost-lily with a fire-bloom to create a steam-chrysanthemum that deals AoE scalding damage.
- **Adaptive Fortress**: The dungeon regrows between runs. Cleared rooms sprout new enemy colonies; walls crack open to reveal hidden grotto chambers. No two runs share the same layout.
- **Root Network Map**: An underground node-map shows which fortress zones are alive, dying, or corrupted. Severing root connections in one zone weakens enemies in connected zones.

## Why This Is Awesome

**Flow State** players will lose themselves in the rhythmic cycle of slash-plant-harvest-dodge. The seed-growing combat system creates a cadence that rewards mastery -- knowing exactly when to plant, when to parry, and when to let a growth bloom creates a trance-like engagement loop that deepens with every run.

**Challenge Seeker** players will find punishing boss encounters against the serpentine Verdant Wyrm and its corrupted lieutenants. The adaptive fortress ensures difficulty never stagnates -- the dungeon learns from your successes and grows deadlier layouts. Optional "Wither Mode" strips your greenhouse between runs for a true roguelike gauntlet.

**Emergent Sandbox** players will obsess over the crossbreeding system. With 120+ seed traits to combine, the discovery space is enormous. Community-sharing of rare hybrid recipes creates a meta-game of botanical experimentation, and the ecosystem map invites creative problem-solving over brute force.
