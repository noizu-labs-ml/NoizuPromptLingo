# Rift Biomancer

**Action RPG / Metroidvania**

## Core Loop
You play as a discordant biomancer -- a scientist exiled from the academies for practicing forbidden living-magic -- exploring a luminous rift that has split reality into layered biomes stacked vertically. Each layer is a distorted echo of the world above, corrupted by the rift's energy. You descend through these layers, absorbing genetic essence from defeated creatures to unlock biomantic abilities (growing chitinous armor, firing acidic spores, sprouting tendrils for grapple traversal), then backtracking through previously inaccessible areas with new powers. The central mystery: the rift resurrects the dead, but the resurrected come back wrong, and your own mutations are accelerating.

## Features
- **Living Mutation Tree**: Instead of a traditional skill tree, abilities grow organically based on what creatures you absorb. Consuming a sphinx grants puzzle-sight (revealing hidden platforms). Consuming a kraken fragment unlocks pressure-manipulation attacks. Mutations are permanent and visible on your character model -- by endgame you are barely recognizable as human.
- **Rift Layering**: The world exists in overlapping parallel states. A button press shifts you between the material layer and the rift layer, each with different enemies, platforms, and physics. Some puzzles require existing in both simultaneously by splitting your biomass into two controlled halves.
- **Discordant Resonance**: Combat revolves around frequency. Every enemy broadcasts a visual "tone" (color + rhythm). Match your attack frequency to theirs for critical damage; clash frequencies to stagger them. This creates a rhythm-combat layer on top of standard action RPG mechanics.
- **Soul Gardening**: Between expeditions, tend a garden in your sanctuary. Plant harvested souls to grow consumable buffs, but neglected souls become hostile ghosts that invade your garden and must be fought off -- turning your hub area into a secondary management challenge.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will enter a trance as the rhythm-combat system clicks. Matching frequencies becomes muscle memory, and the biomantic traversal (tendril grapple, spore double-jump, chitin shield-slide) creates an intoxicating movement vocabulary that makes backtracking feel like playground parkour rather than chores.

**Emergent Sandbox** players will experiment endlessly with the living mutation tree. Because abilities are determined by what you absorb rather than a fixed progression, different playthroughs yield dramatically different builds. Combine unexpected mutations to discover hidden synergies -- sphinx puzzle-sight plus kraken pressure sense reveals secret areas neither ability finds alone.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will be drawn into the central mystery of the rift's resurrection cycle. NPCs you meet in early layers die and return as corrupted versions later, their memories fragmented but their personalities persistent. Environmental storytelling through the shifting layers reveals a civilization that tried to harness the rift and was consumed by it.
