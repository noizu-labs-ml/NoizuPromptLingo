# Void Architect

**Building & Crafting**

## Core Loop
Build your void by summoning ancient amulets from shadowy structures and crafting them into crimson towers that defend against specters attempting to breach your dimensional sanctuary.

## Features
- **Amulets-as-Resources**: Instead of traditional wood or stone, you harvest and craft with ancient amulets that each contain unique dimensional properties - gravity wells, time dilation, matter transmutation, and more
- **Shadow Structure Crafting**: Build structures that exist partially in shadow realms, making them immune to specter corruption but vulnerable to environmental destabilization
- **Crimson Defense Matrix**: Create interconnected defense networks where specters that breach one tower alert all others, cascading automated counter-summons across your void sanctuary
- **Void Expansion Mechanics**: Your void grows not by clearing land, but by stabilizing areas of chaos and making them habitable - expansion summons stronger specters but also yields more powerful amulets
- **Spectral Economy**: Defeated specters drop crystallized dread that can be refined into building materials or used to temporarily power your structures's most dangerous abilities

## Why This Is Awesome
**For Flow State Players**: Watching your void扩张 (expand) is deeply satisfying. Each new amulet unlocks possibilities, each built structure serves multiple purposes, and optimizing your layout for maximum defense and efficiency becomes a meditative puzzle. The game respects your time - buildings work while you're away, and returning to see your sanctuary thriving is consistently rewarding.

**For Emergent Sandbox Players**: The combination of amulet properties, shadow mechanics, and specter enemy types creates endless design space. Want a wall that exists only when you're looking at it? Build it from shadow amulets. Need a tower that slows enemies? Link multiple time dilation amulets. The game never forces one "right" solution - your creativity is the limit.

**For Story-Driven Explorers**: Each ancient amulet you discover comes with a memory of the civilization that created it and the specific specter that corrupted it. Building with these memories means your void is a museum of fallen worlds, and the specters attacking you are personal - they're drawn to the amulets you've claimed from the civilizations they destroyed.
