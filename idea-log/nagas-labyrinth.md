# Naga's Labyrinth

**Stealth Horror / Puzzle Platformer**

## Core Loop
You are a naga -- half serpent, half humanoid -- trapped in a shifting labyrinth built by a mad sorcerer who collects "perfect specimens." Use your serpentine body to slither through tight passages, coil around mechanisms, and climb surfaces no human could. Avoid the sorcerer's other captives who have been driven mad by their imprisonment, solve environmental puzzles to open new wings of the labyrinth, and unravel the sorcerer's obsession to find the exit.

## Features
- **Serpentine Movement System**: Your body is a physics-driven snake from the waist down, allowing you to coil around pillars to reach heights, slither through cracks in walls to access hidden areas, and constrict mechanisms to solve puzzles -- but your long body also means you take up more space and are harder to hide
- **Mad Inhabitant AI**: Other trapped creatures roam the labyrinth with unique AI behaviors -- a blinded cyclops that hunts by sound, a gorgon that petrifies anything in direct line of sight, a minotaur that charges at movement -- each requiring different stealth strategies
- **Labyrinth Shifts**: The maze reconfigures itself on a timer, with walls sliding, corridors collapsing, and new passages opening. You must learn to read the architectural patterns to predict shifts and avoid being crushed or trapped
- **Specimen Journal**: Find journal entries from previous captives documenting their survival strategies, map fragments, and gradual descent into madness, providing both puzzle clues and narrative depth

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** stealth fans will find a genuinely unique challenge in the serpentine movement system where hiding is harder because of your size, but your climbing and coiling abilities open stealth routes that no bipedal character could access, creating a fresh take on a genre that often relies on the same crouch-and-hide mechanics.

**Aesthetic Purist** players will be captivated by the labyrinth's architectural horror -- impossible geometries inspired by Escher and Gaudi, lit by bioluminescent fungi and the eerie glow of magical barriers, with each wing of the maze having a distinct visual identity tied to the sorcerer's obsession with different creature types.

**Story-Driven Explorer** audiences will piece together a dark narrative from environmental clues and specimen journals, gradually understanding that the sorcerer isn't just a collector but is trying to assemble a "perfect chimera" from the parts of his captives -- and your naga form is his masterpiece in progress.
