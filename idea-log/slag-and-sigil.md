# Slag and Sigil

**Tactical RPG / Soulslike**

## Core Loop
Lead a warband of cursed blacksmith-soldiers through a dying world where every weapon you forge carries both mechanical stats and narrative consequences. Combat is turn-based tactical on hex grids, but between battles you operate a traveling forge where smelting down captured enemy equipment yields both crafting materials and fragmented memories of your foes. The sigils you engrave on weapons determine which moral path your warband follows, and there is no going back once a weapon has been blooded.

## Features
- **Memory Smelting**: Break down enemy gear in your traveling forge to extract combat knowledge (new abilities) and story fragments that reveal the enemy's perspective on the war
- **Sigil Consequence System**: Each rune engraved on a weapon permanently shifts your warband's alignment, unlocking different skill trees, story branches, and ending possibilities
- **Hexgrid Carnage**: Tight tactical encounters on destructible terrain where positioning, elevation, and environmental hazards created by forge-sparks can turn the tide

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** players will find deep satisfaction in the permadeath tactical encounters where every unit lost is a named character with their own forging specialty gone forever. **Story-Driven Explorer** players will be captivated by the dual narrative, seeing both sides of the war through the memories extracted from smelted equipment. **Flow State** players will enter a trance-like rhythm alternating between methodical tactical combat and meditative weapon crafting.
