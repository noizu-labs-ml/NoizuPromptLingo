# Phoenix Architect

**Building Crafting / Metroidvania**

## Core Loop
You are a phoenix reborn into a world that has been consumed by frost. Your innate fire is the last warmth in existence. Explore a vast frozen interconnected map, using your flames to melt barriers, forge tools, and build thermal safe zones that serve as checkpoints and fast-travel hubs. Each death triggers your rebirth cycle where you retain blueprints but lose physical structures, forcing you to rebuild smarter each cycle.

## Features
- **Thermal Metroidvania**: Your phoenix fire unlocks new areas -- melt ice walls to reveal passages, warm ancient mechanisms to activate elevators, and ignite celestial forges that grant new crafting recipes, with each upgrade opening previously inaccessible zones
- **Death and Rebirth Building**: When you die, your structures melt away but your blueprints persist. Each rebirth lets you rebuild your thermal stations faster and in better locations, creating a unique loop where death is a design iteration, not a punishment
- **Fire Management**: Your inner flame is a finite resource that depletes as you explore, fight, and build. Gather soul embers from defeated ice creatures to refuel, or sacrifice permanent maximum health to kindle critical structures in emergencies
- **Seasonal Frost Events**: Periodic deep-freeze events advance across the map, destroying unprotected structures and forcing migration to new zones, ensuring the world never feels fully tamed

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will find meditative satisfaction in the core loop of explore, build, die, rebuild where each cycle runs smoother than the last, creating a rhythm of iterative improvement that taps into the same compulsion as factory optimization games but with organic, architectural expression.

**Aesthetic Purist** audiences will be captivated by the visual contrast between the warm amber glow of your phoenix structures spreading across a monochromatic frozen world -- every safe zone you build is a brushstroke of warmth and color painting across a canvas of blue-white ice.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will uncover the history of the frozen world through exploration -- who built the ancient forges, why the frost came, and what the phoenix truly is -- with environmental storytelling that rewards curiosity and a narrative that reframes the death-rebirth mechanic as thematic expression.
