# Starsong Chronicle

**Strategy Simulation**

## Core Loop
Build and expand your luminous celestial palace through interstellar exploration, research ancient artifacts, recruit legendary subjects from across the cosmos, and defend against hydras born from cosmic chaos.

## Features
- City-building in low-gravity celestial environments with physics-based architecture
- Interstellar diplomacy with 12 alien species, each with unique abilities and cultural quirks
- Resource management balancing cosmic energy, starlight essence, and void matter
- Tactical combat system where hydras attack from multiple dimensions simultaneously
- Timeline manipulation mechanic allowing you to revisit past decisions and alter outcomes
- Procedural galaxy generation with over 100 unique star systems to explore
- Musical soundtrack that adapts to your palace's architectural harmony (Star Song System)

## Why This Is Awesome
**Emergent Sandbox** players will love the freedom to design their celestial palace from scratch, experimenting with alien technologies and creating unique societal structures that lead to unexpected diplomatic outcomes. **Story-Driven Explorers** can uncover the mystery of the cosmic hydras through ancient star maps, philosophical debates with alien scholars, and the tragic history of failed celestial dynasties. **Flow State** players achieve immersion through the intricate yet intuitive systems, where hours pass as you balance resources, manage galactic politics, and orchestrate defense against increasingly complex hydra waves.
