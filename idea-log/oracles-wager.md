# Oracle's Wager

**Tactical RPG / Deckbuilder**

## Core Loop
You play as an oracle who sees 3 seconds into the future during combat. Build a deck of prophetic cards that let you manipulate enemy turns, reposition allies, and alter outcomes -- but every prophecy you use costs "certainty," and when your certainty runs out, you lose your future sight and must fight blind. Balance the temptation of perfect information against the resource that powers it.

## Features
- **Foresight System**: At the start of each enemy turn, you see a ghostly preview of exactly what will happen -- which characters will be attacked, where AOEs will land, who will be targeted by debuffs. You then play cards to alter this future before it becomes real
- **Certainty Resource**: Every card played costs certainty that regenerates slowly. Overuse prophecy and you fight blind with powerful units but no information. Hoard certainty and you never have enough cards to change the future you see
- **Prophecy Deck Construction**: Build your deck from 200+ cards organized into foresight schools -- Temporal (delay enemy actions), Spatial (reposition units), Causal (change attack targets), and Entropic (introduce randomness to enemy rolls)
- **Paradox Boss Fights**: Major bosses exist outside linear time and their turns are partially hidden even with foresight, creating puzzle-like encounters where you must deduce their patterns from incomplete information

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** tactical RPG fans will find their genre expertise tested by the foresight system that fundamentally changes how turn-based combat works -- instead of reacting to what happened, you are preventing what *will* happen, requiring a completely different cognitive framework that rewards predictive thinking over reactive optimization.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will be drawn into a narrative about the burden of knowing the future -- the oracle's story explores whether changing fate is worth the cost, with multiple endings determined by how much certainty you sacrificed throughout the campaign versus how much you hoarded for yourself.

**Flow State** deckbuilder enthusiasts will find addictive rhythm in the see-plan-alter cycle of each turn, where the foresight glimpse creates immediate tension, the card play provides satisfying problem-solving, and watching the altered future play out differently than the prophecy delivers a consistent dopamine loop.
