# Rift Bard Chronicles

**Rhythm Action RPG**

## Core Loop

You are a fractured blood mage who casts spells exclusively through rhythm — every ability is a musical phrase played in real-time combat. Explore a luminous overworld broken into coral-themed biomes, each with its own musical key and tempo. Encounter mimics disguised as treasure, NPCs, and even save points. Combat requires matching the beat while adapting to enemy attack patterns that try to disrupt your rhythm. Transcend into blood-mage overdrive by performing perfect 16-bar combos that sacrifice HP for screen-clearing damage. Between battles, uncover the bayou's hidden melody — a meta-rhythm woven through the entire soundtrack that reveals secret paths when hummed into your grimoire at specific locations.

## Features

- **Rhythm-Combat Fusion**: Every spell is a real-time musical phrase. Light attacks are quarter notes, heavy attacks are eighth-note runs, defensive abilities are sustained tones. The combat soundtrack is the product of your inputs layered over the enemy's patterns — boss fights sound like duets.
- **Blood Cadence System**: Sacrifice HP to enter Blood Cadence mode, which doubles tempo and unlocks devastating blood-mage abilities. Staying in Blood Cadence too long causes your health to wither permanently for the rest of the dungeon run. Skilled players ride the edge, entering and exiting cadence at optimal moments.
- **Displacer Beast Chase Sequences**: In certain biomes, a displacer beast stalks you with its own rhythm — a bass ostinato that grows louder as it approaches. You must navigate platforming sections while maintaining your exploration melody, or the beast catches you and forces a rhythm duel.
- **Grimoire Arranger**: Between dungeons, arrange discovered melody fragments in your grimoire to create custom spell loadouts. Different arrangements produce different spell effects — the same notes in a different order can be a fireball or a heal.

## Why This Is Awesome

**Flow State**: This is peak flow-state design. The rhythm-combat fusion means the game literally measures your flow — perfect timing creates musical consonance that buffs damage, while mistakes create dissonance that weakens you. Players who achieve sustained flow don't just play better, they sound better. The game's soundscape becomes a real-time assessment of player performance.

**Challenge Seeker**: Blood Cadence is a glass-cannon death spiral that demands frame-perfect rhythm execution while your health drains. The displacer beast chase sequences add lateral pressure — you're maintaining rhythm while platforming while being hunted. The grimoire arranger creates theory-crafting depth: the meta is discovering non-obvious note arrangements that break encounters.

**Aesthetic Purist**: Coral and luminous gold palette across all biomes, each with a distinct color temperature matching its musical key. The visual presentation synchs to the rhythm — the environment pulses, particles dance on beat, and the UI is minimal but musically expressive. The displacer beast is rendered as a blur of shifting afterimages, perfectly conveying its teleporting nature.
