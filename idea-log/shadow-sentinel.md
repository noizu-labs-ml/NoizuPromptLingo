# Shadow Sentinel

**Action Adventure / Roguelite**

## Core Loop
Guide a lone shadow sentinel through procedurally generated abyssal strongholds to extinguish primordial flames and restore the eternal light to a realm consumed by darkness.

## Features
- Procedural dungeon generation across 5 elemental planes
- Shadow-based movement and combat mechanics
- Light and darkness manipulation abilities
- Roguelite progression with permanent upgrades
- Boss encounters against abyssal champions
- Hidden lore and secrets in each stronghold

## Why This Is Awesome
For the **Flow State** persona: Smooth, responsive movement systems create satisfying combat encounters with risk-reward positioning
For the **Challenge Seeker** persona: Progressive difficulty and permadeath create meaningful mastery loops
For the **Aesthetic Purist** persona: Striking visual contrast between shadow environments and light abilities with beautiful particle effects
