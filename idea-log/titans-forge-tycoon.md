# Titan's Forge Tycoon

**Idle Tycoon / Crafting Simulation**

## Core Loop
Run a legendary forge built inside the heart of a dormant titan, crafting magical weapons for passing heroes who need equipment for their adventures. Manage production chains, unlock mythical crafting techniques, and gradually reawaken the titan whose dreams provide the raw magical energy powering your forge. Balance customer satisfaction, resource gathering, and the titan's awakening cycle.

## Features
- **Titan Dream Mining**: The titan dreams in real-time, and different dream phases produce different magical ores -- nightmare phases yield shadow steel, nostalgia phases produce mithril, prophetic dreams create rare chronium that can only be forged during specific time windows
- **Hero Customer System**: AI-driven heroes arrive with specific quest requirements and budgets, and the weapons you craft actually determine whether they succeed or fail on their adventures -- successful heroes return with more gold and rare materials, failed ones never come back
- **Automated Production Lines**: Hire and train dwarven smiths, enchantment specialists, and quality inspectors to run assembly lines that produce weapons while you're offline, with upgradeable stations and a tech tree spanning 100+ crafting innovations
- **Titan Awakening Events**: Every two weeks (real-time), the titan stirs and the forge enters an "awakening surge" where production speeds triple, rare materials flood in, but the forge geometry shifts as the titan moves, requiring you to redesign your production layout

## Why This Is Awesome
**Emergent Sandbox** players will love optimizing the sprawling production chains where every decision about forge layout, worker assignment, and resource allocation creates emergent efficiency puzzles, and the hero success/failure feedback loop generates emergent narrative about the adventurers whose lives depend on your craftsmanship.

**Flow State** idle game fans will find the perfect balance of active engagement and passive progression, where checking in to reorganize during titan dream transitions, filling rush orders for desperate heroes, and tweaking production ratios provides satisfying micro-decisions in compact play sessions.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will gradually piece together the titan's history through the dream mining mechanic -- each ore vein contains fragments of memory that reveal who the titan was, why it sleeps, and what happens when it fully awakens, creating a slow-burn narrative payoff beneath the tycoon gameplay.
