# Phantom Gravity Coliseum

**Fighting Game / Competitive Arena Brawler**

## Core Loop
Select from a roster of phantom warriors -- spectral combatants bound to a floating coliseum suspended in a gravity flux zone -- and fight in 2.5D arena battles where gravity itself is a weapon. Each phantom wields a signature weapon and a unique gravity manipulation ability. Between rounds, you channel souls harvested from victories to upgrade your phantom's corporeal form, trading ethereal evasion for raw physical power. The coliseum's gravity field rotates, shifts, and inverts mid-match, transforming every stage into a dynamic puzzle of positioning. Ranked play, seasonal ladders, and tournament brackets form the competitive backbone.

## Features
- **Gravity Phases**: Arenas cycle through gravity states every 15 seconds -- normal, zero-G, inverted, and directional pull. Combatants must adapt combos and movement on the fly. A launcher that works in normal gravity whiffs in zero-G; anti-air becomes anti-ground when inverted.
- **Soul Economy**: Winning rounds earns soul fragments. Between matches in a set, allocate souls to buff damage, speed, armor, or gravity control. This creates a meta-layer: do you invest in raw power or in the ability to dictate when gravity shifts occur?
- **Phantom Desync**: Each character has a "desync meter" that, when full, lets you split into two phantom copies for 3 seconds. Control both simultaneously -- one attacks while the other positions -- then collapse back into one, retaining whichever copy was in a superior position.
- **Coliseum Wagering**: In ranked mode, players can wager ranked points on specific gravity phases or round outcomes, adding a psychological warfare dimension. Bluff your build, then punish the opponent's misread.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** players will find a deep competitive well. The gravity phase system means there is no static meta -- optimal play requires reading the rotation timer, adapting combos to the upcoming gravity state, and punishing opponents who fail to transition. Frame data alone is not enough; spatial awareness under shifting physics is the skill ceiling.

**Flow State** players will experience the unique rhythm of adapting to gravity transitions. The 15-second cycle creates a natural beat, and mastering the flow between states feels like learning to dance with the arena itself. High-level play looks choreographed despite being entirely reactive.

**Competitive/Esports** -- The soul economy between rounds adds a strategic layer beyond pure execution. Spectators can read build investments and predict match dynamics, making it watchable as well as playable. The wagering system creates narrative tension for streaming audiences.
