# Serpent's Registry

**Detective Mystery / Narrative Adventure**

## Core Loop
You are a serpent oracle serving as magical coroner for a sprawling fantasy city, using your venom-derived divination to read the final memories of the dead. Each case pulls you deeper into a web of political conspiracy, cult activity, and ancient curses. Investigate crime scenes, interrogate suspects, and piece together contradictions in testimony to build a case file that determines how justice is served.

## Features
- **Venom Divination Mechanic**: Inject controlled doses of magical venom into corpses to experience fragmented flashbacks of their final moments -- too much venom and the vision becomes incoherent, too little and critical details are missing
- **Contradiction Board**: A physical evidence board where you pin testimonies, forensic clues, and flashback fragments, then draw connections that reveal lies and hidden relationships between suspects
- **Branching Case Outcomes**: Every case has 3-5 valid resolutions depending on which evidence you prioritize, with consequences that ripple through later cases and alter which factions gain power in the city
- **Living City Simulation**: NPCs have schedules, secrets, and relationships that evolve between cases -- a witness in case one might become a suspect in case four based on your prior decisions

## Why This Is Awesome
**Story-Driven Explorer** players will be captivated by the layered narrative where every corpse tells a story, every suspect has motives that shift based on your prior cases, and the overarching conspiracy unfolds with genuine literary craft across a 30-hour campaign.

**Aesthetic Purist** audiences will be drawn to the distinctive art direction -- a moody, oil-painting visual style that renders the fantasy city in rich amber and deep indigo tones, with the venom divination sequences rendered as surreal watercolor dreamscapes.

**Challenge Seeker** puzzle fans will find satisfying difficulty in the contradiction system where the game never hand-holds, rewarding careful observation of NPC behavior, environmental details, and the subtle inconsistencies between flashback fragments and witness statements.
