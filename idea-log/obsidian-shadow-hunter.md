# Obsidian Shadow Hunter

**Action Adventure**

## Core Loop
Become an ethereal shadowmancer, transmute through the twilight zone to hunt the obsidian chimera, and summon manifestations to harvest its scattered essence fragments.

## Features
- **Dual-phase combat**: Ethereal form allows transmuting matter; shadow form allows absorbing attacks. Switch mid-combo to exploit chimera weaknesses
- **Twilight zone traversal**: Navigate between material, spectral, and void layers of the twilight zone. Each layer alters physics, enemy behavior, and essence distribution
- **Chimera mutation tree**: The obsidian chimera evolves each hunt based on your transmutation choices—fire-heavy builds spawn pyro-clad variants, shadow builds spawn void-scarred forms
- **Essence crafting system**: Harvested essence shards combine into spells, weapons, and transmutation catalysts. High-risk essence yields devastating but corrupted abilities
- **Boss rush cycles**: Every 5 hunts, the chimera manifests fully for a world-shattering battle. Defeat it to claim rare essence and unlock twilight zone layers
- **Cooperative manifestation**: Summon other players' shadowmancer echoes during boss fights—your manifestations fight alongside theirs in asynchronous play

## Why This Is Awesome
- **Flow State**: Seamless form-switching combat creates trance-like rhythm—dash through matter as ethereal, phase to shadow to absorb a blow, counter with a transmuted blade
- **Story-Driven Explorer**: The twilight zone holds the ruins of fallen shadowmancer civilizations; uncover their journals, learn their transmutations, understand why the obsidian chimera now seeks all essence
- **Challenge Seeker**: Chimera adaptation forces build mastery and experimentation. Mastering all forms requires hundreds of hunts across evolving difficulty curves
