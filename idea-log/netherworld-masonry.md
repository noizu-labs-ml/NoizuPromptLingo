# Netherworld Masonry

**Strategy Tycoon / Building**

## Core Loop
Construct necromantic fortresses from cursed materials, command skeletal laborers, and defend against luminous angelic invasions seeking to cleanse your shadowy domain.

## Features
- Dark building system: craft structures from bones, shadows, and abyssal stone
- Undead workforce: recruit and optimize skeletons, zombies, and wraiths for specialized tasks
- Angelic raid defense: repel celestial invaders with shadow traps, unholy enchantments, and tactical deployment
- Resource management: balance cursed essence, soul fragments, and raw materials from the underworld
- Procedural dungeons: expand territory into procedurally generated abyssal layers
- Multiplayer raids: invade other necromancers' fortresses and steal their treasures

## Why This Is Awesome
**For Flow State Players:** Enter a satisfying rhythm of resource gathering, construction, and defense that reaches hypnotic efficiency - your skeletal workforce moves like clockwork as your fortress rises from the void.

**For Emergent Sandbox Players:** Discover unique fortress designs through experimentation - layer shadow wards with necromantic traps, create choke-points that funnel angels into kill zones, or build impenetrable fortresses that defy conventional siege tactics.

**For Challenge Seekers:** Test your strategic mettle against increasingly sophisticated angelic assaults that adapt to your defenses, requiring constant innovation and tactical brilliance.
