# Platinum Sprite's Bazaar

**Narrative Trading Card Economy Sim**

## Core Loop

You manage a magical bazaar in a crossroads town where adventurers, merchants, and wandering creatures come to trade. Instead of direct combat, you play a card-based negotiation game with every visitor. Each visitor has desires (what they want), secrets (what they'll accept but won't admit), and patience (how many rounds of haggling before they leave). You build a deck of negotiation cards -- flattery, appraisal, bluff, bundle deals, loyalty discounts -- and play them to close trades at maximum profit. Between customers, you invest profits into bazaar expansions, hire staff with unique card abilities, and unlock new visitor types.

## Features
- **Desire Web System**: Every visitor's desires are connected in a hidden web. Fulfilling one desire reveals adjacent desires. A warrior who buys a sword might secretly need healing potions but won't ask unless you offer first. Reading body-language tells reveals hidden desire nodes.
- **Staff Card Synergies**: Hired staff add persistent card effects to your deck. A dwarven appraiser lets you see the true value of any item. A goblin haggler reduces customer patience drain by 1 per round. Staff can be combined at the market board for dual-ability cards.
- **Bazaar Reputation**: Your trading decisions affect faction reputations. Giving fair deals to elf merchants improves elf visitor frequency but angers the dwarf guild. Faction alignment unlocks exclusive inventory and legendary visitor encounters.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Story-Driven Explorer** players will be drawn into the visitor narratives -- every customer has a backstory revealed through trading dialogue and desire webs. The warrior buying a sword is preparing for a quest you learn about through subsequent visits. Regular customers become recurring characters with evolving storylines.

**Emergent Sandbox** fans will experiment with staff combinations, faction alignments, and card synergies to discover optimal bazaar builds. The desire web system means there is always a more profitable trade to find if you invest in the right intel cards.

**Flow State** enthusiasts will settle into the rhythm of customer after customer, building combo chains of negotiation cards that cascade into perfect trades. The card-play mechanic is fast and satisfying once you learn to read visitor tells.
