# Withering Rift Casino

**Idle Tycoon / Deckbuilder Roguelite**

## Core Loop

You manage an interdimensional casino built inside a fractured rift floating above a withering bayou. attract supernatural patrons — mimics in disguise, displacer beasts between hunts, blood mages on break — and extract their essence through rigged games, ambient coral absorption, and strategic bar tabs. Between idle phases, enter rift expeditions as your berserker bouncer: deckbuilder combat encounters where your deck is built from the games and amenities you've installed in the casino. Better casino = better cards. The casino generates resources while you're offline, but the rift is unstable — neglect maintenance and entire wings collapse into the bayou, destroying your investments and releasing angry patrons into the facility.

## Features

- **Casino-Deck Integration**: Every slot machine, card table, and amenity you install doubles as a combat card. Install a "Coral Roulette Table" and you gain a gambling card that deals random damage. Build a "Blood Mage Lounge" and gain a life-drain card. Your casino layout IS your deck strategy.
- **Patron Ecology**: Different supernatural beings are attracted to different amenities and each leaves different resources. Mimics drop treasure but might steal from your vault. Displacer beasts pay well in rift crystals but occasionally teleport your furniture into other dimensions. Balancing your guest list is a management puzzle.
- **Rift Decay System**: The rift your casino sits in slowly collapses. You must periodically send your berserker on maintenance expeditions — deckbuilder runs into the rift's infrastructure — to reinforce the foundation. Fail to maintain the rift and rooms start falling off, permanently destroying their associated cards.
- **Oracle's Odds**: A resident oracle NPC offers to rig games in your favor... for a price. Each rigging boosts idle income but increases the chance that patrons discover the deception and riot. Riots are special crisis events that require emergency deckbuilder combat to resolve.

## Why This Is Awesome

**Flow State**: The idle-tycoon baseline provides a constantly satisfying feedback loop — numbers go up, casino expands, new patrons arrive. The deckbuilder expeditions break the idle rhythm with focused combat challenges that test your deck-building decisions. Returning from an expedition to find your casino has earned enough to unlock a new wing is a perfect reward loop.

**Challenge Seeker**: The rift decay creates real stakes for neglect. On harder modes, decay accelerates and expeditions are longer and more dangerous. Patron riots cascade — one angry mimic can trigger a chain reaction. The deckbuilder combat has permadeath for your bouncer; if your berserker falls, the casino runs unattended until you can afford a replacement, losing rooms and patrons the whole time.

**Aesthetic Purist**: The casino aesthetic is Art Deco meets coral reef — geometric brass fixtures dripping with luminous organic growths, velvet table felt that pulses with bioluminescence, and a rift-sky visible through glass ceilings showing the withering bayou below. Patron designs are characterful and immediately readable: mimics shimmer at the edges, displacer beasts leave afterimage trails as they walk between tables.
