# Primal Druid Bayou

**Strategy Simulation / Base Defense**

## Core Loop
You command a druid sent to reclaim a cursed bayou from an infestation of chimeras. Each cycle, you place totems that channel nature magic, summon dryad defenders, and reshape the terrain itself to funnel monstrous waves into killing corridors. Between waves you forage for reagents, brew potions that grant temporary mutations to your defenders, and unlock deeper roots of the World Tree to expand your territory into increasingly hostile swamp zones.

## Features
- **Terrain Sculpting**: Raise earthen walls, flood channels, and grow thorn barricades in real-time to redirect enemy pathing each wave
- **Totem Synergy System**: Combine elemental totems (fire + water = steam vent, earth + shadow = grasping cryptvine) for emergent defensive combos
- **Dryad Evolution Tree**: Your defenders mutate based on which potions and environmental conditions they experience, producing dozens of hybrid forms

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will lose themselves in the rhythmic cycle of wave defense, foraging, and base expansion -- each phase flows naturally into the next with no jarring transitions or UI interruptions.

**Emergent Sandbox** players will discover that totem synergies and terrain manipulation create dozens of viable strategies, and experimenting with weird combinations (what happens if I flood a steam vent into a shadow totem?) is consistently rewarded.

**Aesthetic Purist** players will be drawn to the lush, painterly bayou environments -- hanging moss swaying in real-time, bioluminescent fungi pulsing with each wave, water reflections that shift with the time-of-day cycle.
