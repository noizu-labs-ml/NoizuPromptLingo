# Plasma Scars of the Leviathan

**Extraction Shooter / Roguelite**

## Core Loop

You are a berserker spellsword who descends into the underbelly of a dying sky realm -- a vertical abyss where the corpse of a leviathan slowly dissolves, bleeding raw plasma into the environment and spawning nightmarish fragment creatures from its scar tissue. Each run begins at the sky realm's edge. You drop into the leviathan's body through a procedural wound system (no two descents use the same entry scar). Inside, you fight through biomes made of crystallized plasma, calcified memories, and still-beating organs the size of cathedrals. Your grappling hook is your lifeline -- it anchors you to flesh-walls during plasma storms, swings you across arterial chasms, and reels in loot from unstable zones. Every run you push deeper, extract what you can, and escape before the leviathan's immune response (a white-blood-cell blizzard) overwhelms you. Die and you leave your scars behind -- your next character can find your previous run's corpse, loot it, and inherit one fractured memory from the fallen berserker.

## Features

- **Organic Dungeon Generation**: The leviathan's interior is procedurally generated from a scar-seed system. Each wound you enter generates a unique layout of chambers, arteries, and plasma lakes. The deeper you go, the more alien and hostile the environment becomes -- walls pulse, floors shift, and the ambient temperature rises until your screen shimmers with heat distortion.
- **Plasma Attunement**: Harvest raw plasma from the leviathan's wounds. Attune it to your grappling hook for elemental effects (plasma-fire chains, plasma-ice anchors, plasma-lightning tethers). Different attunements unlock different traversal and combat strategies, making build variety feel radically different run to run.
- **Scar Inheritance**: When you die, your next run can locate your previous body. Recover your gear, plus gain a "scar" -- a permanent minor buff with a thematic downside. A berserker who died to fire might grant +15% fire resistance but -10% max health. Stack too many scars and your character becomes powerful but fragile, creating a natural difficulty curve.
- **White-Out Extraction Event**: The leviathan's immune system triggers randomly during runs. A white-out blizzard of antibodies floods the current zone. You have 90 seconds to reach the extraction point or lose everything. The antibody storm is visible from across the map -- a towering white wall approaching -- creating visceral tension as you grapple toward the exit with your loot.

## Why This Is Awesome

**Challenge Seeker** players will find the difficulty curve they have been craving. The scar inheritance system means the game gets harder in proportion to how much you lean on previous-run bonuses. Speedrunners will chase "clean" runs with zero inherited scars. Hardcore players will stack every scar they can and see how deep they can push before the combined downsides become unmanageable. The white-out extraction event is pure adrenaline.

**Flow State** players will discover that the grappling-hook traversal inside the leviathan is genuinely meditative at its best. Swinging through bioluminescent arterial chambers, the plasma-amber glow of the environment reflecting off cavern walls, the rhythmic hook-anchor-release-hook cadence -- it becomes almost hypnotic. The game is at its best when you are deep in a run, in the zone, threading between fragment creatures without breaking momentum.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will piece together the leviathan's story from environmental clues. Why did it die? What was the sky realm's role? The calcified memories embedded in the walls are literal narrative fragments -- audio logs, visual flashbacks, and text inscriptions that tell the story of a civilization that built the sky realm on the back of a living god. The more you extract, the more you understand that you are looting a sacred being, and the moral weight builds with every run.
