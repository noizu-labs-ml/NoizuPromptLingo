# Shadow Chronomancer

**Action RPG**

## Core Loop
Battle through fractured time phases while wielding chronomancy shards to defeat infinite temporal dragons that threaten the void between worlds.

## Features
- Phase-shift mechanic allowing temporal manipulation of combat encounters
- Procedural time-fork dungeons that branch based on your choices
- Shadow-infused chronomancy spell system with elemental combinations
- Time-dilation combat where actions age or rejuvenate enemies
- Roguelite progression with persistent shadow essence upgrades

## Why This Is Awesome
Flow State players will love the seamless phase-shift combat that rewards chaining abilities in perfect temporal rhythm. Emergent Sandbox fans can experiment with wildly different spell combinations and time-dilation strategies to break encounters in unexpected ways. Challenge Seekers face truly punishing temporal puzzles and boss battles requiring precise timing and mastery of the chronomancy system.
