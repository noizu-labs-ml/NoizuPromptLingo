# Serpent Cartographer's Vow

**Narrative Exploration / Walking Simulator with Survival Layers**

## Core Loop

You are a blind cartographer who maps the Naga-infested marshlands by sending a bonded serpent companion to scout ahead. The serpent "sees" for you, describing terrain in poetic fragments that you translate into a hand-drawn map. You navigate by reading your own map while your serpent warns of dangers it senses. The marshland changes with the fog cycle -- paths that existed in morning mist vanish by afternoon, and naga territories shift nightly. Your goal is to chart a safe pilgrimage route for a caravan of refugees waiting at the marsh edge.

## Features
- **Serpent Senses**: Your companion describes the world in sensory metaphors ("warmth ahead, many hearts beating, two are large") rather than literal descriptions. You must interpret these clues to mark dangers, resources, and safe paths on your map.
- **Living Map**: Your hand-drawn map is the actual UI -- you navigate by looking at what you drew, not at the game world directly. If you misinterpret the serpent's description and mark a naga nest as a campsite, the refugees will walk into danger when they follow your charted route.
- **Fog Cycle**: The marshland reshuffles every in-game day. Previously safe routes become quicksand, new naga territories emerge, and resource locations shift. The serpent's descriptions change accordingly, requiring constant remapping.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Story-Driven Explorer** players will be captivated by the central tension: your map is someone else's lifeline, and every misinterpretation has consequences. The blind cartographer's perspective creates a uniquely intimate relationship with the world, mediated entirely through the serpent's poetic descriptions.

**Aesthetic Purist** fans will appreciate the game's visual language -- you "see" the world through your map's ink illustrations, which bloom and smudge with the marsh's humidity. The map itself becomes the game's art, a hand-crafted artifact that tells the story of your journey.

**Flow State** enthusiasts will find their rhythm in the loop of send serpent, receive description, interpret, draw map, navigate, repeat. The cycle becomes meditative once you learn to trust the serpent's language and your own drawings.
