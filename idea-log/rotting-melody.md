# Rotting Melody

**Rhythm / Survival Horror**

## Core Loop

You are a bard carrying the last uncorrupted melody in a world where sound itself is rotting. Navigate through a collapsing bayou by matching rhythmic inputs to the decayed music of the environment. Every correct note push back the rot; every missed note lets it spread. Enemies are dissonant creatures born from corrupted songs, and you defeat them by out-playing their chaotic rhythms with harmonic counter-melodies. The game is simultaneously a rhythm game and a survival horror game -- your health bar is the song's integrity.

## Features

- **Song Integrity Meter**: Your health bar IS the song. Miss too many notes and the melody collapses into atonal noise -- enemies grow stronger, the environment visually decays, and eventually the song dies (game over). Play well and the world blooms with amber light and harmony.
- **Harmonic Combat**: Enemies attack on the beat with visible rhythm patterns. You must play a counter-melody that harmonizes with their attack pattern to pacify or destroy them. Each enemy type has a unique musical signature you must learn and respond to.
- **Mimic Instruments**: Scattered throughout the bayou are instruments that offer new melodic abilities -- but some are mimics that play false notes designed to throw off your timing. Identifying mimic instruments by audio cues (slightly off-key, reversed attack envelopes) is critical.
- **Composed Environments**: The bayou itself is made of music. Rivers are bass lines. Fireflies are high-hat patterns. Rain is a shuffle beat. As the rot spreads, instruments go out of tune, rhythms desync, and the world becomes an auditory nightmare. Healing zones are literally musical sanctuaries.
- **Gravity Beats**: Certain sections require you to manipulate gravity to reach higher platforms, but gravity controls are tied to the downbeat. Jump on the one, redirect gravity on the three. Platforming and rhythm are the same system.

## Why This Is Awesome

**Flow State**: Rotting Melody is pure flow. The rhythm IS the game. Every input you make produces sound that blends into the soundtrack. When you are playing well, you are not hitting buttons -- you are performing a song that is saving a world. The feedback loop between correct play and auditory beauty is immediate and intoxicating. Miss a note and you hear the world suffer; nail a passage and you hear it heal.

**Story-Driven Explorer**: The narrative is told through the music itself. Each zone has a melody that was once whole but is now fragmented. Collecting melody fragments from bosses and hidden areas gradually restores the original composition, and with it, the story of what happened to this world. The final boss is a duet where you play against the source of the rot itself -- and winning means completing the song together, not destroying it.

**Emergent Sandbox**: Advanced players will discover that the harmonic combat system supports real musical theory. Playing specific chord progressions against enemy patterns creates unique reactions. The community will inevitably produce guides mapping enemy weaknesses to actual scales and modes. Speedrunners will find that maintaining perfect song integrity through an entire run triggers a hidden final zone with an alternate ending.
