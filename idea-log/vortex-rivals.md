# Vortex Rivals

**Competitive Fighting Game / Esports**

## Core Loop
Choose your battlemage, customize your spell loadout from a shared pool of 60+ abilities, and enter 1v1 arena combat where the stage itself is a collapsing rift that reshapes every 30 seconds. Win rounds by depleting health or by exploiting stage hazards to ring out your opponent. Ranked matchmaking, seasonal tournaments, and a replay theater with frame-by-frame analysis.

## Features
- **Rift Stages**: Every arena is a floating chunk of reality caught in a dimensional vortex. Every 30 seconds, gravity shifts, platforms dissolve, walls materialize, and elemental hazards (lava geysers, ice sheets, gravity wells) spawn. Players who master stage timing gain a tactical edge separate from character skill.
- **Modular Spellcraft**: Instead of fixed character movesets, every battlemage has 3 slots for spell fragments (projectile, area, dash, counter, trap). Mix and match from a shared pool. A pyromancer can equip an ice counter; a chronomancer can equip a fire trap. Builds are saved as loadouts, and the meta shifts as players discover new combinations.
- **Momentum System**: Landing consecutive hits builds a momentum gauge that unlocks enhanced versions of your equipped spells. At max momentum, your battlemage enters a temporary awakened state with a unique ultimate. Dropping a combo resets momentum, creating a risk-reward loop around aggression versus patience.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** gets a mechanically deep fighter with true competitive depth. The modular spellcraft system means matchup knowledge is exponential -- you cannot simply memorize "how to fight a pyromancer" because that pyromancer might be running a trap-heavy control build or a rushdown burst build. Frame data is exposed in-game, and training mode includes AI that replays specific tournament opponents' tendencies.

**Flow State** players will find their rhythm in the Momentum System, which rewards sustained aggression with escalating visual and audio feedback. Landing a 10-hit combo feels like conducting an orchestra -- each spell flows into the next with distinct audio cues, and the momentum gauge pulses in time with the soundtrack. Even in defeat, a well-played round feels satisfying.

**Emergent Sandbox** fans (yes, in a fighting game) will thrive in the spellcraft meta-game. With 60+ fragments in 5 categories across 3 slots per character, the build space is enormous. Community labs will form around discovering optimal and off-meta builds. The Rift Stages add another emergent layer: a build that's weak on flat ground becomes dominant when the stage shifts to a vertical gravity well.
