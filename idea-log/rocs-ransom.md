# Roc's Ransom

**Strategy Simulation / Competitive PvP**

## Core Loop
You command a guild of airborne mercenaries mounted on rocs -- massive birds of prey -- competing against other players in a persistent shared sky realm. You manage your guild's resources, train roc riders, and outfit your flock with weapons and armor. Then you launch into real-time tactical skirmishes over floating islands rich with resources. Capture islands, extract their wealth, defend your claims, and negotiate or war with rival guilds. The sky realm shifts weekly as islands drift, merge, and fracture, forcing constant strategic adaptation.

## Features
- **Aerial Command System**: Battles play out on a 3D tactical map with full altitude control. Position your rocs above for dive-attack bonuses, below for evasion, or at cloud level for concealment. Wind currents create natural highways and bottlenecks. Terrain is three-dimensional and alive -- thunderstorms ground low-fliers, thermal updrafts launch daring flanking maneuvers, and volcanic islands spew ash that blocks line of sight.
- **Guild Estate Management**: Between battles, manage your guild hall -- a floating fortress you customize and expand. Assign riders to training regimens, breed rocs for desirable traits (speed, armor, elemental affinity), research new saddle-weapons, and negotiate trade agreements with NPC sky-merchants. Every management decision has a tangible combat consequence.
- **Drift Season**: Every real-world week, the sky realm undergoes a "drift event." Islands move, new ones surface from the cloud sea, and old ones crumble. This creates weekly strategic resets that prevent stale metas and reward adaptable commanders. Each drift season has a leaderboard, seasonal rewards, and narrative events tied to the shifting geography.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** will find deep tactical depth in the 3D combat. Altitude advantage, wind exploitation, and roc breeding create a skill ceiling that rewards hundreds of hours of play. The drift season format means no strategy stays dominant forever -- you are always solving a new puzzle.

**Emergent Sandbox** players will thrive in the persistent PvP world. Alliances, betrayals, proxy wars, and trade empires all emerge organically from player interaction. The weekly drift events create natural flashpoints -- when a resource-rich island surfaces in contested airspace, the resulting free-for-all generates stories that spread beyond the game.

**Aesthetic Purist** will be drawn to the game's sky-realm art direction. Floating islands rendered in rich detail -- ancient temples overgrown with vines, crystalline spires refracting sunlight, volcanic atolls trailing smoke across the cloud ceiling. The rocs themselves are magnificent: varied species with distinct silhouettes, each armored and weaponized in ways that reflect their rider's strategy. Aerial combat at sunset is genuinely breathtaking.
