# Shadows of Defiance

**Survival Horror / Action Adventure**

## Core Loop
Scavenge light sources and temporal artifacts in a dark celestial realm while evading wraiths that feed on determination, then choose between sacrificing your light to survive others or keeping it to escape alone.

## Features
- **Light as Currency:** Your determination (represented by light) is both health and resources—spending it reveals paths but makes you vulnerable
- **Procedural Twilight Zone:** Each playthrough generates a new celestial landscape with unique geography, light sources, and wraith spawn patterns
- **Emergent Encounters:** Wraiths adapt their hunting strategies based on player behavior—aggressive players face coordinated packs, stealthy players face ambush scenarios
- **Time-Bending Defense:** Briefly accelerate or decelerate personal time to dodge attacks, create diversions, or outmaneuver pursuing enemies
- **Moral Agency System:** Every encounter offers choices—share light with survivors (risking yourself), steal light from others (risking humanity), or find third-path solutions through exploration
- **Atmospheric Soundscape:** Audio guides players—distant screams indicate wraith proximity, heartbeat intensifies as determination fades, celestial hums signal safe zones

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State Appeal:** Combat encounters create rhythmic tension as players balance risk and reward in split-second decisions. The temporal mechanics offer satisfying "almost caught" moments where time acceleration feels like a perfectly timed dodge. Resource management flows naturally from environmental interaction rather than menu management.

**Emergent Sandbox Appeal:** The procedural generation combines with environmental storytelling to create memorable emergent narratives every run. Players discover new strategies—using fallen survivors as distractions, collapsing celestial bridges to block pursuers, or overloading light wells to create temporary radiant zones. Wraith AI creates dynamic cat-and-mouse scenarios that reward creativity over rote memorization.

**Story-Driven Explorer Appeal:** The narrative unfolds through environmental details—abandoned journals from previous survivors, desperate messages etched in celestial dust, the gradual realization that wraiths were once people who lost their determination. The central theme explores the nature of hope: Is determination worth hoarding, or does its power come from sharing? Multiple endings reflect these choices, making each run feel personally meaningful.
