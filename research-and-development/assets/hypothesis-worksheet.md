## Step 4: Hypothesis Statement

**H1 (Alternative hypothesis):**
> If [intervention], then [population] will show [outcome] [direction] by at least [threshold] compared to [comparison] within [timeframe].

**H0 (Null hypothesis):**
> There is no meaningful difference in [outcome] between [intervention] and [comparison] for [population].
