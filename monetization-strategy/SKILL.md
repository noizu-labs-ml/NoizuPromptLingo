# Monetization Strategy

Strategic decision framework for choosing and planning passive income streams.

## Overview

This skill helps you evaluate which income streams align with your skills, interests, timeline, and risk tolerance. It serves as the **entry point** to the larger passive income system — use it before diving into specific implementation skills.

**Core Purpose:**
- Assess your strengths and constraints
- Compare three income streams (AI Templates, Content Publishing, POD)
- Select optimal stream(s) for your situation
- Plan a sequenced rollout strategy
- Identify resource requirements and timelines

## When to Use This Skill

- **New to passive income** — Want guidance on where to start
- **Comparing multiple streams** — Need to prioritize
- **Optimizing sequencing** — Which stream first?
- **Understanding tradeoffs** — Time, effort, reward
- **Planning a portfolio** — Diversification across streams

## Three Income Streams

### 1. AI Templates
- **Revenue:** One-time or subscription, $27-497
- **Time to revenue:** 2-4 weeks
- **Effort:** 4-6 hrs/week
- **Best for:** Technical expertise, prompt engineering, automation
- **Y1 ceiling (top quartile):** $5,000/month
- **Y1 median (consistent executor):** $500-1,500/month

### 2. Content Publishing
- **Revenue:** Newsletter subscriptions + sponsorships, $5-50/month
- **Time to revenue:** 2-3 months (list building), 4-6 months (paid tier)
- **Effort:** 3-4 hrs/week (ongoing)
- **Best for:** Writing ability, teaching skills, authority building
- **Y1 ceiling (top quartile):** $2,500/month
- **Y1 median (consistent executor):** $200-800/month

### 3. Print on Demand
- **Revenue:** Margin per item, $15-40/item ($5-15 margin)
- **Time to revenue:** 2-6 weeks
- **Effort:** 2-3 hrs/week (design batches)
- **Best for:** Creative/design skills, niche audience understanding
- **Y1 ceiling (top quartile):** $1,000/month
- **Y1 median (consistent executor):** $100-400/month

## Decision Framework

### Best Matches by Profile

**Technical Builder / Engineer**
- Primary: AI Templates → Secondary: POD (technical niches)
- Start templates immediately, add POD after 3 months

**Writer / Content Creator**
- Primary: Content Publishing → Secondary: AI Templates
- Build audience first (6-12 mo), then add templates

**Designer / Creative**
- Primary: POD → Secondary: AI Templates (design-focused packages)
- Start POD, ramp volume, test templates

**Business Person / Marketer**
- Primary: Any (choose by sub-skill) → Secondary: All
- Master one, scale with ads, then layer others

**Generalist**
- Primary: Content Publishing → Secondary: POD or Templates
- Build authority first, layer passive streams

## Portfolio Strategies

### Conservative (Single Stream)
Focus 100% on AI Templates to $5K/month. All effort on validation, build, scale. Revisit diversification after 12 months.

### Moderate (Primary + Secondary)
- Months 1-4: AI Templates (faster revenue)
- Months 2-8: Content Publishing (slower burn)
- Months 8-12: Content drives template discovery
- **Synergy:** Templates for revenue, content for SEO/audience

### Aggressive (Diversified)
- Months 1-4: AI Templates (revenue baseline)
- Months 3-8: Content Publishing (audience building)
- Months 6-12: POD (batch design, volume play)
- **Synergies:** Template customers see content, content audience buys templates, POD strengthens community

## Skill Dependencies

### AI Templates Path
**Must have:** Strong in your technical niche, understand audience pain points, comfortable with platform mechanics (Gumroad, etc.)
**Should have:** Copywriting ability, product scoping skills

### Content Publishing Path
**Must have:** Write clearly and consistently, deep topic knowledge, comfortable putting yourself out there
**Should have:** SEO understanding, community engagement skills

### POD Path
**Must have:** Design sense, specific audience understanding, comfortable with AI image generation
**Should have:** Quick iteration speed, print production understanding

## Red Flags

**AI Templates:**
- Oversaturated niches fail fast — validate with trl-market-intelligence first
- High refund rates without proper scoping — test with buyers early
- Platform competition growing — act fast on underserved niches

**Content Publishing:**
- Longest to monetize — patience required
- Consistency critical — one missed week kills momentum
- Free-to-paid conversion is hard — strong free content needed first

**Print on Demand:**
- Low margins require high volume — 80/20 rule applies (few designs make money)
- Design quality matters — bad design = zero sales
- Difficult to scale without paid ads — organic POD growth is slow

## Quick Decision Guide

Answer these 5 questions:

1. **Primary strength?** Technical → Templates | Writing → Content | Creative → POD
2. **Weekly hours?** <5 → POD | 5-10 → Any | 10+ → Content
3. **Risk tolerance?** Conservative → POD | Moderate → Templates | Aggressive → Content
4. **Timeline?** Need money NOW → POD/Templates | 3-6 months → Any | 12+ months → Content
5. **Portfolio or focus?** One stream → Single focus | Multiple → Start primary, add secondary in Q2

## Expansion Streams

The three core streams (AI Templates, Content Publishing, POD) are the foundation. Once you have consistent revenue from at least one core stream, consider these expansion opportunities:

| Stream | What It Is | When to Add | Prerequisites |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Digital Communities** (Skool, Circle) | Paid membership community around your niche | After 500+ email subscribers | Established authority, content to share |
| **Micro-SaaS** | Small software tools solving niche problems | After validating demand via templates | Coding ability, validated pain point |
| **AI Agent Marketplaces** | Sell custom GPTs, Claude tools, or MCP servers on emerging platforms | When platforms mature and have buyer traffic | Technical depth, existing AI template portfolio |

### Why Not Start Here?

- **Communities** require an existing audience to seed -- cold-start is brutal
- **Micro-SaaS** has ongoing maintenance costs that templates don't -- validate the problem first with a template, then upgrade to SaaS if demand justifies it
- **Agent marketplaces** are still emerging -- the economics are unproven and platforms are volatile

### When to Revisit

Add expansion streams to your portfolio review (see **trl-conversion-engineer** `references/portfolio-strategy.md`) at the 6-month and 12-month marks. The criteria: you have a validated niche, an audience of 500+, and your core streams are generating $1K+/month.
