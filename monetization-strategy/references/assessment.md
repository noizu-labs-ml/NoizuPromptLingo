# Monetization Strategy Assessment

> Comprehensive self-assessment prompt to evaluate strengths, constraints, and optimal income stream(s). Run this as the **first step** before niche research or product building.

## Table of Contents

1. [What to Provide](#what-to-provide)
2. [The Prompt](#the-prompt)

---

## What to Provide

Gather this information before running the assessment:

- **Skills:** Technical, creative, teaching/communication, business
- **Constraints:** Weekly hours, budget, runway, risk tolerance
- **Current situation:** Existing audience, current revenue, geography
- **Goals:** Revenue target, timeline, work preferences

---

## The Prompt

```markdown
I want to develop a personalized passive income strategy. Help me assess where I'll succeed.

### About Me

**Skills Inventory:**
- Technical skills: [coding, APIs, automation, software development, specific areas]
- Creative skills: [writing, design, visual thinking, brand development]
- Teaching/communication: [teaching experience, audience interaction, community building]
- Business skills: [marketing, sales, audience understanding, metrics tracking]

**Constraints:**
- Weekly time availability: [e.g., "5-8 hours/week", "10+ hours/week", "only weekends"]
- Financial resources: [e.g., "no budget", "$100-200/mo", "$500+/mo for ads"]
- Startup runway: [e.g., "need income in 2 months", "can invest 6 months"]
- Risk tolerance: [Conservative, Moderate, or Aggressive]

**Current Situation:**
- Existing audience: [None, small community, established email list, large following, etc.]
- Current revenue streams: [None, freelancing, employment, part-time, etc.]
- Geographic/time zone: [Relevant for content timing/monetization]

**Goals:**
- Revenue target: [e.g., "$500/mo in Y1", "$1000/mo", "primary income eventually"]
- Timeline: [e.g., "first revenue in 4 weeks", "building for 12 months"]
- Work preference: [Batch work vs. consistent weekly, geographic flexibility]

### Analysis Requested:

1. **Strengths Assessment** — Which streams (AI Templates, Content Publishing, POD)
   align with my skills? What unique advantages do I have?

2. **Constraints Mapping** — Which streams fit my time/financial constraints?
   Which require least startup investment?

3. **Timeline Reality Check** — When could I realistically see first revenue?
   How long until meaningful income ($1000+/month)?

4. **Dependency Analysis** — Do I have prerequisite skills?
   What would I need to develop? How long?

5. **Recommendation** — Primary stream + secondary diversification.
   Why this stream, realistic timeline, what to do first.

6. **Risk Mitigation** — Specific failure risks and how to reduce them.
```
