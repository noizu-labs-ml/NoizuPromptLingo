## Digital Product Platforms

### Gumroad

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Fee model** | 10% + $0.50 per transaction (direct sales) |
| **Marketplace sales** | 30% (via Gumroad Discover) |
| **Payment processing** | +2.9% + $0.30 (Stripe, on top of platform fee) |
| **Monthly fee** | None |
| **Total effective cost** | ~12.9% + $0.80 per direct sale |
| **Merchant of Record** | Yes (handles VAT/GST/sales tax globally since Jan 2025) |
| **Refund policy** | Platform keeps its 10% + $0.50 on refunds |

**Pros:** Zero monthly cost, built-in Discover marketplace, simple setup, handles global tax compliance. **Cons:** High total fees when combined with payment processing, keeps fees on refunds, Discover marketplace takes 30%.

**Best for:** First product launch, testing product-market fit, solo creators.

---

### Lemon Squeezy

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Fee model** | 5% + $0.50 per transaction |
| **International surcharge** | +1.5% for non-US transactions |
| **Monthly fee** | Free plan available; Sweet plan ~$24/mo; Juicy plan ~$66/mo |
| **Affiliate fee** | +3% per affiliate-referred order |
| **Payout fees** | 0% US bank accounts; 1% international |
| **Merchant of Record** | Yes (handles global tax) |

**Pros:** Lower transaction fees than Gumroad, developer-friendly API, handles tax compliance, supports subscriptions and license keys natively. **Cons:** No built-in marketplace/discovery, smaller ecosystem.

**Best for:** SaaS products, subscription-based digital products, developers who want API control.

---

### Stan Store

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Fee model** | 0% transaction fee (Stan takes nothing per sale) |
| **Monthly fee** | $29/mo (Creator) or $99/mo (Creator Pro) |
| **Annual pricing** | $25/mo ($300/yr) or $79/mo ($948/yr) billed annually |
| **Payment processing** | Stripe/PayPal processing fees still apply (~2.9% + $0.30) |

**Pros:** Zero transaction fees (only pay Stripe/PayPal processing), built-in course builder, booking/calendar tools, community features, mobile-optimized storefront. **Cons:** Monthly fee whether you sell or not, no built-in marketplace discovery, designed for social-first creators.

**Best for:** Creators selling courses + digital products + bookings, social media-driven sellers (Instagram, TikTok).

---

### Etsy

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Listing fee** | $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months) |
| **Transaction fee** | 6.5% of sale price + shipping + gift wrap |
| **Payment processing** | 3% + $0.25 (US); 4% + local currency (international) |
| **Offsite Ads fee** | 12% (mandatory if >$10K/yr) or 15% (opt-in if <$10K/yr) |
| **Total effective cost** | ~10-12% before Offsite Ads |

**Pros:** Massive built-in search traffic, established buyer trust, good for templates and POD crossover. **Cons:** High cumulative fees, mandatory Offsite Ads for larger sellers, heavy competition, limited branding control.

**Best for:** Digital templates with broad appeal, POD crossover products, reaching non-tech buyers.

## Content Monetization Platforms

### Substack

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Free publishing** | Unlimited subscribers, no cost |
| **Paid subscriptions** | 10% of paid subscription revenue |
| **Payment processing** | +2.9% + $0.30 (Stripe) + 0.7% recurring billing fee |
| **Total effective cost** | ~14% of paid revenue |
| **Built-in discovery** | Yes (Substack network, recommendations) |

**Pros:** Zero cost until you monetize, built-in discovery network, simple setup, full subscriber export, strong newsletter culture. **Cons:** Limited analytics, limited design control, 10% cut adds up at scale, no advanced automation.

**Best for:** Writer-first newsletters, building a paid subscriber base, content creators who want simplicity.

---

### Patreon

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Platform fee** | 10% (standard for all creators who published after Aug 4, 2025) |
| **Legacy rates** | 5% (Lite), 8% (Pro), or 12% (Premium) for pre-Aug 2025 creators -- grandfathered |
| **Payment processing** | Additional processing fees per transaction |
| **Currency conversion** | 2.5% fee |
| **iOS in-app purchases** | 30% Apple fee (if patron subscribes via iOS app) |

**Pros:** Strong community/membership features, tiered access model, established creator platform. **Cons:** iOS fee devastates margins, 10% flat rate is steep for new creators, no built-in content discovery.

**Best for:** Community-driven creators, tiered membership with exclusive content, creators with existing audiences.

---

### Medium

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Cost to publish** | Free (requires paid Medium membership to join Partner Program) |
| **Revenue model** | Partner Program: share of member subscription revenue |
| **Earnings based on** | Member reading time, engagement (claps, highlights, responses), Boost bonus |
| **Search traffic bonus** | Higher earnings for member reads from Google search (since Nov 2025) |
| **External traffic bonus** | Higher earnings for engagement originating from outside Medium (since Oct 2025) |
| **New member bonus** | One-time earnings when non-member joins to read your paywalled story (since Feb 2026) |
| **Email notification bonus** | Higher earnings when members read via email notification links (since Dec 2025) |
| **Minimum payout** | $10 |

**Pros:** Zero cost, built-in discovery via algorithm and search, good SEO authority, no audience needed to start, recent updates reward external traffic and search visibility. **Cons:** Unpredictable earnings, platform controls algorithm, limited branding, you don't own the audience relationship.

**Best for:** Cross-posting for reach and SEO, supplementary income, reaching non-technical audiences.

## Print on Demand Platforms

### Redbubble

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Cost to list** | Free |
| **Default markup** | 10-20% above base price (artist-adjustable) |
| **Account tiers** | Standard (50% of earnings as fee), Premium (20% fee), Pro (no fees) |
| **Excess markup fee** | 50% fee on earnings from markup above 20% (Standard/Premium tiers) |
| **Fee cap** | $150/payment period for Standard and Premium tiers |
| **Built-in marketplace** | Yes |

**Pros:** Zero upfront cost, massive built-in marketplace, handles production/shipping/returns, passive discovery. **Cons:** Tiered fee structure (since Sept 2025) significantly reduces margins for Standard accounts, limited branding control, competitive marketplace.

**Best for:** Passive income through marketplace discovery, testing which designs sell, zero-effort fulfillment.

**Note:** Redbubble's September 2025 fee restructure (account tiers) substantially changed the economics. Standard accounts lose 50% of earnings. Reaching Premium or Pro tier is essential for viable margins. Fee is capped at $150/period, which limits downside for higher earners but doesn't help small sellers.

### Printful / Printify

> Last verified: 2026-03-25

| Attribute | Printful | Printify |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Monthly fee** | Free (Growth plan has fees) | Free (Premium $29.99/mo) |
| **Product cost** | Higher base cost | Lower base cost |
| **You set price** | Full control (50-100%+ markup) | Full control (50-100%+ markup) |
| **Integration** | Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy | Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy |
| **Quality** | Higher consistency | Varies by print provider |

**Pros:** Full pricing control, higher margins than marketplaces, brand your store. **Cons:** You must drive your own traffic, no built-in discovery, requires a storefront (Shopify, etc.).

**Best for:** Branded merch stores, creators with existing audiences, higher-margin products.

## Decision Guides

### Which Platform to Start With (by stream)

**AI Templates / Digital Products:**
```
First product, testing the market?
  --> Gumroad (zero monthly cost, built-in discovery)

Selling subscriptions or SaaS?
  --> Lemon Squeezy (lower fees, license key support)

Social-first creator with courses + bookings?
  --> Stan Store (zero transaction fees, all-in-one)

Broad audience, template-style products?
  --> Etsy (massive search traffic)

Revenue > $2K/month, want lower fees?
  --> Own site + Stripe (3% processing only)
```

**Content Publishing:**
```
Starting a newsletter?
  --> Substack (simplest path, built-in discovery)

Need advanced automation/segmentation?
  --> ConvertKit (marketing-first platform)

Want growth analytics and A/B testing?
  --> Beehiiv (growth-focused tools)

Developer who wants API control?
  --> Buttondown (API-first, markdown-native)
```

> For full newsletter platform comparison, see `content-publishing/SKILL.md` section "Newsletter Platform Comparison."

**Discovery / Audience Building (not monetization platforms, but essential for traffic):**
```
Developer blogging with custom domain?
  --> Hashnode (60K+ active blogs, 1M monthly users, strong SEO, free custom domain)

Broader developer community reach?
  --> Dev.to (large built-in readership, strong SEO)

General audience reach?
  --> Medium (Partner Program for supplementary income, good SEO domain authority)
```

**Print on Demand:**
```
Want passive, zero-effort income?
  --> Redbubble (built-in marketplace)

Want higher margins with your own brand?
  --> Printify (lower base cost) or Printful (higher quality)

Already have a Shopify store?
  --> Printify or Printful integration
```

### Migration Triggers

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Revenue > $2K/mo on Gumroad | Evaluate own site + Stripe |
| 500+ free subscribers on Substack | Launch paid tier |
| Redbubble design hits 50 sales/mo | Create variations, expand to branded store |
| Multiple products + courses | Consider Stan Store (flat monthly vs per-transaction) |
