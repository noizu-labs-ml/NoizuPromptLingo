# Jonah Ruiz — The Bored Scroller

## Demographics
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Age** | 26 |
| **Role** | Barista / part-time graphic design student |
| **Technical Level** | Low |
| **Industry** | Food Service / Creative |
| **Location** | Austin, TX |

## Bio
Jonah is perpetually dissatisfied with his social media feeds but keeps returning to them because he doesn't know where else to go. He's heard vague references to "the old internet" from older friends and finds the idea appealing even if he never actually experienced it. He's not a builder or a researcher — he just wants to find cool stuff online and feel like the internet is as interesting as people say it used to be.

## Goals
1. Spend his downtime finding interesting things online that don't feel like content designed to manipulate him
2. Discover sites outside of mainstream social platforms that feel personal and handmade
3. Have something worth sharing with friends that isn't just a meme or a trending post

## Frustrations
1. TikTok and Instagram feel engineered — he can feel the algorithm pushing him
2. Reddit has gotten corporate; the personality is gone
3. When he tries to find "interesting websites" he gets Buzzfeed and Wikipedia

## Behaviors
- Arrives via a link from a friend, Reddit thread, or newsletter rather than direct
- Browses by category title appeal — clicks into whatever sounds interesting ("Weird Corners," "Small Games," "Illustrated Blogs")
- Doesn't read scores carefully; the overall visual quality grade matters more than individual dimensions
- Likely to upvote things he likes; unlikely to create an account unless the friction is very low
- Will share a specific site or collection link on Discord with friends

## Job to Be Done
> "I have 20 minutes to kill and I want to end up somewhere unexpected that makes me feel like the internet is actually good."

## Relationship to Product
Jonah is a free tier user and likely always will be. He represents high volume, low monetization, but significant social referral value. If gotta.cc gives him three genuinely delightful discoveries in one session, he will tell his Discord. He is the word-of-mouth engine. His experience needs to be compelling within 30 seconds of arriving — category names, homepage featured sites, and design quality are his first impressions.

## Scenarios
1. **Dead Time Discovery** — Jonah is on break at work with 15 minutes. He opens gotta.cc from a link a friend sent. He clicks into "Illustrated Blogs," finds a site with handmade CSS and comics about living in Japan, and spends the rest of his break reading it. He screenshots it and sends it to two friends.
2. **Category Wandering** — On a slow Sunday, Jonah navigates through 4-5 categories without landing anywhere in particular but enjoys the browsing experience. He doesn't create an account or submit anything, but returns the next weekend.
