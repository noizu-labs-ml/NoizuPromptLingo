## Bio
Sandra has spent 15 years covering technology policy and corporate accountability. She relies on primary sources — researchers, niche experts, independent analysts — but finding them online increasingly means sifting through AI-generated content farms. She bookmarks obsessively, uses a citation manager, and has a deep distrust of sources that are too polished or too SEO-optimized.

## Goals
1. Find primary expert sources on niche topics (regulatory filings, technical standards, domain-specific analysis) that aren't indexed prominently by Google
2. Build shareable source collections she can reuse across stories with the same topic domain
3. Quickly assess whether a site is human-authored and depth-credible before spending time on it

## Frustrations
1. AI-generated content is visually indistinguishable from expert content — she needs a signal she can trust
2. Google's top results for technical policy topics are dominated by trade publications with agendas
3. She loses track of good sources she's found before — her bookmarks folder is chaos

## Behaviors
- Filters by Human Authorship and Depth scores above all other dimensions
- Uses collections to organize sites by story beat or topic area
- Shares curated collections with editors and research assistants as living source documents
- Occasionally submits sites found during research if they're high quality and underrepresented

## Job to Be Done
> "I need to find the researcher who actually understands TSMC's fab process constraints — not the 500 think-pieces summarizing the same Reuters wire."

## Relationship to Product
Sandra is a Supporter tier user ($5/mo). She uses gotta.cc primarily as a research tool, not a browsing experience. The Collections feature is her core use case — she builds topic-specific source libraries. She doesn't care about community features or design scores; Depth and Human Authorship are the only dimensions that matter to her. She is a quiet but high-value user whose use case validates the quality scoring system.

## Scenarios
1. **Source Sprint** — Sandra is writing a piece on water rights law. She navigates to gotta.cc's Law / Environmental category, filters to Depth 4+, Human Authorship 4+. She finds eight sites in 20 minutes that her search engine work hadn't surfaced. She adds six to a shared collection and sends the link to her editor.
2. **Credibility Check** — An anonymous tip sends her to a site she's never heard of. She searches gotta.cc to see if it's indexed, checks the Human Authorship score and AI summary. A low Human Authorship score is an immediate red flag that prompts additional verification.
