## Bio
Maya grew up on GeoCities and Xanga, and has spent the last five years trying to reconnect with the web that felt alive. She curates a personal blogroll, participates in several webrings, and subscribes to a handful of RSS feeds with religious devotion. She increasingly resents the algorithmic feeds that only surface what she already knows she likes.

## Goals
1. Discover genuinely interesting sites that she wouldn't find through search or social media
2. Maintain a personal collection of the best stuff she finds across different topics
3. Reconnect with the sense of serendipity that the early web offered — following a link and ending up somewhere unexpected and good

## Frustrations
1. Google surfaces SEO-farmed content over genuine, thoughtful personal sites
2. Social media algorithms optimize for outrage and virality, not quality
3. Most "discovery" tools are still just popularity rankings dressed up as curation

## Behaviors
- Browses category pages rather than searching; she wants to wander, not retrieve
- Shares finds in a weekly personal newsletter with ~200 subscribers
- Star-rates sites in her personal Notion and uses them as reference material for design inspiration
- Checks quality scores to validate her own instincts — high human authorship scores matter most to her

## Job to Be Done
> "When I have an hour to explore, I want to end up somewhere I've never been but that immediately feels worth my time — without having to wade through AI slop to get there."

## Relationship to Product
Maya is gotta.cc's ideal browsing user. She will spend significant time on category pages, create collections, and occasionally submit sites she loves. She is not interested in the business tier — she is a Supporter ($5/mo) who pays because she believes in the mission. She is likely to tell her newsletter readers about the platform if she has a positive experience.

## Scenarios
1. **Saturday Wandering** — Maya opens gotta.cc on a Saturday morning with coffee, navigates to the "Personal Blogs / Technology" category, and sorts by Originality score. She finds three sites she bookmarks and one she submits to her newsletter.
2. **Validating a Discovery** — She's found a site through a blogroll and wants to know if it's worth digging into. She searches gotta.cc for the domain, checks its scores, reads the AI summary, and decides whether to subscribe to its RSS.
