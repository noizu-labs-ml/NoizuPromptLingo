# US-038: View Submitter Profile with Discovery Credit

## User Story

**As a** web nostalgia explorer (P-001),
**I want to** view the profile of someone who submitted a great site,
**So that** I can discover more of their finds and follow their taste as a trusted curator.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I click a submitter's name on a listing, when their profile page loads, then I see their display name, join date, total approved submissions, and an acceptance rate percentage
- [ ] Given I am on a submitter profile, when I browse it, then I see a chronological feed of all their approved submissions with title, category, score, and upvote count
- [ ] Given a user has earned discovery badges (e.g., "First to find" on 10+ sites), when their profile is viewed, then those badges are displayed prominently
- [ ] Given I view my own profile, when I am logged in, then I also see my pending and rejected submissions with a private-only view toggle

## Notes

Discovery credit is key to incentivizing quality submissions. Profile feeds into the follow system (US-040) and the community leaderboard (US-040). Badges are a gamification layer — full badge taxonomy is a future design decision.
