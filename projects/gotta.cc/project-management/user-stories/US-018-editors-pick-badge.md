# US-018: Editor's Pick Badge on Site Cards

## User Story

**As a** Casual Link-Follower (P-004),
**I want to** see which sites have been personally endorsed by the gotta.cc editors,
**So that** I can trust that a site is genuinely excellent and not just algorithmically high-scoring.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given a site has been marked as an Editor's Pick by a moderator (P-005), when I view its card in any listing, then a distinct "Editor's Pick" badge is shown on the card.
- [ ] Given I see the Editor's Pick badge, when I hover or tap it, then a tooltip reads "Hand-picked by the gotta.cc editorial team."
- [ ] Given a site loses its Editor's Pick designation, when its card next renders, then the badge is no longer displayed.

## Notes

The Editor's Pick badge is a human trust signal that differentiates gotta.cc from automated directories. It should be visually distinctive but not overwhelming — a small tasteful mark, not a banner. Connects to US-005 (featured picks in category) and US-013 (editorial summaries).
