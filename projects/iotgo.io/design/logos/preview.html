Design Rationale

Concept: "The Agent Loop" — IoTGo's core differentiator is the closed detect-act loop. Every competitor in IoT fleet management is an observation platform. IoTGo adds the agent that acts. The mark encodes this directly:

The ring represents continuous monitoring — the telemetry loop, the always-on observation that IoT platforms already provide. The forward arrow inside the ring represents autonomous action — "Go." Together they say: "we don't just watch, we act."

The arrow tip merges with the ring at the right edge (the arrow extends past the inner radius into the ring area). This isn't decorative — it represents the agent breaking through passive observation into action. The observation loop and the action impulse become one unified system.

Why not a literal IoT icon? Signal waves, connected nodes, and circuit patterns are used by every IoT company. They signal the category, not the company. This mark signals the differentiator: autonomous action within a loop.

Two-tone logotype: "IoT" in the primary dark color identifies the category instantly. "Go" in teal signals the brand's action-oriented identity and ensures the name reads as two concepts: IoT + Go, not a single word.

Direction-agnostic: The mark works across all three design directions by swapping the fill color — teal (#14B8A6) for Direction A, muted teal (#0D9488) for Direction B, electric green (#00E676) for Direction C. The geometry is constant; only color changes.

Construction

Geometry (200×200 viewBox):

Outer circle: center (100, 100), radius 72
Inner circle: center (100, 100), radius 50
Ring thickness: 22 units
Arrow: triangle from (82, 66) → (82, 134) → (152, 100)
Arrow height: 68 units  |  Arrow width: 70 units
Arrow tip penetrates ring by 2 units (152 − 150 = 2 past inner radius)

Clear space: 28 units from viewBox edge to outer circle (14% of total). Minimum clear space in use = circle radius / 3 (~24px at default size).

Minimum sizes: Full mark readable to 24px. Favicon variant for 16-32px. Combo mark minimum 120px wide.
