# US-096: Live Decision Overlay Commentary Mode

As a **Streamer**, I want a "commentary mode" for replays that adds a narration track where I can record audio synced to replay timestamps, so that I can produce fully self-contained breakdown videos with my voice over the decision overlay.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Commentary mode accessible from the replay player
- Streamer records audio within the app synced frame-by-frame to playback
- Recorded audio is embedded in the MP4 export
- Audio recording re-takes supported per timestamp segment

**Priority:** Could
