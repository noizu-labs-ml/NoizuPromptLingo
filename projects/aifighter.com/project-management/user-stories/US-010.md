# US-010: Share a Highlight Clip to Social Media in One Tap

As a **Curious Casual**, I want a one-tap share button on the post-battle screen that packages a highlight clip with the game's branding so that I can post cool moments to my stories without any editing.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The post-battle screen shows an auto-selected highlight clip (the highest-intensity 10-second segment of the match)
- A single "Share" button opens the native OS share sheet with the clip pre-attached and a default caption
- The caption includes the user's fighter name, outcome, and a referral link to the app store
- Users can preview and trim the clip before sharing; the trim UI is accessible with one additional tap

**Priority:** Should
