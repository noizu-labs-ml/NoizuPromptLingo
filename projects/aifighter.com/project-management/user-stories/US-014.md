# US-014: Submit My Fighter and Get Notified When the Battle Resolves

As a **Curious Casual**, I want to submit my fighter to the ranked arena and receive a push notification when my match resolves so that I can check results whenever I have time without waiting in-app.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Submitting a fighter to ranked queues it for server-side resolution within 5 minutes
- A push notification is sent when the match result is ready, including the outcome and rank change
- Tapping the notification opens the app directly to the post-battle replay screen
- Users can queue up to 3 simultaneous ranked battles; results are batched into a single notification if they resolve close together

**Priority:** Must
