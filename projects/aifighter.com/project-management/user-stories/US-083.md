# US-083: Training Time-Lapse Export

As a **Streamer**, I want to generate a time-lapse video of a fighter's full training session — showing performance graphs and heatmaps evolving over generations — so that I can produce "training montage" content that hooks viewers in under 60 seconds.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Time-lapse compresses a multi-hour training session into a 30–90 second clip
- Renders performance graph curves animating upward and heatmap density increasing over time
- Export available as MP4 at 1080p
- User can choose compression ratio (30s, 60s, 90s output target)

**Priority:** Should
