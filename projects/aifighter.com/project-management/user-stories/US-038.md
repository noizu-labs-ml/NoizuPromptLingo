# US-038: Free Tier Full Feature Access for Education

**As an Educator,** I want the full core gameplay loop — Fighter Studio, Training Gym, Classroom Sandbox, and data export — to be freely accessible without a paywall so that I can use AI Fighter in a public school with zero budget.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- All features listed in the Education portal (classroom creation, sandbox, student accounts, data export, glossary, lesson templates) are available on the free tier with no time limit
- F2P limitations (cosmetic only) apply identically to educator and student accounts; no functional cap is imposed on classroom size up to 35 students
- Upgrade prompts are never shown within the classroom sandbox or education portal views
- Educator accounts are verified via a .edu email or a manual verification request with a 48-hour turnaround

**Priority:** Must
