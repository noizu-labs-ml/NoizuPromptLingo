# US-041: Ranked Leaderboard with Percentile Breakdown

As a **Competitive Grinder**, I want to view a live ranked leaderboard showing my global percentile, win rate, and ELO delta over the last 7 days, so that I can track my climb toward top-10 and produce tier-list content for my audience.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Leaderboard displays top 500 players with ELO, rank tier, and win/loss record
- My own rank is always pinned and visible regardless of position
- 7-day ELO trend sparkline shown per player row
- Percentile label displayed (e.g., "Top 0.4%") adjacent to my rank

**Priority:** Must
