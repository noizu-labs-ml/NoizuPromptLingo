# US-098: Featured Graph Spotlight in Laboratory

As a **Data Artist**, I want the Laboratory to feature a "Graph of the Week" spotlight that highlights aesthetically exceptional builds voted on by the community, so that graph artistry is recognized as a distinct form of achievement separate from competitive ranking.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Weekly spotlight section visible on the Laboratory home screen
- Community voting opens on Monday; spotlight winner announced Sunday
- Winner receives an exclusive "Artisan" graph color theme as a trophy cosmetic
- Spotlight includes a full-resolution static view of the winning graph with the artist's handle

**Priority:** Could
