# US-064: No Energy System or Daily Streak

**As a Dad Gamer,** I want the game to have no energy gates and no login-streak mechanics so that returning after a week feels the same as playing daily.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- There is no energy, stamina, or action-point resource that limits play sessions
- No UI surfaces language about "streak bonuses," "daily rewards," or streak-loss penalties
- Players who are inactive for 7+ days return to the same ranked position they left (no decay within 30 days)
- Onboarding copy explicitly communicates the no-FOMO policy

**Priority:** Must
