# US-012: Receive Specific Tips After Losing a Match

As a **Curious Casual**, I want the post-battle screen to show 2-3 specific, actionable suggestions for improving my fighter so that I know exactly what to change and don't just feel lost after a loss.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- After every loss, the system analyzes the replay and surfaces 2-3 suggestions ranked by estimated impact
- Suggestions are written in plain language (e.g., "Your fighter didn't dodge during the opponent's combo — add an Evasion node connected to your Threat Detector")
- Each suggestion includes a one-tap shortcut to open the relevant section of the Fighter Studio with the suggested change pre-highlighted
- Suggestions are suppressed after wins unless the user explicitly requests a "performance review"

**Priority:** Must
