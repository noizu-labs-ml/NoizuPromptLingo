# US-063: Quick-Tweak Suggestion Card

**As a Dad Gamer,** I want to see a single prioritized suggestion after each battle so that I can make one meaningful improvement in under two minutes.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Post-battle summary displays exactly one "Top Suggestion" card before the full insight list
- The card includes a one-tap shortcut that opens the relevant node in the Fighter Studio
- The suggestion is derived from the battle replay analysis, not generic advice
- The card can be dismissed and reviewed later from the fighter's profile

**Priority:** Must
