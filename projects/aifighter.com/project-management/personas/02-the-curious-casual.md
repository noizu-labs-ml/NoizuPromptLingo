# Persona 02: The Curious Casual

**Name:** Sam Okafor
**Age:** 19
**Location:** Atlanta, GA
**Occupation:** College sophomore (studying business)
**Device:** Samsung Galaxy S24
**Income:** Part-time job, ~$12K/year

---

## Profile

Sam plays everything — Clash Royale, Brawl Stars, TFT Mobile, whatever his friends group chat is into this month. He doesn't know what a neural network is technically, but he understands "train the AI to be aggressive" vs "train it to dodge" from TikTok explainers and ChatGPT. He's drawn to things that feel new and shareable. If he can screenshot a cool moment and get reactions in the group chat, he's engaged.

He doesn't read patch notes or theorycraft. He follows tier lists and copies what's working, then experiments when he gets bored. His attention span for any single game is 2-4 months unless it has a strong social/seasonal loop.

---

## Goals

- Have a fighter that looks cool and wins enough to feel good about
- Share replays and wins on social media / group chats
- Climb ranked ladder to flex on friends
- Understand the graph editor well enough to make meaningful tweaks without needing a CS degree

## Frustrations

- Games that feel like homework or require reading documentation
- Complex UIs with too many options and no guidance on what to try
- Losing repeatedly with no clear path to improvement
- Feeling like experienced players have an insurmountable advantage

## Behaviors

- Plays in 2-3 concentrated sessions per day, 5-10 minutes each
- Screenshots and screen-records cool battle moments
- Starts with templates and modifies them rather than building from scratch
- Joins a clan within the first week if prompted
- Motivated by seasonal ranked resets — fresh start energy
- Will spend $3-5 on a cool fighter skin if his friends see it

---

## Key Scenarios

1. **First session:** Watches the cinematic, picks the "Brawler" template, wins the tutorial battle, adds one node as guided, shares a screenshot of his fighter to Instagram stories
2. **Week 1:** Has tried all 3 templates, settled on one, modified it slightly based on post-battle insights ("your fighter never dodges — add a dodge node?"). Placed Bronze II in ranked
3. **Month 1:** Invited 3 friends to play. They have a mini-rivalry on the ladder. Sam has imported a build from a YouTube video and is climbing with it. Starting to understand why certain node configurations work

---

## Design Implications

- Onboarding must make the graph editor feel like a toy, not a textbook — guided node placement with instant visual feedback
- Templates must be genuinely competitive at lower ranks — Sam shouldn't feel punished for not building from scratch
- Post-battle insights should give specific, actionable suggestions ("add a dodge node for when health < 30%") not just raw data
- Replay sharing needs to be one-tap to social platforms with auto-generated highlight clips
- Seasonal content (new cosmetics, rank reset, limited events) is the primary retention lever for this persona
