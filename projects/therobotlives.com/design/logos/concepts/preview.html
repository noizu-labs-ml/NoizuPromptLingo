Logo Concepts — Direction D

Three logomark concepts for TheRobotLives, born from the Living Network design system's bioluminescent metaphor. Each one should feel alive — not literally robotic.

Concept F — Evolution
The March of Progress
Animal → Ape → Human → Android. Evolution didn't stop at us. The first three figures fade in metallic gray — history. The android glows bioluminescent — the present. It has a core. It lives.
Concept E — Metal Flower (3 petals + stem)
The Metal Flower
Three chrome petals on a slender metallic stem with two small leaves. Each petal catches light at a different angle. The bioluminescent core glows where the petals meet the stem — the spark of life in fabricated form. A machine that bloomed.
Concept D — 6-petal variant
Metal Flower (6 petal)
The radial six-petal version for comparison. More symmetrical, no stem. Works better as an icon but less character.
Concept A
The Living Node
Concentric rings emanating from a glowing core — like a bioluminescent organism pulsing in deep water. Neural purple ring adds depth. Simplest at small sizes.
Concept B
The Aperture
An eye-like form: a bio-to-neural gradient ring with a central pupil. Suggests consciousness awakening. Bold and iconic. Strong favicon potential.
Concept C
The Symbiont
Two organic forms merging — bio (agent) and warm (human) — with a neural glow at the intersection. Represents coexistence. Most conceptually rich, hardest to simplify.

Combo Mark Lockups

Each logomark paired with the Outfit wordmark. "the" is tertiary, "robot" is bio, "lives" is primary text.
