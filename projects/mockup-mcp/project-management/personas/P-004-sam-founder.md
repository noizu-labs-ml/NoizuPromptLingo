## Bio
Sam is building their second startup after a first exit in e-commerce. They are a strong domain expert and operator but cannot code — their technical co-founder handles the stack. Right now Sam needs to communicate product vision clearly to three audiences: seed investors who want to see what they're building, contractors who need detailed enough briefs to produce accurate estimates, and a design hire who starts in six weeks. Sam uses Claude.ai daily for writing and research and has started exploring MCP tools on the recommendation of their CTO.

## Goals
1. Produce mockups good enough for investor pitch decks and demo walkthroughs without hiring a designer yet
2. Create detailed visual briefs for offshore contractors so estimates come back accurate rather than wildly off-base
3. Maintain a shareable, evolving visual representation of the product that the team can react to asynchronously

## Frustrations
1. Freelance designers are expensive for concept-stage mockups that will change weekly — hard to justify before product-market fit
2. Describing product UI in words during investor meetings feels abstract and unconvincing compared to a mockup on screen
3. Contractor estimates diverge by 3-5x when the brief is text-only versus when a wireframe is attached

## Behaviors
- Uses AI chat tools (Claude, ChatGPT) as a primary productivity tool — already comfortable prompting for artifacts
- Sends mockup images via email, Notion, or a shared Google Drive folder
- Does not configure developer tools but will use any web interface that produces shareable links
- Iterates on mockups in rapid bursts (3–5 revisions in a day) when preparing for a pitch or contractor kickoff

## Job to Be Done
> "When I'm preparing to pitch investors or onboard a contractor, I want to generate a credible visual mockup of my product from plain-language descriptions, so I can communicate my vision without waiting for or paying for a designer."

## Relationship to Product
Sam discovers Mockup MCP through the securamcp.com website — likely from a startup newsletter, Product Hunt, or a founder community (YC alumni Slack, Indie Hackers). They use exclusively the web interface and feedback-sharing features. The key value is speed and "good enough" fidelity for pre-seed contexts. They do not care about MCP protocol internals. Churn risk: if the web interface requires technical setup or if output looks too much like a developer wireframe (ASCII-grid, too spartan) to impress non-technical investors.

## Scenarios
1. **Investor pitch prep** — Sam is pitching Series A investors next week. They use the Mockup MCP web interface to generate 5 screens for their core user journey overnight, export them as PNGs, and embed them in a Pitch.com deck. The investor lead comments that the product "looks further along than expected."
2. **Contractor kickoff** — Sam is hiring a freelance React developer to build the MVP. They generate a full mockup set with annotated callouts using Mockup MCP, attach it to the Upwork brief, and receive estimates that align within 20% of each other — versus the previous text-only brief where estimates ranged from $4k to $22k.
