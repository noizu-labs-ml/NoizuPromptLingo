# INK-009: AI-Generated Persona Suggestions

**As an** agency director,
**I want** the AI to suggest realistic target user personas based on my pitch,
**So that** I can include them in client proposals without spending hours on user research.

**Primary Persona:** Aisha Mohammed (#7)
**Phase:** Sketch
**Priority:** P0-Critical

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] After the pitch is finalized, the AI generates 3-5 persona suggestions within 15 seconds
- [ ] Each persona includes a name, role, goal, frustration, and a one-line behavioral summary
- [ ] Personas are visually presented as cards that can be individually reviewed
- [ ] The AI explains its reasoning for why each persona is relevant to the pitch
- [ ] The user can proceed to curation once personas are loaded
