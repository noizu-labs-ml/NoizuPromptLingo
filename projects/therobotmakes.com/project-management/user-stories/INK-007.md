# INK-007: Iterate on Pitch with Feedback

**As a** product manager,
**I want to** give the AI specific feedback on its refined pitch and get an updated version,
**So that** the final pitch reflects my domain expertise and not just generic AI output.

**Primary Persona:** Kevin Tanaka (#8)
**Phase:** Sketch
**Priority:** P1-High

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] User can type inline feedback or corrections alongside the AI-refined pitch
- [ ] The AI generates a new refinement that incorporates the feedback within 10 seconds
- [ ] The full history of pitch iterations is preserved and viewable
- [ ] User can revert to any previous iteration
- [ ] There is no hard limit on iteration count, but a soft nudge appears after 5 rounds suggesting the user proceed
