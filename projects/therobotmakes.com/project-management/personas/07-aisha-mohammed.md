- **Location:** London, UK
- **Income:** £110K
- **Tech comfort:** Moderate — understands architecture, manages developers, doesn't code daily

## Quote

> "Discovery phases take 3 weeks and cost the client £15K. If I could cut that to 3 days, I'd win every proposal."

## Goals

1. Accelerate the discovery/scoping phase for new client projects
2. Produce polished specs and mockups during the sales process to close deals
3. Reduce the back-and-forth between PM, designer, and client on requirements
4. Standardize her agency's planning process across all project managers

## Frustrations

- Clients can't articulate what they want until they see something visual
- Discovery workshops produce sticky notes, not actionable specs
- Estimating projects without mockups leads to scope creep and blown budgets
- Junior PMs produce inconsistent specs — quality depends on who's assigned

## Behaviors

- Runs discovery workshops with clients using Miro and FigJam
- Prices projects based on spec complexity, not time — so better specs = better margins
- Tracks proposal win rate obsessively (currently 35%, target 50%)
- Presents mockups in client-facing decks, not raw wireframes

## Scenario

Aisha has a pitch meeting Friday for a property management dashboard. She opens noizu.ink Monday, enters the brief from the client's RFP, and generates a PRD with personas and stories. Tuesday she refines it, catching three scope ambiguities the RFP didn't address. Wednesday she generates a style guide and mockups in the client's brand colors. Thursday she exports everything into a proposal deck. Friday she presents — the client says "this is exactly what we described, but better structured." She wins the contract. Her dev team builds from the generated spec.
