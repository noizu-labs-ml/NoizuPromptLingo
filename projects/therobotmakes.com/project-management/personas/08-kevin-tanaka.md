## Quote

> "I spend 40% of my time writing specs that engineers tell me are ambiguous. I need a tool that forces clarity upfront."

## Goals

1. Produce unambiguous specs with acceptance criteria that engineers can build from without questions
2. Validate scope with stakeholders using visual mockups before committing engineering time
3. Reduce the spec → build → "that's not what I meant" → rebuild cycle
4. Ship more features per quarter by eliminating planning bottlenecks

## Frustrations

- Engineers interpret user stories differently than intended — especially edge cases
- Design team is backlogged — PM requests wait 2-3 sprints for mockups
- Stakeholders approve text specs and then reject the built feature because it "doesn't look right"
- Writing personas feels like busywork until a disagreement reveals they were needed all along

## Behaviors

- Uses Linear for tickets, Notion for specs, Figma for mockups — three tools, three formats
- Spends Monday mornings writing specs, gets interrupted, finishes them Friday
- Attends product meetups, reads Lenny's Newsletter and Shreyas Doshi threads
- Measures personal productivity by "specs shipped to engineering per sprint"

## Scenario

Kevin needs to spec a new onboarding flow. He opens noizu.ink and describes the feature in two paragraphs. The tool asks "Is this for new users only, or also returning users who haven't completed setup?" — a question that would have surfaced in sprint review, not sprint planning. He generates personas (new user, returning user, admin setting up on behalf of a user), stories with acceptance criteria, and wireframes. He drops the mockup link into the Linear ticket. Engineering starts building the same day. Zero spec-related questions in the thread.
