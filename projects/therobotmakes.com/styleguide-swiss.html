06

Cards

2px black border. Color on top edge signals type. Filled cards invert.

Standard Card
Default container. 2px black border, white fill. The border IS the design. No shadows, no gradients, no radius.
default
Accent Card
Red top border signals importance. Featured items, active selections, primary content.
featured
Filled Card
Inverted for emphasis. Black on white becomes white on black. Use sparingly.
