# Priya Sharma — Plein Air / Sketchbook Artist

**Type:** Secondary  
**Age:** 26 | **Location:** Mumbai / traveling  
**Occupation:** Travel sketch artist, sells prints and zines  
**Platform:** MacBook Pro M3, Apple Pencil (if iPad port happens)  

> "I sketch fast and loose. I need a tool that rewards speed, not punishes it."

## Goals

- Quick gestural sketches with charcoal or ink wash, finished in 5-15 minutes
- Capture the energy of plein air painting without carrying a paint kit
- Mix charcoal sketching with selective watercolor washes (pen-and-wash technique)
- Export at print resolution for zine production

## Frustrations

- Digital brushes feel sluggish — latency kills gestural energy
- Watercolor apps make you wait for drying; she wants instant dry-on-demand
- Can't get the scratchy, variable-density mark of vine charcoal digitally
- Small canvas sizes in mobile apps limit print reproduction

## Usage Context

- Rapid sessions on location (coffee shop, park, transit)
- Laptop screen as canvas — needs the app to work at small window sizes
- Switches between charcoal and watercolor within a single sketch
- Batch exports multiple sketches for print layout

## Key Features She'd Use

- Charcoal and pastel media (particulate, smudge, variable density)
- Watercolor washes over charcoal underdrawing
- Fast brush response (sub-16ms input-to-pixel)
- Compact UI that maximizes canvas area

## Design Implications

- Window must work well at smaller sizes (not just fullscreen)
- Toolbar should collapse or auto-hide to maximize canvas
- Quick-switch between two active media (e.g., charcoal + watercolor)
- "Instant dry" shortcut for artists who want to skip drying simulation
