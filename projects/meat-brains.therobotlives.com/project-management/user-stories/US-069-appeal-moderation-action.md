# US-069: Appeal a Moderation Action

## User Story

**As a** prompt engineer (P-001),
**I want to** appeal a moderation decision I believe was made in error,
**So that** I have recourse when my content is incorrectly removed or my account is wrongfully actioned.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given my content was removed or my account was suspended, when I receive the moderation notification, then it includes a link to submit an appeal
- [ ] Given I submit an appeal with my reasoning, when it is received, then I get a confirmation and my appeal enters a review queue
- [ ] Given a moderator reviews my appeal, when they approve it, then my content is reinstated or my account restriction is lifted and I am notified
- [ ] Given a moderator denies my appeal, when the decision is final, then I receive a notification with the outcome and the appeal is closed
- [ ] Given I have a pending appeal, when I attempt to submit another for the same action, then I am informed an appeal is already in progress

## Notes

Appeals should be handled by a different moderator than the one who issued the original action where possible, to ensure fair review. Each account or piece of content should be limited to one appeal per moderation action.
