Kael Ashward

character · warrior · protagonist 847 words · updated 2026-03-01

Born in the shadow of Thornwall's outer districts, Kael first came to the attention of the Iron Guild at the age of sixteen. His ability to navigate the Ashward Passage without a guide marked him as something exceptional in a city that prized knowledge above all else.

The Guild's initial assessment was cautious. Kael carried no formal credentials, no lineage record, and no recommendation from the merchant houses. What he had instead was an uncanny precision of memory and a temperament that the Guild's examiners described, in their formal notation, as "useful."

Role in the Founding War

During the War of Embers, Kael served as a courier for the Guild's northern operations — a position that placed him at the center of the conflict's most critical communications. The full extent of his involvement remains, to this day, contested by historians within the Guild's own archival branch.

Reading Mode — The 20% Editorial Surface

The only place serif typography appears. When a user opens an entry to read, the interface switches from sans-serif navigation mode to Source Serif 4 at 17px with 1.7 line height. Max-width 65ch enforces reading comfort. The sans→serif switch signals: "you're reading now, not scanning."

The War of Embers

event · evt-0011 canon · v5 1,203 words 0 flags

The War of Embers — fought between 847 and 851 of the Ashward Era — was not a war in the conventional sense. It produced no decisive battles, claimed no territory in any permanent way, and left no victors who could be reliably named in the histories. What it left, instead, was the Iron Guild in the form we recognize today: an institution shaped more by what it survived than by what it intended.

Historians within the Guild's own archival division remain divided on the war's origins. The dominant account attributes the conflict to a resource dispute along the Ashward Passage trade route. A second, minority position holds that the underlying cause was the failure of the succession protocols governing the Thornwall Compact.

The Role of the Iron Guild

Whatever its origins, the war's most consequential development was the Guild's decision to maintain operational neutrality throughout the conflict. This was not passivity — the Guild's courier networks, including those associated with Kael Ashward, remained active across all factions — but a deliberate positioning that would define the institution's identity for the next two centuries.

UI contrast — sans-serif resumes here
Navigation elements, tags, metadata, and buttons always render in Inter regardless of reading mode state. Only paragraph text and headings switch to serif. This boundary is non-negotiable.

Implementation Checklist

  • Inter (UI, 400–600) + JetBrains Mono (data, 400) + Source Serif 4 (reading mode only, 400–700)
  • Dark mode only — #0A0A0C background, no light mode variant
  • Reading mode: serif 17px, 1.7 line height, 65ch max-width, warm reading-text color
  • All non-reading text: sans 14px — developer-tool density standard
  • Command palette (⌘K) with full entry and command search
  • Keyboard shortcuts for all major actions — first-class feature
  • Canon entries: white left-bar indicator (#EDEDF0)
  • Generated entries: warm gold left-bar indicator (#C4956A)
  • Three-panel layout: sidebar (240px) + main + collapsible right panel (280px)
  • All interactive elements have visible focus rings (indigo, 2px, 2px offset)
  • Consistency flags use severity colors with muted backgrounds
  • Graph nodes: sans labels, MT-styled borders, canon/generated left-bar distinction
  • No decorative shadows or gradients
  • All transitions: 150ms ease (no variation except 200ms for panels)
  • prefers-reduced-motion: all transitions disabled
  • Color contrast: all text meets WCAG AA minimum
  • Touch targets: minimum 44px
  • Status bar: entry count, flag count, gen queue, sync status
