# Jake Morrison — The Fiction Podcaster

## Demographics

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Age** | 26-33 |
| **Role** | Creator and host of a fiction podcast |
| **Technical Level** | Novice |
| **Industry** | Podcasting / independent media |
| **Location** | Brooklyn, New York |

## Bio

Jake runs a serialized horror fiction podcast with 50 episodes and 15,000 subscribers. His audience is deeply invested in the mythology and sends corrections when he contradicts earlier episodes. He writes scripts in Google Docs and tracks continuity in a spreadsheet that he rarely remembers to update. He's not technical — he needs tools that work without configuration.

## Goals

1. Stop getting "actually, in episode 23 you said..." comments from listeners
2. Keep track of the mythology across 50+ episodes without a spreadsheet
3. Generate supplementary lore content for Patreon bonus episodes

## Frustrations

1. Audience members maintain better continuity wikis than he does — embarrassing and time-consuming to cross-reference
2. His spreadsheet has 30 tabs and he's terrified of breaking it
3. He tried Obsidian but the learning curve was too steep for a non-technical user

## Behaviors

- Writes one episode script per week, records on weekends
- Maintains a Patreon with 800 paying subscribers who expect bonus lore content monthly
- Reads every listener comment and correction
- Uses Google Docs, Sheets, Anchor, and Canva — nothing more technical than that

## Job to Be Done

> "When I'm writing episode 52 and reference the mythology I established in episode 8, I want to search and verify that detail instantly, so my audience doesn't catch me in a contradiction."

## Relationship to Product

Jake would discover the Knowledge Base through podcast creator communities or a recommendation from another fiction podcaster. He needs the simplest possible onboarding — paste scripts, get entities extracted. The generation feature for Patreon bonus content is a revenue driver for him. He'd churn if the UI feels "developer-y" or if he can't figure out how to import his existing scripts.

## Scenarios

1. **Script consistency check** — Before recording, Jake pastes his draft script into Knowledge Base and the consistency engine cross-references it against all 50 previous episodes' entries. It flags one contradiction he'd missed.
2. **Patreon bonus generation** — Jake asks the generation engine to write "an in-universe field report" about a location from his podcast. He edits it lightly and posts it as a Patreon exclusive.
