## Bio

Maya left a senior engineering role at Stripe two years ago to build indie SaaS products. She runs a URL shortener with analytics and a webhook testing tool, both generating ~$4K MRR combined. She handles everything: product decisions, code, deploys, customer support, marketing, finances. She's disciplined about GTD and tracks personal goals alongside work, but her system is duct-taped across five tools.

## Goals

1. **See everything in one place** — personal tasks, both products' backlogs, deploy status, uptime, and life goals in a single morning view
2. **Delegate ops toil to agents** — monitoring, bug triage, deploy verification, and release notes are time sinks she'd gladly hand to AI
3. **Maintain work-life boundaries** — she tracks exercise, reading, and social commitments with the same rigor as shipping features; needs them co-visible but not co-mingled
4. **Scale without hiring** — agents as virtual team members let her operate two products at the quality of a small team

## Frustrations

- **Tool tax:** Linear for tasks, Notion for docs, UptimeRobot for monitoring, Todoist for personal, Apple Calendar for schedule — nothing talks to anything
- **Context switching:** Opening five tabs to understand why error rates spiked after a deploy
- **AI is dumb in current tools:** Linear's AI can summarize but can't actually triage a bug, link it to a deploy, and suggest a fix
- **Personal items are second-class:** Work tools treat personal todos as noise; personal tools can't show her deploy status

## Behaviors

- Starts every day with a "daily review" ritual (15 min) — currently manual across tools
- Ships 2-3 deploys per day across both products
- Monitors uptime obsessively but manually (checks dashboard 4-5x/day)
- Uses keyboard shortcuts heavily; hates mouse-dependent UIs
- Reads changelogs and docs in dark mode; light mode is a dealbreaker

## Job to Be Done

> "I want one surface where I can see my full operational life — both products, personal goals, deploy health — and AI agents that handle the monitoring, triage, and reporting I'm currently doing manually so I can focus on building."

## Key Scenarios

1. **Morning planning:** Opens Today view, sees planner agent has re-prioritized based on overnight monitoring alerts. Adjusts and starts.
2. **Bug from monitoring:** Monitor agent detects error spike on Product A, auto-creates P2 ticket linked to last deploy, Maya reviews context and fixes in 10 minutes instead of 30.
3. **Weekly OKR check-in:** Planner agent drafts progress update from completed items. Maya reviews, adjusts next week's priorities.
4. **Personal + professional collision:** Has a dentist appointment blocking afternoon — sees it in Today view alongside sprint commitments, re-plans accordingly.
