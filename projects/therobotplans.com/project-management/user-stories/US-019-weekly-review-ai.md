## User Story

As an **Alex Russo (Productivity Enthusiast)**, I want a weekly review with an AI-generated summary of accomplishments, missed items, and suggestions so that I can reflect on my week and adjust my approach without spending an hour manually compiling a review.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A weekly review agent generates a structured report covering: items completed, items missed/deferred, habit streak status, and time allocation by domain
- [ ] The report includes AI-generated insights such as recurring blockers, overcommitment patterns, and underutilized time slots
- [ ] The review presents 3-5 actionable suggestions for the coming week (e.g., "You deferred 'write docs' three times — consider breaking it into smaller tasks")
- [ ] Users can annotate the review with personal reflections that are saved alongside the generated report
- [ ] Past weekly reviews are archived and searchable, forming a personal productivity journal over time

## Notes

The weekly review is a staple of GTD and other productivity systems, but most people skip it because it is tedious to compile. The AI does the compilation; the human does the reflection. The archived reviews become a valuable longitudinal dataset — over months, the AI can identify seasonal patterns, burnout indicators, and growth trends. Consider scheduling the review to auto-generate on a configurable day (default: Sunday evening).
