# Persona 02: Sarah — The Low-Vision Explorer

## Demographics
- **Age:** 34
- **Location:** Portland, OR
- **Occupation:** Freelance technical writer
- **Vision:** Retinitis pigmentosa — tunnel vision with declining peripheral, light-sensitive; legally blind but has some central vision remaining

## Technology Profile
- **Screen reader:** VoiceOver on macOS (partial use — toggles on/off depending on fatigue)
- **Browser:** Safari with custom CSS overrides (large text, high contrast)
- **Preferences:** Dark mode mandatory, large readable fonts, no flickering/strobing
- **Display:** 27" monitor at 1280x720 effective resolution, face close to screen
- **Current games:** Zork remasters, IF Comp entries, some Baldur's Gate with mods

## Goals
- Find a game that works across her vision spectrum — readable when she can see, fully functional when she switches to VoiceOver on bad eye days
- Explore rich world lore at her own pace without time pressure
- Experience the interactive fiction and branching narratives
- Customize visual presentation (font size, contrast, spacing) without breaking functionality

## Frustrations
- Games that are "dark mode" but use low-contrast text (gray on slightly-less-gray)
- Accessible games that look ugly — she can still *see*, and aesthetics matter
- Timed events that punish slow reading
- Settings that reset on page reload
- Games that work with screen readers OR visually, but not both simultaneously

## Behaviors
- Adjusts browser zoom between 150-300% throughout a session
- Toggles between reading visually and listening via VoiceOver mid-game
- Takes breaks every 30-45 minutes due to eye fatigue
- Screenshots game text to read later in her preferred reader app
- Values long, descriptive prose over terse system messages

## Relationship to Blade of Eternity
Sarah validates the "blind-first, sighted-compatible" principle. The Editorial typography (18-20px Literata, 1.75 line height, 65ch max width) and dark palette (#E5E5E5 on #0A0A0A = 17.4:1 contrast) serve her visual needs. The game must remain coherent when she switches between visual reading and VoiceOver mid-session.

## Key Scenario
Sarah is exploring Mordoon's vaults, reading room descriptions visually. Her eyes tire. She enables VoiceOver without reloading. The game state, focus position, and narrative log must remain intact — VoiceOver picks up exactly where her eyes left off.

## Success Metric
Sarah can seamlessly transition between visual and screen reader use without losing game state or context.
