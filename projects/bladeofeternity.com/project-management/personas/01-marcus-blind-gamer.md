# Persona 01: Marcus — The Blind Power Gamer

## Demographics
- **Age:** 28
- **Location:** Atlanta, GA
- **Occupation:** Customer support specialist (remote)
- **Vision:** Totally blind since birth

## Technology Profile
- **Screen reader:** NVDA on Windows (primary), VoiceOver on iPhone
- **Browser:** Firefox
- **Typing speed:** 90+ WPM
- **Gaming setup:** Mechanical keyboard, stereo headphones, no monitor
- **Current games:** AudioQuake, A Hero's Call, Entombed, MUD clients (Mushclient)

## Goals
- Find a multiplayer game where blindness isn't a disadvantage — where he competes on equal footing
- Experience a persistent world that remembers his choices and reputation
- Join a clan and participate in territory wars
- Master combat mechanics deeply enough to top PvP leaderboards

## Frustrations
- "Accessible" games that are shallow — 2 hours of content wrapped in a press release
- Games that bolt on accessibility after launch (broken tab order, unlabeled buttons, silent state changes)
- Audio-only games that sacrifice depth for the gimmick
- Multiplayer games where sighted players have an inherent information advantage

## Behaviors
- Tests every new "accessible game" announcement within 48 hours
- Reports bugs with precise screen reader output transcripts
- Writes detailed forum posts comparing combat systems across games
- Stays up late grinding crafting systems
- Will abandon a game instantly if the onboarding has unlabeled form fields

## Relationship to Blade of Eternity
Marcus is the core audience. The ARIA live region architecture, keyboard contract, and combat batching system exist because of players like him. He'll stress-test every interaction pattern during rapid PvP combat and find the edge cases where announcements collide or focus traps break.

## Key Scenario
Marcus enters a PvP duel. He needs combat prose, HP alerts, and action menu updates to arrive in a predictable sequence without announcement collisions. He's issuing commands every 2-3 seconds and relies entirely on the narrative channel to understand positioning and physics outcomes.

## Success Metric
Marcus plays 20+ hours/week for 3+ months without hitting an accessibility-blocking bug.
