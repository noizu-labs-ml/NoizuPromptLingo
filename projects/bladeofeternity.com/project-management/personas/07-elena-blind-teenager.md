# Persona 07: Elena — The Blind Teenager Discovering Gaming

## Demographics
- **Age:** 16
- **Location:** Miami, FL
- **Occupation:** High school sophomore
- **Vision:** Blind since age 3 (retinoblastoma)

## Technology Profile
- **Screen reader:** VoiceOver on iPhone (primary device), VoiceOver on school-issued MacBook
- **Browser:** Safari
- **Typing:** Competent but not fast (~40 WPM on physical keyboard, slower on phone)
- **Gaming experience:** Audio games on iOS (A Blind Legend, Blindfold games), some Alexa story games
- **Social:** Active on blind teen Discord servers, follows #BlindGamers on social media

## Goals
- Find a game she can play *with* her sighted friends, not a "blind game" they'd never try
- Experience the kind of gaming her classmates talk about — quests, clans, PvP, loot
- Build a character and reputation over time, not just play one-off levels
- Feel like a regular gamer, not a special-needs case

## Frustrations
- Audio-only games feel isolating — her sighted friends won't play them
- "Accessible" games for kids are patronizing — she's 16, not 6
- She watches (listens to) her friends play Fortnite and feels excluded
- Web games rarely work properly with VoiceOver on iPhone

## Behaviors
- Plays primarily on iPhone — her laptop is for school
- Asks friends to describe game mechanics before deciding to invest time
- Prefers games she can discuss in group chats
- Will abandon a game if she needs sighted help to get past the tutorial
- Shares discoveries with her blind teen Discord community

## Relationship to Blade of Eternity
Elena tests the mobile VoiceOver experience and the onboarding flow. If she can complete character creation and her first combat encounter solo on an iPhone, the accessibility architecture works. She's also the crossover case: a blind player who wants to play with sighted friends. Tyler (Persona 06) and Elena being in the same clan is the game's thesis proven.

## Key Scenario
Elena's sighted friend Tyler tells her about a browser game he's been playing. She opens bladeofeternity.com on her iPhone in Safari. VoiceOver reads the page structure cleanly. She creates a character, enters Rune, and completes her first quest — all without asking for help. She messages Tyler: "I'm in your clan now."

## Success Metric
Elena completes onboarding on mobile VoiceOver in under 15 minutes without sighted assistance, and plays actively with sighted friends.
