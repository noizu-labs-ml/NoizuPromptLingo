# US-211: Environmental Combat Tactics

**Persona:** Jamie — IF enthusiast, sighted, narrative quality focused
**Priority:** P1
**Epic:** Advanced Combat & Tactics

## Story
As Jamie, I want to use the environment itself as a weapon — collapsing ceilings, flooding chambers, igniting oil slicks, pushing enemies off ledges — so that each fight location offers unique creative solutions that reward reading the environment carefully.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Environmental interaction actions appear in combat action menu when relevant objects are in range: "Pull ceiling lever", "Ignite oil slick", "Push to ledge" — discoverable without requiring prior knowledge
- [ ] Physics engine resolves environmental attacks: collapse computes falling debris and impact zones, fire propagates per fire system (US-153), flood fills available space, push checks ledge proximity and target weight vs player strength
- [ ] Environmental attacks require aiming at the environmental feature, not the enemy: "You pull the lever — the ceiling groans and collapses onto the Warlord and his guards"
- [ ] Friendly fire from environmental attacks explicitly warned: "This will affect all creatures in the zone — 2 allies included. Proceed?" with cancel option
- [ ] Environmental hazard persistence narrated on subsequent rounds if still active: "The fire spreads — the eastern corner is now impassable"
- [ ] Discovery of environmental opportunities narrated naturally during room entry and examination: "The ancient chains holding the chandelier look corroded — one good strike might bring it down"
- [ ] Dramatic narration generated by LLM for successful environmental kills: multi-sentence set-piece befitting the method
- [ ] Environmental tactics grant bonus XP to encourage engagement; amount and type shown in post-combat summary

## Notes
Jamie's interactive fiction background means they approach each room as a puzzle: what can I use? The environmental interaction menu must surface options organically so players don't need to guess verbs — this mirrors the best IF design. The physics engine integration is what makes these feel real: a fire that actually spreads (using US-153 propagation), a ceiling that falls on a computed impact zone, a flood that rises over rounds. The LLM narration for environmental kills is the highest-return investment — "you pushed the orc off the ledge" becomes a 3-sentence moment of genuine drama. Discovery narration during room entry is the hook that teaches players the mechanic: if the corroded chains are described on entry, attentive players (Jamie especially) will remember them when combat starts. Friendly fire warnings are non-negotiable for environmental attacks — the blast radius must be disclosed before commitment. Bonus XP for environmental tactics is a game design nudge to reward creativity over brute force.
