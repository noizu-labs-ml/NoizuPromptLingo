# US-069: Clan Territory Control and Defense

**Persona:** Tyler — Sighted MMO Refugee
**Priority:** P1
**Epic:** Clans

## Story
As Tyler, I want my clan to claim, develop, and defend territories so that geographic control translates to economic and strategic advantage that makes clan membership worth fighting for.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Territories are discrete named zones with defined borders, resource nodes, and a control flag
- [ ] `TERRITORY STATUS` announces controlling clan, contested status, defense rating, and active bonuses
- [ ] Claiming an uncontrolled territory requires a minimum clan size and a planted standard ritual (takes N rounds, interruptible)
- [ ] Territory bonuses (resource yield, tax income, defensive buffs) are announced when players enter controlled zones
- [ ] Clan officers receive real-time Channel notifications when territory is attacked or flag status changes
- [ ] Territory map is accessible as a text grid or a navigable list: "Zone: Ironpeak Ridge | Owner: Iron Covenant | Status: Contested | Resources: Iron ore (rich), Coal (moderate)"
- [ ] Losing a territory triggers a clan-wide announcement with details of the attacker

## Notes
Tyler plays for the political meta-game. Territory must feel consequential — not cosmetic. The defense rating system should reward active investment (garrison, upgrades) over passive holding. Accessibility note: the territory "map" must be fully navigable without visual spatial awareness — the list representation is primary.
