# US-192: Character Aging & Legacy System

**Persona:** Jamie — Interactive fiction enthusiast focused on narrative quality
**Priority:** P2
**Epic:** Character Progression & Classes

## Story
As Jamie, I want an optional aging system where my character grows older over real time — gaining wisdom while losing physical edge — and can eventually retire into a legacy NPC that new characters encounter so that my character's story has a beginning, middle, and meaningful end.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Aging is opt-in at character creation with a clear explanation of mechanical consequences; default is no aging; opting in grants a permanent "Storied Life" passive bonus to all XP gain (+10%) as incentive
- [ ] Character age advances one in-game year per configurable real-world period (default: 30 days); aging milestone announcements delivered via polite ARIA: "Your character has reached the age of 40. The years have left their mark."
- [ ] Aging effects applied at milestone years: physical stat decay (-1 Strength, Agility at age 40, 50, 60) offset by wisdom gains (+2 Intelligence, Charisma at same milestones); net effect neutral at any single milestone
- [ ] Retirement option becomes available at age 60 or any point after; retirement triggers a dedicated narrative flow with LLM-generated retrospective based on character's history, achievements, and backstory
- [ ] Retired character becomes a persistent NPC in their home city or origin region; NPC name and appearance derived from character data; NPC may offer new characters quests, lore, or items referencing their history
- [ ] Legacy NPC dialogues generated by LLM using retired character's backstory, achievements, faction standings, and titles; minimum three unique dialogue branches referencing specific character history
- [ ] Player who retired the character receives a "Legacy" account marker and may view their legacy NPC's location and interactions via a retired characters panel
- [ ] Aging characters display age in character sheet and inspection panel; SR reads: "Age: 43 — Storied Life aging active"

## Notes
Jamie is drawn to narrative completeness — the idea that a character can have an arc, conclude, and leave a mark on the world. This is a deeply IF-influenced design; think of it as the game-as-novel with a proper final chapter. The opt-in requirement is absolute: aging with mechanical penalties is not appropriate to impose on players without consent, especially Marcus (who would view physical stat decay as a competitive disadvantage). The +10% XP bonus makes aging genuinely attractive as a trade-off, not just a flavor choice. The legacy NPC system is the most technically complex element: LLM-generated dialogue grounded in specific character history requires careful prompt design and context management. Dave will appreciate the depth; Jamie will love the narrative closure. Ship this in a post-launch expansion when the LLM integration pipeline is mature.
