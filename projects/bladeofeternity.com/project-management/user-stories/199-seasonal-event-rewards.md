# US-199: Seasonal Event Rewards

**Persona:** Carol — Parent of a blind child concerned with safety and appropriateness
**Priority:** P2
**Epic:** Character Progression & Classes

## Story
As Carol, I want seasonal limited-time events with unique rewards that persist after the event ends — with age-appropriate content and visible progress tracking — so that my family can participate in special content without missing out due to timing or content concerns.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Minimum four seasonal events per year aligned with broad calendar themes (winter celebration, spring renewal, summer festival, harvest/autumn) with content appropriate for all age ratings
- [ ] Seasonal event status announced on login when an event is active: "The Winter Solstice Celebration is underway! Special quests and rewards are available until December 31st."
- [ ] Event progress tracked in a dedicated seasonal panel accessible via main menu: keyboard-navigable list of event-specific objectives with completion status and reward preview; SR reads each objective fully
- [ ] All seasonal rewards (cosmetic titles, companion skins, special items) persist permanently after the event ends; no reward locked behind time-gated repetition preventing casual players from completion
- [ ] Event content rated and labeled: event panel header includes content advisory consistent with the game's age rating; any event content above standard rating requires parental consent acknowledgment before access
- [ ] Seasonal quests integrate with the LLM narrative engine to deliver thematic storytelling relevant to the season; narration adjusts based on character's region, class, and backstory
- [ ] Event participation designed for variable session frequency: objectives completable in 30-minute sessions over the event period without requiring daily login; catch-up mechanics available for players who start late
- [ ] Seasonal event rewards visible in character profile and inspection panel with seasonal tag; SR announces "Winter Solstice 2027: Frostmantle" when reading a seasonal cosmetic title or item

## Notes
Carol's priority is safety and predictability: she needs to trust that bringing her child to a seasonal event will not expose them to inappropriate content as it does in some other games. The content advisory label in the event panel is the parental visibility mechanism — Carol can see at a glance whether a new event is within bounds before her child encounters it. The persistence of rewards addresses the FOMO problem that harms players with disabilities who may have interruptions in play (medical appointments, school pressure) during event windows. The 30-minute session design respects Lena's and Carol's household schedule. The catch-up mechanic is critical for fairness: a player who joins an event two weeks in should still be able to earn all rewards through normal play, not daily grinding. Raj will stream seasonal events, providing organic content for Blade of Eternity's social presence.
