# US-059: Night at Mordoon Heritage Integration

**Persona:** Jamie — Sighted IF Enthusiast / Literature Grad Student
**Priority:** P1
**Epic:** World & Narrative

## Story
As Jamie, I want the "Night at Mordoon" Twine story to be woven into the living game world as discoverable lore, canonical geography, and recurring narrative echoes so that players who know the source material feel continuity rather than discontinuity with it.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] All 40 Twine passages are reviewed and mapped to: in-game room, NPC, lore entry, or historical event
- [ ] Key Twine locations appear as actual navigable rooms in Mordoon with descriptions that honor the source text's voice
- [ ] Canonical characters from the Twine story appear as NPCs with consistent characterization and knowledge of their story history
- [ ] Lore entries discoverable via `examine` and `ask` reference Twine narrative events as in-world history
- [ ] Players who complete the Twine story before playing receive a canon import: their Twine choices map to a character backstory option
- [ ] The "mask or the knife" choice (US-049) is the canonical consequence-tracked choice derived from the Twine story
- [ ] Twine passage text is never reproduced verbatim — it is transformed into in-world lore/description with new prose
- [ ] A heritage credit is accessible via `history mordoon` — acknowledging the Twine story as the world's founding document

## Notes
- Jamie has read the Twine story and will notice inconsistencies — accuracy of heritage integration is a trust signal
- "Canon import" for Twine players is a stretch goal for v1; core requirement is that the Twine world is faithfully represented
- Twine prose should not be directly copy-pasted — it should be re-crafted in the game's AI-consistent voice
- Character continuity: NPCs derived from Twine must reference their Twine history if asked about it
- This story requires a heritage mapping document as a deliverable before implementation can begin
