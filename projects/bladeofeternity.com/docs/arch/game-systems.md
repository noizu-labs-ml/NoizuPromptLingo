# Game Systems

Systems carried forward from the original Blade of Eternity (~2013-2014), evolved for the new architecture.

## World

- **Cities**: Rune (starter — Town Square, Financial District, Battle Centre), Mordoon (city of the dead — vaults, festival grounds), and planned new locations
- **Districts**: Functional zones per city with shops, services, and resident NPCs
- **Travel**: Inter-city movement with physics-simulated terrain and random encounters
- **Housing**: Player-owned spaces with customizable interiors
- **Time/Weather**: Dynamic cycles affecting visibility, NPC behavior, and available actions

## Combat

- **PvP Duels**: Real-time physics-based combat — positioning, weapon reach, terrain advantage matter
- **Battle Tent**: NPC challenges with configurable difficulty tiers
- **Skills**: Longshot (range advantage), Elusion (evasion), Whirlwind (multi-hit) — expanded with physics interactions (knockback, environmental use)
- **Catacombs**: Dungeon crawling with emergent encounters and spatial navigation
- **Status Effects**: Narrated through the status ARIA channel — buffs/debuffs announced as they apply and expire

## Economy

- **Currency**: Gold, crystals, tokens (multiple tiers for different transaction types)
- **Crafting**: Material gathering from world zones, recipe-based creation with quality variance
- **Shops**: NPC merchants with supply/demand dynamic pricing
- **Trading**: Player-to-player economy with escrow
- **Jobs**: Employment system with rank progression and income generation

## Community

- **Clans**: Player organizations with territories, wars, shared resources, and governance
- **Chat**: Real-time via Phoenix Channels, rendered in narrative log
- **Events**: AI-generated seasonal events that evolve with player participation (the Festival of the Living remembers last year)
- **Crimes**: Risk/reward criminal activities with clan territory implications and reputation effects

## Interactive Fiction

- **Quests**: Branching narratives with persistent world consequences
- **Lore**: Discoverable world history woven into room descriptions and NPC dialogue
- **Choice**: Moral decisions affecting reputation, NPC relationships, and available narrative paths
- **Heritage Content**: "Night at Mordoon" (40-passage branching Twine story) integrated as in-world experience
