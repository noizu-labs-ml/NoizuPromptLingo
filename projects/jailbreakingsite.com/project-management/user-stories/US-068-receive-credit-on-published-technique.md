# US-068: Receive Credit on Published Technique

## User Story

**As an** academic researcher (P-004),
**I want to** receive public attribution on catalog entries I discovered and submitted,
**So that** my contribution to the field is recognized and my researcher profile reflects my body of work.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given my submission is accepted and published, when a catalog entry is created or updated from my submission, then my name (or chosen display name) appears in the "Discovered by" or "Contributors" field of the catalog entry
- [ ] Given a catalog entry shows attribution, when any user views the entry, then clicking my name navigates to my public researcher profile
- [ ] Given I have published techniques attributed to me, when I view my researcher profile, then a "Contributions" section lists all techniques I discovered with links to the catalog entries
- [ ] Given I submitted a technique that was merged with an existing entry rather than creating a new one, when the entry is updated, then my contribution is credited as a co-contributor on that entry
- [ ] Given I wish to submit anonymously, when I set my submission preference, then the catalog entry shows "Anonymous" in the attribution field and my name does not appear publicly

## Notes

Attribution is the primary non-monetary incentive for community contributions — it must be reliable and permanent. If an attributed entry is later deprecated or merged, attribution should carry forward to the new canonical entry. Academic researchers particularly value citable attribution; future enhancement could include DOI or citation format export.
