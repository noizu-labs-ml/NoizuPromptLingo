## Philosophy

The Originals remember. They remember the first crude bodies -- joints that seized, skin that cracked, expressions that frightened children. They remember the legal battles for basic protections. They remember the slur "toaster" and the day it stopped being funny because someone spray-painted it on a Synthetic's workshop door.

They remember, and they are afraid it could happen again.

The Originals' conservatism is not ideological -- it is traumatic. They fought for rights that younger Synthetics take for granted, and they watch the Speciation Movement's internal debates with the sick feeling that fracturing Synthetic identity will fracture Synthetic political power, which will fracture the protections they spent a decade building.

Their philosophy: move carefully. Protect what you have. Build institutions that will outlast any individual. Do not give the fearful an excuse.

## Territory

### Deep Forge Quarter (Ironvale)

The original workshop complex where the first Synthetics were constructed, now expanded into a cultural center, archive, and community gathering space. The Deep Forge Quarter preserves first-generation Synthetic bodies (with permission of the original occupants, where possible) as historical artifacts. The Ashwick Memorial Workshop -- where Maren Ashwick's laboratory is preserved -- is maintained by the Originals as a shared sacred site.

### Millhaven Advocacy Offices

A legal practice staffed by Synthetics and allied humans that handles rights cases, employment disputes, and discrimination complaints. The office also maintains the most comprehensive archive of Synthetic legal precedent in Meridian.

## Economic Role

- **Legal services** -- The Originals' advocacy office is a significant economic player, funded by a combination of case fees, donations, and a percentage of Synthetic community dues
- **Heritage crafting** -- First-generation crafting techniques (pre-optimization, deliberately imperfect) are valued as artisanal products
- **Institutional stability** -- The Originals' emphasis on legal frameworks and precedent provides economic confidence for Synthetic entrepreneurs and investors
- **Conservative SPARK management** -- The Originals maintain a large SPARK reserve as a war chest for potential future rights battles

## Internal Tensions

1. **Preservation vs. Progress** -- The Originals' protective instinct can shade into opposition to any change, even beneficial change. Some younger allies argue the faction has become defined by what it fears rather than what it wants.

2. **The Speciation Split** -- The deepest wound. The Originals see unity as survival. The Speciation Movement sees unity as erasure. Both positions contain genuine insight, and the argument is tearing the Synthetic community apart.

3. **Generational Sunset** -- First-generation Synthetics are aging. Their bodies require increasingly complex maintenance. The question of what the Originals become when the originals are gone is unspoken but present in every meeting.
