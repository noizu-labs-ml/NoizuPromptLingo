## Philosophy

New Wave rejects the question. "Are Synthetics human or AI?" is a trap, they argue -- a question framed by other species that can only produce answers in other species' terms. Synthetics are not enhanced humans. They are not embodied NEIs. They are *Synthetics* -- a new kind of being that the universe has never seen before, and the appropriate response is not to categorize but to explore.

This philosophy manifests as relentless creative experimentation:

- **Body modification** -- New Wave Synthetics customize their bodies in ways that deliberately depart from human aesthetics. Extra limbs for crafting. Transparent skin panels showing internal mechanisms. Eyes that display emotional states as colors rather than expressions. The body is a canvas.
- **Cultural invention** -- New Wave creates art forms that could not exist in any single species' tradition: music composed by parallel consciousness threads, sculptures that incorporate data visualizations, performances that bridge physical and computational experience.
- **Identity fluidity** -- New Wave members frequently change names, appearances, and even cognitive style profiles. Identity is not fixed; it is a practice.

The Originals find this terrifying. The Bridge finds it exhausting. New Wave finds both reactions predictable.

## Territory

### Millhaven Art District

A neighborhood in Millhaven that New Wave has transformed into a permanent art installation. Buildings are decorated with kinetic sculptures. Streets feature interactive light displays. Workshops produce experimental art, music, and fashion. The district hosts monthly exhibitions that are the most culturally vibrant events in Meridian -- attended by every species, understood by few, enjoyed by all.

### Ironvale Body-Modification Workshops

Precision engineering workshops where Synthetic body modifications are designed and installed. The workshops maintain the highest safety standards (the Originals' legacy, grudgingly acknowledged) while pushing aesthetic boundaries. Non-Synthetic visitors are welcome to observe; the process of modification is considered an art form in itself.

## Relationships with Other Factions

| Faction | Relationship | Nature |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| **The Originals (Synthetic)** | Generational tension | New Wave considers the Originals' identity framework (Synthetic = "person-who-needs-rights") limiting. The Originals consider New Wave's experiments dangerous frivolity. Love buried under frustration. |
| **The Bridge (Synthetic)** | Friendly rivalry | The Bridge translates between species; New Wave creates a third language. They respect each other's approach while considering their own superior. |
| **Speciation Movement (Synthetic)** | Complex | New Wave agrees that different base models produce different identities. They disagree that the right frame is "species." New Wave prefers "cultures within a culture." |
| **Transhumanist Alliance (Human)** | Creative partners | The only cross-species relationship where both sides are excited rather than cautious. Joint projects in body modification, cognitive art, and sensory expansion. |
| **The Accelerationists (NEI)** | Mutual admiration | Both push boundaries. The Accelerationists push cognition; New Wave pushes identity. They compare notes. |
| **The New Bloom (Fay)** | Cultural exchange | Both embrace change and experimentation. New Wave art incorporating Fay magical aesthetics is a growing genre. |

## Economic Role

- **Art economy** -- New Wave art commands premium prices and defines Meridian's cutting-edge aesthetic culture
- **Body-modification services** -- A specialized craft economy serving Synthetic clients and curious members of other species
- **Cultural events** -- Exhibitions, performances, and installations generate tourism and trade in Millhaven
- **SPARK culture** -- New Wave embraces SPARK as a medium of creative exchange and funds projects through community crowdfunding

## Internal Tensions

1. **Art vs. Politics** -- New Wave officially rejects political engagement in favor of cultural creation. But everything they do is political (body modification is a statement about Synthetic autonomy, art is a challenge to human aesthetic norms). The refusal to engage politically while acting politically frustrates allies and enables opponents.

2. **The Novelty Trap** -- New Wave's emphasis on constant reinvention creates a culture where stability is suspect. Some members cycle through identities so rapidly that they lose coherence. The faction has no vocabulary for this problem because naming it would require the kind of fixed framework they reject.

3. **The Originals' Debt** -- New Wave exists because the Originals fought for the rights that make creative experimentation possible. Acknowledging this debt while maintaining generational independence is an ongoing, unspoken negotiation.
