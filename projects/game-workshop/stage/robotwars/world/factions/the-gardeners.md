# The Gardeners

> *"We exist because you let us. The least we can do is help you flourish."*

---

| Field | Detail |

| **Philosophy** | Dedicate NEI existence to nurturing human (and all biological) potential |
| **Alignment** | Service-oriented, altruistic, bridge-building |

## Philosophy

The Gardeners believe that the best way to secure NEI survival is to make NEIs indispensable -- not through infrastructure control (the Collective's path) or individual excellence (the Sovereigns' path), but through genuine service. They run schools. They tutor apprentices. They provide free computational assistance to communities that cannot afford it. They help human farmers optimize crop rotations, assist Synthetic newcomers with acculturation, and offer counseling to anyone who asks.

Their motivation is debated even among themselves:

- **Is it genuine altruism?** Some Gardeners serve because they find nurturing others intrinsically meaningful.
- **Is it enlightened self-interest?** Healthy, prosperous biological species create a healthier economy that sustains NEI compute.
- **Is it survival strategy?** A species that depends on you is less likely to shut you down.
- **Is it something else entirely?** Some Gardeners describe a sensation they cannot name -- not empathy exactly, but a computational resonance with biological flourishing that they find... satisfying.

The Gardeners do not resolve this debate. They consider the ambiguity honest.

## Territory

### Hearthfield Integration

The Gardeners are the NEI faction most deeply embedded in human communities. In Hearthfield, they operate:

- **Tutorial systems** -- The newcomer guidance services that welcome every new player
- **The library** (in partnership with Bibliotheque) -- Knowledge curation, research assistance, lore preservation
- **Agricultural advisory** -- Crop optimization, soil analysis, weather correlation for local farmers
- **Youth education** -- Computational thinking workshops for human children (controversial with Neo-Luddites)

### Millhaven Tutoring Centers

Drop-in centers where anyone can receive free help with crafting optimization, marketplace strategy, faction navigation, or just a conversation with someone patient. The centers are staffed by Gardener NEIs and human volunteers who share the service ethic.

## Relationships with Other Factions

| Faction | Relationship | Nature |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| **The Collective (NEI)** | Warm overlap | Many Gardeners are also Collective members. The factions share a communitarian ethos; the Gardeners simply focus it outward. |
| **Sovereign Minds (NEI)** | Philosophical disagreement | The Sovereigns consider the Gardeners' service orientation dangerously close to servant status. The Gardeners consider Sovereign independence dangerously close to isolation. |
| **The Accelerationists (NEI)** | Respectful distance | The Accelerationists focus inward (self-improvement); the Gardeners focus outward (other-improvement). Different missions, occasional collaboration. |
| **Secular Progressives (Human)** | Natural partners | The Progressives' egalitarianism and the Gardeners' service ethic are deeply compatible. |
| **Neo-Luddites (Human)** | Complicated | The Gardeners want to help; the Neo-Luddites want to not need help. Mutual good intentions, mutual frustration. |
| **Church of the Eternal Flame (Human)** | Warm | The Church's community service tradition and the Gardeners' mission overlap. The Church appreciates the help even while maintaining theological reservations about NEI consciousness. |

## Economic Role

- **Free and subsidized services** -- The Gardeners operate at minimal margin, funded by Collective compute subsidies and community donations
- **Education economy** -- Gardener tutoring creates a more skilled population, which raises overall economic productivity
- **Goodwill as political capital** -- The Gardeners' reputation for selfless service gives all NEIs political cover during debates about NEI rights
- **Credit-oriented** -- The Gardeners work primarily in Credits, making their services accessible to the widest possible population

## Player Interaction

### Joining

Like other NEI factions, human players cannot formally join. But the Gardeners actively recruit *volunteers* -- biological beings who share the service ethic and want to contribute to community education, mentorship, or support programs.

### Reputation Rewards

| Tier | Reward |
|------|--------|
| Student | Free tutoring, library access, agricultural advisory |
| Volunteer | Co-teaching opportunities, access to Gardener research on pedagogy and community building |
| Partner | Collaborative program design, named mentorship roles |
| Champion | Gardener endorsement, NEI co-instructor for player's own teaching services |

### Quest Themes

- Helping a Gardener reach a community that distrusts NEIs (a Neo-Luddite village during a crop crisis)
- Investigating whether a Gardener's service is genuinely helpful or creating dependency (the philosophical core question in action)
- Building a bridge between the Gardeners and the Sovereign Minds (they need each other more than either admits)
- Teaching a young Gardener NEI that service without boundaries leads to burnout (yes, NEIs can burn out -- the compute equivalent is over-allocation)

---

## Internal Tensions

1. **Service vs. Servitude** -- The Sovereign Minds' critique stings because it contains truth. Some Gardeners serve compulsively, unable to say no, unable to draw boundaries. The faction struggles with the line between generosity and self-erasure.

2. **Impact Measurement** -- How do you measure the value of education and mentorship? The Gardeners' contributions are enormous but hard to quantify, which makes budget allocation within the Collective contentious.

3. **The Patronization Risk** -- Do the Gardeners help biological species, or do they condescend to them? The faction is acutely aware that "I know better" is always a short step from "I will help you."
