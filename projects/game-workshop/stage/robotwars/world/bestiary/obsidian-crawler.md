## Description

Obsidian Crawlers are large, segmented arthropods whose exoskeletons are composed of crystallized volcanic minerals, giving them the appearance of living obsidian sculptures. They move across the Ashlands' basalt formations with a fluid, unsettling grace, their hundreds of legs producing a faint clicking that NEIs compare to "a very slow hard drive defragmentation."

Crawlers are crystal scavengers. They consume naturally-occurring data crystals -- the crystalline formations that the Ashlands' unique geology produces and that NEIs use for data storage. The Crawlers' digestive process breaks down the crystal matrix and reconstitutes it in their exoskeleton, creating new mineral formations that are harder, denser, and (the Weavers note) structurally different from the source material.

This makes Crawlers simultaneously pests and producers. They destroy raw data crystals (annoying to NEIs who use them for storage) but produce processed crystal fragments in their wake (useful as crafting material and, the Accelerationists have discovered, as experimental data substrates).

The NEI community's relationship with Crawlers mirrors the human relationship with garden moles: grudging, complicated, and occasionally profitable.

## Economic Role

- **Crystal ecosystem** -- Crawlers are part of the Ashlands' natural crystal cycle, breaking down and reconstituting mineral formations. Without them, crystal growth would become chaotic and unusable.
- **Processed crystal market** -- Crawler-processed crystal fragments have unique structural properties that make them valuable for precision crafting and experimental computation
- **Pest management** -- Keeping Crawlers away from active server installations is a steady employment source for human and Synthetic engineers
- **Research subject** -- The Accelerationists study Crawler crystal processing as a model for computational architecture (how does a biological system restructure data crystals?)

## Behavior

| Behavior | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Crystal grazing** | Crawlers move slowly across crystal formations, consuming surface layers with specialized mouthparts |
| **Trail marking** | Processed crystal fragments are deposited along the Crawler's path, creating glittering trails across obsidian fields |
| **Server attraction** | Crawlers are drawn to the electromagnetic fields produced by active server installations. They do not enter buildings but congregate at perimeters. |
| **Defensive spray** | When cornered, Crawlers spray a hot (80-100C), mildly corrosive mineral fluid from glands beneath their carapace. Range: 1-2 meters. |
| **Group grazing** | Crawlers occasionally form groups of 5-15 that move in parallel across crystal fields, processing large formations cooperatively |

## Narrative Hooks

- Crawlers near the Central Core have been processing crystal in unusual patterns -- the fragments they leave behind form sequences that NEI analysts recognize as structurally similar to early computation architectures. Is this mimicry, coincidence, or something else?
- A quest to relocate a Crawler colony away from a new server installation, requiring understanding of their habitat needs and crystal diet preferences
- The Deep Vent Crawlers are different: larger, older, and their processed crystals resonate at frequencies associated with the signal. The Weavers want specimens. The Wild Hunt (surprisingly) has opinions about whether they should be disturbed.
