# Thornmere Swamp

> *"The water glows. The trees walk when no one watches. This place is old."*

---

## Identity

A vast bioluminescent wetland -- the second Fay stronghold and the most magically potent biome in Meridian. Dark water reflecting bioluminescent mushrooms, floating platforms connected by vine bridges, ancient ruins half-submerged, and will-o'-wisps that may or may not be sentient. Not dangerous in the horror sense. Dangerous in the way deep water is dangerous: it demands respect.

## Hazards

| Hazard | Severity | Cause | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|-------|------------|
| Navigation loss | Moderate | Paths change with water levels and Fay influence | Local guide, Fay reputation, landmarks |
| Wisp misdirection | Moderate | Following the wrong wisp | Learn to distinguish helpful from misleading wisps |
| Magical saturation effects | Minor (non-Fay) | High magical concentration | Adaptation over time, Fay-crafted protection |
| Wild Hunt encounter | Variable | Pact violation, disrespectful behavior | Respect protocols, ask permission |

## Seasonal Changes

| Season | Visual | Mechanical | Cultural |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Spring** | Water rises, bioluminescence brightest, frog choruses loudest | Floating Village drifts to new positions, new growth | Spring renewal rituals |
| **Summer** | Densest canopy, maximum night bioluminescence | Most beautiful, most disorienting | Festival of Long Light |
| **Autumn** | Water drops, ruins exposed | Sunken Library accessible deeper, artifacts surface | Archaeological season |
| **Winter** | Ice traps bioluminescence under surface -- swamp glows under glass | Unique winter-only resources | Festival of Deep Memory |

---

## Unique Mechanics

- **Navigation Challenge** -- Maze-like. Paths change. Maps become outdated. Guides are valuable.
- **Magical Saturation** -- Fay abilities maximal, NEI computation disrupted, Synthetic sensory anomalies, human intuition enhanced
- **Ancient Ruins** -- Exploration content with lore and unique recipes
- **Respect Required** -- Wild Hunt patrols enforce behavioral standards
