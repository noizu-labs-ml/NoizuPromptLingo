# Copperwood

> *"The trees remember your name. Walk gently."*

---

## Identity

Copperwood is an ancient deciduous forest locked in perpetual autumn. The canopy is a cathedral of copper, amber, gold, and russet leaves that never fully fall. It is the primary homeland of the Fay and the most magically saturated biome in Meridian. The forest is alive in ways other biomes are not -- trees turn to face visitors, mushrooms glow in response to footsteps, paths rearrange based on the traveler's intent.

## Seasonal Changes

| Season | Visual | Mechanical | Cultural |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Spring** | Two-tone canopy (new green + eternal copper). Wildflower floor. | Streams fast with snowmelt, new mushroom species | Spring planting rituals |
| **Summer** | Dense canopy, deep shade. Bioluminescence peaks. | Night exploration rewarded, fungi abundant | Festival of Long Shadows |
| **Autumn** | Peak beauty -- molten copper ocean canopy. Great Drift (coordinated leaf spirals). | Best magical reagent harvests | The Great Drift |
| **Winter** | Copper-and-white mosaic (snow on leaves). Very quiet. | Storytelling season, Heartwood becomes winter salon | Fay storytelling festivals |

## Narrative Hooks

- The Old Growth quest (with Old Hawthorn) spans four in-game seasons, building the deepest NPC relationship
- The Weavers' Workshop connects to the magic-computation mystery
- Moonpetal Glade access requires genuine Fay trust -- a relationship that cannot be rushed
- The Listening Stones are an ancient communication network that predates every other infrastructure in Meridian
- The forest's path-shifting mechanic teaches patience and relationship-building as gameplay skills

---

## Unique Mechanics

- **Path Finding** -- Reputation-based navigation. High Fay rep = direct routes; low rep = detours; hostile intent = lost
- **Magical Gathering** -- Season, moon phase, and ritual affect resource availability
- **Fay Territory Buff** -- Fay characters gain enhanced abilities
- **Respect Mechanic** -- Permission-based harvesting; builds relationship through patience
