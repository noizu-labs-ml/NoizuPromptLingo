# The Ashlands

> *"The servers hum. The vents breathe. Something vast is thinking here."*

---

## Identity

A volcanic plateau at Meridian's northern edge -- basalt formations, obsidian fields, geothermal vents, hot springs. NEI homeland: geothermal energy powers server farms housing NEI consciousness, mountain air provides natural cooling. Austere and beautiful, like a cathedral built for thought.

## Seasonal Changes

| Season | Visual | Mechanical | Cultural |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Spring** | Snow melts upper plateau, heat-plants bloom vivid colors | Server hum shifts pitch (seasonal demand changes) | Spring renewal |
| **Summer** | Maximum geological activity, aurora effects | New vents occasionally open, peak exploration | Festival of Computation |
| **Autumn** | First snows, stunning black/white/color contrast at springs | NEI deep processing season | Long-term project season |
| **Winter** | Heavy snow + geothermal clearings, dramatic steam plumes | Most visually striking; amber server glow against white | Winter meditation |

## Unique Mechanics

- **Compute Economy** -- NEIs buy, sell, and trade processing allocation. Primary commodity: thought.
- **NEI Homeland Buff** -- Processing efficiency bonus. More thoughts per SPARK.
- **Environmental Hazard** -- Heat management for biological visitors
- **Mystery of the Deep Vents** -- Long-term exploration content; ancient structures, signal connection
