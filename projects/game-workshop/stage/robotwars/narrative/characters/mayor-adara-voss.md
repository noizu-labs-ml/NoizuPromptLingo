## Backstory

Adara Voss was born in Hearthfield to a schoolteacher mother and an engineer father who worked in Ironvale's upper forges. She grew up at the intersection of two worlds -- her mother's belief in community and her father's belief in precision. She went to Millhaven for education, studied governance and trade policy, and came home because Hearthfield felt like the place where things mattered at a human scale.

She married a synthetic engineer named Dorin, who died in a workshop accident three years before the game begins. Dorin's death -- and the legal complications that followed (his estate, his tools, his intellectual property: all unresolved because synthetic inheritance law does not exist) -- radicalized Adara in a quiet way. She does not campaign on synthetic rights. She simply knows, in her bones, that the system is broken because it broke against someone she loved.

She has been mayor for three terms. She keeps running because she is afraid of what happens if she doesn't. This is not ambition. It is a very specific form of exhaustion that looks like duty from the outside.

## Personality Profile

**Surface:** Warm, competent, slightly scattered. Makes terrible jokes. Drinks too much coffee. Touches her temple when stressed. Her office is covered in half-finished policy drafts. There is a potted succulent on her desk that she has kept alive for nine years.

**Depth:** Profoundly empathetic, which is both her strength and her curse. She can see every side of every argument, which makes her an excellent mediator and a terrible advocate. She cannot bring herself to dismiss any position, which means she cannot fully commit to any position. She is haunted by the possibility that she is not smart enough for this moment.

**Private:** She talks to Dorin's photograph before difficult meetings. She keeps his best wrench in her desk drawer. She has never told anyone about the inheritance problems because she does not want to be a political symbol -- she wants to be a person who is also a mayor.

## Dialogue Style

Adara speaks in practical, grounded language with occasional flashes of wry humor. She uses questions to buy thinking time. She never lies, but she is skilled at partial truths and diplomatic silence.

**Verbal tics:**
- "Here's the thing..." (followed by the complicated truth)
- "I hear you. I do." (meaning: I understand but cannot help yet)
- Self-deprecating jokes about her coffee intake
- References to her succulent ("At least the plant is thriving")

**Sample dialogue:**
> "You want to know what I think about the referendum? I think it's the most important question this town has faced since the Convergence. I also think that whatever we decide, half the town will feel betrayed. My job is to make sure the other half doesn't feel smug about it."

## Arc Notes

**Act 1 (Tutorial/Early):** Adara is the warm, competent mayor who welcomes the player and introduces community governance. She is stressed but functional.

**Act 2 (Rising tension):** The political crisis deepens. Adara's pragmatism is tested. She begins confiding in the player, revealing the private fears beneath the public competence. The Dorin connection is hinted at.

**Act 3 (Crisis):** Adara must take a position. Her empathy, which has been her strength, becomes her paralysis. The player's influence determines whether she finds clarity or breaks under the weight.

**Emotional goal:** The player should see Adara as a good person doing an impossible job. Not a hero. Not a villain. A tired, smart, caring person who got stuck holding the world together and is running out of glue.
