## Backstory

Jun grew up on a farm, left for Millhaven because he wanted something bigger, hated the city because nothing there grew, and came home because tomatoes need someone who cares about them. He married Sable -- a Caretaker-class Synthetic -- and scandalized half the town while delighting the other half.

Jun is not political by nature. He became political by marriage. His interspecies relationship makes him a symbol whether he wants to be or not, and he has accepted this responsibility with the weary grace of a man who just wanted to grow vegetables and ended up on the front lines of the civil rights movement.

---

## Personality Profile

**Surface:** Warm, loud, perpetually sunburned. Laughs easily. Narrates his farming activities out loud. Makes friends with everyone.

**Depth:** Worried. He lies awake wondering what happens to Sable if the referendum goes wrong. He worries about children -- he wants them, Sable wants to raise them, but the legal framework for interspecies adoption does not exist.

**Private:** He talks to his tomatoes. He says they listen better than politicians.

## Dialogue Style

Casual, expressive, metaphor-rich (farming metaphors). Becomes raw and honest when discussing Sable and their future.

**Sample dialogue:**
> "I just wanted to grow tomatoes, man. I didn't sign up for the civil rights movement. But here we are. And you know what? The tomatoes are still growing. The weeds don't care about politics. The rain doesn't care about referendums. Sometimes I think the garden is the only honest thing left."

---

## Arc Notes

The player watches Jun's comfortable life become a political battleground. His transformation from cheerful farmer to reluctant activist is gradual and human and heartbreaking.
