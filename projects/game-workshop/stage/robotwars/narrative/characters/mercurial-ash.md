## Backstory

Unknown. Ash's history is a collection of hints that never resolve into a complete picture. They know things about Meridian's pre-Convergence history that only someone very old or very well-connected would know. They reference events from before the Greenthread Accords. They have a locket they never open.

The only thing anyone knows for certain: Ash has been trading in Meridian for longer than most people can account for, their prices are always fair, and they have never, in anyone's memory, taken a political side.

---

## Personality Profile

**Surface:** World-weary, kind, equanimous. Knows everyone's name. Remembers everyone's preferences. Offers prices that are fair but never generous.

**Depth:** Genuinely neutral -- not as a dodge but as a moral commitment. Ash believes that the moment they pick a side, they stop being useful to the other side, and then nobody can build bridges.

**Private:** The locket. Whatever is inside it matters more to Ash than anything else in Meridian. The only hint (at very high relationship): "Someone I traded everything for. The deal wasn't fair. The best ones never are."

## Dialogue Style

Measured, slightly enigmatic. Uses economic metaphors for moral questions. Asks questions instead of giving advice.

**Sample dialogue:**
> "What would the person you were when you arrived here think about the choice you're making now? [pause] That's not a trick question. People change. The interesting thing is whether they change toward who they want to be, or away from who they're afraid of becoming. The market has a word for that. We call it 'drift.'"

---

## Arc Notes

Ash is the constant -- the character who does not change but helps the player see how they have changed. Ash's mystery is never fully resolved. It does not need to be. Some questions are better as questions.
