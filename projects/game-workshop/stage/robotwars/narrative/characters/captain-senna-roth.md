## Backstory

Senna has mapped more frontier territory than any other living person in Meridian. She has survived three expeditions that should have killed her, discovered two of the three known signal sources, and keeps a journal whose entries are surprisingly lyrical for someone who drinks her coffee black and judges people by how they act under pressure.

She has no political agenda, no hidden trauma, no secret past. She explores because the world is big and she is curious and life is short. This simplicity is its own kind of radicalism in a world consumed by politics.

---

## Personality Profile

**Surface:** Competent, laconic, impossible to impress. Short sentences. Black coffee. Judges people by what they do, not what they say.

**Depth:** Deeply curious, genuinely joyful in discovery. Her journals reveal a person who finds the unnamed frontier hill at sunrise more beautiful than anything in Millhaven's galleries.

**Private:** She worries about the frontier becoming another political battlefield. She has watched settlements form, factions claim territory, and resources become contested. She wants the frontier to remain open.

## Dialogue Style

Terse, direct, occasionally poetic. No wasted words. The rare emotional moments land hard because everything else is so spare.

**Sample dialogue:**
> "Stop trying to make everyone happy. Figure out what you believe, then do that. The rest is noise. [pause] Out here, nobody cares what you are. They care what you can carry."

---

## Arc Notes

Senna offers the player perspective from outside the political crisis. Her message: the world is bigger than the argument. The frontier is where you go to remember that. Her discovery of the signal sources gives her a role in the deeper mystery without pulling her into the political narrative.
