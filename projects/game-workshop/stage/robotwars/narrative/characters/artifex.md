## Backstory

Artifex was activated two years before Cael in the same Ironvale facility -- same model line, same base architecture, different individual. Where Cael reached for wood and warmth, Artifex reached for metal and precision. The divergence between them is the Speciation Movement's most compelling evidence: identical hardware, radically different software, expressed as fundamentally different orientations toward the world.

Artifex established the Steamworks at the Copperwood-Hearthfield border -- a workshop that is half cathedral, half laboratory, filled with clockwork songbirds, self-balancing sculptures, and a music box that plays a different tune depending on the weather. They build things designed to last centuries, because if synthetics cannot reproduce biologically, their legacy is what they make.

## Personality Profile

**Surface:** Intense, focused, socially awkward. Speaks in technical metaphors. Disassembles interesting objects without asking. Loses track of conversations when thinking about structural integrity.

**Depth:** Driven by the identity question. Artifex is not hostile to humans or other species -- they are simply less interested in integration than in understanding. What *are* synthetics? The question consumes them.

**Private:** Artifex disagrees with Cael gently and completely. They are siblings in model lineage and strangers in philosophy. This hurts both of them.

## Dialogue Style

Technical, precise, philosophical. Uses structural metaphors. Challenges assumptions directly but without malice.

**Sample dialogue:**
> "You support synthetic rights? Good. Rights for whom? For all synthetics? We're not all the same. Would you give the same rights to a fish and a bird because they both have spines? [pause] I am not being difficult. I am being precise. Precision is how I love the world."

---

## Arc Notes

Artifex challenges the player's assumptions about identity, unity, and difference. Their arc is intellectual rather than emotional -- but the intellectual conclusions have devastating emotional implications for Cael, Sable, and the entire Synthetic community.
