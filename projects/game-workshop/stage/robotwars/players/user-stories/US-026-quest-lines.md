# US-026: Completing Species-Agnostic Quest Lines

**As a** player of any species
**I want to** engage with quest lines that introduce me to the world's characters and mechanics through narrative-driven gameplay
**So that** I build faction reputation, deepen NPC relationships, and feel integrated into the community

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Six species-agnostic quest lines available in Hearthfield:
  - "The Good Neighbor" (Mayor Voss): complete 5 favors for different-species NPCs
  - "The Librarian's Index" (Bibliotheque): collect 8 lore fragments from exploration
  - "The Apprentice" (Cael): learn 3 crafting recipes and create a gift
  - "The Old Growth" (Old Hawthorn): tend the hidden garden for 4 seasons
  - "Market Day" (Orin the Measured): buy and sell, achieve a profit target
  - "The Frontier Beckons" (Vex): join an expedition to map unexplored territory
- [ ] Quests are optional (pure homesteading is always viable)
- [ ] Completing quests builds faction reputation, deepens NPC relationships, and seeds emotional investment
- [ ] Quest completion provides Credit rewards, unique items, and recipe unlocks

## Category
Narrative

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- By completing these quests, the player should have: a homestead they're proud of, 3-5 NPCs they care about, rising reputation with 2+ factions, and awareness that not everything in Meridian is perfect.
- The Old Growth quest spans 4 in-game seasons (4 real weeks) -- the longest onboarding arc.
- See narrative/story-spine.md "QUEST" phase for the full quest line table.
