# US-008: Species-Specific Daily Rhythms

**As a** player of any species
**I want to** experience daily loop variations unique to my species (humans eat/sleep, NEIs manage compute budgets, synthetics maintain bodies, fay perform seasonal rituals)
**So that** my species choice feels mechanically distinct and not just cosmetic

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Human players must eat 2-3 times/day; food quality affects stamina and mood; skipping meals incurs crafting/movement debuffs
- [ ] NEI players manage compute budget: every action costs SPARK; must earn more than spent; no sleep required
- [ ] Synthetic players need periodic maintenance (parts + Credits); neglect causes degradation; Identity Processing moments offer character development choices
- [ ] Fay players gain power in home biome, weaken when far; seasonal magic shifts abilities; evening rituals maintain territory connection
- [ ] Each species' loop feels natural and thematic, not punishing
- [ ] Cross-species players can observe and empathize with other species' constraints

## Category
Core Gameplay

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- Human food sources range from foraged (free, 1-star) to restaurant (expensive, 5-star with buffs).
- NEI compute budget is the game's most novel mechanic: existence literally costs money.
- See design/core-loop.md "Species-Specific Loop Variations" for full details.
