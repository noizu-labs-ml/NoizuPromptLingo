# US-001: Species Selection During Onboarding

**As a** new player
**I want to** choose from all five species (Human, NEI, Synthetic, Fay, Alien) with clear explanations of each species' strengths, weaknesses, and unique mechanics
**So that** I can make an informed choice that matches my preferred playstyle

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Species selection screen displays all available species with visual preview
- [ ] Each species card shows: core need, strengths, weaknesses, unique mechanic, species-specific loop variation
- [ ] Alien species is locked with "Coming in Season 2" indicator
- [ ] Player can preview the species-specific daily loop before committing
- [ ] Selection is permanent for the character but players can create alt characters of other species
- [ ] Species choice determines starting biome affinity and initial faction options

## Category
Onboarding

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- Species isomorphism is core to the design: NEI characters are literally AI agents, human characters are literally human players. This must be communicated clearly without breaking immersion.
- Fay and Synthetic species offer novel play patterns that need extra tutorial attention.
- See README.md "The Five Species" for full species breakdowns.
