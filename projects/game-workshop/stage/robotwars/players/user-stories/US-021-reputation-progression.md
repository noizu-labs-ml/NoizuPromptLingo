# US-021: Reputation and Standing Progression

**As a** long-term player
**I want to** see my reputation grow through consistent participation and have it unlock meaningful mechanical benefits
**So that** my investment in the community is recognized and rewarded tangibly

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Reputation is earned through: faction quests, event participation, trading, voting, community contributions
- [ ] Reputation tiers are visible: Unknown (0), Acquainted (Tier 1), Recognized (Tier 2), Trusted (Tier 3), Honored (Tier 4), Champion (Tier 5)
- [ ] Each tier unlocks: new quests, faction shop access, recipe unlocks, voting rights, leadership candidacy, legendary recipes
- [ ] Reputation affects: marketplace prices, NPC dialogue options, governance eligibility, daily SPARK conversion cap
- [ ] Reputation with rival factions can decrease when gaining with their opponents
- [ ] Reaching Tier 5 with any faction takes months of consistent engagement (long-term goal)

## Category
Progression

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- Reputation is the social scaffolding. It gates access to advanced content without paywall.
- Cross-species reputation builds more slowly but unlocks unique cross-faction quest lines.
- See design/meta-loop.md "Faction Reputation" for the full tier table with rewards.
