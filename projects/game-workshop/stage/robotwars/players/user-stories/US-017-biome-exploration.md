# US-017: Exploring New Biomes

**As an** Explorer player
**I want to** travel through 8 visually distinct biomes with unique resources, creatures, and lore
**So that** every new area feels like a genuine discovery and exploration has tangible rewards

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] 8 biomes are accessible: Starter Meadows, Market Commons, Hearthwood Forest, Copper Coast, Iron Ridge Mountains, The Datafields, Twilight Marsh, The Frontier
- [ ] Each biome has distinct visual identity, ambient audio, resource types, and weather patterns
- [ ] Biome progression flows from safe (Meadows) through moderate (Mountains, Datafields) to dangerous (Frontier)
- [ ] Traveling between biomes takes real time and consumes stamina
- [ ] First visit to a new biome triggers a discovery notification with biome lore
- [ ] Species affinity: Fay gain bonuses in Hearthwood, Humans on Copper Coast, Synthetics in Iron Ridge, NEIs in Datafields

## Category
Core Gameplay

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- Biomes are places you want to live, not survive. The Frontier is dangerous but beautiful.
- "Danger" means resource competition, territorial disputes, and environmental challenges -- not horror.
- See README.md "Biomes" table for full biome specifications.
