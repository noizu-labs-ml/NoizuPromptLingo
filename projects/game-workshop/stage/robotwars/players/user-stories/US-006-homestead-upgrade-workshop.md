# US-006: Upgrading Cottage to Workshop

**As a** Builder player
**I want to** upgrade my cottage to a workshop by investing 500 Credits and 50 Reputation
**So that** I unlock specialized crafting stations, a shop front, and expanded garden capacity

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Upgrade path is visible in homestead management UI showing current progress toward requirements
- [ ] Workshop upgrade unlocks: 2 crafting station specialization choices, expanded storage, small shop front, optional apprentice quarter
- [ ] Garden expands from 4 to 12 crop slots with irrigation system
- [ ] Upgrade triggers a visual transformation of the homestead (construction animation over real time)
- [ ] Neighbors and NPCs comment on the upgrade ("Nice workshop, I might stop by!")
- [ ] Achievement notification and reputation bonus upon completion

## Category
Progression

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- This is the first major milestone. Players should feel proud and motivated to continue toward Campus (2,000 Credits + 200 Rep + faction quest).
- Crafting station choices commit the player to a specialization path: Smithing, Herbalism, Artistry, Technology, or Enchantment.
- See design/meta-loop.md "Homestead Evolution" for details.
