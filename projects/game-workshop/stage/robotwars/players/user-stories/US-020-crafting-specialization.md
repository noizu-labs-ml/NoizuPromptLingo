# US-020: Choosing a Crafting Specialization

**As a** Builder player
**I want to** choose a crafting specialization path (Smithing, Herbalism, Artistry, Technology, or Enchantment) that unlocks tiered recipes
**So that** I develop a unique skill identity and can provide specialized goods the market needs

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Specialization choice is available upon upgrading to Workshop homestead
- [ ] Each path has clear visual identity: Forge (Smithing), Laboratory (Herbalism), Studio (Artistry), Terminal (Technology), Altar (Enchantment)
- [ ] Technology tree shows: 12 Apprentice -> Specialization branch -> Journeyman -> Artisan -> Master -> Legendary (cross-path fusion)
- [ ] 40% of recipes unlock through practice ("craft 50 iron tools"), 25% through faction reputation, 20% through exploration, 10% through research, 5% purchased/traded
- [ ] Species affinity bonuses: Fay excel at Enchantment, Synthetics at Smithing, NEIs at Technology, Humans at Herbalism
- [ ] Cross-path recipes require inputs from multiple specializations (encourages trade between specialists)

## Category
Progression

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- Total recipe count: 124 (40 Apprentice + 50 Journeyman-Artisan + 30 Master + 4 Legendary).
- Legendary recipes like "Resonant Forge Hammer" require Master Smithing + Artisan Enchantment.
- See design/meta-loop.md "Technology Tree" and design/mechanics/secondary-mechanics.md "Crafting System" for details.
