# US-019: Discovering Hidden Locations

**As an** Explorer player
**I want to** discover hidden locations (secret groves, abandoned workshops, ancient fay sites) through thorough exploration
**So that** I'm rewarded for careful play with unique recipes, rare materials, and lore fragments

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Hidden locations exist in all biomes except Starter Meadows and Market Commons
- [ ] Discovery requires: exploring off the main paths, using survey equipment, or following environmental clues
- [ ] Hidden locations contain: unique crafting recipes, rare materials, lore fragments, faction quest triggers
- [ ] Ancient sites in deeper biomes trigger faction quest chains and historical lore
- [ ] First-discoverer achievements grant naming rights for Frontier discoveries
- [ ] Discovery notifications are dramatic and rewarding (visual + audio + journal entry)

## Category
Core Gameplay

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- Exploration rewards: new area mapped (common), resource node (common), hidden location (uncommon), ancient site (rare), frontier discovery (rare).
- The Twilight Marsh contains ancient fay ruins. The Frontier has alien artifacts in Season 2+.
- See design/mechanics/secondary-mechanics.md "Exploration Rewards" for full discovery table.
