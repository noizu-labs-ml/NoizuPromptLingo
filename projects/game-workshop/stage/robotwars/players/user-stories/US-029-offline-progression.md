# US-029: Offline Progression and Notifications

**As a** busy player
**I want to** have my homestead continue operating when I'm offline (gardens grow, shops sell, orders process) and receive a clear summary when I return
**So that** my time investment is respected and the world feels alive even when I'm not playing

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Gardens grow on real-time growth timers regardless of player presence
- [ ] Shops remain open if stocked with inventory (agent-staffed shops provide customer service)
- [ ] Apprentice NPCs continue assigned tasks: gathering, tending, crafting basic items
- [ ] Marketplace orders process automatically
- [ ] Properties are safe from theft/damage while offline (no griefing)
- [ ] Return-to-game notification panel shows: overnight sales, garden status, faction events, marketplace price changes
- [ ] Notification panel is sorted by importance with dismissable items

## Category
Platform

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- Offline progression is essential for casual players who can only play 15-30 minutes per day.
- "The world continues" is a core design principle -- persistent and cumulative, no resets.
- See design/core-loop.md "Offline Progression" and design/mechanics/secondary-mechanics.md for offline behavior rules.
