# US-030: Experiencing the Seasonal Cycle

**As a** long-term player
**I want to** experience four distinct seasons (each lasting 1 real week) that affect resource availability, crafting bonuses, social events, and political calendar
**So that** the world feels dynamic and revisiting activities each season offers fresh experiences

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Four seasons rotate on a 4-real-week cycle: Spring (planting, rain, renewal), Summer (peak harvest, markets, exploration), Autumn (harvest festivals, elections, crafting), Winter (indoor crafting bonuses, community gatherings, planning)
- [ ] Season changes affect: available resources, crop types, crafting quality bonuses, biome appearance
- [ ] Each season has signature community events (Spring Renewal Festival, Summer Grand Market Fair, Autumn Harvest Dance, Winter Hearth Gathering)
- [ ] Political calendar aligns with seasons: elections in autumn, policy debates in winter, implementation in spring
- [ ] Season transition is announced with a world-wide event and visual/audio changes
- [ ] Fay players experience seasonal magic shifts (ability changes per season)

## Category
Core Gameplay

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- 28 in-game days = 4 real weeks. The seasonal cycle is the meta-loop's heartbeat.
- Seasonal exclusivity drives crafting strategy: Frost Berries only in Winter, Fire Lilies only in Summer.
