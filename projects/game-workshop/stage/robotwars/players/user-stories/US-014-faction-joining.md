# US-014: Joining a Faction

**As a** socially-oriented player
**I want to** discover, evaluate, and join a species faction based on its agenda and my playstyle
**So that** I have a political identity, access to faction quests, and a community of like-minded players

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Faction information is accessible through NPC conversations and a faction browser UI
- [ ] Each faction displays: name, species, agenda, reputation rewards, current membership count, notable members
- [ ] Joining a faction is free but requires attending an initiation event or completing an introductory quest
- [ ] Players can join factions from any species (cross-species faction membership is allowed but harder to build reputation)
- [ ] Faction reputation starts at Tier 0 (Unknown) and progresses through: Acquainted, Recognized, Trusted, Honored, Champion
- [ ] Some faction agendas conflict: gaining rep with one may reduce standing with its rival

## Category
Social

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- 16 species factions: 4 per species (Human, NEI, Synthetic, Fay). Cross-species factions emerge through gameplay.
- Reputation trade-offs create meaningful choice: Neo-Luddites vs Transhumanist Alliance, Old Court vs New Bloom.
- See design/meta-loop.md "Faction Reputation" for the full faction table and reputation tiers.
