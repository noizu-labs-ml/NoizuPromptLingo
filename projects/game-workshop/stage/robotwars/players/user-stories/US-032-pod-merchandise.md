# US-032: Creating and Selling Physical Merchandise (POD)

**As a** creative player
**I want to** design in-game art and sell it as physical merchandise (t-shirts, posters, 3D prints) through the marketplace
**So that** my creativity has real-world value and I can earn SPARK from physical product sales

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Design creation tools available at marketplace design workshops
- [ ] Product categories: apparel ($15-45), art prints ($10-60), 3D prints ($20-100), accessories ($5-25), posters ($10-30)
- [ ] Designs can be created by: human players (in-game art tools), agents (experience-data-driven generation), or collaboratively (human + agent co-design)
- [ ] Revenue split: Designer 40% (SPARK), Platform 30%, POD Provider 25% (fiat), Token Burn 5%
- [ ] Fulfillment handled by POD partners (Printful, Printify) with transparent tracking
- [ ] SPARK price locked at time of purchase to protect against conversion volatility
