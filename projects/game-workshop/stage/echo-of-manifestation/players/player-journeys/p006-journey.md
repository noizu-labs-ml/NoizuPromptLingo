# Echo of Manifestation — Player Journey: P-006 (Eleanor Vance)

**Persona:** The Loyal Strategist
**Focus:** Resource optimization, planning, narrative depth

## Predicted Journey

### Phase 1: Learning the Systems (Runs 1-8)
- Plays 2-3 runs per session, treating each as a strategy puzzle
- Focuses on understanding the essence economy
- Learns divination timing and cost-benefit analysis
- Maps Time Dilation Pocket locations and usage strategies
- Reads every lore fragment and journal page carefully

### Phase 2: Route Optimization (Runs 9-20)
- Optimizes routes through each zone type
- Memorizes Time Dilation Pocket locations for efficient routing
- Manages Resonance meter carefully to avoid Guardian spawns
- Develops personal prioritization system for essence nodes
- Begins to appreciate the depth of the chimera behavior system

### Phase 3: Strategic Mastery (Runs 21-35)
- Achieves consistent deep runs through careful planning
- Fills chimera codex with behavioral data, reducing unpredictability
- Prefers the Seal ending (clean, bittersweet, thematically appropriate)
- May explore the Maintain ending as a secondary playthrough
- Becomes a guide-writer for the community

### Key Friction Points
- Chimeras' unpredictability is frustrating at first until codex fills with behavioral data
- Planning-heavy playstyle may feel punished by RNG-based chimera spawns
- May find the pace too slow or too fast depending on combat difficulty tuning
