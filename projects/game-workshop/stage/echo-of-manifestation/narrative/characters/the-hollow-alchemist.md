# Echo of Manifestation — The Hollow Alchemist

**Role:** Antagonist figure — Survivor who embraced the Zone
**Theme:** Corruption, power through surrender, the seduction of the echo
**Lore Fragments:** 7 confrontation encounters

A previous survivor — once a brilliant researcher named Caelum, the First Alchemist's apprentice — who entered the Zone seeking knowledge and found it. He traded his name, his humanity, and his connection to the real world for complete understanding of the Zone's architecture.

The Hollow Alchemist is not evil. He is convinced. He believes the Zone is not a prison but a crucible — that those who master it can reshape reality itself. He sees the player's desire to escape as small-minded and Echo's existence as tragic but necessary.

He appears at the exit of each zone, growing more translucent and more Zone-like with each encounter. His seven encounters escalate from mocking commentary through psychological warfare to full combat. His combat style adapts to mirror the player's most-used strategies.

In the final encounter, the truth is revealed: he was the First Alchemist's apprentice. He entered the Zone voluntarily to save Amara. He failed. His hostility was a curriculum — every combat encounter was a test to prepare the player for what he could not defeat.
