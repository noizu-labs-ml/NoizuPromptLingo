# Echo of Manifestation — The Zone Itself

**Role:** Environment-as-character
**Theme:** Entropy, hunger, the impersonal force that merely IS
**Lore Fragments:** 8 environmental narrative sequences

The Twilight Zone is not intelligent. It is persistent. It does not plan, it does not hate, it does not want. It is a wound in the membrane between what is real and what is imagined, and wounds do not have intentions — they simply bleed.

The Zone remembers. It absorbs every intention, every emotion, every transmutation performed within it. It recycles the dead, spawning chimera from the patterns of those consumed during the Collapse. It grows when fed and contracts when starved. It is the boundary made manifest — the pause before the first choice, given geography.

The Zone is not the enemy. It is a victim — a tool driven by the Core, powered by a child's suffering, expanding to serve no purpose the child ever wanted.
