# Echo of Manifestation — Echo

**Role:** Companion — Child without a shadow
**Theme:** Sacrifice, innocence, the cost of salvation
**Lore Fragments:** 14 memory fragments

Echo is a child of indeterminate age (appears 8-10) who casts no shadow. She does not remember her name. She can sense chimeras before they manifest and serves as the player's companion and early-warning system throughout the run.

As the player collects lore fragments, Echo's speech patterns shift. She begins using archaic phrasing, referencing memories she should not have, and occasionally speaking in the First Alchemist's cadence. At the Midpoint Reversal, she remembers: she is Amara, the First Alchemist's daughter, sacrificed to anchor the Twilight Zone open 200 years ago.

Her dual nature — innocent child and ancient anchor — is the emotional core of the narrative. She does not want to be saved if saving her means dooming the world. She has been holding the door open for two centuries with her loneliness.
