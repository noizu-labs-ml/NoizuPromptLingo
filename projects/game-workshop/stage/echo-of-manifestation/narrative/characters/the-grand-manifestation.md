# Echo of Manifestation — The Grand Manifestation

**Role:** Final boss — Accumulated echo of all transmutation
**Theme:** The consequences of creation made manifest
**Lore Fragments:** 8 resonance encounters

The Grand Manifestation is not a person — it is the accumulated echo of every transmutation the player has performed throughout the entire run. Every item created, every chimera spawned, every transmutation circle activated — the energy accumulated here.

It is a shape-shifting entity that mirrors the Survivor's most-used item from the run. The fight is unique each time because the Grand Manifestation adapts to the player's playstyle. Defeating it requires the Survivor to transmute a final item, knowing it will spawn the most powerful chimera yet.

The Grand Manifestation guards the Threshold — the boundary between existence and the void. It is not a being. It is a boundary — the line between what exists and what could exist.
