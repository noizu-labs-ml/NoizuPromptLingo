# Echo of Manifestation — Achievements

52 achievements across five categories. All conditions are skill- and knowledge-based; none depend on random drops or RNG.

---

## Combat (15)

### [ACH-01]: First Transmutation
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Transmute your first item and survive the resulting chimera encounter.
- **Condition:** Complete any transmutation and kill the spawned chimera without taking damage from it.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-02]: Chimera Slayer
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Defeat 100 chimeras across all runs.
- **Condition:** Cumulative chimera kill count reaches 100.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-03]: Apex Predator
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Defeat 1,000 chimeras across all runs.
- **Condition:** Cumulative chimera kill count reaches 1,000.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-04]: Zone Warden
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Defeat the zone boss of each of the 8 zones in a single run.
- **Condition:** Kill all 8 zone bosses within one run without dying.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-05]: Flawless Containment
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Clear a zone boss without taking any damage.
- **Condition:** Defeat any zone boss while taking zero damage during the entire boss encounter (entry to loot).
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-06]: Overloaded Catalyst
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Have 5 or more active chimeras simultaneously and clear them all.
- **Condition:** Reach 5+ simultaneous hostile chimeras from transmutations, then kill every one of them without leaving the room.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-07]: Pacifist Transmuter
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Reach zone 4 without killing any chimeras spawned from transmutation.
- **Condition:** Reach the entrance of zone 5 with zero chimera kills (chimeras must be avoided or escaped, not killed).
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-08]: Weaponized Alchemy
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Kill a chimera using only transmuted items as weapons — no base weapons equipped.
- **Condition:** Deal the killing blow to a chimera while having no base weapon in either hand slot. Only transmuted or crafted items may deal damage.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-09]: Chain Reaction
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Kill 3 chimeras within 3 seconds of each other using transmutation splash damage.
- **Condition:** Three chimera deaths must occur within a 3-second window, and at least one must die from splash/area damage caused by a transmutation reaction.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-10]: The Rejection
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Survive a Grand Manifestation attempt without killing the Manifestation.
- **Condition:** Enter Grand Manifestation state, survive for 60 seconds, and escape the room without killing the Manifestation entity.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-11]: Speed Purge
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Clear a zone boss in under 30 seconds.
- **Condition:** Zone boss health goes from 100% to 0% within 30 seconds of the first hit landing.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-12]: Elemental Mastery
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Kill one chimera of each elemental type in a single run.
- **Condition:** Within one run, deal the killing blow to at least one chimera of every elemental affinity present in the game (Fire, Frost, Toxic, Arcane, Void).
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-13]: Desperate Measures
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Kill a chimera while at 1 HP remaining.
- **Condition:** Deal the killing blow to any chimera while current HP is exactly 1. Healing immediately before or after is allowed; at the moment of the killing blow, HP must be 1.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-14]: The Catalogue
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Defeat at least one of every chimera type in the game.
- **Condition:** Cumulative across runs, kill at least one instance of all 27 chimera types.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-15]: Grand Manifestation Victor
- **Category:** Combat
- **Description:** Defeat the Grand Manifestation.
- **Condition:** Kill the Grand Manifestation entity in any run, by any means.
- **Hidden:** Yes
- **Tracking:** In-run

## Exploration (12)

### [ACH-16]: Threshold
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Enter zone 2 for the first time.
- **Condition:** Cross the zone boundary from zone 1 into zone 2.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-17]: Descent Complete
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Reach the final zone (zone 8) in a single run.
- **Condition:** Enter zone 8 without dying from zone 1.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-18]: Cartographer
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Reveal 100% of a zone's map in a single run.
- **Condition:** Fully reveal every tile in any single zone (all rooms, corridors, and secret passages discovered) in one run.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-19]: Master Cartographer
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Reveal 100% of all 8 zones' maps in a single run.
- **Condition:** Fully reveal every tile across all 8 zones in one continuous run.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-20]: Secret Keeper
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Discover your first secret room.
- **Condition:** Enter a secret room by interacting with a hidden passage or using a transmutation to reveal it.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-21]: All Secrets Laid Bare
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Discover all secret rooms in a single zone.
- **Condition:** Enter every secret room in any one zone within a single run. Each zone has a fixed number of secret rooms; all must be found.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-22]: Shortcut Syndicate
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Unlock all permanent shortcuts between zones.
- **Condition:** Activate every shortcut anchor point across all 8 zones. Shortcuts persist across runs once activated.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-23]: Resource Hoarder
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Carry the maximum possible transmutation materials into a single room.
- **Condition:** Enter a room while at inventory capacity for transmutable materials (all material slots filled).
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-24]: The Empty Room
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Find and enter the Empty Room.
- **Condition:** Locate and enter the Empty Room — a hidden room that appears only when the player enters a zone carrying zero transmutable items.
- **Hidden:** Yes
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-25]: Zone Speedrun
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Clear zones 1 through 8 in under 20 minutes.
- **Condition:** From the moment zone 1 loads to the moment the zone 8 boss is defeated, elapsed time is under 20 minutes. No skips or cheats — all required rooms must be visited.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-26]: Environmental Awareness
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Kill 10 chimeras using only environmental hazards.
- **Condition:** Cumulative across runs, kill 10 chimeras where the killing blow is dealt by an environmental hazard (trap, falling debris, toxic vent, etc.) rather than player weapons or transmutations.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-27]: The Well-Traveled Path
- **Category:** Exploration
- **Description:** Complete 50 runs total.
- **Condition:** Cumulative run count reaches 50. A run counts as complete when the player dies, escapes, or finishes the final zone.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

## Lore (10)

### [ACH-28]: First Echo
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Collect your first lore fragment.
- **Condition:** Pick up any lore fragment for the first time.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-29]: Fragment Collector
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Collect 32 lore fragments (half the total).
- **Condition:** Cumulative unique lore fragments collected reaches 32 out of 64.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-30]: The Full Record
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Collect all 64 lore fragments.
- **Condition:** Cumulative unique lore fragments collected reaches 64 out of 64.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-31]: Voice in the Static
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Complete all NPC dialogue interactions in a single run.
- **Condition:** Exhaust all possible dialogue options with every NPC encountered in a single run. Missing an NPC in a room you skip voids this.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-32]: The Archivist
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Read every lore fragment in the codex within a single run.
- **Condition:** Open and view all 64 lore fragments in the in-game codex during one run. Requires having collected all 64 beforehand and reviewing them all.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-33]: Unreliable Narrator
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Find the contradictory lore entry.
- **Condition:** Collect both fragments of a deliberately contradictory lore pair (two fragments that tell the same event from irreconcilable perspectives). Both must be in the codex.
- **Hidden:** Yes
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-34]: The Witness
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Experience all three endings.
- **Condition:** Complete the game once via each of the three ending paths (Escape, Submission, Transcend). Cumulative across runs.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-35]: Echo Chamber
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Transmute an item at every lore-relevant transmutation site in a single run.
- **Condition:** There are fixed locations where lore text references specific transmutation rituals. Perform a transmutation at all such marked locations within one run.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-36]: The Name
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Discover the true name of the Manifestation.
- **Condition:** Collect a specific set of 5 lore fragments that, when all are in the codex, unlock a hidden codex entry revealing the Manifestation's true name. Requires fragments from at least 4 different zones.
- **Hidden:** Yes
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-37]: Silent Understanding
- **Category:** Lore
- **Description:** Complete an entire run without reading any new lore fragments.
- **Condition:** Finish a run (death or zone 8 completion) without picking up a single lore fragment. Lore fragments in the environment must be actively avoided.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

## Challenge (10)

### [ACH-38]: Barehanded
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Complete a full run using only unarmed attacks and transmutations — no weapons equipped at any point.
- **Condition:** From zone 1 entry through zone 8 boss defeat or run-ending death, no weapon item may enter either hand slot at any time. Unarmed attacks and transmutation effects are the only permitted damage sources.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-39]: One Transmutation Run
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Complete a full run using exactly one transmutation.
- **Condition:** Perform precisely one transmutation for the entire run (zone 1 through zone 8 boss). The transmutation counter at run end must read exactly 1.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-40]: No-Death Clear
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Complete all 8 zones in a single run without dying.
- **Condition:** Defeat the zone 8 boss with zero deaths recorded during the run. Continues or revives of any kind are not permitted.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-41]: Minimalist
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Complete a full run carrying no more than 5 items in your inventory at any time.
- **Condition:** Inventory count never exceeds 5 items at any checkpoint from zone 1 entry through zone 8 boss defeat. Transmutation materials in material slots do not count toward the 5.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-42]: Glass Cannon
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Complete a full run without equipping any armor or defense items.
- **Condition:** From zone 1 entry through run end, no armor, shield, or defense-boosting item may be equipped in any slot. Damage reduction from transmutations or innate abilities is permitted.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-43]: Speed Demon
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Complete a full run in under 15 minutes.
- **Condition:** Zone 1 entry to zone 8 boss defeat, elapsed time under 15 minutes.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-44]: The Alchemist
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Perform all 38 recipes in the game across your career.
- **Condition:** Cumulative across runs, successfully execute every recipe at least once.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-45]: Recipe Roulette
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Perform 10 different recipes in a single run.
- **Condition:** Within one run, successfully transmute using 10 distinct recipes. Duplicate recipes do not count toward the total.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-46]: Iron Stomach
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Complete a full run without using any healing items.
- **Condition:** From zone 1 entry through zone 8 boss defeat, zero healing consumables used. Healing from transmutation side effects or environmental sources is permitted.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-47]: Solo Manifestation
- **Category:** Challenge
- **Description:** Defeat the Grand Manifestation while no chimeras are active.
- **Condition:** At the moment the Grand Manifestation's health reaches zero, zero other chimeras can be alive or spawning. The player must have cleared all chimeras before the killing blow.
- **Hidden:** Yes
- **Tracking:** In-run

## Meta (5)

### [ACH-48]: Awakening
- **Category:** Meta
- **Description:** Reach Insight level 10.
- **Condition:** Cumulative Insight level reaches 10.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-49]: Deep Understanding
- **Category:** Meta
- **Description:** Reach Insight level 50.
- **Condition:** Cumulative Insight level reaches 50.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-50]: Transcendent Mind
- **Category:** Meta
- **Description:** Reach Insight level 99 (maximum).
- **Condition:** Cumulative Insight level reaches 99.
- **Hidden:** No
- **Tracking:** Across runs

### [ACH-51]: The Transcend
- **Category:** Meta
- **Description:** Achieve the Transcend ending.
- **Condition:** Reach Insight level 60 or higher, have all 64 lore fragments collected in the codex, perform a no-death run from zone 1 through zone 8, and defeat the Grand Manifestation in the final encounter without dying.
- **Hidden:** Yes
- **Tracking:** In-run

### [ACH-52]: Echo Complete
- **Category:** Meta
- **Description:** Unlock all other achievements.
- **Condition:** All 51 other achievements are unlocked. This is the capstone achievement for 100% completion.
- **Hidden:** Yes
- **Tracking:** Across runs
