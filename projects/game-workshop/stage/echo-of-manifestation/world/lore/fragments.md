## Zone 1: Faded Chapel

### LF-016: The Merchant's Paradox

- **Location Hint:** Appears reflected in the water's surface only at the exact moment the market clock strikes an hour that doesn't exist
- **Insight Tier Required:** 4

The First Alchemist thought he was *buying* power from the other side. He was actually *selling* something to it. The price was already paid. It is still being paid. It will always be being paid, because the transaction has no tense.
