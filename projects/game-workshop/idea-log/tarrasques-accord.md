# Tarrasque's Accord

**Narrative Story**

## Core Loop
Make choices that reshape the tarrasque's nature and your own path to determine whether you resurrect or transcend with it.

## Features
- **Dynamic Decision System:** Choices branch into interconnected narrative webs where seemingly minor decisions cascade into major consequences decades later
- **Tarrasque Bond:** Build relationship through actions — heal the creature and it reveals visions of apocalyptic futures; attack it and it adapts, learning from your tactics
- **Transcendence Mechanics:** Two viable endings require different approaches — resurrection demands understanding the paradox within the tarrasque; transcendence requires surrendering your humanity to merge with it
- **Time Loops:** When you die, rewind to key decision points with retained knowledge. The tarrasque remembers your previous attempts and reacts differently
- Parcels of Wonder:** Discover moments of genuine awe — ancient murals depicting the creature's origin, a dying star glimpsing through portal rifts, silent sanctuaries frozen in paradox
- Moral Ambiguity Engine: No clearly "right" choice. Every resolution comes with costs and questions that linger beyond credits

## Why This Is Awesome
- **Flow State:** Time loop replays reduce frustration — death teaches rather than punishes, maintaining momentum. The bond system creates emotional investment that carries through choices.
- **Emergent Sandbox:** Relationship with the tarrasque evolves organically based on your actions across loops. The creature develops adaptive behaviors, fears, and even compassion — emergent AI that writes its own story within yours.
- **Story-Driven Explorer:** The narrative unfolds through environmental storytelling and creature interactions rather than dialogue trees. Paradox fragments reveal the history of transcended paradoxes, ancient cycles, and what happens when beings cannot be resurrected. The two endings represent player philosophy, not plot branching.
