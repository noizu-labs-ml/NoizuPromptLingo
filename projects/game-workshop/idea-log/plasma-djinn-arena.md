# Plasma Djinn Arena

**Competitive Fighting / Esports**

## Core Loop
Choose a djinn fighter -- an elemental being of pure plasma sculpted into humanoid form -- and battle in 1v1 or 2v2 arena combat. Djinn don't have fixed movesets; instead, they absorb elements from the arena environment. Stand near a fire vent and your next three attacks gain burn properties. Touch a water fountain and your dash becomes a wave that pushes opponents. The arena itself cycles through elemental zones every 30 seconds, forcing constant repositioning and adaptation. Combos are built on the fly from whatever elements you've absorbed, creating an infinite moveset that rewards creativity, reaction speed, and spatial awareness. Ranked play, tournament support, and a replay system built for spectating.

## Features
- **Elemental Absorption**: Your djinn can hold up to 3 absorbed elements simultaneously. Combine them for compound attacks -- fire plus wind creates a flaming tornado, ice plus earth creates a glacier spike, plasma plus void creates a black hole punch. Over 60 compound attacks to discover.
- **Shifting Arenas**: 12 tournament maps, each with unique elemental zones that cycle on different timers. Some zones are permanent fixtures, others appear randomly. Map knowledge is critical but not sufficient -- you must also read the element cycle and position proactively.
- **Djinn Customization**: Modify your djinn's base form with cosmetic shells, aura effects, and victory animations. No gameplay-affecting purchases -- the only advantage is skill.
- **Replay Theater**: Full replay system with slow-motion, free camera, element trail visualization, and input display. Built for content creators and competitive analysis. Share clips directly from the replay viewer.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** players will find near-infinite depth in the elemental combination system. Mastering even half the compound attacks takes months. Tournament-level play demands real-time adaptation that separates the good from the great.

**Flow State** players will achieve peak fighting-game flow -- reading the opponent, absorbing the right elements, executing compound combos, and positioning for the next cycle all in one fluid sequence that feels like conducting an elemental symphony.

**Aesthetic Purist** players will be drawn to the visual spectacle -- plasma djinn are rendered with volumetric lighting, and every elemental combination produces unique particle effects that fill the arena with cascading colors and physics-driven visual chaos.
