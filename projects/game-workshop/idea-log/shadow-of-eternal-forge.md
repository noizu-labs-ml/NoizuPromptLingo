# Shadow of Eternal Forge

**Action RPG**

## Core Loop
Explore a mysterious underground fortress, discover ancient treasures, forge legendary weapons, and battle dragons in an eternal cycle of heroism.

## Features
- Procedural underground dungeon generation with unique biomes and themes
- Deep forging system combining collected materials into increasingly powerful weapons
- Dragon evolution system where enemies grow stronger as you progress
- Narrative-driven exploration with environmental storytelling about fallen civilizations
- Character progression tied to forging mastery and dragon-slaying expertise
- Co-op multiplayer for 2-4 players to conquer dragon lairs together

## Why This Is Awesome
For **Flow State** players, the seamless blend of exploration, combat, and forging creates an hypnotic gameplay loop where you lose track of time while mastering systems. **Story-Driven Explorers** will uncover the tragic tales of forgotten kingdoms through environmental details, ancient texts, and the spirits of fallen warriors. **Challenge Seekers** face progressively harder dragons and deeper dungeon layers, with permadeath hardcore mode and leaderboards for fast completions.

## Target Audience
- **Age Bracket:** T (Teen 13+)
- **Rating:** Fantasy Violence, Mild Language, Alcohol Reference
- **Platform:** PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch (Cross-platform save)
