# Oracle's Deception

**Detective Mystery / Narrative Investigation**

## Core Loop

You are a luminous oracle hired to investigate a series of essence thefts across a coral-paved city built atop a dried bayou. Interrogate suspects, search crime scenes for clues using your grimoire's revelatory magic, and piece together timelines by reading the residual emotions left on objects. The twist: every NPC has a chance of being a mimic, and the mimic responsible for the thefts is actively framing innocent citizens. Your curiosity drives you deeper but also feeds the mimic — every question you ask, every secret you uncover, makes the mimic stronger and more convincing in its disguises. Solve the case before the city tears itself apart from suspicion.

## Features

- **Emotional Resonance Investigation**: Use your oracle abilities to read emotional residue on objects and locations. This reveals fragmented visions of past events, but the mimic's presence corrupts these readings — you must cross-reference multiple sources to separate truth from planted false memories.
- **Mimic Suspect System**: Every playthrough randomly assigns the mimic identity among 30+ NPCs. The mimic adapts to your investigation strategy, providing consistent but subtly wrong testimonies. Catching inconsistencies requires careful note-taking and pattern recognition across multiple interviews.
- **Blood-Mage Interrogation**: Sacrifice your own HP to force truthful answers during interrogations. The more HP you spend, the more reliable the information — but you enter each confrontation weaker, and some suspects are dangerous. Choosing when to use brute-force truth extraction versus careful deduction is a core resource management challenge.
- **Fractured Timeline Reconstruction**: Assemble evidence on a visual timeline board. The UI lets you place events, link contradictions, and build a case. When you're ready, present your findings to the city council — but if your timeline has gaps or you accuse the wrong person, the real mimic exploits your failure.

## Why This Is Awesome

**Flow State**: The investigation loop has a hypnotic rhythm — visit a location, scan for emotions, catalog clues, cross-reference with testimony, update the timeline. Skilled players develop efficient investigation routes that minimize mimic exposure while maximizing information gathered, entering a focused analytical flow where the case pieces snap together.

**Challenge Seeker**: The mimic adapts. If you rely heavily on grimoire readings, it starts planting false emotional residue. If you lean on interrogations, it provides elaborate alibis. The highest difficulty removes the emotional resonance ability entirely — pure deduction from witness testimony and physical evidence. The clock is always ticking; take too long and the city collapses into riots.

**Aesthetic Purist**: Art nouveau meets Southern Gothic — the coral city is all flowing organic architecture draped in Spanish moss, with luminous gaslight illuminating cobblestone streets. The emotion-reading visuals are abstract and evocative: washes of color that suggest rather than show. Character designs are distinctive and readable, with subtle visual tells that reward observant players.
