# Rage of the Minotaur

**Survival Horror / Action**

## Core Loop
You are a survivor trapped in an infinite labyrinthine temple that reconfigures its corridors every time the Minotaur kills you. Scavenge relics for light and defense, solve architectural puzzles to unlock sealed passages, and evade the Minotaur -- an AI-driven predator that learns your habits across runs. Death is not the end; each run reshapes the labyrinth and your accrued knowledge persists as map fragments and shortcut keys.

## Features
- **Adaptive Predator AI**: The Minotaur learns from your behavior across runs. Favor left-hand turns? It will begin ambushing from the left. Always hide in alcoves? It learns to charge through alcove walls. After 5 deaths to the same tactic, the game warns you to change strategy. After 10, it begins counter-countering your counters.
- **Living Architecture**: The temple itself is a hostile entity. Corridors compress and extend, staircases invert, and rooms flood with sand or blood based on an internal logic tied to ancient Labyrinthian rituals you must decipher. Solving the ritual logic lets you predict and exploit shifts rather than merely surviving them.
- **Relic Fusion**: Scavenged relics (torches, shields, compasses, glyphs) can be fused at altar rooms to create unique survival tools. A torch fused with a compass becomes a flame that always points toward the nearest exit. A shield fused with a glyph creates a barrier that temporarily confuses the Minotaur's pathfinding. Over 80 fusion recipes, many undocumented.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** faces the ultimate survival horror opponent: an enemy that gets smarter the more you play. The adaptive AI means there is no "solved" strategy. Speedrunners will compete on how few deaths they can reach the center in, while casual players will find their own emergent solutions through relic fusion experimentation. The final confrontation requires you to use every trick the Minotaur has learned against it.

**Story-Driven Explorer** uncovers the labyrinth's history through environmental murals, ritual inscriptions, and the Minotaur's own behavior patterns (it patrols specific routes that correspond to its pre-curse life as a temple guardian). The deeper narrative is about a civilization that built the labyrinth as a prison for its own sins, and the Minotaur is not the monster -- the temple itself is.

**Flow State** emerges during the exploration phases between Minotaur encounters. The labyrinth's ambient sound design (dripping water, distant stone grinding, your own echoing footsteps) creates a hypnotic trance state. Relic fusion at altars provides calm, methodical puzzle-solving breaks between the high-adrenaline chase sequences.
