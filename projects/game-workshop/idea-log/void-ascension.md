# Void Ascension

**Survival Horror / Psychological Thriller**

## Core Loop
Manage your Sanity (drawn from Despair) while surviving endless nights of shattered wraith attacks in a tower that transcends reality itself.

## Features

- **Emotional Resource Management**: Despair is your Sanity—when it hits zero, the tower "transcends your blood" (permadeath). Every wraith encounter drains Sanity; finding midnight amulets restores it.

- **Temporal Distortion Mechanics**: The tower exists outside normal time. Rooms shift when you turn away (Schrodinger's door paradox), corridors loop, gravity fluctuates. Sound and visuals warp to match your Sanity level.

- **The Transcendence System**: Progress isn't measured by floors climbed, but by how long you withstand the night. Each successful dawn unlocks deeper tower secrets, but also more aggressive wraith variants.

- **Environmental Storytelling**: Read the journals of previous chronomancers who failed—or who chose a different fate. Their blood stains mark their transcendence points.

- **Procedural Horror Generation**: Wraith appearances, room layouts, and psychological triggers are procedurally generated but seeded by your play patterns (the tower learns your fears).

## Why This Is Awesome

**For Flow State Players:**
- The tension between exploration (revealing room layouts to find amulets) and survival (staying hidden from wraiths) creates hypnotic, rhythmic gameplay loops that reward mastery without breaking immersion

**For Story-Driven Explorers:**
- Every amulet recovered contains a memory fragment from a past chronomancer—assembling these fragments reveals the tower's true nature and why it demands your blood transcendence

- The tower itself is a character: an intelligent, malevolent entity that communicates through environmental manipulation and personalized hallucinations

**For Challenge Seekers:**
- No saves—permadeath is enforced by the premise ("transcend your blood")
- Multiple difficulty tiers increase wraith aggression, reduce amulet spawn rates, and expand the tower's psychological toolkit
- Perfect runs unlock deeper lore layers and alternative ending paths, but require flawless execution
