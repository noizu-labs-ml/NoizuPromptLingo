# Phantom Codex

**Detective Mystery / Narrative Adventure**

## Core Loop
You are a Phantom -- a spirit bound to an enchanted grimoire that can read the psychic residue left on objects. Investigate crime scenes by touching evidence to relive fragmented memories from the item's perspective. Piece together timelines, cross-reference testimonies, and confront suspects with contradictions. Every case rewires your understanding of the city and its hidden magical underworld.

## Features
- **Object Memory System**: Touch any evidence item (a dagger, a love letter, a cracked helm) to enter its memory stream -- a first-person flashback from the object's point of view. Objects remember differently: a sword recalls violence in sharp red flashes; a cloak remembers whispered conversations as muffled audio. You interpret these sensory fragments to build timelines.
- **Contradiction Confrontations**: During suspect interrogations, present evidence to challenge statements. The confrontation plays out as a rhythm-free debate where you must match the emotional register of the suspect (calm them with gentle evidence, press them with aggressive evidence) to break through magical enchantments hiding the truth.
- **Grimoire Codex**: Your grimoire auto-organizes clues into a visual case board with red-string connections. As you fill in the codex, it reveals hidden magical laws governing the city -- laws that the perpetrators are exploiting. Understanding these laws becomes key to solving later, more complex cases.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Story-Driven Explorer** gets a 40-hour noir narrative set in a city where magic is illegal but ubiquitous. Each of the 12 main cases peels back another layer of corruption, eventually revealing that the Phantom's own death is connected to the conspiracy. Multiple endings depend on which truths you uncover and which you choose to bury.

**Challenge Seeker** will appreciate the escalating deduction difficulty. Early cases are straightforward "whodunit" affairs, but later cases involve time-manipulation magic that scrambles memory fragments, suspects who can edit their own memories, and evidence that was planted by objects that lie. The final case requires you to solve your own murder using clues planted across every previous case.

**Aesthetic Purist** will be captivated by the art direction: a 1920s Art Deco city rendered in muted sepia tones that bleed into vivid color when you enter an object's memory stream. Each memory has a distinct visual filter based on the object's nature -- metallic memories shimmer, organic memories breathe and pulse, paper memories unfold like origami.
