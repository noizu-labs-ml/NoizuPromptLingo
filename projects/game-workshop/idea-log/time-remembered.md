# Time Remembered

**Adventure / RPG / Narrative**

## Core Loop
Explore fractured timelines to recover lost memories, then use temporal fragments to heal a dying world where light has been stolen by shadow.

## Features
- **Temporal Manipulation:** Shift between past, present, and future versions of each location to solve puzzles and unlock secrets
- **Memory System:** Collect memory fragments that reveal the story of how the world fell to darkness and what the shadow phoenix seeks
- **Flow Combat:** Chain abilities seamlessly as a chronomancer, slowing time for enemies while accelerating your own movements
- **Emergent Puzzle Solutions:** Environmental interactions and temporal powers combine in unexpected ways based on player experimentation
- **Dynamic World Evolution:** Your actions in the past reshape the present and future, creating branching narratives and consequences
- **Emotional Resonance System:** The wonder of each recovered memory strengthens your connection to the world, unlocking new abilities

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State Appeal:** The time-slowing combat allows players to enter a rhythmic zone where complex fights feel like a dance. Temporal powers create satisfying chains of actions that feel both powerful and graceful, with clear visual and audio feedback reinforcing that "in the zone" feeling.

**Emergent Sandbox Appeal:** The temporal manipulation system creates countless emergent possibilities. Past actions alter present conditions in predictable but surprising ways. Players can freeze a falling bridge in the past, grow a vine to climb in the present, or accelerate decay to create new paths. The combinations encourage experimentation and creativity.

**Story-Driven Explorer Appeal:** Every memory fragment reveals another piece of the world's tragic history—a world where light itself was stolen by an entity that feeds on wonder. The narrative unfolds through environmental storytelling, character interactions, and the emotional weight of restoring beauty to a broken timeline. Players feel the impact of their discoveries both narratively and mechanically.
