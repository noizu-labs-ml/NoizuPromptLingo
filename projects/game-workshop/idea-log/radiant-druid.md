# Radiant Druid

**Strategy Simulation / City Builder**

## Core Loop
You are the Radiant Druid, spiritual guardian of a sentient forest that grows in real-time. Balance the needs of woodland creatures, migrating fantasy races, and the forest itself by zoning groves, assigning dryad caretakers, and defending against encroaching corruption. Every season, the forest blooms new districts based on your zoning decisions, and you must adapt your layout to accommodate whatever grows.

## Features
- **Living Zoning System**: Unlike traditional city builders where you place buildings, you zone emotional and elemental energy (radiance, growth, stillness, wildness). The forest interprets your zones and grows appropriate structures: taverns become mushroom halls, factories become resin forges, temples become moonlit clearings. No two playthroughs produce the same city.
- **Creature Council**: A negotiation system where fantasy races (satyrs, centaurs, dryads, sprites, naga) petition for territory. Each race transforms the forest differently. Satyrs cultivate music groves that boost happiness; centares open trade routes but trample delicate undergrowth. You broker compromises through a card-based debate minigame.
- **Corruption Fronts**: Periodically, blight storms push corruption fronts across the map. You deploy druid abilities (purify, wall-of-thorns, radiant bloom) to hold the line while creatures evacuate. Lost districts regrow differently, forcing strategic retreat and renewal.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will sink into the meditative rhythm of seasonal planning. The forest grows on a gentle cadence, giving you time to admire the procedural architecture before the next decision point arrives. The ambient soundscape shifts with each season -- birdsong in spring, wind chimes in autumn -- creating a deeply relaxing management loop.

**Aesthetic Purist** will be drawn to the hand-painted art style inspired by Studio Ghibli forests. Each grown structure has 10+ visual variants influenced by adjacent zones, time of day, and seasonal state. The game is designed as much to be watched as played, with a built-in photo mode and timelapse replay of your forest's growth.

**Emergent Sandbox** fans will find near-infinite replayability in the Living Zoning System. Because the forest interprets rather than executes your commands, surprising combinations emerge: a radiance zone next to a wildness zone might produce a phosphorescent mushroom amphitheater, or it might spawn a territorial will-o-wisp colony that drives away your sprites. Learning these emergent interactions is the game's deepest layer.
