# Verdant Druid's Machine

**Building / Crafting Sim**

## Core Loop
You are a druid who discovered that nature and technology are not opposites -- they are the same thing. Build a sanctuary by growing mechanical plants: gear-flowers that produce components, vine-springs that store kinetic energy, bark-circuitry that processes sunlight into data. Start with a single seed in a barren wasteland and cultivate a self-sustaining bio-mechanical ecosystem. Each plant species you engineer serves a function -- some generate power, others refine materials, others defend against rust storms that periodically sweep the landscape. Research cross-pollination to create hybrid species with emergent properties no tutorial predicted.

## Features
- **Bio-Mechanical Botany**: 80+ plant species to discover and cultivate, each with mechanical outputs. Combine any two through cross-pollination to create hybrids -- results are governed by a deep genetics system with dominant and recessive traits.
- **Ecosystem Simulation**: Your plants interact with each other and the environment in emergent ways. A steam-fern next to a condensation-lily creates a self-watering loop. A magnetree near a coil-vine generates free electricity. Discovery is organic and rewarding.
- **Rust Storm Seasons**: Periodic environmental hazards that corrode exposed metal components. Engineer protective canopy structures, rust-resistant cultivars, or underground root networks to weather each storm.
- **Sanctuary Visitors**: Wandering traders, displaced creatures, and fellow druid-engineers visit your sanctuary. Trade rare seeds, complete botanical commissions, and attract permanent residents who contribute their own expertise.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Emergent Sandbox** players will spend hundreds of hours cross-pollinating plants just to see what happens, sharing bizarre hybrid discoveries with the community, and building increasingly complex automated production chains.

**Aesthetic Purist** players will be captivated by the art direction -- Art Nouveau meets cyberpunk, where copper gears bloom from emerald stems and crystalline pollen refracts into holographic data displays.

**Flow State** players will settle into the meditative rhythm of planting, observing, harvesting, and expanding, where each cycle naturally flows into the next without friction or interruption.
