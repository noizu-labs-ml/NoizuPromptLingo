# Psalm of the Hollow

**Rhythm Action / Gothic Horror**

## Core Loop

You are a mystic trapped in an ancient monastery swallowed by the void. The only way to survive -- and to push back the darkness -- is through sacred hymns encoded as rhythm challenges. Notes cascade down the screen in patterns derived from actual liturgical modes and polyphonic counterpoint. Hit the rhythm correctly and your hymn generates light that repels the shadow creatures clawing through the cloister walls. Miss too many notes and the monastery dims, corridors collapse into the void, and abominations breach the sanctuary. Between rhythm sequences, you explore the monastery in first person, discovering fragments of the monks' final rituals, each one unlocking a new hymn (song) with unique mechanics -- canonic rounds, polyrhythmic overlays, call-and-response duels with boss entities.

## Features

- **Hymn Combat**: Every song is a battle. Rhythm accuracy generates a sphere of holy light. Perfect streaks trigger "Gloria Surges" -- screen-filling displays of radiance that purge entire rooms of shadow.
- **Monastery Exploration**: First-person exploration between songs. Discover illuminated manuscripts, broken bells, and corrupted frescoes that tell the story of the monastery's fall. Each discovery rewrites the rhythm charts for the next hymn.
- **Counterpoint Bosses**: Boss encounters layer multiple rhythm tracks simultaneously. You must maintain the cantus firmus (bass line) while hitting the coloratura (melody) -- effectively playing two songs at once against a creature that tries to disrupt your tempo.
- **Sorrow Meter**: The monastery tracks an emotional state. Play aggressively and the void recoils but the building's structural integrity suffers. Play gently and the monastery heals but shadows encroach. The meter creates a constant strategic tension.

## Features

- **Dynamic Difficulty by Emotion**: The game reads your rhythm confidence. Play well and the hymns grow more complex, the monastery reveals hidden chambers, and the sorrow meter tilts toward melancholic beauty. Struggle and the game simplifies patterns while the monastery offers mercy -- a candle in the dark, a helpful ghost, a respite room.

## Why This Is Awesome

**Flow State** players will find nirvana in the hymn combat system. The intersection of rhythm mastery and gothic atmosphere creates a trance state where you stop thinking about individual notes and start feeling the architecture of sacred music. The escalation from simple chants to polyphonic boss battles is a curated flow experience.

**Aesthetic Purist** players will be overwhelmed by the visual and auditory cohesion. The monastery is rendered in muted stone, candlelight, and stained glass that fractures when the void breaches. The soundtrack is fully diegetic -- every note you hear is a note you play. The art direction draws from medieval illuminated manuscripts and Baroque church architecture.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will piece together the tragedy of the monastery through environmental storytelling. The monks' final days are told through fragmented journal entries, half-destroyed murals, and the hymns themselves -- each song's structure hints at which monk composed it and what they were facing when they wrote it.
