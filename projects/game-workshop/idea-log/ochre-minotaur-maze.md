# Ochre Minotaur Maze

**Puzzle / Dungeon Crawler**

## Core Loop
Navigate procedurally generated labyrinthine temples where every wall is painted in ochre pigments that tell stories, hide clues, and occasionally come alive. As a blind oracle who sees only through the murals, you must interpret the painted narratives to deduce the maze's layout, solve pigment-based puzzles, and avoid the minotaur guardian that stalks the corridors. Each temple tells a different mythological story, and solving the mural puzzles literally rewrites the maze around you.

## Features
- **Mural Vision**: You perceive the world entirely through wall paintings, requiring you to interpret artistic symbolism to understand room layouts, trap locations, and enemy positions
- **Pigment Rewriting**: Solve puzzles by applying different ochre pigments to blank wall sections, literally painting new passages, platforms, and hiding spots into existence
- **Minotaur AI**: An adaptive adversary that learns your puzzle-solving patterns and begins anticipating which murals you'll interact with, forcing you to vary your approach

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will become immersed in the meditative cycle of reading murals, interpreting symbols, and painting solutions, where each puzzle flows naturally from the last. **Challenge Seeker** players will engage with the escalating minotaur AI that provides genuine psychological pressure, creating cat-and-mouse tension in every corridor. **Aesthetic Purist** players will be mesmerized by the unique visual style where the entire world is experienced through ancient cave-painting aesthetics rendered in warm ochre tones.
