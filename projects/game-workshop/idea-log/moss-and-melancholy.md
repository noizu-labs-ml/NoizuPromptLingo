# Moss and Melancholy

**Casual Zen / Building Crafting**

## Core Loop
Restore an abandoned shrine in a forgotten moss-covered valley by cultivating bioluminescent moss gardens, repairing ancient stone structures, and coaxing the resident spirit back to wakefulness. There are no enemies, no fail states, and no time pressure. Craft moss arrangements that produce different emotional resonance frequencies, which attract different forest spirits who each contribute unique abilities to your restoration. The shrine grows more beautiful with every session, and the melancholy spirit's memories return piece by piece.

## Features
- **Moss Cultivation**: Grow and arrange dozens of bioluminescent moss species that light up in patterns at night, with each arrangement producing different harmonic frequencies
- **Spirit Recollection**: Attract and befriend forest spirits by matching your moss garden's emotional resonance to their personality, each one unlocking new restoration tools and story fragments
- **Shrine Restoration**: Progress at your own pace through non-linear restoration milestones, where every repaired structure, cleared pathway, and garden bed contributes to the shrine's revival

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will find pure relaxation in the gentle loop of planting, arranging, observing, and restoring, where every action is its own reward and nothing demands urgency. **Aesthetic Purist** players will be captivated by the subtle beauty of bioluminescent moss illuminating ancient stonework in evolving color palettes as the garden matures. **Story-Driven Explorer** players will gently unravel the melancholy spirit's backstory through environmental storytelling, each restored area revealing another fragment of a bittersweet tale about loss and renewal.
