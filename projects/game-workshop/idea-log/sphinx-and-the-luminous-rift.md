# Sphinx and the Luminous Rift

**Puzzle Adventure / Narrative Exploration**

## Core Loop
You are a sphinx -- ancient, winged, and bound by riddle-law -- who discovers a luminous rift splitting the desert sky. The rift is bleeding gravity, pulling floating islands down from the heavens and scattering them across the dunes. To close the rift, you must explore each fallen island, solve the riddles locked within, and absorb their gravitational essence into your own being. The twist: every riddle you solve is a piece of your own forgotten past, and the rift may be your own creation. Gameplay alternates between aerial exploration (soaring between floating islands, riding thermals, diving through gravity wells) and ground-based puzzle solving where you interrogate the environment itself using your sphinx abilities: truth-sight (reveals hidden mechanisms), riddle-speak (compels the environment to answer questions), and burden-of-knowledge (forces a correct answer but at a cost to your maximum health).

## Features
- **Riddle-World Fusion**: Puzzles are not abstract minigames -- they are woven into the architecture. A temple door opens only when you deduce which constellation the builder's guild revered, evidence for which is scattered across three islands. There are no puzzle UIs; the world itself is the puzzle interface.
- **Progressive Memory Recovery**: Each solved riddle restores a fragment of the sphinx's memory, presented as playable flashback vignettes. These vignettes recontextualize earlier areas -- a locked door you passed three hours ago suddenly makes sense with new knowledge, incentivizing return visits.
- **Gravity Song System**: The rift emits harmonic frequencies that you can harmonize with. Matching the rift's song lets you temporarily alter local gravity -- making water flow upward, reversing falling debris into stepping stones, or suspending enemies mid-air. Every island has a unique gravity melody to learn.
- **Interrogation Combat**: When hostile creatures (corrupted scholars, rift-mutated scavengers) block your path, you fight by asking questions. Pose riddles they cannot answer to weaken them; answer their riddles correctly to break their morale. Pure knowledge combat -- no weapons, only wit.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Story-Driven Explorer** players will be hooked by the nested narrative -- the sphinx's past, the civilization that built the floating islands, and the rift's true nature all unfold through environmental discovery rather than cutscenes. Every solved puzzle is both a gameplay reward and a story revelation, making progression feel meaningful on two levels simultaneously.

**Aesthetic Purist** players will be enchanted by the visual design: a vast desert under a cracked sky, with luminous floating islands trailing gravitational dust, ancient temples carved from impossible angles, and a sphinx protagonist whose wing-feathers ripple with absorbed gravity energy. The gravity song system produces visual harmonics -- ripples of light that dance across surfaces -- creating a synaesthetic aesthetic.

**Emergent Sandbox** players will experiment with the gravity song system to discover unintended solutions. The harmonic gravity manipulation is systemic rather than scripted -- if you can match the frequency, you can bend gravity anywhere, creating player-driven shortcuts, sequence breaks, and creative puzzle solutions the designers never anticipated.
