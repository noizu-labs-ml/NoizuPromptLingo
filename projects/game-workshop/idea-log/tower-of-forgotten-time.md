# Tower of Forgotten Time

**Action Adventure / Metroidvania**

## Core Loop
Ascend the Shattered Tower by harnessing chronomancy powers to survive combat, solve temporal puzzles, and uncover why the midnight wraith must be resurrected.

## Features

- **Chronomancer Combat System**: Time-based abilities on cooldown:
  - *Blink Dash*: Phase through space and time (i-frames, mid-air use)
  - *Chrono Shield*: Return to your state 3 seconds ago when hit (buffer for mistakes)
  - *Temporal Stasis*: Freeze enemies in place mid-attack for combo extensions
  - *Ripple Strike*: Attack ripples across 3-second windows—hit once, damage repeats in past/future

- **Shattered World Architecture**: The tower exists in three temporal phases simultaneously. Shift between Past (ruins, pristine monsters), Present (shattered, wraith-infested), and Future (reclaimed by void, eldritch geometry). Each phase reveals different paths, hidden items, and enemy weaknesses.

- **The Midnight Resurrection Paradox**: Your central quest—to resurrect the midnight wraith—seems contradictory but reveals itself as a choice:
  - *Pact Ending*: Resurrect the wraith and gain its power to reshape time at the cost of your own timeline
  - *Seal Ending*: Destroy the wraith's anchors and trap it forever, losing access to the Future phase permanently
  - *Absorb Ending*: Become the wraith's vessel, transforming the tower's timeline structure entirely

- **Metroidvania Progression**: Unlock new chronomancer abilities by defeating tower guardians. Backtracking is rewarded with newly accessible areas, faster routes, and lore expansions. The map exists across all three temporal phases—you must navigate which phase each path exists in.

- **Atmospheric Storytelling**: The tower was once a celestial sanctum until a forbidden transcendence ritual shattered it across time. Midnight amulets scattered throughout contain memory fragments of former chronomancers—their voices, their regrets, their warnings.

## Why This Is Awesome

**For Flow State Players:**
- Combat encourages rhythmic, chainable moves—stasis → combo → ripple strike → blink creates satisfying, fluid combat loops
- Platforming integrates time manipulation (blink to recover from falls, shift phases mid-air to find unseen platforms)
- No jarring load transitions between temporal shifts—smooth visual/audio fades maintain immersion

**For Story-Driven Explorers:**
- The central mystery (why resurrect something "evil?") unfolds through environmental storytelling, amulet memories, and a surprising midpoint revelation
- Three distinct temporal phases each have their own visual identity, soundscapes, and narrative beats—you're exploring three worlds wrapped into one
- Multiple endings are gated by exploration depth—hidden amulets, secret guardian fights, and obscure phase-shift routes unlock alternate conclusions

**For Challenge Seekers:**
- Boss Guardians are time-themed: Past (armor you must strip layer-by-layer chronologically), Future (predicts your attacks, forces you into your own past moves), Present (frantically phases between states, punishes indecision)
- "New Game+" mode: Enemies have temporal awareness—they remember your attack patterns from previous runs and adapt
- Completion requires mastering all three temporal phases in tandem—switching mid-jump, mid-combo, during boss fights creates high-skill depth
