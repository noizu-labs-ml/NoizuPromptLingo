# Sanctuary of Whispers

**Narrative Story / Exploration**

## Core Loop
Create your empath druid and journey through a luminous celestial temple, awakening sleeping spirits and gathering healing artifacts while seeking redemption for the shattered truth of your ancestral sorrow.

## Features
- Empathy-based dialogue system where emotional resonance unlocks story branches
- Atmospheric exploration of an ethereal celestial temple with secrets in every shadow
- Spirit collection and awakening mechanic tied to emotional intelligence
- Non-linear narrative with multiple endings based on compassion choices
- Beautiful hand-painted environments with dynamic lighting that responds to mood

## Why This Is Awesome
Story-Driven Explorers will find deep emotional resonance in every character encounter and spirit awakening. Aesthetic Purists will revel in the stunning visual design where every scene is a living painting. Flow State players can lose themselves in the meditative exploration, with no fail states—only emotional discoveries and narrative consequences.
