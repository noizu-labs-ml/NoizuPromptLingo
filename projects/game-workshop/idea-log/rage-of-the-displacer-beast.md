# Rage of the Displacer Beast

**Roguelike Action RPG**

## Core Loop
You play as a displacer beast cub mutated by void energy, tearing through procedurally generated rift dungeons. Each run, you absorb the essence of slain creatures to evolve new tentacle abilities and shadow-based movement powers, pushing deeper into the fractured planes until you either transcend into a legendary apex predator or die and start over with permanent meta-progression unlocks.

## Features
- **Rift Evolution System**: Every monster you consume unlocks mutations on your tentacles -- extra reach, phasing strikes, shadow blink, and over 120 combinable traits that create unique builds each run
- **Displacement Combat**: Master a positional combat system where attacks hit where you *were* a fraction of a second ago, rewarding aggressive movement and baiting enemy strikes into your afterimage
- **Den Building Between Runs**: Spend meta-currency to construct and decorate a planar den that grants passive buffs and attracts companion creatures who join your next expedition
- **Apex Boss Encounters**: Every five floors, face legendary creatures (rocs, leviathans, tarrasques) with multi-phase fights requiring specific mutation combos to counter

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will lose themselves in the rhythmic cycle of blink-strike-evade combat where each room is a kinetic puzzle of positioning and timing, with run times perfectly tuned for "just one more rift" sessions that evaporate hours.

**Emergent Sandbox** fans will experiment endlessly with the 120+ mutation combinations, discovering that stacking shadow-phase with reach extension creates a completely different playstyle than going full aggression with multi-tentacle flurries, ensuring no two builds feel the same.

**Challenge Seeker** roguelike devotees will find ruthless depth in the higher rift tiers where enemies adapt to your most-used mutations, forcing constant strategic pivots and rewarding players who master the full mutation library rather than relying on a single overpowered combo.
