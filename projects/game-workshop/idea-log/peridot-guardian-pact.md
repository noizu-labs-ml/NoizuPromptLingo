# Peridot Guardian Pact

**Monster Collection / Social Multiplayer**

## Core Loop
Capture and bond with crystalline guardians born from peridot veins in a shattered floating archipelago. Each guardian's personality, abilities, and crystalline growth patterns evolve based on how you train and care for it. Form pacts with other players' guardians to create fusion forms for co-op raid encounters against colossal rogue crystals that threaten the archipelago's stability. Trade guardian cuttings with friends to cross-breed rare crystal types.

## Features
- **Crystal Bond System**: Guardians develop distinct AI personalities based on your training style, affecting their combat behavior, dialogue, and crystal growth patterns
- **Fusion Pact Raids**: Two or more players combine guardians into temporary fusion forms, with the resulting creature's abilities determined by both parents' traits and the players' coordination
- **Archipelago Restoration**: Clear corrupted crystal deposits in co-op to restore floating islands, unlocking new biomes, guardian species, and social hubs for your guild

## Why This Is Awesome
**Emergent Sandbox** players will spend endless hours breeding and cross-breeding guardians to discover hidden crystal combinations and unique fusion results. **Story-Driven Explorer** players will uncover the ancient lore of the archipelago through guardian memories, learning why the islands shattered and what the crystals were originally meant to protect. **Aesthetic Purist** players will be captivated by the stunning crystalline art direction, where every guardian is a living gemstone with light refraction effects that make each one visually unique.
