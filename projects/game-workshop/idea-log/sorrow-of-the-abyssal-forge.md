# Sorrow of the Abyssal Forge

**Survival Horror / Dungeon Crawler**

## Core Loop
You are a monk who descends into an abyssal forge -- a vast underground complex where ancient smiths once forged weapons from sorrow itself. The forge is now overrun with wraiths born from the accumulated grief of countless souls whose despair was smelted into steel. Your goal is to reach the heart of the forge and shatter the Sorrow Anvil before it completes a ritual that will flood the surface world with manufactured despair. Gameplay alternates between tense exploration of procedurally arranged forge chambers and deliberate crafting sessions where you smelt the sorrow of defeated wraiths into temporary weapons and tools. Every item you craft carries a grief cost: the more powerful the weapon, the more it dims your lantern light, making subsequent exploration harder. Resource management is a constant act of balancing combat power against visibility in a world where darkness kills.

## Features
- **Grief Crafting**: Smelt sorrow from defeated enemies into weapons and tools, but each crafted item permanently dims your lantern -- stronger gear means darker dungeons
- **Procedural Forge Layouts**: The abyssal forge rearranges its chambers each run, with room types tied to different emotional themes (rage foundries, despair vaults, regret galleries) that affect enemy behavior
- **Lantern Light System**: Your primary survival tool is light -- it reveals traps, repels weaker wraiths, and marks safe rooms. Every crafting decision is a trade-off against this fundamental resource

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** players will find the grief-crafting system to be one of the most elegant risk-reward loops in survival horror: do you forge the ultra-rare sorrow blade that trivializes the next boss but leaves you nearly blind for three floors afterward? Every decision cascades.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will be pulled in by the environmental storytelling -- the forge chambers tell the stories of the souls whose sorrow was processed here. Inscriptions on the walls, half-forged weapons with names engraved on them, and spectral echoes that replay final moments create a haunting narrative tapestry.

**Aesthetic Purist** players will be captivated by the visual design: abyssal blacks punctuated by the warm amber glow of your lantern, spectral enemies rendered in cold blue-white, and molten sorrow flowing through channels in the forge floors in viscous, hypnotic patterns.
