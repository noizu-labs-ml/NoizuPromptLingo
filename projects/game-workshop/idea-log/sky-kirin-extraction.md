# Sky Kirin Extraction

**Extraction Shooter**

## Core Loop

You drop onto floating sky islands suspended above an infinite void, hunt for ancient kirin artifacts worth fortunes on the black market, and must extract via airship before a deadly void storm closes in. The islands are populated by AI-controlled elemental creatures and other players with the same objective. You can choose to engage, evade, or ally with other extractors, but only those who make it to the airship with artifacts in inventory keep their loot. Death means losing everything you carried in.

## Features
- **Void Storm Timer**: Every match has a visible void storm approaching from below. The storm rises faster than players can descend, creating a hard time limit that forces movement decisions. Islands lower in the chain have rarer artifacts but tighter storm windows.
- **Kirin Artifact Passive Effects**: Each artifact you carry grants a passive ability but also attracts specific enemy types. A flame kirin horn lets you walk on thermal vents but draws fire elementals. Carrying three artifacts stacks passives and attract rates, making you powerful but conspicuous.
- **Airship Negotiation**: The extraction airship has limited seats. If more players arrive than seats available, the last 30 seconds become a tense negotiation or standoff. Players can vote to extend the airship's stay by sacrificing artifacts to fuel it, but those artifacts are gone forever.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** players will thrive in the risk-reward tension of going deep for rare artifacts while managing the void storm timer. Every decision -- fight, flee, loot, extract -- carries permanent consequences in an extraction format that respects player time with 20-minute matches.

**Emergent Sandbox** fans will discover emergent strategies: baiting fire elementals toward rival extractors with flame artifacts, forming temporary alliances at the airship, or hiding on islands and ambushing extractors on their way out. The social dynamics are unscripted and memorable.

**Flow State** enthusiasts will find their rhythm in the descent -- jump, glide, loot, listen, move. The loop of island-to-island traversal with escalating void storm pressure creates a natural forward momentum that feels like controlled falling.
