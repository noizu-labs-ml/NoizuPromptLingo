# Scarlet Spellthief Heist

**Stealth / Detective Mystery**

## Core Loop
You are a scarlet-robed spellthief operating in a city governed by magical law. Each mission is a heist: infiltrate warded mansions, bypass enchanted security systems, and steal arcane artifacts for shadowy clients. But every heist is also a detective case -- study blueprints, interrogate contacts, gather intel on ward patterns, and deduce the optimal entry point and extraction route. Your spellbook is limited: carry the wrong spells and you are trapped; carry the right ones and you ghost through undetected. Between missions, investigate a meta-mystery: someone is framing you for thefts you did not commit, and the evidence leads deeper into a conspiracy involving the city's archmage council.

## Features
- **Spell Loadout Planning**: Before each heist, select from 24 spells organized into schools (illusion, transmutation, abjuration, divination). Only 6 can be active at once. Ward analysis intel helps you pick the right kit, but incomplete intel means gambling.
- **Dynamic Security AI**: Enchanted wards, patrolling construct guards, and scrying sensors all communicate. Trigger one alarm and the entire building's security posture escalates in real time -- doors seal, wards intensify, guards converge.
- **Evidence Board**: A cork-board investigation system connects clues from completed heists. Pin photos, draw string connections, and gradually unravel the framing conspiracy across a 20-mission campaign.
- **Reputation System**: Clients rate your stealth performance. High reputation unlocks richer targets and better black-market spell vendors. Low reputation means desperate, dangerous jobs with minimal payoff.

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** players will enter deep concentration during the heist execution phase, where every second counts and spell cooldowns create a rhythmic tension-release cycle.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will be hooked by the noir narrative -- the spellthief is not a villain but a pawn in a political game between archmages, and every stolen artifact reveals another piece of the conspiracy.

**Challenge Seeker** players will chase "Ghost" ratings on every mission -- complete the heist without triggering any alarm, without being seen, without leaving a single magical trace.
