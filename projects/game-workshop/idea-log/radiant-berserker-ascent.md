# Radiant Berserker Ascent

**Action RPG / Roguelite**

## Core Loop
You play as a berserker touched by radiant light, climbing a shattered tower that exists between the celestial plane and the mortal world. Each floor is a procedurally generated arena where you fight through mobs of djinn and corrupted angels. Combat revolves around a risk-reward fury system: taking damage fills your Radiance meter, which powers devastating area attacks, but letting it overflow causes self-harm. Between floors you visit a dream-shrine where you choose permanent upgrades from a randomized pool, customizing your berserker into a tanky juggernaut, a glass cannon, or something in between.

## Features
- **Radiance Fury System**: Damage taken converts to radiant energy -- vent it early for safe attacks or let it build for screen-clearing supernovas that also hurt you
- **Shattered Tower Floors**: Each floor rearranges its layout, enemy composition, and environmental hazards every run, with boss encounters every 5 floors
- **Dream-Shrine Upgrades**: Between floors, choose from 3 random permanent perks that stack and interact (e.g., "Radiance drains slower" + "Radiance attacks heal 10%" = sustainable berserking)

## Why This Is Awesome
**Challenge Seeker** players will find the Radiance Fury system to be a constant high-wire act -- the optimal strategy lives right on the edge of self-destruction, rewarding mastery with exhilarating power spikes that feel earned.

**Flow State** players will appreciate the seamless loop: combat flows into upgrades flows into combat with zero loading screens, and the one-more-floor pull is nearly irresistible.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will uncover fragments of lore scattered through the tower -- journal pages from previous climbers, echoes of the celestial war that shattered the tower, and a branching narrative that resolves differently based on how far you ascend and which dream-shrine choices you make.
