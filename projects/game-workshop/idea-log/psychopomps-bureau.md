# Psychopomp's Bureau

**Puzzle / Narrative Comedy**

## Core Loop
You are a newly hired psychopomp (spirit guide for the dead) working at the Bureau of Transmigration, a hilariously bureaucratic afterlife agency. Each level presents a newly deceased soul with unresolved baggage that you must sort through by solving inventory puzzles, filing the correct cosmic paperwork, and navigating office politics between rival afterlife departments who compete for soul quotas.

## Features
- **Soul Sorting Puzzles**: Each soul arrives with a chaotic inventory of memories, regrets, and unfinished business that you must organize into the correct cosmic filing categories -- but the filing system keeps changing based on which deity is running the department this week
- **Office Politics Faction System**: Navigate rivalries between the Reincarnation Department, the Eternal Rest Division, and the aggressive Ghost Protocol recruitment team, making alliances that unlock new puzzle-solving tools
- **Chain of Authority Puzzle Chain**: Complex multi-room puzzles where you need approval stamps from different departments, each with their own absurd requirements -- the Department of Karmic Debts won't stamp anything until you resolve a grudge from the soul's past life
- **Soul Backstory Investigation**: Dig through memory fragments to discover why each soul died and what they need to move on, with darkly comedic writing that balances genuine emotional moments with absurd bureaucratic humor

## Why This Is Awesome
**Flow State** puzzlers will find satisfying rhythm in the core loop of sorting, filing, and resolving soul baggage, with each puzzle solution triggering a deeply satisfying cosmic "approved" stamp animation and the soul peacefully dissolving into the afterlife.

**Story-Driven Explorer** players will become invested in the individual soul stories -- a medieval knight terrified of reincarnation, a workaholic CEO who literally cannot stop filing reports even in death, a cat who insists it was human -- each written with genuine emotional depth beneath the comedy.

**Aesthetic Purist** audiences will appreciate the distinctive art style that blends 1970s government office brutalism with cosmic celestial motifs -- fluorescent-lit hallways that stretch into infinity, filing cabinets that contain miniature galaxies, water coolers dispensing the river Lethe.
