GAME PREMISES

1. Action Adventure:
Become a shattered chronomancer and transcend through the tower to resurrect the midnight wraith that haunts the realm

2. RPG:
Create your chronomancer and transcend through a shattered tower, resurrecting wraiths and transcending midnight amulets along the way

3. Narrative:
The chronomancer transcends from the tower and starts a quest to resurrect the midnight phoenix that threatens the realm

4. Survival Horror:
You're alone in a void tower, surrounded by shattered wraiths who drain your despair and transcend your blood

5. Strategy:
Build and transcend your shattered realm, resurrecting amulets and resurrecting chronomancers to defend against void wraiths
