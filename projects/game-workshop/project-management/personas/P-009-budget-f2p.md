## Bio
Liam is a first-year university student living on a tight budget between tuition and rent. He's been a hardcore mobile gamer since age 14 and refuses to spend real money on games out of principle and necessity. He's developed a reputation in gaming communities for achieving endgame content without spending a cent, and takes pride in outperforming players who "pay to win."

## Goals
- Reach high-level rankings without spending money
- Find games where skill matters more than wallet size
- Build a presence in gaming communities through skill demonstrations

## Frustrations
- Games that make paid content strictly necessary for progression
- Energy systems that force spending to continue playing
- Being matched against whales in competitive modes who paid for better gear

## Behaviors
- Plays 3-5 hours daily, mostly during evening
- Never spends on any game — considers it a point of pride
- Focuses on competitive games where skill matters (MOBA, FPS)
- Guides others on F2P strategies in Discord communities

## Job to Be Done
> "When I have limited free time between work and study, I want to play games where my performance isn't gated by spending, so I can feel my time investment is valued regardless of my budget."

## Relationship to Product
Liam is highly engaged and vocal advocate when a game respects F2P players. He generates content (guides, build recommendations, streams) and attracts other F2P players to games he endorses. He will quit immediately if he feels forced to spend to compete.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Liam reaches a progression wall in a new RPG and researches whether it's possible to progress without spending before continuing
- **Scenario 2:** Liam discovers a competitive game where cosmetics are the only paid items and becomes a vocal advocate for its "fair" monetization
