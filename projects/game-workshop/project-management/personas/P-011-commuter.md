# P-011: Maria Rodriguez

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 33 |
| Occupation | Graphic designer |
| Location | Buenos Aires, Argentina |
| Device | Xiaomi Redmi Note 13 |
| Tech comfort | Medium |

## Bio
Maria spends 90 minutes daily commuting on Buenos Aires Metro. She uses this time to play casual mobile games that work offline when subway WiFi is spotty. She plays to disconnect from work stress between home and office but rarely spends money on games — her annual gaming budget is under $20, spent only on games she's played for months.

## Goals
- Reclaim commute time as personal space rather than lost time
- Find games that work reliably without internet
- Have a consistent reliable escape pattern during daily routine

## Frustrations
- Games forcing online connection when she has no data
- Ads interrupting gameplay during short sessions
- Games with energy systems that cut short her commute

## Behaviors
- Plays 30-45 minutes daily during commute only
- Prefers offline-capable puzzle and casual games
- Spends under $20 annually, only on long-term favorites
- Deletes games that require online connection during commute

## Job to Be Done
> "When I'm on my daily commute with intermittent internet, I want games I can play continuously regardless of connection, so I can maintain a reliable stress-relief routine."

## Relationship to Product
Maria is a low-engagement but consistent player when her practical needs are met. She rewards games that respect her offline use and commute-friendly session length. She silently moves on when games fail to work in her use case.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Maria opens a puzzle game during her morning commute and it fails to load due to lack of internet
- **Scenario 2:** Maria spends $5 to remove ads from a matching game she's played daily for six months
