## Bio
Marcus is a diehard MOBA player who transitioned from League of Legends to mobile after his desktop died freshman year. He plays with a squad of 4 friends who met in his dorm during freshman year—they coordinate across Discord, rotate main roles, and take ranked seasons seriously but not professionally.

## Goals
- Maintain a positive win rate in ranked play with his squad
- Collect skins for his main champions (he mains support and jungle)
- Avoid "tilt" from toxic teammates—gaming should stay fun

## Frustrations
- Skin prices that feel inflated for mobile graphics quality
- Meta shifts that force him to relearn after spending on a previous meta
- Limited cosmetic options for his favorite champions

## Behaviors
- **Gaming**: Plays 2-3 hours/day with the squad, plus 1 hour solo queue. Heavily plays during weekends (6+ hours Saturday).
- **Spending**: Spends $15-35/month on skins and battle pass. Has spent $60 on a legendary skin bundle for his main champion once.
- **Genre preferences**: MOBA, competitive multiplayer, team-based tactics.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I reunite with my friends online, I want to compete together as a coordinated team, so I can feel the camaraderie and shared victory."

## Relationship to Product
Marcus stays for the social experience with friends and competitive fairness. He leaves when matchmaking breaks or the meta becomes unfun for his role. Skin sales keep him engaged between seasons.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Pre-game lobby — Squadmate says "Let's all buy the new bundle on split pay," Marcus hesitates at $30 but agrees to keep the group vibe going.
- **Scenario 2:** Ranked queue — Loses 3 games in a row with teammates dodging or trolling, considers quitting for the night but stays because of peer pressure.
