# P-003: Hiroshi Tanaka

## Bio
Hiroshi discovered his passion for RPGs through Pokémon on Nintendo Switch before switching fully to mobile. He treats every game as a completion challenge—every achievement, every max-stat build, every secret discovered. He's built a reputation in his school's gaming club as the go-to guy for build guides.

## Goals
- Complete every achievement and collect every unlockable in his main games
- Max out character builds using optimal strategies (theorycrafts on Reddit and Discord)
- Find the next RPG with depth that rivals his favorites

## Frustrations
- Gated content behind time-limited events that he missed due to school/exams
- PvP power creep that invalidates months of character building
- Auto-battle systems that remove the skill component he loves

## Behaviors
- **Gaming**: Plays 3-4 hours daily. Heavily plays during school breaks and after homework.
- **Spending**: Spends $5-15/month on storage expansion and time-savers when grinding becomes tedious. Once spent $100 on a "must-have" character during a birthday sale.
- **Genre preferences**: Turn-based RPGs, card battlers, gacha RPGs with strategic depth.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I immerse myself in a game world, I want to master every system and character, so I can feel the satisfaction of complete understanding."

## Relationship to Product
Hiroshi stays for system depth, meaningful progress systems, and fair completion requirements. He leaves when games introduce impossible grind or start invalidating his progress with power creep.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Post-exam — Logs in after missing a 2-week limited event, realizes he's permanently locked out of a character needed for completion.
- **Scenario 2:** Theorycrafting — Spends 2 hours on Discord discussing optimal team comps, then spends $8 on stamina refills to grind materials for his build.
