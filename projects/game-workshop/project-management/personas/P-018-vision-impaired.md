## Bio
Rachel is legally blind with 10% vision remaining and uses VoiceOver exclusively for all phone interactions. She's been a gamer since childhood and refuses to give up her hobby despite accessibility barriers. She searches relentlessly for native app accessibility, often contacting developers directly to ask about VoiceOver support patterns. She never plays games that aren't fully accessible and advocates loudly for accessibility in gaming.

## Goals
- Continue her lifelong hobby with technology adapted to her needs
- Find games with native VoiceOver support, not "good enough" workarounds
- Advocate for accessible gaming in her professional and personal networks

## Frustrations
- Games claiming "accessible marketing" with broken VoiceOver implementation
- Developers who have never tested their apps with VoiceOver
- Being excluded from multiplayer modes requiring screen sharing

## Behaviors
- Uses VoiceOver exclusively — never relies on visual feedback
- Tests游戏 accessibility before ever considering spending
- Advocates for accessibility in professional and personal networks

## Job to Be Done
> "When I want to play a new game, I want verified native accessibility support clearly documented, so I don't waste time discovering broken workarounds after download."

## Relationship to Product
Rachel represents the accessibility edge case that reveals whether a product genuinely cares about inclusion. She provides detailed accessibility feedback but also becomes a fierce advocate for games that get it right. Her professional network includes accessibility professionals across tech companies.
