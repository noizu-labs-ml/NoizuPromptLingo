## Bio
Alex is a senior CS student who treats mobile gaming as his competitive outlet. Between coding bootcamps and algorithms study, he spends 2-3 hours daily grinding ranked matches in a tactical shooter. He streams occasionally to a small Twitch following and dreams of going semi-pro after graduation.

## Goals
- Reach the top 500 leaderboard before the season ends
- Master weapon loadouts and map rotations for competitive advantage
- Build a following that can translate into monetization

## Frustrations
- Pay-to-win players who buy their way ahead with better loadouts
- Matchmaking that pits him against wallet warriors despite equal skill
- Limited customization options that reflect competitive status

## Behaviors
- **Gaming**: Plays 2-3 hours daily, mostly during evening hours. Weekends: 4-6 hour marathon sessions.
- **Spending**: Spends $20-50/month on battle passes, operator skins, and occasionally weapon bundles for competitive aesthetics.
- **Genre preferences**: Tactical shooters, FPS, competitive multiplayer.

## Job to Be Done

## Relationship to Product
Alex stays for the competitive ranking system and depth of skill expression. He leaves when games monetize skill mechanics (power creep through gear) or when matchmaking becomes unplayable due to skill-matched whales.
