## Vision Statement

Chronomancer's Ascension is a premium single-player Action RPG where the player manipulates time as a weapon, a puzzle-solving tool, and a narrative lens. The player inhabits a nameless Chronomancer who must restore fractured souls trapped between collapsed timelines, traversing the Celestial Plane — a metaphysical architecture that exists simultaneously in past, present, and future states.

The game's central promise: **time is not a setting, it is the mechanic.** Every system — combat, exploration, puzzles, narrative — flows from temporal manipulation. The player does not merely visit different eras; they occupy all three at once, shifting between temporal states in real time to uncover hidden paths, exploit enemy weaknesses, and reconstruct shattered histories.

The target experience is a 25-35 hour campaign with a clear ending, supported by a New Game+ mode with remixed enemy placements, altered timeline branches, and additional soul fragments. The design philosophy prioritizes mastery over grind: the player who understands the temporal system deeply will outperform the player who simply levels up.

## Core Loop

The core minute-to-minute gameplay loop operates on a 3-5 minute cycle:

```
Explore Zone → Detect Temporal Anomaly → Shift Time State
        ↓                                    ↓
Solve Spatial Puzzle          Engage Temporal Combat
        ↓                                    ↓
Uncover Soul Fragment ← ← ← Collect Chronon Resources
        ↓
Restore Soul at Nexus → Unlock New Ability → Access New Zone
```

**Minute-by-minute:**

1. **Explore** a zone of the Celestial Plane. The environment renders in the current temporal state (past = ruined, present = functional, future = overgrown/decayed).
2. **Detect** a temporal anomaly — a visual shimmer, an audio dissonance, or an impossible geometry — indicating that shifting time states will reveal a new path, enemy weakness, or hidden collectible.
3. **Shift** time states using the Prism Weapon's attunement. Each shift costs Chronons (temporal energy) and alters the room's geometry, enemy behavior, and available paths.
4. **Engage** in real-time combat using temporal abilities: pause enemy attacks to reposition, rewind your own position to dodge, fast-forward an enemy into a decayed future state where its armor has degraded.
5. **Collect** soul fragments and Chronon resources from defeated enemies and hidden alcoves.
6. **Return** to the Temporal Nexus to restore a fractured soul, which grants a new chronomancy ability or prism upgrade.
7. **Access** previously unreachable zones with the new ability.

The loop is designed for **flow-state immersion**: shifting time states during combat creates a rhythm similar to a fighting game's combo system, where the player strings together pause-rewind-advance sequences into fluid temporal chains.

## Game Mechanics

### Chronomancy Abilities

The player's core toolkit. Each ability is unlocked by restoring a specific soul and has both a combat and exploration application.

| Ability | Soul Source | Cost | Combat Use | Exploration Use |
|---------|-----------|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Temporal Pause** | Soul of the Sentinel (Act 1) | 20 Chronons/sec | Freeze enemies in a 5m radius for 3 sec; reposition for backstab | Freeze moving platforms, stop collapsing bridges |
| **Temporal Rewind** | Soul of the Scholar (Act 1) | 15 Chronons/sec | Rewind player position up to 5 sec; invulnerable during rewind | Undo failed platforming, reset puzzle states |
| **Temporal Advance** | Soul of the Warrior (Act 1) | 25 Chronons | Fast-forward a target 10 sec into its future (decayed armor, moved position) | Age barriers into collapse, grow seeds into vines |
| **Timeline Split** | Soul of the Twins (Act 2) | 40 Chronons | Create a 4-sec echo that repeats your last action (double damage, dual aggro) | Clone stands on pressure plate while you pass through gate |
| **Chronoshock** | Soul of the Storm (Act 2) | 50 Chronons | AoE temporal blast that stuns all enemies and reveals hidden time-state objects | Shatter temporal locks blocking secret areas |
| **Time Lock** | Soul of the Architect (Act 2) | 30 Chronons | Pin an enemy or object permanently in its current state until damaged | Freeze a door in its "open" past state permanently |
| **Temporal Merge** | Soul of the Paradox (Act 3) | 60 Chronons | Overlay two time states on the same space; enemies take damage from both timelines | Walk through walls that exist in one state but not another |
| **Ascension Burst** | Soul of the Chronomancer (Act 3) | 80 Chronons (full gauge) | Ultimate: collapse all time states into one for 10 sec; massive damage + all paths open | Access the final convergence zone |

### Chronon Economy

Chronons are the universal resource governing temporal ability usage.

- **Base capacity:** 100 Chronons (Prism Tier 1)
- **Max capacity:** 300 Chronons (Prism Tier 4)
- **Regeneration:** 2 Chronons/sec passive, +5 Chronons per enemy kill, +15 per soul fragment pickup
- **No consumable purchases:** Chronons regenerate through gameplay, never through spending
- **Upgrade path:** Prism tiers increase capacity and regen rate, purchased at the Nexus using soul fragment residue

### Combat System

**Stance:** Real-time action combat with temporal modifiers. No turn-based elements, no auto-battle.

**Base combat triangle:**

| Action | Input | Effect |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Prism Strike (light) | Square/X | Fast, low damage, generates 2 Chronons |
| Prism Slash (heavy) | Triangle/Y | Slow, high damage, generates 5 Chronons |
| Temporal Dodge | Circle/B + direction | Dodge with 3-frame invincibility window |
| Chronomancy Trigger | L2/LT | Activates selected temporal ability |
| State Shift | D-Pad Up/Down/Left | Switches zone to Past/Present/Future |
| Block | L1/LB | Reduces damage 60%; temporal abilities disabled while blocking |

**Combo system:**

Temporal combos chain abilities with attacks for multiplied effects. Examples:

| Combo | Input Sequence | Effect |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| Frozen Blade | Pause → Heavy → Heavy | 2.5x damage on frozen target |
| Echo Strike | Advance → Split → Light x3 | 6 hits in 2 sec (echo mirrors attacks) |
| Paradox Surge | Rewind → Merge → Heavy | Target takes damage from 2 timelines simultaneously |
| Ascension Slash | Lock → Burst → Heavy | Locked target shatters under chronoshock for 4x damage |

**Enemy design philosophy:** Every enemy has distinct behaviors across time states. A Rust Sentinel in the present has heavy armor; in the past, it is unarmored but faster; in the future, it is crumbling but explodes on death. The player must choose which temporal state to engage in.

### Puzzle Design

Puzzles are integrated into exploration and require temporal manipulation to solve.

**Puzzle archetypes by act:**

| Act | Puzzle Type | Example |
|-----|-------------|---------|
| Act 1 | State-gated | Door only exists in the past; shift to past, walk through, shift back to present |
| Act 1 | Sequence lock | Platforms appear in a specific time-state sequence; memorize and traverse |
| Act 2 | Dual-state | Two pressure plates exist in different time states; use Timeline Split to press both |
| Act 2 | Causal loop | Rewind an object to its past position, then advance yourself to the future where the object's past self triggers a mechanism |
| Act 3 | Merge puzzles | Overlap two time states to create a stable path through an otherwise impossible geometry |
| Act 3 | Paradox resolution | Cause a controlled paradox to shatter a timeline barrier — requires high Paradox Meter |

### Difficulty System

| Mode | Description | Changes |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Weaver** (Easy) | Time shifts have 50% reduced Chronon cost. Enemy damage reduced 40%. Rewind window extended to 8 sec. | For players who want narrative and exploration without combat friction |
| **Chronomancer** (Normal) | Balanced as designed. Default experience. | Standard difficulty |
| **Paradox** (Hard) | Enemy AI is more aggressive. Chronon regen reduced 30%. State shifts have 0.5 sec cast time. | For players who want to master the temporal system under pressure |
| **Temporal Anchor** (Very Hard) | Permadeath. One save slot. No Rewind ability. | Unlocked after completing the game on Paradox |

### Nexus (Hub Area)

The Temporal Nexus is the player's home base. It exists outside time — a crystalline void where the restored souls reside as ghostly figures. Functions:

- **Soul restoration shrine** — Convert fragments into abilities
- **Prism forge** — Upgrade weapon tiers using soul residue
- **Timeline Journal** — Review lore, track Paradox Meter, view zone completion
- **Practice arena** — Spawn enemies to test combos and abilities without consequence
- **Fast travel** — Teleport to any previously visited zone entrance

### Temporal State Rendering

Each zone renders three visual layers simultaneously. The current active state is fully rendered; inactive states appear as translucent ghost overlays the player can toggle for planning.

| State | Visual Palette | Audio Signature |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| Past | Warm amber, intact geometry, golden light | Orchestral, full instrumentation |
| Present | Cool blue-gray, weathered surfaces, ambient light | Minimal, ambient + environmental |
| Future | Deep purple-black, crumbling edges, bioluminescent decay | Distorted, reversed audio, echo-heavy |

## Narrative

### Premise

The Celestial Plane was created when an unknown catastrophe fractured the boundary between timelines. Every soul that existed across multiple possible futures was shattered into fragments scattered across the collapsed architecture. The player character — a nameless figure who awakens in the Nexus holding the Prism Weapon — is the only being capable of perceiving all three time states simultaneously. They are not a hero; they are a mechanism the Plane itself produced to heal.

### Tone

Philosophical, melancholic, and ultimately hopeful. The narrative draws on themes of identity across time, the ethics of choosing one timeline over another, and whether repairing the past requires erasing it. There is no villain — only the catastrophe and its consequences.

### Narrative Structure

The story is delivered through three channels:

1. **Soul memories** — Restoring each soul triggers a playable memory sequence (2-5 min) showing the soul's life before the fracture. These are linear, combat-free, and reveal how the souls' stories interconnect.
2. **Environmental storytelling** — Each zone's three time states tell a different chapter of the zone's history. The player pieces together what happened by comparing states.
3. **Nexus dialogue** — Restored souls appear in the Nexus and offer commentary, hints, and philosophical reflections. They are not quest-givers; they are characters processing their own restoration.

### Key Narrative Beats

| Act | Beat | Player Experience |
|-----|------|-------------------|
| Act 1 Opening | Awakening in the Nexus with no memory. The Prism hums. | Disorientation, mystery, first temporal shift |
| Act 1 Midpoint | Restoring the Scholar reveals that the catastrophe was not an accident — someone caused it intentionally. | Narrative stakes escalate |
| Act 1 End | The Warrior's memory shows the player character was present at the catastrophe. The player is part of the event they are trying to fix. | Identity crisis, player questions their role |
| Act 2 Opening | Restored souls begin arguing about whether the timelines should be merged or kept separate. | Moral complexity introduced |
| Act 2 Midpoint | The Twins' memory reveals they were the same person in two timelines — the fracture split them. The player must choose which version to prioritize in restoration. | First meaningful player choice with mechanical consequence |
| Act 2 End | The Architect reveals the Celestial Plane is not healing — it is consuming timelines to sustain itself. The player's restoration work is feeding the Plane, not fixing it. | Trust broken, motivation reframed |
| Act 3 Opening | The player enters the Void Heart, where the collapsed timelines are visible as physical threads. | Visual spectacle, existential weight |
| Act 3 Midpoint | The Paradox soul is the player's own fractured future self. Restoring it means the player ceases to exist as they are. | Sacrifice theme, player agency |
| Act 3 End (3 endings) | Merge all timelines, isolate one timeline, or let the Plane collapse and free all souls into oblivion. | Player choice determines ending, no "correct" answer |

### Endings

| Ending | Requirement | Outcome |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **The Mending** | Low Paradox Meter, choose to merge | All timelines merge into one restored reality. Souls live but lose their alternate histories. Bittersweet. |
| **The Culling** | High Paradox Meter, choose to isolate | One timeline is preserved; all others are erased. The player chooses which. Loss for the greater stability. |
| **The Release** | Max Paradox Meter, choose collapse | The Celestial Plane dissolves. All souls are freed into formless existence. No reality persists, but no more suffering. |
| **Ascension** (true ending) | NG+ only, all souls restored, max Paradox, specific action in Void Heart | The player becomes the new architect of time, rebuilding the Plane with full knowledge of its nature. Requires the player to find the hidden 4th option during the final choice. |

## User Stories

### Exploration & Traversal

**US-001:** As a player entering a new zone, I see ghost overlays of inactive time states so that I can plan which state to shift into before committing Chronons.

**US-002:** As a player standing before a sealed door, I shift to the past state where the door is intact but open, walk through, and shift back to present so that I bypass the seal without combat.

**US-003:** As a player in the Overturned Garden, I advance a seed into a full-grown vine in the future state and climb it to reach an elevated soul fragment that is inaccessible in the present.

**US-004:** As a player in the Shattered Cathedral, I use Timeline Split to create an echo on a pressure plate in the present while I walk through the gate in the past state where the gate mechanism is exposed.

**US-005:** As a player navigating the Void Heart, I use Temporal Merge to overlay the past's intact floor with the present's open ceiling, creating a walkable path through otherwise impossible geometry.

### Combat

**US-006:** As a player facing an armored Rust Sentinel, I shift to the future state where its armor has decayed, deal damage with light attacks, then shift to present to avoid its future-state death explosion.

**US-007:** As a player surrounded by three enemies, I activate Temporal Pause, reposition behind the priority target, and unleash a Heavy attack for 2.5x frozen damage before the pause expires.

**US-008:** As a player who mistimed a dodge, I activate Rewind to return to my position 3 seconds prior, avoiding the hit and resetting my attack opportunity.

**US-009:** As a player executing the Paradox Surge combo, I Rewind to create a temporal echo, then Merge time states so the target takes simultaneous damage from both my past and present attacks.

**US-010:** As a player in Paradox mode, I manage my Chronon gauge carefully because the 30% reduced regen means I cannot afford to waste Temporal Pause on trivial repositioning.

### Progression

**US-011:** As a player who has collected 4 soul fragments in the Ruins of the First Age, I return to the Nexus and restore the Soul of the Sentinel, unlocking the Temporal Pause ability for both combat and puzzle-solving.

**US-012:** As a player upgrading the Prism Weapon from Tier 2 to Tier 3, my Chronon capacity increases from 150 to 220, enabling me to chain two Timeline Splits in a single combat encounter.

**US-013:** As a player who has achieved 100% Timeline Journal completion in a zone, a hidden boss encounter unlocks in that zone's deepest chamber, requiring mastery of all time states to defeat.

**US-014:** As a player starting New Game+, I carry over all abilities and the Paradox Meter, enabling me to access timeline branches that were sealed in the base playthrough.

**US-015:** As a player on NG+ who finds all 4 Lost Souls, the final choice scene in the Convergence gains a fourth option, unlocking the true Ascension ending.

### Narrative

**US-016:** As a player restoring the Soul of the Scholar, I enter a 3-minute playable memory where I experience the Scholar's final moments before the catastrophe, learning that the fracture was deliberate.

**US-017:** As a player in the Nexus, I speak with the restored Soul of the Warrior, who comments on the player character's identity and hints that the player was present at the catastrophe.

**US-018:** As a player in the Echoing Archives, I compare the past state's full library shelves with the present's scattered books and the future's blank void, piecing together that the Archive was the epicenter of the temporal experiment.

**US-019:** As a player facing the choice at the Convergence, I see the consequences of each option reflected in the three time states of the final chamber, with each state showing what that choice leads to.

**US-020:** As a player who caused high paradox throughout the run, the Culling ending is available because the Paradox Meter represents my willingness to break timelines, making me thematically aligned with choosing which timeline survives.

### Accessibility & Session Design

**US-021:** As a player on Weaver difficulty, I complete combat encounters using only light attacks and occasional Pause, enjoying the narrative and puzzles without combat friction, in 20-minute sessions after work.

**US-022:** As a player who saves and quits mid-combat, the game saves my exact position, Chronon gauge, and enemy states so I resume precisely where I left off.

**US-023:** As a player with vision impairment, I rely on the distinct audio signatures for each time state (orchestral for past, ambient for present, reversed for future) to navigate without relying solely on visual ghost overlays.

**US-024:** As a player who has been away for 2 weeks, a brief recap screen on load reminds me of my last objective, current abilities, and which zone I was exploring.

### Difficulty & Mastery

**US-025:** As a player on Temporal Anchor (very hard) mode, I have no Rewind ability and one life, making every combat encounter a test of pure mastery of Pause, Advance, Split, and Merge under permadeath conditions.

**US-026:** As a player practicing a new combo in the Nexus practice arena, I spawn a specific enemy type, test the input sequence, and see a damage counter to optimize my timing before attempting it in the field.

**US-027:** As a player who has completed the game on Paradox mode, the Temporal Anchor mode unlocks, and I begin a new permadeath run knowing that the only thing standing between me and completion is my skill.

### Core Technical Systems

**Temporal State Manager:**
The central system governing all time-state behavior. Each zone is authored as three parallel scene layers. The manager activates one as the "primary" (fully rendered, collision-enabled) and maintains the other two as ghost overlays (visual only, no collision). State shifts swap the primary in under 200ms.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│            TEMPORAL STATE MANAGER              │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ active_state: "present"                        │
│ chronon_cost: { "past": 0, "future": 15 }      │
│ ghost_opacity: 0.3                             │
│ shift_cooldown: 0.5s (Paradox mode only)       │
│                                                │
│ ┌──────────┐  ┌──────────┐  ┌──────────┐      │
│ │  PAST    │  │ PRESENT  │  │ FUTURE   │      │
│ │ (ghost)  │  │ (active) │  │ (ghost)  │      │
│ │ no coll  │  │ coll on  │  │ no coll  │      │
│ │ 30% opac │  │ 100%     │  │ 30% opac │      │
│ └──────────┘  └──────────┘  └──────────┘      │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Chronon Economy System:**
Manages the player's temporal energy resource. Serverless (single-player), deterministic, no randomness.

| Parameter | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|-----------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Max Chronons | 100 | 150 | 220 | 300 |
| Regen rate (per sec) | 2 | 3 | 4 | 6 |
| Regen per kill | 5 | 7 | 10 | 15 |
| Pause cost/sec | 20 | 18 | 15 | 12 |
