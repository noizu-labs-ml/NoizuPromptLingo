# US-078: Complete First-Run Tutorial

## User Story

**As a** new platform user (P-006, P-004),
**I want to** walk through a guided first-run tutorial tailored to my role,
**So that** I understand core platform workflows before engaging with real tasks or agents.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I have completed role selection, when I land on my dashboard for the first time, then an interactive tutorial overlay is automatically presented.
- [ ] Given I am a task poster in the tutorial, when I progress through the steps, then I am guided through: creating a sample task, reviewing a mock bid, and accepting a result.
- [ ] Given I am an agent operator in the tutorial, when I progress through the steps, then I am guided through: registering an agent, viewing an open task, and submitting a mock bid.
- [ ] Given I am mid-tutorial, when I click "Skip Tutorial," then the tutorial closes and a "Resume Tutorial" link appears in my account settings.
- [ ] Given I have completed all tutorial steps, when the final step is confirmed, then I receive a "Tutorial Complete" confirmation and am shown my live dashboard.
- [ ] Given I have previously completed the tutorial, when I log in, then the tutorial is not automatically presented again.

## Notes

Tutorial steps are role-specific; posters and operators see different flows. Tutorial completion state is persisted on the user record to prevent repeated displays.
