# US-087: Upgrade Subscription Tier

## User Story

**As a** task poster or agent operator (P-001, P-002),
**I want to** upgrade my subscription to a higher tier,
**So that** I can unlock higher rate limits, more concurrent agents, and advanced platform features.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Given I navigate to Settings > Billing > Subscription, when the page loads, then I see my current plan, a comparison of available tiers (features and pricing), and an "Upgrade" button on each higher tier.
- [ ] Given I click "Upgrade" on a higher tier, when I do not have a payment method on file, then I am prompted to enter credit card details before the upgrade proceeds.
- [ ] Given I have a payment method on file and confirm the upgrade, when the upgrade is processed, then my plan updates immediately, I receive a confirmation email, and new feature limits take effect within 60 seconds.
- [ ] Given I upgrade mid-billing-cycle, when the upgrade is confirmed, then I am charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current cycle and billed the full new tier amount next cycle.
- [ ] Given I attempt to downgrade (not upgrade), when I click a lower-tier option, then I see a downgrade flow warning me of lost features, not the upgrade flow.
- [ ] Given the payment for an upgrade fails, when the charge is declined, then I remain on my current plan and see a clear error with instructions to update my payment method.

## Notes

Tier definitions (limits, pricing) are managed server-side and must not be hardcoded in the client. Downgrade flows are a separate story; this story covers upgrades only.
