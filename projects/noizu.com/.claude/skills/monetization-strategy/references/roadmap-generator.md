## What to Provide

Your decisions so far:
- Chosen primary stream (and secondary, if any)
- Time available and budget
- Revenue targets and timeline
- Skills you have vs. need to develop

## The Prompt

```markdown
Based on my situation and chosen stream(s), create a detailed implementation roadmap.

### My Situation:
- Chosen primary stream: [AI Templates / Content Publishing / POD]
- Secondary stream (if any): [None / AI Templates / Content Publishing / POD]
- Time available: [hours/week]
- Budget for ads/testing: [amount]
- First revenue target: [date]
- Year 1 revenue goal: [$X/month]
- Skills I have: [list]
- Skills I need to develop: [list]

### Generate:

**1. 90-Day Implementation Plan** (week-by-week)
- Weeks 1-2: Foundation (research, skill development, setup)
- Weeks 3-4: Validation (test market demand, create sample)
- Weeks 5-8: Build (first product/content/designs at scale)
- Weeks 9-12: Launch & Optimize (go to market, first customers, iterate)

For each phase: time allocation, activities, deliverables, milestones, metrics.

**2. Platform Roadmap**
- Initial platform choice and timing
- Secondary platform expansion trigger
- Ad platform launch timing
- When to build own site

**3. Revenue Milestones**
- Path from $0 to first $100
- How to reach $500/month
- Scaling to $1,000/month
- Strategy for $5,000/month

**4. Traffic & Audience Plan**
- Initial traffic/customer acquisition
- Organic growth strategy
- Paid ads strategy and budget allocation
- Community engagement approach

**5. Resource Requirements**
- Tools needed (free and paid)
- One-time vs. recurring costs
- When costs become ROI-positive

**6. Risk Mitigation**
- Early warning signs (when to pivot)
- Go/No-Go decision points
- Success validation criteria

**7. Secondary Stream Integration** (if applicable)
- When to launch, synergies, resource reallocation
```

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific** — "Get 5 sales at $47 average" not "increase sales"
2. **Include time estimates** — Every activity needs hours
3. **Define success metrics** — Numbers at each milestone
4. **Plan for adjustment** — Include checkpoints to course-correct
5. **Track everything** — Weekly metrics review habit
