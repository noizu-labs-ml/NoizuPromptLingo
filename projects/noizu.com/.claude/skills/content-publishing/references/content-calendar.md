## 5. Content Mix Ratios

### Recommended Content Balance

| Category | % of Output | Purpose | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|---------|
| **Evergreen tutorials** | 40% | SEO traffic, long-term discovery | "How to set up Docker Compose" |
| **Timely analysis** | 20% | Trending traffic, authority | "What GPT-5 means for developers" |
| **Opinion/hot takes** | 15% | Engagement, shareability | "Why you should stop using Redux" |
| **Comparisons** | 15% | Search traffic, decision-stage readers | "Bun vs Deno vs Node in 2026" |
| **Case studies** | 10% | Conversion, paid tier | "How I built X in 2 weeks" |

### Funnel Stage Balance

| Stage | % of Content | Platform | Goal |
|-------|-------------|----------|------|
| **Top of funnel** | 50% | Dev.to, Medium | Discovery, email capture |
| **Middle of funnel** | 30% | Free Substack | Trust, engagement |
| **Bottom of funnel** | 20% | Paid Substack teasers | Conversion |

### Platform Distribution

| Platform | Posts/Month | Content Type | Role |
|----------|------------|--------------|------|
| **Dev.to** | 4-6 | Tutorials, comparisons | Discovery engine |
| **Substack (free)** | 4 | Insights, analysis | Relationship building |
| **Substack (paid)** | 2-4 | Deep dives, exclusives | Revenue |
| **Medium** | 2-4 | Cross-posts | Secondary reach |
| **LinkedIn** | 4-8 | Summaries, threads | Professional network |
