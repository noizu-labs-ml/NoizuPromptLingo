# Kraken's Call

**Action Adventure RPG**

## Core Loop
As the luminous shaman of the abyss, channel obsidian void energy to defeat the elder krakens that corrupt the ocean depths while amassing a collection of ancient amulets.

## Features
- **Shamanic Void Mastery**: Wield the forbidden shamanic arts of channeling void energy, creating obsidian constructs, manipulating ocean currents, and banishing abyssal corruption
- **Krakens of the Deep**: Battle 5 elder krakens, each governing a different ocean trench and wielding unique elemental powers (fire, ice, lightning, gravity, psychic)
- **Amulet Collection**: Discover 45 ancient amulets across sunken temples, kraken lairs, and forgotten civilizations—each amulet grants unique abilities while stacking synergistic effects
- **Abyssal Expedition**: Explore 8 interconnected ocean biomes (twilight zone, midnight zone, abyssal plain, hydrothermal vents, ancient ruins, frozen trenches, bioluminescent forests, the void gate)

## Why This Is Awesome
**Story-Driven Explorers** will be captivated by the tale of betrayal—the luminous shamans once served the ocean's balance until the krakens grew jealous of their power. Your journey to restore harmony reveals ancient prophecies, sunken kingdoms, and the tragic truth behind the conflict.

**Emergent Sandbox** players will master the depth-first combination system: 45 amulets × 12 void channeling techniques × 4 combat schools (physical, elemental, psychic, temporal) = thousands of emergent build paths. Environmental destruction, physics-based kraken combat, and the ability to create your own void portals create endless creative expression.

**Flow State** enthusiasts will enter deep flow through the rhythmic combat loop: dodge → channel → amulet activation → kraken combo → environmental exploitation → amulet chain. The seamless ocean traversal, instant load times between biomes, and hypnotic bioluminescent environments create an immersive, meditative experience.
