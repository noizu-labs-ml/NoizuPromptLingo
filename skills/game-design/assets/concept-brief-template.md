# Concept Brief Template

One-page game concept document for rapid ideation and evaluation.

```markdown
# [Game Title]

## Core Fantasy
[One sentence: What does the player feel? What is the dream they're living?]

## Genre & Platform
- **Genre**: [Primary genre + sub-genre]
- **Platform**: [Primary: iOS/Android/PC/Console]
- **Perspective**: [2D/3D, first-person/third-person/isometric/top-down]

## Core Loop
[Diagram or 3-5 step description of the minute-to-minute gameplay]

1. [Action] → 2. [Reward] → 3. [Choice] → 4. [Challenge] → (repeat)

## Meta Loop
[What carries between sessions? What is the long-term goal?]

## Unique Selling Points
1. [USP 1: What makes this different?]
2. [USP 2: Why would someone choose this over competitors?]
3. [USP 3: What's the viral moment?]

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: [Player persona name — reference persona files]
- **Age**: [Rating]
- **Play style**: [Casual/Mid-core/Hardcore]

## Comparable Titles
| Title | What to Learn | What to Do Different |
|-------|--------------|---------------------|
| [Game A] | [Mechanic they do well] | [How we improve on it] |
| [Game B] | [Mechanic they do well] | [How we improve on it] |
| [Game C] | [Mechanic they do well] | [How we improve on it] |

## Monetization Model
- **Model**: [Premium / F2P+IAP / F2P+Ads / Hybrid]
- **Primary revenue**: [What drives the most income?]
- **Secondary revenue**: [Supplementary income stream]

## Scope Estimate
- **Team size**: [1-5 / 5-15 / 15+]
- **Timeline**: [3-6 / 6-12 / 12-24 months]
- **Budget**: [<$50K / $50-200K / $200K-$1M / $1M+]

## Concept Art / Reference
[Links to visual references, mood boards, comparable screenshots]

## Open Questions
- [Question 1 that needs resolution]
- [Question 2 that needs resolution]
- [Question 3 that needs resolution]
```
