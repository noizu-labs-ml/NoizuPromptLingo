## Platform-Specific Optimization

### ChatGPT

1. **Wikipedia Presence** -- establish/improve company Wikipedia articles
2. **Review Platform Dominance** -- 70%+ ratings on G2/Capterra
3. **Major Publication Relationships** -- Forbes, TechRadar, Business Insider
4. **Structured Content** -- schema markup increases citation 28-40%

### Perplexity

1. **Authentic Reddit Engagement** -- build karma, share expertise without promotion
2. **YouTube Content** -- 15-30 min videos with transcripts and chapters
3. **Content Freshness** -- 76.4% of highly-cited pages updated within 30 days
4. **Community Platforms** -- Yelp, G2, Gartner, TripAdvisor

### Google AI

1. **YouTube Dominance** -- comprehensive topical content
2. **LinkedIn Authority** -- long-form articles, expertise sharing
3. **Quora Engagement** -- depth, data, credentials
4. **Fan-Out Query Strategy** -- 161% more likely to cite pages ranking for fan-out queries
5. **Schema Priorities** -- High: FAQPage, HowTo, Organization, Article; Medium: Product, LocalBusiness, Review

### Copilot

- Forbes and business publication PR
- Gartner and analyst relationships
- Enterprise review platforms (Software Advice, SourceForge)
