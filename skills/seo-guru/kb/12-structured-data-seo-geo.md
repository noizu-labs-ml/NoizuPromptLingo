## Key Findings

- John Mueller (Google, 2025): structured data doesn't directly influence rankings but significantly impacts engagement
- GPT-4 accuracy: 16% -> 54% correct responses when content uses structured data
- Average AI query: 23 words vs 4 words for traditional Google searches
- BrightEdge: ~30% CTR increase from rich snippets via structured data

## Primary Schema Types

1. **Article** -- blog posts and editorial content
2. **FAQ** -- question-answer formats (highest AEO impact)
3. **LocalBusiness** -- geographic business information
4. **Product** -- e-commerce items
5. **HowTo** -- tutorials and guides

## Best Practices for AI Optimization

- **Semantic Consistency**: align structured data with actual content vocabulary
- **Precise Entities**: specific identifiers, not generic labels
- **Geographic Context**: include location data even for online services
- **Regular Updates**: refresh dateModified and pricing continuously
- **JSON-LD Format**: preferred by Google over microdata/RDFa

## Implementation Strategy

1. Start with 2-3 essential markup types for your business
2. Prioritize high-traffic pages first
3. Validate with Rich Results Test
4. Expand gradually

## The Hybrid Future

Traditional SEO captures ~80% of searches (2025), while GEO addresses rising AI assistants. Structured data is the common denominator optimizing both classic search and AI citation simultaneously.
