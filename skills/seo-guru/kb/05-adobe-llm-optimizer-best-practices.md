## LLM vs SEO: Key Differences

| Aspect | SEO | LLM |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Foundation | Index-based | Token-based (trained data) |
| Authority Signal | Link authority | Brand mentions |
| JS Rendering | Supported | Very limited |
| Indexing | Real-time indexing | RAG |

Key: LLMs prioritize content relevance and brand presence over backlinks. Earned third-party mentions and citations are crucial.

## Strategic Campaign Planning

1. **Topic Identification**: Target high-value topics aligned with customer intent
2. **Competitive Gap Analysis**: Spot competitor mentions to identify opportunities
3. **Intent Clustering**: Group similar prompts using topic clustering
4. **Credibility Assessment**: Apply E-E-A-T standards

### Visibility Improvement Cycle

Analyze -> Plan -> Act -> Adapt (iterative)

## Onsite Optimization

### Technical Accessibility

- Review robots.txt and CDN settings for AI agent crawling
- Use URL Inspector for blocked/inaccessible pages

### Content Refresh and Structure

- Update 10-15% of page content regularly
- Add citations and references to authoritative sources
- Implement structured headers (H1, H2, H3)

### FAQ Integration

- Natural-language FAQs based on prompt analysis
- Conversational formats addressing common questions

## Offsite Optimization

Key channels LLMs frequently cite:
- **Wikipedia** -- updated, well-sourced pages
- **Reddit and Quora** -- authentic, helpful participation
- **Affiliate articles/reviews** -- collaborate with publishers
- **YouTube and social media** -- answer common questions
- **News and PR** -- reputable outlet placement

## Measuring LLM Visibility

### Key Metrics

- **Mentions**: Brand frequency in responses
- **Citations**: How often LLMs reference your content
- **Sentiment**: Positive, neutral, or negative
- **Position**: Placement within responses (first, middle, last)

Combined into a **visibility score**.

## Understanding Agentic Traffic

AI bots extract facts from your pages to generate answers, potentially citing brand without driving traditional engagement. Agentic traffic often targets deeper pages, not homepages.

Visibility and influence are decoupled from traffic in the LLM era.
