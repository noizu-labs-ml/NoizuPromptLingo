## Decision Triggers

Use these thresholds to make portfolio decisions. Check monthly.

### Double Down (Shift Resources TO This Stream)

| Trigger | Condition | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| High RPH | Revenue/Hour > $20 AND growing | +2-4 hrs/week from lowest stream |
| Breakout product | Single product > $500/month | Create variants, invest in marketing |
| Audience surge | Subscriber growth > 20%/month for 2+ months | Increase publishing frequency |

### Maintain (Keep Current Allocation)

| Trigger | Condition | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Healthy RPH | Revenue/Hour $8-20 | Maintain hours, focus on quality |
| Steady growth | 5-15% MoM growth | Continue current strategy |

### Investigate (Something's Off)

| Trigger | Condition | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Low RPH | Revenue/Hour < $8 for 2+ months | Deep-dive: what's not converting? |
| Flat growth | < 5% MoM for 3+ months | Re-evaluate niche, platform, or content |
| High refunds | Template refund rate > 15% | Product quality issue — fix immediately |
| Declining opens | Newsletter open rate dropping month over month | Subject lines? Content quality? List hygiene? |

### Sunset (Reduce to Maintenance)

| Trigger | Condition | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Persistent underperformance | RPH < $5 for 3+ months AND declining | Reduce to 1 hr/week maintenance |
| No product-market fit | 0 sales after 2 months with 3+ products | Pivot niche or pause stream entirely |

### Expansion (Add New Stream)

| Trigger | Condition | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Primary stream stable | RPH > $15 for 3+ months with < 10h/week | Consider secondary stream |
| Audience request | Multiple requests for adjacent products | Evaluate adjacent stream |
| Revenue milestone | Total portfolio > $1K/month | Review expansion streams (see trl-monetization-strategy) |
