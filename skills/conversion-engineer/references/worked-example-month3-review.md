## Portfolio Review — March 2026

### Executive Summary

Three months in. Templates are generating revenue but below projections. Content is building audience slowly. POD hasn't launched yet (per plan). Total revenue: $487/month, well below the $300 Month 3 projection for combined — wait, actually above. The template revenue is carrying things, but growth is flattening. Content needs to start driving template traffic soon.

**Article performance:**

| Article | Platform | Views | Reactions | Newsletter Signups |

- 189 subscribers in 3 months is ahead of the 150 target. Growth is accelerating (50 → 60 → 79 per month).

**Upcoming decision:** When to launch paid tier. At 189 subscribers, we're close to the 200 minimum viable threshold. But consistent weekly publishing has only been 6 weeks (started irregular). Wait one more month.

| Content → Merch | No | -- | -- | -- |
| Merch → Content | No | -- | -- | -- |
| Templates → Merch | No | -- | -- | -- |

### Content Mix

- Total pieces published: 4 articles + 4 newsletters = 8
- Pieces with cross-sell element: 2 articles (template footnote) + 1 newsletter (template mention) = 3
- Cross-sell ratio: 37.5% — **slightly over the 30% target**
- Action: Next month, make 1 of the articles purely value, no CTA

### Bridge Assessment

- **Content → Templates (0.07% of views):** Below the 0.5-2% benchmark, but the K8s article drove 3 direct purchases. The rate is low because the Docker article had low traffic, not because the bridge is broken. When articles get views, the bridge works.
- **Templates → Content (28% of buyers):** Above the 8-15% benchmark. The README CTA is working well. 12 of 43 total buyers have subscribed to the newsletter. This is excellent.

## Rebalancing Analysis

### Current Allocation vs Performance

| Stream | Hours/Week | Revenue/Month | RPH | Growth | Decision |
|--------|-----------|--------------|-----|--------|----------|
| Templates | 6.5h | $423 | $16.27 | +48% | **Maintain** |
| Content | 3h | $64 (attributed) | $5.33 | Accelerating | **Maintain** |
| POD | 0h | $0 | N/A | Not started | **Per plan** |

### Decision Rule Evaluation

| Rule | Condition | Met? | Action |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Double Down | RPH > $20 AND growing | No (RPH is $16.27) | Not triggered |
| Maintain | RPH $8-20 AND stable | Yes (Templates) | Keep current hours |
| Investigate | RPH < $8 for 2+ months | Content at $5.33, but expected pre-revenue | Not triggered (too early) |
| Early Stage Exception | Stream < 3 months | Both streams qualify | Do not rebalance yet |

### Recommendation: No Rebalancing

**Rationale:** Both streams are < 3 months old. The early stage exception applies. Templates are performing well. Content is building audience ahead of schedule. Changing allocation now would be premature.

**One adjustment:** Shift 0.5h/week from template maintenance to content writing. Templates have 3 products live and need less active development. Content needs more articles to maintain the growth trajectory.

## Decisions & Actions

### Keep Doing
- Publishing to Dev.to with template footnotes (the bridge works when articles get traffic)
- Including newsletter CTA in product READMEs (28% conversion is excellent)
- Writing about topics that overlap with template products (K8s article → K8s kit sales)

### Start Doing
- Refresh the K8s Troubleshooting Kit listing (declining sales signal needs attention)
- Plan the paid newsletter tier (target: launch at 250 subscribers, probably Month 5)
- Write one "pure value" article per month with zero cross-sell (maintain the 70/30 rule)

### Stop Doing
- Cross-posting to Medium (210 views, 0 conversions — not worth the 30 minutes)
- Discounting products at launch (Docker kit at $37 undervalues; launch at $47 with a 48-hour $37 code instead)
