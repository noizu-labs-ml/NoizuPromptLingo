# Style Guide Examples: Ipso The Lorem, Inc.

> 10 worked examples demonstrating how to construct style guides from the six design system specifications. Covers 5 pure styles and 5 mixed (80/20) combinations.

---

## About Ipso The Lorem, Inc.

**Ipso The Lorem, Inc.** is a creative technology consultancy founded in 2019. With roughly 45 employees across design, engineering, and strategy, they help mid-market businesses design and ship digital products. Their tagline: *"We turn your rough draft into the real thing."*

Ipso serves as our example company because their breadth creates natural style guide diversity: an enterprise RFP portal demands different visual signals than a junior designer recruitment site, which demands different signals than an experimental creative showcase. Same company, ten legitimate contexts, ten different style systems.

The name itself is a meta-reference — placeholder text for a company that turns placeholders into real things.

## How to Use These Examples

1. **Read the methodology first:** [process/style-guide-construction.md](../../process/style-guide-construction.md)
2. **Pure style examples (01-05)** show direct derivation from a single style spec
3. **Mixed style examples (06-10)** demonstrate the 80/20 mixing process
4. **Look for decision rationale**, not just final values — each example explains *why* it chose specific colors, fonts, and component treatments within the style system's constraints

Each example derives Ipso-specific brand values from the source specs. The hex values are not copy-pasted from the reference — they're chosen *within* each style system's palette structure to fit Ipso's brand identity for that context.

## Mixing Ratios Demonstrated

| Mix | Compatible? | What to Watch For |
|-----|-------------|-------------------|
| MT 80% + ED 20% | Yes (SKILL.md) | How editorial typography softens minimal tech's austerity |
| CE 80% + MT 20% | Yes (SKILL.md) | How tech polish modernizes corporate formality |
| CP 80% + BE 20% | Novel | How expressive moments elevate playfulness without chaos |
