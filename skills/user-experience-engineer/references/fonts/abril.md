# Abril (Display / Text / Titling)

## Identity

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Designers** | Veronika Burian and José Scaglione |
| **Foundry** | TypeTogether |
| **Year** | 2011 |
| **Adobe Fonts** | Yes — Abril (Display and Text) available via Adobe Fonts with Creative Cloud |
| **Weights** | Display: Light through Black (6 weights, 12 styles); Text: Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold Italic, Medium, Semibold — 10 text styles total |

## Classification

**Contemporary Modern/Transitional Serif — Newsface.** Abril is explicitly a modern interpretation of the classic newsface tradition. It has characteristics of both modern (Didone) serifs — strong vertical stress, high contrast between thick and thin strokes — and transitional forms. It was purpose-built for intensive editorial use across print and digital, and its Display and Text variants are drawn as distinct designs optimized for their respective scales.

## Character

Abril is what happens when two serious type designers study the editorial serif tradition and engineer something purpose-built for contemporary publishing. Where historical newsfaces were optimized for letterpress on newsprint, Abril is optimized for both print and screen reproduction at the full range of contemporary editorial applications. The Display variants carry authority and news-desk energy — high contrast, confident serifs, the visual voice of a confident publication. The Text variants are more restrained: the contrast reduces, proportions widen slightly, spacing opens up, and the overall color harmonizes for comfortable reading at 9–12pt. Burian and Scaglione have stated the design competes directly with classic newsfaces like Utopia and Nimrod for space economy, while delivering a more contemporary aesthetic.

The superfamily structure — 22 styles across Display and Text optical families — is serious professional infrastructure.

## Best Use Cases

- **Newspaper design** — display headers, section titles, and body text in a unified system
- **Magazine typography** — covers, article headers, editorial body text
- **Digital editorial platforms** — news sites, magazine apps, digital publications
- **Annual reports and corporate publications** requiring newspaper-style hierarchy
- **Brand identity for media organizations** — the Display variants work for masthead-level branding

## Tone and Mood

Credible, contemporary, editorial authority. Abril projects the confidence of a well-resourced publication: it knows its typographic history but is not nostalgic about it. The Display variants are emphatic — assertive without shouting. The Text variants are professional and efficient. Neither precious nor bland. The overall register is modern European journalism and design press.

## Strengths

- Purpose-built dual-system (Display + Text) drawn from scratch for each optical scale
- Supports 50+ languages including Central European and Northern European Latin
- Full small caps with numbers and punctuation in Text weights
- Directly competitive with Utopia and Nimrod for space economy in newsprint contexts
- Available on Adobe Fonts — friction-free for CC subscribers
- Designed by TypeTogether — one of the most respected type foundries for editorial systems

## Weaknesses

- Strong editorial character limits versatility in luxury branding or purely decorative contexts
- Less distinctive in non-editorial settings — the newsface genre is specific
- Less global exposure than some Adobe-produced fonts
- Cyrillic/non-Latin extension less complete than some competitors

## Comparison

| Font | Relationship |
|------|-------------|
| **Mercury** | Mercury is older and has the grading system innovation; Abril is more contemporary and available at lower licensing cost; both are serious editorial serifs |
| **Utopia** | Both compete in the editorial newsface space; Abril has a more contemporary feel; Utopia has Adobe Originals heritage; space economy roughly comparable |
| **Source Serif Pro** | Source Serif is more neutral and utilitarian; Abril has stronger editorial personality and optical-size specialization |

## CSS Snippet

```css
/* Adobe Fonts (Typekit) */
font-family: "abril-display", "abril-text", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif;

/* Editorial display */
.news-headline {
  font-family: "abril-display", Georgia, serif;
  font-weight: 700;
  font-size: 2.5rem;
  line-height: 1.1;
}

/* Editorial body text */
.article-body {
  font-family: "abril-text", Georgia, serif;
  font-weight: 400;
  font-size: 16px;
  line-height: 1.65;
}
```
