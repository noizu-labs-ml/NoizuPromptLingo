## Core Concept

GEO = structuring content so AI systems find, understand, and cite it. Unlike SEO's focus on rankings, GEO focuses on becoming a cited source in AI-generated answers.

## How Generative Search Engines Work

1. **Query Fan-Out** -- AI breaks complex questions into sub-queries
2. **RAG Retrieval** -- pulls passages from web pages
3. **Synthesis** -- combines multiple sources into coherent response
4. **Citation** -- attributes to original sources

## GEO vs SEO

| Factor | Traditional SEO | GEO |
|--------|-----------------|-----|
| Output | Ranked link lists | Synthesized narrative |
| Queries | Short (4 words avg.) | Conversational (23 words avg.) |
| Success Metric | Rankings & CTR | Citations & brand mentions |
| Focus | Keywords & backlinks | Content structure & authority |

Constants: E-E-A-T matters, technical optimization essential, quality content wins, backlinks influence via live web search.

## Why GEO Matters

- ChatGPT: 800+ million weekly active users
- Google AI Overviews: billions of monthly appearances
- Apple integrating Claude and Perplexity into Safari
- Sessions average 6 minutes vs seconds on Google
- Higher conversion rates than organic search
- Vercel: 10% of signups from ChatGPT
- **Critical**: overlap between Google top results and AI-cited sources dropped from 70% to below 20%

## Six Content Optimization Strategies

### 1. Ensure AI Crawler Access
- Check robots.txt for AI bot restrictions
- Verify Cloudflare settings
- Look for "ChatGPT-User" in server logs
- Avoid client-side JS rendering
- Keep content accessible (no auth walls)

### 2. Structure for Extraction
- Clear H1/H2/H3 hierarchies
- Bullet points and numbered lists
- Lead with direct answers
- 2-3 sentence paragraphs max
- 30-40% higher AI visibility for structured content

### 3. Target Sub-Query Variants
Content must rank for component queries AI generates, not just full user questions.

### 4. Include Authority Signals
- Expert quotes with name, title, company
- Statistics with named sources
- First-hand case studies
- Clear author credentials

### 5. Maintain Freshness
Content >3 months old sees sharp citation decline. Refresh quarterly.

### 6. Build Off-Site Authority
- Unlinked brand mentions across web
- Get mentioned in already-cited pages
- Reddit, YouTube, forum participation
- Wikipedia entry if eligible

## Platform Specifics

- **ChatGPT**: 70% of AI search, comprehensive well-sourced content
- **Google AI Overviews/Mode**: traditional ranking signals + schema markup
- **Perplexity**: citation-focused, freshness preference, high SaaS conversion
- **Gemini**: fastest-growing, integrates with Google search
- **Claude**: well-structured logical content, Safari integration coming

## Measuring GEO

- **Share of Voice**: most important metric -- brand frequency across AI responses
- **Competitive Rank**: position vs competitors
- **Citation Tracking**: which pages, how often
- **Brand Mention Accuracy**: how AI describes you
- **AI Referral Traffic**: "ChatGPT-User" in logs

Manual testing: 10-20 queries monthly across platforms.

## Common Mistakes

Content: keyword stuffing, thin content, stale info, unsourced claims, dense text
Technical: blocking AI crawlers, JS-only rendering, content behind interactive elements
Strategy: treating GEO/SEO separately, mass AI-generated articles, only optimizing own domain, no AI metrics
