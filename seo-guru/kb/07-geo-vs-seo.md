## Three-Dimensional Comparison

| Dimension | SEO | GEO | AEO |
|-----------|-----|-----|-----|
| **Primary Goal** | Rank in top 10 organic | Get cited in AI responses | Win featured snippets & answer boxes |
| **Target Platforms** | Google, Bing, Yahoo | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Overviews | Google Featured Snippets, voice assistants |
| **Core Metric** | Position, CTR, organic sessions | Mention rate, citation rate | Answer box ownership, position-zero rate |
| **Key Signals** | Backlinks, authority, keyword relevance, Core Web Vitals | Entity clarity, statistical density, source triangulation, freshness | Schema markup, Q&A formatting, structured data |
| **Content Format** | Long-form pages, blog posts | Fact-dense paragraphs with citations | Short direct answers, FAQs, tables |
| **Time to Impact** | 3-6 months | 2-8 weeks | 1-4 weeks |

## Key Statistics

- Gartner forecast: organic search traffic to commercial sites declines 25% by end of 2026
- BrightEdge: AI Overviews now appear on 40%+ of Google results
- Carnegie Mellon (KDD 2024): documents with higher statistical density, clearer entity markup, and corroborating sources are cited significantly more frequently

## The RAG Pipeline

1. **Query Understanding** -- LLM interprets question, identifies entities
2. **Retrieval** -- search returns candidate documents
3. **Source Selection** -- filter by relevance, authority, recency, information density
4. **Response Generation** -- synthesize answer with citations

## GEO-Friendly Content Characteristics

- **Entity clarity**: explicit definitions, not assumed knowledge
- **Quantified claims**: "Reduces costs by 34%" beats "industry-leading"
- **Source triangulation**: third-party corroboration increases AI confidence
- **Freshness signals**: publication dates, update timestamps, current-year references

## AEO vs GEO

| Aspect | AEO | GEO |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Focus | Featured snippets, answer boxes | Multi-paragraph AI responses |
| Mechanism | Schema-driven | Entity clarity & statistical density |
| Ideal length | 40-60 words or bulleted lists | Varies by context |
| Platform scope | Google, Bing (specific) | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini (cross-platform) |

AEO work strengthens GEO outcomes by improving machine-readability for RAG systems.

## When to Use Each

- **SEO**: Revenue depends on transactional queries, clicks to product pages
- **GEO**: Informational queries, AI has high market share in vertical, brand needs AI recommendations
- **Both**: Protecting existing traffic while capturing AI-referred traffic (recommended)
