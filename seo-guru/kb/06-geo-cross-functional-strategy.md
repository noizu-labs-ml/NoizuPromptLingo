## Integrated Approach

GEO requires cross-departmental collaboration, not siloed marketing:

- **SEO Teams** -- technical optimization and keyword relevance
- **Content Marketers** -- comprehensive, authoritative material serving as LLM source
- **PR Professionals** -- placements amplifying reach and authority
- **Social Media Managers** -- engagement signals influencing AI visibility

## Actionable Recommendations

1. Create original research and proprietary insights that LLMs cite
2. Build topical authority across interconnected content pieces
3. Secure media coverage and backlinks from authoritative sources
4. Amplify content through social channels for engagement signals
5. Optimize for both traditional search and LLM training data inclusion

## Key Insight

The more your brand is mentioned across the web, the more AI systems associate your brand with your topic area. Content freshness matters significantly -- content older than 3 months sees fewer AI citations. GEO favors semantic clarity over SEO tricks.
