## SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

- On-page: keyword placement, meta tags, heading structures
- Technical: site speed, mobile responsiveness, structured data
- Off-page: backlinks, domain authority, brand signals
- Content: relevance, comprehensiveness, user intent alignment

## AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Optimization for AI-powered answer engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity):
- Direct answers to user questions
- Cited sources and attribution
- Factual accuracy and expertise signals
- Structured, parseable information

Key strategies:
- Authoritative, comprehensive guides
- Clear formatting and bullet points
- E-E-A-T signals
- Original research and data
- Earning AI citations

## GEO (Geographic Engine Optimization)

Note: This source uses GEO for Geographic/Local optimization, distinct from Generative Engine Optimization:
- Google Business Profile optimization
- Local keyword targeting
- Location-specific landing pages
- NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone)
- Review management
- Local link building

## Comparison

| Aspect | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|--------|-----|-----|-----|
| Primary Goal | Rank in search results | Appear in AI responses | Dominate local searches |
| Audience | General search users | AI platform users | Location-based users |
| Content Focus | Keywords + relevance | Direct answers + sources | Local intent + location |
| Key Metrics | Rankings, traffic, CTR | Citation rate, answer inclusion | Local visibility, foot traffic |

## Why All Three Matter

1. SEO drives baseline organic traffic and domain authority
2. AEO builds credibility with AI platforms and captures citations
3. GEO captures high-intent local searches and foot traffic

All three are complementary, not competing.

## Actionable Recommendations

### SEO
- User intent-focused keyword research
- Core Web Vitals optimization
- Topical authority through content clusters
- High-quality backlinks
- Structured data markup

### AEO
- Detailed, well-sourced Q&A content
- Author bios with credentials
- Original research and unique insights
- Clear formatting with headers and lists
- Monitor AI platform citations

### GEO (Local)
- Google Business Profile optimization
- Location-specific landing pages
- Consistent local citations
- Customer review management
- Hyperlocal content
- Local organization partnerships
