## Key Themes Across Sources

1. **AI SEO is additive to traditional SEO** -- every source agrees, not a replacement
2. **Crawlability is the #1 blocker** -- robots.txt and Cloudflare defaults block AI bots
3. **Schema markup = 28-40% citation lift** -- FAQPage is highest-impact single optimization
4. **Content freshness decays at ~13 weeks** -- quarterly refresh minimum
5. **Answer-first structure** -- first 1-2 sentences must directly answer the heading
6. **Statistics with attribution** -- 40% higher visibility, cite every 150-200 words
7. **Platform preferences differ significantly** -- ChatGPT favors Wikipedia, Perplexity favors Reddit
8. **Share of voice > ranking position** -- AI results are non-deterministic
9. **First 30% of content matters most** -- 44% of LLM citations come from there
10. **Off-site mentions matter as much as on-site** -- brand mentions = backlinks for AI
