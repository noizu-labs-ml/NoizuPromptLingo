Typography

Serif is king. Lora for headings (warm, calligraphic), Source Serif 4 for body text (screen-optimized reading), Inter for UI chrome (never serif for controls), JetBrains Mono for metadata and system data. The serif/sans split visually separates "what you're reading" from "how you navigate."

Consistency Flags

Three severity levels. Red = hard contradiction. Goldenrod = possible conflict. Blue = gap to fill. The only loud colors in the interface — they appear only in the consistency checker.

✕
Hard Contradiction
"Kael Ashward" is described as born in Year 12, but "The Winter Campaign" entry places Kael as a 30-year-old commander in Year 7 — 5 years before his birth.
⚠
Possible Conflict
"Thornwall" entry says the city has "no standing army," but "The Iron Compact" references Thornwall's "garrison forces." These may refer to different periods — review recommended.
+
Gap to Fill
"Kael Ashward" references the "Ashward family forge" in three entries, but no entry exists for this location. Consider generating a location entry.

Entry Detail — Reading Experience

The heart of the product. Entry body text at 18px, 1.7 line height, 65ch max-width, Source Serif 4. Generous margins. Warm cream reading background. This must feel like reading a page from a well-typeset book.

Character · NOIZUAI-4 · KB-0001
Kael Ashward
■ Canon v4 · 1,247 words · edited 3h ago

Kael Ashward was born in the lower district of Thornwall during the third year of the Iron Compact, the only child of journeyman smith Torin and market herbalist Mara. The forge smoke that perpetually hung over the district became the air he breathed.

His apprenticeship under Master Vorn Ashward — a distant uncle — began at age nine, unusually early even by guild standards. By fourteen he had mastered the basics of decorative ironwork. By seventeen, he was forging blades that senior members examined with the quiet envy of men who recognize talent they cannot claim for themselves.

The event that would define his reputation came in the winter of Year 29, when he completed the Ashward Blade — a ceremonial longsword commissioned by the Lord Protector of Thornwall for presentation to the Blacksmith Guild on the occasion of its centenary. The blade was said to hold an edge through three seasons of use without honing.

Implementation Checklist

  • Lora (headings, 600–700) + Source Serif 4 (body, 400) + Inter (UI, 400–600) + JetBrains Mono (metadata, 400)
  • Entry body text: 18px, 1.7 line height, 65ch max-width — the reading experience
  • Warm cream #FAF9F6 background — never pure white
  • Canon entries: solid 3px left border in --canon
  • Generated entries: dashed 3px left border in --generated
  • All entry cross-references hyperlinked in scholarly blue
  • Consistency flags use correct severity color (red / goldenrod / blue)
  • Knowledge graph nodes use serif labels with MT-styled chrome
  • All interactive elements have visible focus rings (--focus-ring)
  • All buttons and navigation use sans-serif — never serif for controls
  • Metadata (tags, dates, IDs) uses JetBrains Mono
  • No box-shadows heavier than 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)
  • Generation typewriter animation respects prefers-reduced-motion
  • Color contrast: all text meets WCAG AA minimum
  • Touch targets: minimum 44px for all interactive elements
  • Light mode only — no theme toggle
