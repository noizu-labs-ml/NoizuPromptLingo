👤 Character ● Canon

Kael Ashford

entry-0047 Created 2024-01-14 Last edited 2024-03-01 1,240 tokens 12 connections

Born in the northern reaches of the Ashward Province, Kael grew up hearing stories of the old wars — tales his father swore were embellished but his grandmother insisted were understatements. The truth, as he would discover, was more complicated than either.

His mother, a cartographer for the Merchant's Guild, spent three months each year mapping the Northern Trade Routes. Kael would accompany her on summer journeys, developing an early familiarity with the roads and ruins that would later define his adult life.

The Treaty of Dusk

Kael was 34 when he witnessed the signing of the Treaty of Dusk at the ruins of Thornwall — an event that ended the Century War and ostensibly brought peace to the four city-states. Whether it succeeded is a question the Chronicles will not answer cleanly.

✦ Generated Pending author review — based on 4 canon entries

Historical records suggest that Kael was present at the founding of Thornwall fifteen years prior to the Treaty, serving under General Vassia during the city's construction. This would make him a witness to both the city's birth and its eventual destruction — a narrative arc that may be worth developing explicitly.

Implementation Checklist

Complete before shipping any page or component.

  • Source Serif 4 (headings + body, 400–700) + DM Sans (UI, 400–600) + JetBrains Mono (data, 400)
  • Warm parchment #F7F3ED background — never cold white, never stark
  • Border radius: 12px on cards, 16px on large cards, pill (9999px) on buttons and badges
  • Canon entries: solid 3px left border in warm brown (#2C2520)
  • Generated entries: dashed 3px left border in amber (#D97706)
  • Entry type chips with type-specific colors and emoji icons
  • Category cards on dashboard with bento layout and spring hover
  • Discovery toast with spring animation for new connections
  • Generation progress uses pulsing violet dot, not a progress bar
  • Graph nodes: subtle shadows, type-colored dots, scale-up on hover
  • Graph edges: rounded line caps (stroke-linecap: round)
  • Warm shadows throughout: rgba(44, 37, 32, …) not rgba(0,0,0,…)
  • All interactive elements have visible focus rings (--focus-ring)
  • Amber accent (#F59E0B) never used for text smaller than 24px — fails contrast
  • prefers-reduced-motion: all animations disabled, generation dot becomes static
  • Color contrast: all text meets WCAG AA minimum
  • Touch targets: minimum 44px height/width on all interactive elements
  • Dark mode available via data-theme="dark" attribute on html element
  • Light mode is the default
