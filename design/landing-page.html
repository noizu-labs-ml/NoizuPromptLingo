QUESTIONS

Answers.

Is this legal?

Yes. Defender runs against endpoints you own and authorize. Catalog techniques are published under responsible disclosure. We follow CVE-equivalent processes with a coordinated disclosure timeline for all novel findings.

Do you publish working jailbreaks?

Detection signatures and mitigations are public. Full reproduction steps require authenticated access with verified security professional credentials. The bar is deliberate: defenders need parity with threat actors.

What models does this cover?

All major commercial LLMs: GPT-4o, Claude 3.5+, Gemini 2.0, Llama 3.3, Mistral, and Mixtral. Plus model-agnostic techniques that apply regardless of architecture. Filter the catalog by what you actually deploy.

How is this different from OWASP LLM Top 10?

OWASP gives you 10 risk categories. We give you 412 specific techniques with reproduction steps, detection signatures, and hands-on labs. It's the difference between knowing SQL injection is a risk and knowing exactly how to find and fix it.

Can I use this for my team?

Team plans include shared dashboards, assigned training paths, and Defender scans for your production endpoints. Enterprise plans add SSO, self-hosted scanning, and dedicated support from a member of our research team.

How often is the catalog updated?

Weekly. New techniques are indexed from research papers, responsible disclosures, and community submissions from verified members. A monthly "State of Jailbreaking" report tracks emerging trends and model-specific vulnerability shifts.
