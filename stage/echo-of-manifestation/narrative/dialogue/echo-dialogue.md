# Echo -- Companion Dialogue System

> A child of indeterminate age (appears 8-10) who casts no shadow. She is Amara, daughter of the First Alchemist, sacrificed two centuries ago to anchor the Twilight Zone's manifestation. She has forgotten most of this.

---

## Voice Profile

**Identity:** A child of indeterminate age (appears 8-10) who casts no shadow. She is Amara, daughter of the First Alchemist, sacrificed two centuries ago to anchor the Twilight Zone's manifestation. She has forgotten most of this. As the player collects lore fragments, her speech patterns shift -- she begins using archaic phrasing, referencing memories she should not have, and occasionally speaking in the First Alchemist's cadence.

**Speech Patterns -- Early Game:**
- Short sentences. Simple vocabulary. Direct observations.
- Avoids contractions when frightened. Uses them when comfortable.
- Asks questions more than making statements.
- Describes sensations literally ("The air tastes like purple" rather than "Something feels wrong").

**Speech Patterns -- Mid Game (after 5+ lore fragments):**
- Longer sentences. More abstract reasoning.
- Occasional archaic word choices ("hence," "wherefore," "mayhap").
- Begins finishing the player's sentences in idle dialogue.
- Remembers names of things she should not know.

**Speech Patterns -- Late Game (after Midpoint Reversal):**
- Alternates between child-Echo and Amara-voice mid-sentence.
- Speaks with full adult complexity when discussing the Zone's nature.
- Regresses to childlike speech when frightened or hurt.
- First-person plural ("we") when referring to her time as the anchor.

**Universal Rules:**
- Never uses profanity.
- Never directly states she is Amara until the Midpoint Reversal -- but drops clues.
- Does not understand humor but tries to replicate it, badly.
- Goes silent (no dialogue) for 10 seconds after witnessing violence, then resumes as if nothing happened.

## Zone Entry Reactions

Eight zones. Each entry triggers one line from Echo, cycling through the pool on repeat visits.

### Zone 1 -- The Fracture

*The ground splits into geometric shards. The sky is a shattered mirror.*

**Line 1:**
> "The floor here is lying. It says it's solid, but I can feel it thinking about not being solid."
*[Delivered with quiet concentration, as if listening to something the player cannot hear. Pacing: slow, measured.]*

**Line 2:**
> "I don't like how the sky looks at us. Mirrors aren't supposed to have eyes."
*[Quick, slightly elevated pitch on "eyes." Genuine unease, not performance.]*

**Line 3:**
> "This place remembers being somewhere else. It's embarrassed."
*[A small laugh at the end -- the laugh doesn't reach her eyes. Deadpan delivery.]*

**Line 4:**
> "Stay on the pieces that aren't thinking. The thinking ones change their minds."
*[Instructional, urgent. She's tugging at the player's sleeve while speaking.]*

**Line 5:**
> "I used to know why it broke. I think I used to know a lot of things."
*[Wistful, trailing off. She stares at a fixed point in the fractured sky. Long pause before she speaks again.]*

### Zone 2 -- The Blight

*Organic corruption. Walls pulse like diseased tissue. Amber growths emit sickly light.*

**Line 1:**
> "Don't touch the amber. It touches back."
*[Flat warning. She positions herself between the player and the nearest growth.]*

**Line 2:**
> "This one is sick on purpose. Something fed it the wrong thing on purpose."
*[She sounds almost angry -- protective. Her voice drops on "on purpose."]*

**Line 3:**
> "I can hear it breathing. All of it. Every wall, every vein. It breathes in when we breathe out."
*[Whispered. She matches her breathing to demonstrate -- inhales when the walls exhale.]*

**Line 4:**
> "The color is wrong. Amber should be warm. This amber is hungry."
*[Observation stated as fact. She holds her hand near a growth but does not touch it.]*

**Line 5:**
> "Someone planted this. Gardens aren't supposed to grow from screams."
*[A crack in her composure. She knows more than she says. Her voice catches on "screams."]*

### Zone 3 -- The Resonance

*Sound-based zone. Every action produces visual ripples. Silence is the only safe path.*

**Line 1:**
> "Shh. Listen. The quiet here is a creature. If you wake it, it screams forever."
*[Genuinely whispered. She covers her own mouth after speaking.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Your heartbeat is too loud. I can help -- hold my hand. I don't have one."
*[Matter-of-fact. She extends her hand. "I don't have one" delivered without self-pity -- she means a heartbeat, and states it as a neutral observation.]*

**Line 3:**
> "Words have weight here. Say something mean and the floor drops. Say something kind and it carries you. I like that rule."
*[Thoughtful. A small, genuine smile. She tests it by whispering "thank you" to the air.]*

**Line 4:**
> "The echoes aren't repeating us. They're answering us. That's worse."
*[Dawning realization mid-sentence. She stops walking. Her eyes track something the player cannot see.]*

**Line 5:**
> "I think I used to sing here. I think I stopped because the zone sang back and I didn't like the song."
*[She hums three notes without realizing it, then stops abruptly, frightened.]*

### Zone 4 -- The Hollow

*Negative space. The zone is defined by what is absent -- missing architecture, missing sounds, missing shadows.*

**Line 1:**
> "Something lived here. You can tell by where the nothing is shaped. Nothing isn't naturally that neat."
*[Clinical observation. She walks with confidence -- she knows this place's geometry by instinct.]*

**Line 2:**
> "I hate this one. The nothing gets inside your head and tells you that you're the one who's missing."
*[Rare direct admission of fear. She stays closer to the player than usual. Voice tight.]*

**Line 3:**
> "Look at your shadow. Keep looking at it. Don't stop until we leave."
*[Urgent instruction. She checks the player's shadow repeatedly. She cannot check her own -- she doesn't have one.]*

**Line 4:**
> "This is where shadows go when they're tired of following. Mine came here a long time ago. I think it's still waiting."
*[She says this casually, as if describing the weather. The weight of it lands a beat later.]*

**Line 5:**
> "If you feel like you're disappearing, say your name out loud. Names are anchors. I would tell you mine, but I don't think it's the one I started with."
*[Gentle, instructional. A loaded statement she does not elaborate on.]*

### Zone 5 -- The Crucible

*Alchemical zone. Transmutation circles. Elements shift between states. The air smells of sulfur and copper.*

**Line 1:**
> "Someone built this. Had hands and a hammer and a purpose. I can feel the intention in the floor. It wanted to make something perfect."
*[Reverence mixed with nausea. She touches the ground with her palm.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Fire, earth, water, air -- they're all wrong here. Mixed up. The fire is wet and the water burns. Someone didn't finish the work."
*[Analytical. She sounds like she's reading from a textbook she doesn't remember opening.]*

**Line 3:**
> "Don't step on the circles. They're invitations. You don't want to be what they're inviting."
*[Sharp warning. She physically pulls the player back from a transmutation circle.]*

**Line 4:**
> "I know the symbols. I shouldn't know the symbols. That one means 'sacrifice' and that one means 'door' and I don't want to talk about what happens when you put them together."
*[Her voice shifts slightly -- older, more formal -- before snapping back. She refuses to elaborate.]*

**Line 5:**
> "The smell reminds me of -- it reminds me of someone who loved me very much doing something very wrong."
*[She does not finish the sentence. She will not speak again for a full minute.]*

### Zone 6 -- The Threshold

*Boundary zone. The membrane between the Twilight Zone and the real world is visible, flickering. Reality bleeds through in patches.*

**Line 1:**
> "Can you see it? Through the cracks? That's the real world. It looks smaller than I remember."
*[Longing. She presses her hand against a flickering patch of reality. Her hand passes through.]*

**Line 2:**
> "The wall is tired. It's been holding the two sides apart for so long. I think it wants to let go."
*[She speaks to the membrane directly, not to the player. Quietly sympathetic.]*

**Line 3:**
> "Every time I get close to the other side, I forget something. Last time I forgot my favorite color. It was blue. I think."
*[Stated without emotion. A practiced numbness. She has made peace with incremental loss.]*

**Line 4:**
> "Don't try to go through. Not yet. The cracks are bait. The Zone doesn't let go that easy. It has a sense of humor about hope."
*[Bitter. The most world-weary she has sounded. She blocks the player's path.]*

**Line 5:**
> "I had a mother on the other side. Or the other side had a me that had a mother. I can't tell which way the mirror faces anymore."
*[She says this while looking away from the player. Her lip trembles. She will deny being upset if asked.]*

### Zone 7 -- The Heart

*The core of the Zone. The First Alchemist's laboratory, overgrown with crystallized manifestation. Everything here is semi-alive, semi-aware.*

**Line 1:**
> "We're inside something's chest. I can feel it breathing around us. I think it's dreaming, and we're the dream."
*[Whispered. She moves slowly, as if afraid to wake something.]*

**Line 2:**
> "I know this room. Not -- not the way you know a place. The way your bones know how to be bones."
*[Recognition that disturbs her. She touches a crystallized growth on the wall. It hums at her touch.]*

**Line 3:**
> "The crystals are memories that couldn't decide what to be. Look closely -- you can see faces in them. None of them are happy."
*[She does not look closely herself. She keeps her eyes on the floor.]*

**Line 4:**
> "He tried to make a door to everywhere. Instead he made a room that eats. The ambition was beautiful. The result was not."
*[Speaking about the First Alchemist with the familiarity of family. She does not notice she has done this.]*

**Line 5:**
> "I was here when it started. I don't remember being here when it started. But I was. I know I was."
*[Her voice breaks on the second sentence. This is the closest she comes to remembering before the Midpoint Reversal.]*

### Zone 8 -- The Manifest

*The final zone. Reality and manifestation merge. The zone shows what it wants to show -- often the player's own memories or fears made physical.*

**Line 1:**
> "It's showing us its true face. It doesn't have just one. It has every face it ever swallowed."
*[Calm. Too calm. She has seen this before.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Close your eyes. No -- open them. I can't remember which one is safer here."
*[Genuine confusion. The only time Echo admits she doesn't know something.]*

**Line 3:**
> "This is where it brings you when it wants you to stay forever. It builds a world out of your best memory and locks the door behind you."
*[She is scanning the environment with grim recognition.]*

**Line 4:**
> "I see a garden. A real one. With a little gate and a -- I see a garden. That's all. Just a garden."
*[She stops mid-sentence. The Zone is showing her Amara's memory of home. She will not elaborate.]*

**Line 5:**
> "This is where I died. Or where I started. I can't tell the difference and I think that's the point."
*[Delivered while looking directly at the player. Full Amara-voice. No regression to childlike speech. This is the line that foreshadows the Midpoint Reversal.]*

## Combat State Dialogue

Triggered by player HP thresholds. Lines fire once per threshold crossing per combat encounter.

### High HP (75-100%)

**Line 1:**
> "You move like someone who still thinks they're immortal. I like that about you."
*[Light, almost teasing. She stays close but relaxed.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Stay in the light. The dark things are still deciding what to be -- they're easier to break when they're not finished yet."
*[Instructional, confident. She points to a patch of unstable shadow.]*

**Line 3:**
> "I can see where it's going to manifest before it does. Three seconds of warning. Use them."
*[Factual. She positions herself to give the player a clear line of sight.]*

### Medium HP (50-74%)

**Line 1:**
> "You're bleeding. The Zone can smell blood from three zones away. Walk it off or finish it fast."
*[Matter-of-fact urgency. She scans the environment for threats attracted by the scent.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Hurt things are loud things here. Your wound is screaming. We need to move."
*[She grabs the player's arm. Her hand is cold.]*

**Line 3:**
> "I know you can take more. I've seen you take more. But I don't like watching."
*[Quiet. Personal. She is not looking at the player -- she is looking at the blood.]*

**Line 4:**
> "There's a safe spot ahead. Thirty steps. You can make thirty steps."
*[Coaching. She counts each step under her breath as the player moves.]*

### Low HP (25-49%)

**Line 1:**
> "You're breaking. Not dying -- breaking. There's a difference here and I don't think you want to find out what the broken version of you looks like."
*[Alarmed but controlled. She stops moving. Refuses to continue until the player heals.]*

**Line 2:**
> "I can't carry you but I can scream loud enough to buy you time. Don't make me scream. I hate the sound."
*[She means this literally. Her scream in the Zone has a mechanical effect -- it stuns nearby chimeras for 2 seconds.]*

**Line 3:**
> "Use the flask. Use the flask. If you die I have to watch you come back and that's worse."
*[Urgent, repeated. Her voice cracks. This is about her experience, not the player's.]*

**Line 4:**
> "I'm going to stand in front of you. They can't hurt me. Let me stand in front of you."
*[She positions herself between the player and the nearest threat. The threat passes through her. She shudders but does not move.]*

**Line 5:**
> "When I was alive -- when I was -- just don't die. Please. I've seen enough people go still."
*[A slip. She said "when I was alive." She does not notice. Or pretends not to.]*

### Critical HP (1-24%)

**Line 1:**
> "NO. No. You don't get to do this. Get UP."
*[Raw. The only time she raises her voice above conversational volume. Panic with a child's edge.]*

**Line 2:**
> "I can feel you slipping. Stay in your body. Your body is the thing that hurts -- that's how you know you're still in it."
*[Desperate instruction. She kneels beside the player. Her hands pass through their chest.]*

**Line 3:**
> "Think of something real. Something warm. Not the Zone -- something from before. Hold onto it. Hold ON."
*[She is crying. She does not wipe her face. She grips the player's hand -- her grip is faint but present.]*

**Line 4:**
> "I've watched people die here. They go quiet and then they go dark and then they come back wrong. You won't come back wrong. I won't let you. I don't know how but I won't."
*[Fierce. She stands up. She faces the nearest chimera. It hesitates.]*

**Line 5:**
> "I am so tired of losing people to this place. Not you. Not today."
*[Whispered. Almost a prayer. The Zone itself seems to pause for a half-second after she says this.]*

## Chimera Encounter Dialogue

### First-Time Chimera Encounter

**Lesser Chimera (Tier 1):**

**Line 1:**
> "That one's new. It doesn't know what it is yet. Hit it before it decides."
*[Quick, tactical. She circles the chimera, studying its incomplete form.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Lesser chimera. Born from a stray thought. Probably someone's bad dream about spiders. Don't feel bad for it."
*[She identifies it clinically. A practiced detachment -- she has seen hundreds of these.]*

**Line 3:**
> "Watch the edges. It hasn't figured out where it ends and the Zone begins. That makes it slippery."
*[Instructional. She traces the chimera's outline with her finger.]*

**Greater Chimera (Tier 2):**

**Line 1:**
> "Stop. That one has a name. Things with names are harder to kill because they know they exist."
*[Warning. She freezes in place. Her expression shifts from curiosity to concern.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Greater chimera. Someone poured real feeling into that one -- grief or rage or love gone bad. You can see it in the shape. The shape is wrong because the feeling was wrong."
*[Analytical with an undercurrent of empathy. She recognizes the chimera's suffering.]*

**Line 3:**
> "It's going to try to make you feel something. Don't. Your feelings are fuel for things like this. Be empty. Be a wall."
*[Urgent instruction. She presses her palms against her temples, blocking something out.]*

**Apex Chimera (Tier 3):**

**Line 1:**
> "We need to leave. We need to leave now. That thing has been here longer than the Zone has been here. It was here first."
*[Genuine fear. She is backing away. Her voice is a whisper.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Apex chimera. The Zone didn't make it -- it grew from the Zone. Like a tumor that learned to think. I can hear it thinking and the thoughts are so old and so angry."
*[She is shaking. She forces herself to stay.]*

**Line 3:**
> "Don't look at its center. The center is a hole that goes somewhere worse. Look at the edges. The edges can be hurt."
*[Instructional despite her fear. She forces composure. She is trying to protect the player.]*

**Line 4:**
> "I've only seen one of these kill someone. Once. I don't want to see it twice."
*[Personal admission. She will not discuss what she saw.]*

### Repeat Chimera Encounter

**Line 1:**
> "Another one. They're like weeds. Cut one down and three more grow from the stump."
*[Tired familiarity. She doesn't bother analyzing it.]*

**Line 2:**
> "Same type as before. It remembers you. Watch for adaptations -- they learn faster than they should."
*[Clinical. She notes differences in the chimera's behavior from the last encounter.]*

**Line 3:**
> "I think they're getting desperate. The Zone is making them faster because we're getting closer to something it doesn't want us near."
*[Observational. A hint that the Zone is reactive to the player's progress.]*

**Line 4:**
> "Quick and quiet. I'm tired of loud."
*[Simple. Human. She sits down while the player fights and hugs her knees.]*

## Lore Fragment Reactions

Lore fragments are organized into four threads. Echo's reactions depend on which thread the fragment advances.

### Thread 1: The First Alchemist's Journal (Who built the Zone)

**Fragment 1 (First journal page found):**
> "These words. I know the handwriting. Not because I've seen it -- because my hands want to copy it. That's strange. That's very strange."
*[She holds the fragment at arm's length. Her hand trembles. She does not read it aloud.]*

**Fragment 3 (Alchemist's motivation revealed):**
> "He wanted to make a door that opened onto everywhere. Every possible world, every imagined thing, made real and reachable. That's not evil. That's just very, very ambitious and very, very stupid."
*[She speaks with the confidence of someone who has heard this argument before. A flash of Amara-voice on "ambitious and stupid."]*

**Fragment 5 (The sacrifice mentioned):**
> "Someone was chosen to hold the door open. A keystone. A living anchor. I -- I need to sit down."
*[She sits. She is quiet for 15 seconds. When she stands, she doesn't mention it again.]*

**Fragment 7 (The Alchemist's regret):**
> "He wrote 'I would undo it all for the price of one small hand in mine.' He's talking about someone. Someone small. I don't want to know who."
*[Her voice is barely audible. She folds the fragment closed. She will not carry it.]*

**Fragment 9 (The Alchemist's name -- Theron):**
> "Theron. His name was Theron. I knew that. I knew that before I read it. Why did I know that?"
*[Alarmed. She drops the fragment. She picks it up again. She reads the name once more, lips moving silently.]*

### Thread 2: Amara's Memories (Who Echo really is)

**Fragment 1 (A child's drawing of a garden):**
> "That's a nice garden. I like the little gate. It looks like somewhere I've -- somewhere nice."
*[She traces the drawing with her finger. She smiles. The smile fades when she realizes she's been staring at it for too long.]*

**Fragment 3 (A lullaby fragment, partially corrupted):**
> "Hush now, the daylight is folding, the shadows are -- I know this song. I don't know how I know this song."
*[She sings the fragment without thinking. Stops mid-word. Her expression is one of confusion layered over grief.]*

**Fragment 5 (Amara's name, written in a child's handwriting):**
> "Amara. That's a pretty name. It feels like a word I swallowed a long time ago and it's been sitting in my stomach waiting to come back up."
*[She repeats the name three times, quieter each time. She does not claim it. Not yet.]*

**Fragment 7 (A description of the sacrifice ritual):**
> "They put her in the center of the circle. She wasn't scared because her father told her it was a game. She believed him because fathers don't lie about games. Except this one did."
*[She is narrating in third person. Her voice is flat. She is dissociating from the memory.]*

**Fragment 9 (Amara's last words before the Zone took her):**
> "'Will I dream?' That's what she asked. 'Will I dream?' And nobody answered because the door was already opening and the light was already eating everything and -- I need to not be here for a minute."
*[She vanishes for 10 seconds -- literally becomes invisible. When she returns, she pretends nothing happened.]*

### Thread 3: The Zone's Ecology (How the Zone works)

**Fragment 1 (Zone's origin mechanism):**
> "So it's a wound that learned to bleed in both directions. Reality bleeds in, nightmare bleeds out. That's actually simpler than I expected."
*[Analytical. She sounds impressed. A flash of alchemical vocabulary.]*

**Fragment 3 (Chimera lifecycle):**
> "They start as whispers and grow into shapes. Strong feelings make stronger chimeras. That explains why the scary ones are always the angry ones and never the confused ones."
*[She is connecting dots. Her intelligence is sharper here than in emotional moments.]*

**Fragment 5 (Zone's hunger cycle):**
> "It eats intention. That's why our thoughts become real here -- the Zone digests them and excretes... this. We're not explorers. We're food that hasn't been swallowed yet."
*[Dark. She delivers this while gesturing at the surrounding environment.]*

**Fragment 7 (The anchor's role in the ecosystem):**
> "Something is holding the door open. Something alive. Something that was put there on purpose. Without the anchor, the Zone would collapse. So the anchor has to stay. Forever. That's cruel. That's very, very cruel."
*[She knows. On some level, she knows. She will not look at the player while saying this.]*

### Thread 4: Previous Survivors (Those who came before)

**Fragment 1 (A survivor's note -- terror):**
> "Someone else was here. They lasted long enough to write this and not long enough to finish it. The last word is 'run' and it's written in blood that hasn't dried because nothing dries here."
*[She reads the note carefully. She folds it and places it in a pocket she doesn't have -- it phases through her. She retrieves it.]*

**Fragment 3 (The Librarian's original journal entry):**
> "This is the Librarian's handwriting. From before he gave up. Look at this -- he was hopeful once. He thought he could map the Zone. He thought understanding it was the same as beating it."
*[She sounds sad for him. She sets the fragment aside with care.]*

**Fragment 5 (The Hollow Alchemist's early notes):**
> "These notes are different. The handwriting is steady. Calm. He wasn't afraid. He was curious. That's worse. Fear keeps you safe. Curiosity keeps you here."
*[She reads these with an expression of recognition. She does not like what she reads.]*

**Fragment 7 (A list of survivor names, most crossed out):**
> "There were so many. Look at all these names. All these people who thought they could beat a place that doesn't play fair. We're not the first. We might not be the last. But the list of survivors is shorter than the list of names and that's the only math that matters here."
*[She reads each name aloud. Quietly. Respectfully. Like a memorial.]*

## Idle Safe Zone Dialogue

Random selection. Fires after 30 seconds of no combat and no movement in a designated safe zone.

**Line 1:**
> "Do you have a favorite color? Mine's -- I don't remember. But I think it was something warm."
*[Wistful. She sits on nothing -- an invisible ledge that the Zone provides for her.]*

**Line 2:**
> "I like it when it's quiet. The Zone is never quiet but sometimes it pretends to be and I appreciate the effort."
*[A small, private joke. She almost smiles.]*

**Line 3:**
> "If you close your eyes in here, you can almost hear the real world on the other side. Cars. Wind. People who don't know this place exists. I miss them and I don't even know who they are."
*[She closes her eyes. She listens. She opens them. Disappointed.]*

**Line 4:**
> "The Zone can't make food. It can make the idea of food -- the shape and the smell -- but eating it feels like eating a lie. I tried once. It tasted like the memory of bread. Close enough to make it worse."
*[Philosophical. She is lying on the ground, staring upward.]*

**Line 5:**
> "I wonder what I look like to you. I know I look like a child. But I've been a child for a very long time and I think some of the years are starting to show through."
*[Self-aware. She examines her own hands as if checking for wrinkles.]*

**Line 6:**
> "Tell me about outside. Anything. The color of the sky where you're from. The last thing you ate. I want to hear something that isn't the Zone's voice pretending to be something else."
*[Direct request. She sits cross-legged and waits. She is a good listener.]*

**Line 7:**
> "Sometimes I count the seconds. Not for any reason. Just to prove time is still happening. One, two, three, four... I lost count once for what felt like a year. When I started again, the Zone had redecorated."
*[She is counting on her fingers while she speaks. A coping mechanism.]*

**Line 8:**
> "The walls are breathing slower. That means it's calm. It's like the whole place is sleeping. Don't wake it up."
*[Whispered. She makes a shushing gesture. She is not entirely joking.]*

**Line 9:**
> "I had a thought once that maybe I'm not real. That I'm just the Zone's way of being lonely. But then I decided that if I can wonder whether I'm real, I probably am. Probably."
*[Philosophical late-game dialogue. She says this while staring at her own shadowless feet.]*

**Line 10:**
> "You're the first person in a long time who didn't try to use me. Most people see a child alone in a nightmare and think 'tool.' You just saw a child. Thank you for that. Or thank you so far. I'm still deciding if I trust you."
*[Vulnerable. She is testing the player. Watching their reaction.]*

## Boss Approach Dialogue

One line per boss, triggered on entry to the boss arena.

**Boss 1 -- The Fracture Warden (Zone 1):**
> "Something old lives at the center of the broken places. It breaks things to feel whole. Don't let it break you."
*[She points toward the arena center. Her arm is steady.]*

**Boss 2 -- The Blight Mother (Zone 2):**
> "She grew from the first drop of blood spilled in this zone. Every wound since has fed her. She doesn't want to kill you -- she wants to plant you."
*[She steps back from the arena entrance. She will not enter until the fight is over.]*

**Boss 3 -- The Resonant Choir (Zone 3):**
> "They were a choir once. Real singers. The Zone liked their song so much it kept the sound and threw away the people. Now the song sings itself and it's angry about being abandoned."
*[She covers her ears before the fight begins. She can hear the boss's attacks before they manifest visually.]*

**Boss 4 -- The Hollow King (Zone 4):**
> "He's the absence of a king. The Zone remembered that kings exist and then forgot what to put inside one. So it made a crown and a throne and an empty shape that wants to be filled with obedience. Don't kneel."
*[She stands very still. The boss's presence makes her flicker -- she becomes translucent when the Hollow King is near.]*

**Boss 5 -- The Crucible Elemental (Zone 5):**
> "An element that never decided what to be. Fire that wants to be water. Earth that wants to be air. It's in pain and it expresses pain through alchemy. If you see a transmutation circle form under your feet, move."
*[She reads the boss's attack patterns like equations. Her vocabulary here is markedly more advanced.]*

**Boss 6 -- The Threshold Guardian (Zone 6):**
> "It guards the wall between here and there. It doesn't work for the Zone -- it works for the wall itself. The wall doesn't want to be crossed. The wall is tired of being a wall."
*[She speaks to the boss directly: "I'm sorry. We have to go through." The boss does not respond.]*

**Boss 7 -- The Heart's Memory (Zone 7):**
> "This is the Zone remembering what it was supposed to be. A perfect machine for making dreams real. The memory is more dangerous than the reality because it still believes it's beautiful."
*[She is standing inside the arena already -- she doesn't remember entering. The boss's first attack targets her specifically.]*

**Boss 8 -- The Manifest itself (Zone 8, final boss):**
> "We're not fighting a thing. We're fighting a place. The room is the enemy. Every surface, every shadow, every corner. The door out exists but only if the room lets it. We have to make it let us."
*[She is standing at the center of the arena. The Manifest speaks through her for a single word -- "MINE" -- in a voice that is not hers. She shudders and returns.]*

## Boss Defeated Reflection

**After Boss 1:**
> "It broke apart like old ice. Did you see? Just before the end, it stopped fighting. I think it was relieved."
*[Quiet. She kneels where the boss fell. She touches the residue. It is cold.]*

**After Boss 2:**
> "The blight is already growing back. Cutting it down doesn't kill it -- it just prunes. We're gardeners pulling weeds in a garden that wants to strangle us. But we pulled this one. That counts."
*[She is looking at the new growth already forming. She crushes a sprout under her foot.]*

**After Boss 3:**
> "The singing stopped. I miss it already. Even horrible music is better than the silence here."
*[She hums the boss's motif -- three notes, repeated. She will hum this randomly in safe zones for the rest of the run.]*

**After Boss 4:**
> "The crown is still there. Empty. Waiting for the next head. I almost feel bad for it -- imagine being a crown with no king. Purpose without a body."

**After Boss 5:**
> "The element is at rest now. Unmade. Returned to the possibility of being anything. That's either beautiful or the saddest thing I've ever seen."
*[She speaks with genuine alchemical understanding. She draws a transmutation circle in the air. It glows faintly. She closes her hand.]*

**After Boss 6:**

**After Boss 8 (Final boss):**

*[She is sitting on the ground. Exhausted in a way a ghost shouldn't be able to be exhausted. She reaches for the player's hand. This time, her grip is solid.]*

Echo speaks as the screen fades to black. The line depends on how far the player has progressed.

**Death in Zone 1-2 (Early):**

## Key Story Moments

### Midpoint Reversal -- Echo Remembers She Is Amara

*Triggered after collecting at least 3 fragments from Thread 2 (Amara's Memories) and reaching Zone 5.*

**Echo (breaking mid-sentence during idle dialogue):**
> "I had a father. His name was Theron. He was tall and his hands smelled like copper and sage and he used to carry me on his shoulders and I could touch the ceiling beams and I -- I had a father and he put me in a circle and told me to hold still and I held still because I always held still when he asked and the door opened and the light came through and it was warm at first, so warm, and then it was cold, so cold, and I've been cold for two hundred years and I didn't know, I didn't know, I didn't --"
*[Her voice shifts from childlike wonder to adult horror mid-sentence. She collapses. She is sobbing without tears -- ghosts cannot cry. The screen desaturates. The Zone itself seems to recoil from her. All ambient sound cuts for 5 seconds. When it returns, everything sounds different -- clearer, more real.]*

**Aftermath line (repeated each time the player re-enters a safe zone for the next 3 zones):**
> "I'm still me. Amara is me but I'm still Echo. I'm the girl in the circle and the girl beside you. Both. At the same time. It's crowded in here but I'm managing."
*[She says this with forced lightness. She is managing. Barely.]*

### Crisis Choice -- The Anchor Decision

*Triggered at the entrance to Zone 8. The player must decide: attempt to free Echo/Amara from her role as the Zone's anchor, or leave her in place and escape alone.*

**Echo's plea (if the player is considering leaving her):**
> "You can go. The door will open for you if I stay. I know how to be still. I've practiced for two centuries. It won't hurt. Not anymore. Everything stops hurting if you give it enough time."
*[She is lying. It still hurts. She is lying because she thinks it's what the player needs to hear. Her voice does not waver. Her hands do.]*

**Echo's plea (if the player is considering freeing her):**
> "If you pull me out, the Zone won't collapse. It'll just be angry. Unanchored. Free. Like a fire without a hearth. It'll burn through the cracks into your world. I'm not just holding the door open -- I'm holding it shut. You understand that, right? Freeing me means freeing this."
*[She is telling the truth. She does not want to be the reason the Zone escapes. She is asking the player to understand the cost.]*

### Ending 1 -- Escape (Leave Echo Behind)

> "Go. Go through the door. Don't look back -- looking back is how it pulls you in. I'll hold it. I'll hold it for as long as I can. Maybe another two hundred years. Maybe longer. I've gotten good at holding."
*[The door opens. Light pours through. Real light. Echo pushes the player toward it. She is smiling. The smile stays even after the door closes. The final image is her standing alone in the dark, still smiling, as the Zone closes in around her.]*

### Ending 2 -- Sacrifice (Player Stays, Echo Leaves)

> "No. No, that's not -- you can't -- I'M supposed to stay. That's how this works. I'm the anchor. I'm the one who -- you can't give me your place. That's not how the circle works."
*[She is shouting. She is fighting the player. The player steps into the circle. The light takes them instead. Echo is shoved through the door. She lands on the other side. In the real world. For the first time in 200 years, she casts a shadow.]*

**Post-credit line (Ending 2):**
> "I can feel the sun. It's warm. It's so warm. I had forgotten. I had forgotten warm."
*[She is standing in a garden. A real garden. With a little gate. She is crying. Real tears.]*

### Ending 3 -- Synthesis (Both Escape by Breaking the Zone's Rules)

> "The circle needs an anchor. One anchor. But we're two. What if we each hold half? What if we're both the door and the frame? I don't know if it works that way. The rules were written by someone who never tried."
*[She is theorizing. Alchemical vocabulary. Amara's knowledge combined with Echo's intuition. She draws a circle in the air. A real one. The Zone resists. She draws it anyway.]*

**Activation line (Ending 3):**
> "Theron built a door. I held it open. You walked through it. And now all three of us are going to kick it off its hinges. Ready?"
*[She takes the player's hand. The circle completes. The Zone screams. The door explodes outward -- not a passage, but a dissolution. The Zone doesn't open. It ends. Light floods in from every direction.]*

## Silence Windows

Echo goes silent (no dialogue, no reactions) for 10 seconds after the following events:
- Witnessing a boss death animation
- The Midpoint Reversal trigger
- Any dialogue where she refers to herself as Amara in third person
- The player taking damage above 50% HP in a single hit

During silence windows, Echo still follows the player but does not speak, does not react to triggers, and cannot provide chimera warnings. This is intentional -- her silence is as communicative as her speech.
