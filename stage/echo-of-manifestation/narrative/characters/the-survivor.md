# Echo of Manifestation — The Survivor (Player Character)

**Role:** Protagonist — Alchemist trapped in the Twilight Zone
**Theme:** Duty vs desire; the rescuer who needs rescuing
**Lore Fragments:** N/A (player character)

The Survivor is an itinerant transmuter scraping by in a post-collapse society. Known only by their title, they collect essence from the Twilight Zone's boundary to sell on the black market. During a routine collection run, they witness a child fall through the thinning boundary and dive in after her without thinking. The boundary seals behind them.

The Survivor is not a hero. They are a pragmatist who made an impulsive choice and now must live with the consequences. Their alchemical abilities are the only tool they have, but every transmutation inside the Zone feeds it, spawns chimeras, and makes the Zone larger. The central tension: the power that keeps them alive is the power making everything worse.
