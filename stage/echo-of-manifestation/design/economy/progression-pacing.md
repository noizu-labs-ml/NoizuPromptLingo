## Milestone Summary

### Level 10 — First Footing (1,875 XP)
- Starting Essence: 25 essence per run
- Divination Tier 2

The player has their first meaningful loadout advantage and can see slightly more through the Divination system.

### Level 20 — Tunnel Sense (6,250 XP)
- Pocket Sense (TDP glow brighter)
- Divination Tier 3
- Starting Essence active

Exploration feels safer. Twisted Dimensional Pockets are easier to spot, reducing blind deaths.

### Level 30 — Alchemist's Hand (13,125 XP)
- Recipes: Flash Bomb, Essence Wick
- Divination Tier 4 available soon
- Lore Gate: Threshold Memories open

Transmutation opens up as a real strategic option. Lore starts bleeding in.

### Level 40 — Armed Entry (22,500 XP)
- Starting Weapon: Iron Dagger
- Dodge Window extended by 1 frame
- Movement Speed +3%
- Recipes: Binding Salve, Echo Lure

The run no longer starts naked. A baseline combat toolkit is available from room 1.

### Level 50 — Cartographer's Dawn (34,375 XP)
- Recipe: Shadow Bait
- Zone Map: Surface Ruins (full map for Layer 1)
- Lore Gate: Manifestation Echoes
- Chimera Weakness Highlight (partial)

First full zone map unlocks. The Surface Ruins can now be navigated with confidence. Bosses show faint weakness cues.

### Level 60 — The Third Eye (48,750 XP)
- Divination Tier 5 (maximum)
- Recipes: Resonance Oil, Warding Incense
- Movement Speed +6% (cumulative)

Maximum Divination reached. The information game is fully online. All hidden rooms and traps are revealed.

### Level 70 — Deep Cartography (65,625 XP)
- Zone Map: Sunken Archive
- Dodge Window +2 frames (cumulative)
- Recipes: Echo Lure, Spectral Balm

Second zone map unlocked. The midgame zones are now navigable without brute-force mapping.

### Level 80 — The Archive Burns (85,000 XP)
- Zone Map: Flesh Gardens
- Lore Gate: Architect's Confession
- Recipe: Void Tonic

Deep lore unlocked. The player understands what the Manifestation was and why the world collapsed.

### Level 90 — Resonant Soul (106,875 XP)
- Resonance Dampener (reduces manifestation pull)
- Starting Essence+ (50 essence per run, replaces 25)
- Recipe: Purification Flare
- Loadout Slot 2

A fully kitted start. The dampener makes the endgame survivable. Essence economy is comfortable.

### Level 99 — Echo Complete (128,700 XP)
- Loadout Slot 3
- Zone Maps: Choir of Glass, Throne of Echoes
- Lore Gate: The Last Manifestation
- All recipes, all zone maps, full combat bonuses

Full meta-progression complete. The player enters every run with maximum loadout flexibility and complete knowledge of the world.

## XP Sources Reminder

| Action | Insight XP | Notes |
|--------|-----------:|-------|
| Kill a creature | 1-3 | Varies by creature tier |
| Discover a new room | 5 | First discovery per run only |
| Complete a transmutation | 3 | Any recipe |
| Kill a zone boss | 15-25 | Varies by zone |
| Lore fragment collected | 8 | First pickup only |
| Survive a Twisted Dimensional Pocket | 10 | Per pocket cleared |
| Complete a full run (death or exit) | 20 | Base bonus |
| Complete a zone for the first time ever | 50 | One-time per zone |
| Discover a secret area | 12 | Per discovery |
| Clear a zone without taking damage | 30 | Per zone per run |

**Average run yield:** ~80-120 Insight XP for a mid-skill player reaching Layer 2. Expert players clearing deep runs can earn 200-300 Insight per run.

## Time to Level Estimates

Estimates based on an average of 100 Insight XP per run, with each run taking roughly 25-40 minutes.

| Milestone | Cumulative XP | Runs Needed | Approx. Playtime |
|-----------|--------------:|------------:|-----------------:|
| Level 5 (Divination Tier 2) | 625 | ~6 | 2-4 hours |
| Level 10 (Starting Essence) | 1,875 | ~19 | 8-13 hours |
| Level 20 (Pocket Sense) | 6,250 | ~63 | 26-42 hours |
| Level 30 (Flash Bomb) | 13,125 | ~131 | 55-87 hours |
| Level 40 (Iron Dagger) | 22,500 | ~225 | 94-150 hours |
| Level 50 (Shadow Bait) | 34,375 | ~344 | 143-230 hours |
| Level 60 (Divination Tier 5) | 48,750 | ~488 | 203-325 hours |
| Level 75 (Loadout Slot 2) | 75,000 | ~750 | 313-500 hours |
| Level 90 (Starting Essence+) | 106,875 | ~1,069 | 445-713 hours |
| Level 99 (Loadout Slot 3) | 128,700 | ~1,287 | 536-857 hours |

**Design note:** The curve is intentionally long. A dedicated player who improves run yield over time (earning 150-200 XP per run by mid-progression) will compress the later milestones significantly. The early tree (levels 1-30) is designed to feel generous and reward experimentation. The mid tree (30-60) is the meat of progression. The late tree (60-99) is for mastery players who want a long-term goal.
