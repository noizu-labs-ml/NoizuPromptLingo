## Runs Needed to Reach Key Insight Milestones

Using the formula from the Insight Tree: XP per level = 50 + (N * 25), cumulative XP at level N = sum from 1 to N.

| Milestone | Level | Cumulative XP Required | Runs from Zero (at ~1,182 XP/run) | Runs from Previous Milestone | Approx. Playtime |
|-----------|-------|----------------------|-----------------------------------|------------------------------|-----------------|
| First Footing | 10 | 1,875 | ~2 (with first-run bonus) | 2 | 1-1.5 hours |
| Tunnel Sense | 20 | 6,250 | ~5 | 3 | 3-5 hours |
| Alchemist's Hand | 30 | 13,125 | ~11 | 6 | 5-9 hours |
| Armed Entry | 40 | 22,500 | ~19 | 8 | 9-16 hours |
| Cartographer's Dawn | 50 | 34,375 | ~29 | 10 | 14-24 hours |
| The Third Eye | 60 | 48,750 | ~41 | 12 | 20-34 hours |
| Deep Cartography | 70 | 65,625 | ~56 | 15 | 28-47 hours |
| The Archive Burns | 80 | 85,000 | ~72 | 16 | 36-60 hours |
| Resonant Soul | 90 | 106,875 | ~90 | 18 | 45-75 hours |
| Echo Complete | 99 | 128,700 | ~109 | 19 | 55-91 hours |

**Notes on estimation:**
- Early runs yield less XP (fewer rooms discovered, fewer lore fragments, lower tier kills). First few runs average ~80-120 XP.
- Mid-progression runs (Insight 30-60) average ~150-200 XP as the player explores more thoroughly and survives deeper.
- Late-progression runs (Insight 60+) average ~200-300 XP from experienced play, secret area discovery, and damageless zone clears.
- The "Runs from Zero" column uses a blended average of 1,182 XP/run, which is optimistic for early runs but conservative for late runs.
- Actual playtime varies significantly based on skill. The ranges above assume 25-40 minutes per run.
