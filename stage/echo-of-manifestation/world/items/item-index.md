> Every act of creation is also an act of summoning.

This catalog contains all 38 transmutation recipes available to the Survivor across 8 item categories and 6 power tiers. Each recipe is presented with full item statistics, the chimera echo it spawns, and the lore thread it carries.

**Distribution:** Melee (5) | Ranged (5) | Barrier (5) | Trap (5) | Healing (5) | Utility (5) | Explosive (4) | Shield (4)

| Tier | Essence Cost | Power Level | Unlock Window |
|------|-------------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | 10-20 | Base | Starting / Insight 0-9 |
| 2 | 25-40 | Improved | Insight 10-25 / Zone 1-2 |
| 3 | 45-65 | Strong | Insight 30-45 / Zone 3-4 |
| 4 | 70-90 | Powerful | Insight 50-65 / Zone 5-6 |
| 5 | 95-120 | Very Powerful | Insight 70-85 / Zone 7 |
| 6 | 125-160 | Ultimate | Insight 90+ / Zone 8 |
