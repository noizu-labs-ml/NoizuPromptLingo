## Design Validation Checklist

Every major feature should be validated against all five journeys:

- [ ] Does this feature serve at least 2 of the 5 personas?
- [ ] Does this feature actively harm or annoy any persona?
- [ ] Is the feature accessible within the persona's typical session length?
- [ ] Does the feature respect the persona's motivation (not forcing them into unwanted gameplay)?
- [ ] Does the feature have a natural monetization touchpoint that doesn't feel coercive?
