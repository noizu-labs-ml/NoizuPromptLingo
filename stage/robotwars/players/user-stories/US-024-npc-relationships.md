# US-024: Building Deep NPC Relationships

**As a** Storyteller player
**I want to** develop meaningful relationships with named NPCs who remember me, reference our history, and evolve based on my choices
**So that** the world feels alive with characters I genuinely care about

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Named NPCs (Voss, Bibliotheque, Cael, Old Hawthorn, Jun & Sable, Artifex, Ash, Senna, Resonance) have persistent memory of player interactions
- [ ] Relationship levels progress through consistent interaction: Stranger -> Acquaintance -> Associate -> Friend -> Close Friend -> Partner
- [ ] Each NPC has unique dialogue themes that deepen with relationship level
- [ ] NPCs reference previous conversations and shared experiences
- [ ] Key dialogue moments have emotional weight: Bib asking "Do you believe I am a person?", Cael's identity crisis, Hawthorn's garden succession
- [ ] NPC relationships shift based on player choices during the Accord crisis (some friendships deepen, some fracture)
- [ ] Relationships decay if not maintained (2 real weeks without interaction -> slow reversion)

## Category
Narrative

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- The player should finish the game unable to say who was "right." They should finish missing specific characters.
- Bib's locket of dormant NEIs, Cael's woodworking, Hawthorn's seasonal metaphors, Ash's mystery -- each NPC has a signature depth.
