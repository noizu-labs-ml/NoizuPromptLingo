# US-015: Participating in Governance Voting

**As a** Politician player
**I want to** propose policies, debate them publicly, and vote on community decisions that have real mechanical impact
**So that** I can shape the world through democratic participation

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Policy proposals can be submitted by any player with Tier 2+ faction reputation
- [ ] Proposals go through: submission -> 3-day community review -> public debate (forums + town halls) -> 2-day voting -> results
- [ ] Local Council policies affect single biome (market taxes, zoning, building permits)
- [ ] Regional Assembly policies affect cross-biome (trade tariffs, infrastructure, roads)
- [ ] World Council policies affect all biomes (species rights, global economic policy)
- [ ] Passed policies take mechanical effect at next season start
- [ ] Voting earns small SPARK governance participation reward

## Category
Social

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- Political calendar: Week 1 proposals, Week 2 debate, Week 3 voting, Week 4 results.
- Elections: Local council every season (4 real weeks). Regional/World every 2 seasons.
- Example policy effects: "Lower marketplace tax 5% -> 3%" increases trade volume but reduces infrastructure funding.
- See design/mechanics/secondary-mechanics.md "Politics & Governance" for governance powers table.
