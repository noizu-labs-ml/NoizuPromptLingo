# US-005: First Craft and Marketplace Sale

**As a** new player
**I want to** craft my first item from gathered materials and sell it on the marketplace for Credits
**So that** I experience the complete gather-craft-sell loop and understand the economy's basics

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] At least 3 Apprentice recipes are available immediately (e.g., Iron Hoe, Basic Bread, Healing Tonic)
- [ ] Crafting interface clearly shows material requirements, quality prediction, and estimated sale price
- [ ] First marketplace listing is fee-free (waived listing fee for tutorial)
- [ ] Market Commons or local market stall is within 2 minutes walking distance from starter plots
- [ ] Selling completes with a visible Credit increase and a brief explanation of market dynamics
- [ ] Player receives a prompt about SPARK tokens and how Credits differ from SPARK

## Category
Core Gameplay

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- This is the "aha moment" for the economy. The player must feel that making and selling things is satisfying and profitable.
- Quality determination formula: (Material Quality x 0.4) + (Tool Quality x 0.2) + (Crafting Skill x 0.3) + (Random x 0.1).
- See design/mechanics/secondary-mechanics.md "Crafting System" for full details.
