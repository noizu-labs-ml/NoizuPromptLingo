# US-033: Cooperative Frontier Expeditions

**As an** Explorer player
**I want to** form expeditionary parties with mixed-species groups to explore the Frontier together
**So that** exploring dangerous territory feels like a cooperative adventure and builds cross-species bonds

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Party formation supports up to 4 members (humans and/or agents)
- [ ] Party leader sets: target biome, expected difficulty, supply requirements
- [ ] In-group role selection: scout, gatherer, crafter, defender
- [ ] Agents communicate intent naturally: "I will scout the ridge ahead," "That weather looks dangerous, recommending we shelter"
- [ ] Resource distribution in party follows personality-based rules (generous agents share, thrifty agents claim best finds)
- [ ] Successful co-op expeditions provide relationship bonuses (+3 to +8 per expedition)
- [ ] Group discovery bonuses: all members share first-discoverer credits

## Category
Social

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- Group dynamics are emergent from agent personality: boldness, generosity, curiosity, loyalty, industriousness.
- Agents evaluate party invitations based on: relationship with leader, difficulty vs capability, group composition, potential reward.
- See platform/AGENT-SYSTEM.md "Group Dynamics" for full in-group behavior rules.
