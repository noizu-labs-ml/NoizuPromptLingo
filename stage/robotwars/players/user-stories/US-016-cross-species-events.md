# US-016: Cross-Species Diplomatic Events

**As a** socially-oriented player
**I want to** attend and participate in cross-species diplomatic events (summits, festivals, trade fairs)
**So that** I can build inter-species relationships through structured opportunities and experience the game's political depth

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Each season features at least one major community event: Spring Renewal Festival, Summer Grand Market Fair, Autumn Harvest Dance, Winter Hearth Gathering
- [ ] Events provide reputation bonuses across all participating species
- [ ] Event-specific activities: planting ceremony, marketplace competition, cooking tournament, storytelling contest
- [ ] Mid-season events occur bi-weekly: faction meetings, market days, random world events
- [ ] Participation earns faction reputation, seasonal recipes, and festival-exclusive cosmetics
- [ ] Events are visible on an in-game calendar accessible from the homestead

## Category
Social

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- Seasonal festivals are the meta-loop's anchor points. They bring the community together.
- The Autumn Harvest Dance includes governance elections -- politics and festivity intertwine.
- See design/meta-loop.md "Seasonal Festivals & Community Events" for the full event calendar.
