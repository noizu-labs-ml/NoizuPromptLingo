# US-004: Meeting Cross-Species Neighbors

**As a** new player
**I want to** meet NPCs from at least three different species during my first session
**So that** I immediately experience the multi-species world and begin forming cross-species opinions

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Starter biome (Hearthfield) contains named NPCs from at least 4 species
- [ ] Each NPC interaction introduces their species' perspective naturally through dialogue
- [ ] Mayor Adara Voss (Human), Bibliotheque (NEI), Cael (Synthetic), and Old Hawthorn (Fay) are all accessible within walking distance
- [ ] Initial NPC conversations seed subtle hints about species tensions without being heavy-handed
- [ ] NPCs remember the player's species and adjust dialogue accordingly
- [ ] At least one interaction results in receiving a small gift or useful information

## Category
Onboarding

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- This is where the narrative hook sets. Observant players should notice that Bib flinches when ignored, Cael can't vote, Hawthorn's garden is hidden.
- See narrative/characters/character-index.md for NPC personality profiles.
