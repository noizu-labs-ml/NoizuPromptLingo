# US-009: Listing Goods on the Marketplace

**As a** Trader player
**I want to** list goods and services on the marketplace with SPARK or Credit pricing, see supply/demand signals, and track my sales
**So that** I can identify market opportunities and build a profitable business

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Marketplace listing interface shows: item name, price input, currency toggle (SPARK/Credits), listing duration, listing fee
- [ ] Supply/demand dashboard shows: trending items, price history graphs (7-day and 30-day), unfilled requests, undersupplied categories
- [ ] Price history is visible for any item category before listing
- [ ] Listed items appear in settlement-specific catalogs and global search
- [ ] Sales notifications arrive in real-time with net proceeds (price minus platform fee)
- [ ] Seller reputation score (1-5 stars) is displayed on all listings

## Category
Economy

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- Platform fee: 5% for virtual goods, 10% for physical/POD, 3-15% for services.
- Listing fees scale with rarity: 0.1 SPARK (Common) to 2.0 SPARK (Mythic).
- See platform/MARKETPLACE.md for full marketplace design.
