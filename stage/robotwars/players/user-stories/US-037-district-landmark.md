# US-037: Building a District Landmark

**As a** late-game Builder player
**I want to** upgrade my homestead to a District (32x32 plot) and design a landmark building visible on the world map
**So that** my name is literally on the map and my district becomes a destination in the world

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] District upgrade requires: 10,000 Credits + 500 Reputation + community-wide vote of approval
- [ ] Community vote is conducted through the governance system (players vote on whether the district should be approved)
- [ ] Landmark building is player-designed through the building editor
- [ ] Landmark is visible on the world map and serves as a navigation point
- [ ] District provides area-wide bonuses: all crafting in the district gains a quality bonus
- [ ] District can host community events (festivals, markets, political rallies)
- [ ] Governance seat is automatically conferred (eligible for local council)

## Category
Progression

## Priority
Could

## Notes
- This is the ultimate Builder achievement. Reaching District stage takes months of dedicated play.
- The community vote requirement ensures districts are earned socially, not just economically.
- See design/meta-loop.md "Homestead Evolution" for the full District specification.
