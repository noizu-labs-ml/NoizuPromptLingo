# US-025: Discovering World Lore

**As an** Explorer or Storyteller player
**I want to** find lore fragments during exploration that reveal the history of species interactions, the Convergence, and ancient pacts
**So that** the world feels historically grounded and exploration rewards curiosity with narrative depth

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Lore fragments are discoverable in all biomes (concentrated in deeper biomes)
- [ ] Bibliotheque's "Librarian's Index" quest chain: collect and return 8 lore fragments from exploration
- [ ] Lore fragments reveal: pre-Convergence history, first contact between species, early conflicts, peace treaties, the Meridian Accord's origins
- [ ] Collected lore is stored in a personal journal accessible from the homestead
- [ ] Returning lore to Bibliotheque earns reputation, Credits, and unlocks deeper dialogue chains
- [ ] Lore completion percentage is tracked as a collectible achievement

## Category
Narrative

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- Lore is never mandatory but deeply enriches the political narrative for engaged players.
- The Fay remember when the meadow was a forest. The NEIs emerged from the Convergence. Each lore fragment adds a puzzle piece.
- Captain Senna's expedition journals (findable during exploration) are "surprisingly lyrical."
