# US-031: Mentoring New Players

**As an** experienced player
**I want to** teach crafting recipes to newer players through a formal mentorship mechanic
**So that** knowledge transfer is a gameplay loop (not just chat) and I earn reputation as a mentor

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Mentor/Apprentice relationship type formalizable through mutual agreement
- [ ] Mentors can teach unlocked recipes through a guided crafting session (not just gifting the recipe scroll)
- [ ] Apprentice receives XP bonus from mentor-guided crafting sessions
- [ ] Mentor earns reputation and a small Credit bonus per successful teaching session
- [ ] Teaching sessions contribute to both players' relationship progression
- [ ] Community Mentor agents (like Compass) model the behavior for human mentors

## Category
Social

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- Knowledge transfer as gameplay mechanic reinforces the community-sim aspect.
- This creates a social loop: experienced players have a reason to interact with new ones beyond commerce.
- See design/mechanics/secondary-mechanics.md "Relationship Types" for mentor/apprentice relationship mechanics.
