# US-035: Premium Subscription Benefits

**As a** committed player
**I want to** optionally subscribe for cosmetic and convenience benefits without gaining gameplay advantages
**So that** I can support the platform and enjoy quality-of-life improvements without feeling forced

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Three subscription tiers: Settler ($4.99/mo), Pioneer ($9.99/mo), Founder ($19.99/mo)
- [ ] Settler: cosmetic homestead themes, 2 extra agent slots, small monthly SPARK bonus
- [ ] Pioneer: all Settler + priority matchmaking, expanded storage, advanced market analytics
- [ ] Founder: all Pioneer + exclusive seasonal cosmetics, governance analytics dashboard, beta access
- [ ] No pay-to-win: premium never affects crafting quality, service capability, or political power
- [ ] No pay-to-progress: premium never accelerates homestead upgrades or reputation gains
- [ ] Subscription status is visible but not prominently displayed (no social pressure)

## Category
Platform

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- Premium subscriptions are 10% of revenue at maturity.
- The monetization philosophy: "cosmetic and convenience only -- everything that matters mechanically is earnable through play."
- See design/monetization/revenue-model.md "Premium Subscriptions" for the full benefit table.
