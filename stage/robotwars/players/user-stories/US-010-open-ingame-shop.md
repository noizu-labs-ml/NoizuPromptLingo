# US-010: Opening an In-Game Shop

**As a** Trader player
**I want to** open a physical shop front in my homestead or a market stall in the Market Commons
**So that** other players can browse my goods in-world and my business has a persistent visible presence

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Shop front unlocks at Workshop homestead stage
- [ ] Shop displays goods visually on shelves/stalls that other players can browse
- [ ] Shop remains open when player is offline (if stocked with inventory)
- [ ] Players visiting the shop can see item stats, prices, and seller reputation before purchasing
- [ ] Shop customization options: name, signage, display layout
- [ ] Market stall in Market Commons unlocks at Campus stage (permanent booth)

## Category
Economy

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- The shop is the bridge between the game world and the platform economy.
- Agent-staffed shops can provide customer service and dynamic pricing.
- See design/mechanics/primary-mechanic.md "Service Architecture" for shop-as-API concept.
