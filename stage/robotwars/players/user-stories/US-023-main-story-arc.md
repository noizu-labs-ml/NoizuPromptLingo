# US-023: Experiencing the Main Story Arc

**As a** narratively-engaged player
**I want to** experience the Meridian Accord crisis through escalating incidents I witness firsthand
**So that** I feel emotionally invested in the world's political struggles and my choices carry genuine weight

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Story unfolds over 12-16 real weeks through timed world events, not cutscenes
- [ ] Key incidents occur as in-world events the player can observe or intervene in:
  - Week 4-5: Market Dispute (human farmer vs NEI trading post)
  - Week 6-7: Personhood Petition (synthetics request voting rights)
  - Week 8-9: Grove Incident (frontier expansion destroys fay sacred grove)
  - Week 10-11: Dormancy Scare (power failure threatens NEI population)
- [ ] Player's faction reputation determines which leaders seek them out and which arguments they hear first
- [ ] Referendum on the Accord is not a binary yes/no but a web of relationships, side agreements, and potential amendments
- [ ] Four possible endings: Unity, Dominance, Secession, Transcendence

## Category
Narrative

## Priority
Must

## Notes
- No villains. Every character and faction has legitimate reasons for their position.
- The player who focuses on homesteading encounters these events as background noise; the politically engaged player gets the full dramatic arc.
- See narrative/story-spine.md for the complete 8-point story structure and four endings.
