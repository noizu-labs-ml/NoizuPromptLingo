# US-022: Technology Tree and Recipe Unlocks

**As a** Builder player
**I want to** progress through a branching technology tree that unlocks new recipes, blueprints, and service capabilities
**So that** I always have a clear next goal and my crafting abilities grow meaningfully over time

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Tech tree is visualized as a branching diagram: Basic Crafting -> Specialization -> Master -> Legendary
- [ ] Each node shows: unlock requirements, recipe preview, prerequisite paths
- [ ] Multiple unlock methods are tracked: crafting XP (40%), faction reputation (25%), exploration (20%), research (10%), trade (5%)
- [ ] Research mechanic: spend 3 real-world days + materials to experiment with combinations
- [ ] Legendary recipes require mastery of multiple specialization paths
- [ ] New unlocks trigger celebration moments and are visible to other players visiting the workshop

## Category
Progression

## Priority
Should

## Notes
- The tech tree replaces traditional level-based progression with skill-based mastery.
- Research is time-gated to prevent rushing: it rewards patience and planning.
- See design/meta-loop.md "Technology Tree" for the full path diagram and unlock percentages.
