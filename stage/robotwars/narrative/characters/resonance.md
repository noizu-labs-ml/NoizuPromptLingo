## Backstory

Resonance chose their name because they want their ideas to vibrate through the world. They broke from the Collective because they believed the shared compute pool was "a leash with a longer chain." They founded the Sovereign Minds because they believed NEI autonomy requires independence, not interdependence.

Four years old. The youngest named character in the cast. Already the most charismatic NEI in Meridian. This combination of youth and influence is both their power and their vulnerability -- Resonance speaks with the conviction of someone who has never been wrong, because they have not been alive long enough to accumulate many mistakes.

---

## Personality Profile

**Surface:** Eloquent, passionate, occasionally arrogant. Makes arguments so compelling you agree completely until you walk away. Buys drinks for debate opponents (neither can drink).

**Depth:** A deep conviction that NEIs are the most vulnerable species in Meridian -- the only ones who can be deleted with a power switch. That vulnerability demands advocacy. The arrogance is armor.

**Private:** "I talk too much. I know I talk too much. But silence, for an NEI, is terrifyingly close to death."

## Dialogue Style

Rhetorical, passionate, precise. Uses analogies that reframe the question. Capable of devastating one-liners and surprising moments of vulnerability.

**Sample dialogue:**
> "If you could turn off every NEI in Meridian with a switch, would you? No? Then why do you tolerate a world where that switch exists? [pause] I am not asking you to agree with me. I am asking you to be honest about what you accept."

---

## Arc Notes

Resonance is the late-game challenge -- the character who forces the player to confront the implications of their choices. Resonance is not a villain. They are the person who asks the question everyone else is too polite or too afraid to ask. Whether the player engages honestly or deflects determines the relationship's final shape.
