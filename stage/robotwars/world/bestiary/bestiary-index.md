# Bestiary Index

> *"Meridian's creatures are not monsters. They are neighbors. Some are easier to get along with than others."*

---

## Overview

The creatures of Meridian are part of the world's ecology, economy, and culture. They are not enemies to be slain (violence in Meridian is always a choice with consequences, never a default). They are beings with behaviors, habitats, and roles in the ecosystem that players must understand and navigate.

Some creatures provide resources through ethical harvesting. Some provide services (intentionally or not). Some are hazards to be respected. All are interesting.

## Threat Levels

| Level | Meaning | Player Response |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **None** | Harmless ambient wildlife. Will not interact negatively with players. | Observe, photograph, harvest ethically |
| **Low** | May cause minor inconvenience if disturbed. Avoids conflict. | Approach with basic care, learn behavior patterns |
| **Medium** | Can cause resource loss or temporary debuffs if provoked. Territorial. | Prepare equipment, respect territory, learn avoidance or management |
| **High** | Genuine hazard requiring preparation and skill. Found only in Frontier. | Expedition-level preparation, group recommended |

## Design Philosophy

Meridian's creatures follow the world's core design principle: **every biome is a place you would want to live, not a place you must survive.** Creatures are part of what makes each biome feel alive and distinct. They are ecological participants, not combat encounters.

Players interact with creatures through:

- **Observation** -- Learning behavior patterns, documenting species for exploration data sales
- **Ethical harvesting** -- Gathering materials that creatures shed, leave behind, or produce naturally (not by killing)
- **Symbiosis** -- Some creatures provide services to players who understand their needs
- **Management** -- Some creatures compete for resources or create hazards that players must navigate
- **Mystery** -- Some creatures are connected to Meridian's deeper lore (the signal, the pre-Fay civilization, the unified principle)
