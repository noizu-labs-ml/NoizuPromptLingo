## Description

Crystal Bats are small, translucent-winged bats whose echolocation has evolved to resonate with crystalline structures. Their sonar produces frequencies that cause crystals to vibrate sympathetically, creating a detailed acoustic map of underground crystal formations. In effect, Crystal Bats can "see" through rock -- detecting crystal veins, cavern structures, and mineral deposits by singing at the stone and listening to what sings back.

Their wings are nearly transparent, with a faint prismatic quality that splits light into rainbow edges when they fly through illuminated caverns. Their fur is pale silver-gray, and their eyes -- largely vestigial -- are clouded blue-white. They are wholly creatures of the dark, and they are beautiful in it.

Crystal Bats are colonial, roosting in clusters of 100-500 on cavern ceilings. Their roosting sites are always located near significant crystal formations, and experienced miners have learned that following Crystal Bat flight paths leads to productive veins.

## Economic Role

- **Mining indicator** -- Crystal Bat populations mark productive crystal caverns. Following bat migration routes between cave systems is a standard prospecting technique.
- **Survey service** -- Trained Bat interpreters (usually Fay or experienced miners) can read bat echolocation patterns to map underground crystal deposits without drilling
- **Crafting material** -- Shed wing membrane has unique optical properties (prismatic light splitting) used in premium optical instruments and decorative items
- **Cave tourism** -- Crystal Bat flights through illuminated caverns are spectacular. Ironvale offers guided cave tours timed to bat emergence.

## Behavior

| Behavior | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Echolocation** | Continuous in flight. Frequencies tailored to crystalline resonance. Sounds like high-pitched singing to human ears. |
| **Colony roosting** | Dense clusters on cavern ceilings, always near crystal formations |
| **Migration** | Colonies shift between cave systems seasonally, following crystal growth patterns and temperature changes |
| **Swarm defense** | If a roost is disturbed, bats launch simultaneously in a disorienting cloud. Not dangerous but very dramatic. |
| **Crystal response** | Certain crystal formations resonate visibly when bathed in Crystal Bat echolocation -- they glow, vibrate, or produce audible harmonics |

## Narrative Hooks

- The Crystal Caverns beneath Ironvale connect to the Deep Vents in the Ashlands. Crystal Bat migration between the two has increased dramatically in recent years. What is changing underground?
- A quest to map a new cave system using Crystal Bat behavior as a guide -- following the bats reveals a cavern that contains crystal formations no one has seen before, in geometric patterns that are too regular to be natural.
- NEIs report that Crystal Bat echolocation frequencies interfere with (or enhance?) their processing in proximity to the Deep Vent structures. The Weavers find this extremely interesting.
