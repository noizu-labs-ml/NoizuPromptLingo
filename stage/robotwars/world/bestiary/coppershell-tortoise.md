## Description

Coppershell Tortoises are the gentle giants of Copperwood's understory. Their shells are living records of the forest -- composed of layered plates that grow one ring per year, each ring a different shade of copper, bronze, and verdigris depending on the forest's health during that growing season. A 150-year-old Coppershell's shell is a timeline of Copperwood's ecological history, readable by anyone who knows the color code.

The tortoises move through Copperwood at a pace that makes the Fay look hurried. They eat fallen fruit, mushrooms, and decaying plant matter, dispersing seeds across the forest floor as they go. Their dung is a nutrient-rich soil amendment that the forest ecosystem depends on for understory regeneration.

Coppershells are fully comfortable with Fay presence (they have been neighbors for millennia) and mildly curious about other species. A player who sits quietly near a Coppershell for ten minutes will likely find it has shuffled closer to investigate -- not because it wants anything, but because anything new on the forest floor is worth a slow look.

## Drops and Resources

| Resource | Method | Rarity | Use |
|----------|--------|--------|-----|
| **Shed shell plate** | Collected from molting sites (tortoises shed individual plates, not whole shells) | Uncommon | Premium crafting material -- naturally iridescent, copper-toned, extremely durable |
| **Coppershell dung** | Gathered from forest floor (fresh only; decomposes quickly) | Common | Superior fertilizer for mushroom cultivation and forest-floor gardening |
| **Forest amber drops** | Coppershells occasionally rest under trees that drip amber resin; the amber collects on their shells and can be carefully removed | Rare | Jewelry, preservation medium, mild magical focus |

**Important:** Coppershell Tortoises are protected under Fay territorial law. Harvesting shell plates from living tortoises is a pact violation enforceable by the Wild Hunt. Only naturally shed plates may be collected.

---

## Economic Role

- **Premium crafting material** -- Shed Coppershell plates are among the most beautiful natural materials in Meridian, used for high-end jewelry, decorative panels, and musical instruments
- **Ecological service** -- Seed dispersal and soil enrichment. The tortoises maintain forest regeneration without intervention.
- **Historical record** -- Shell ring analysis provides ecological data spanning centuries, valuable for agricultural planning and Fay seasonal research
- **Cultural value** -- The Fay consider Coppershells semi-sacred. Items crafted from shed shell carry cultural significance (and a price premium) in Fay-adjacent markets.

## Behavior

| Behavior | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Grazing path** | Each Coppershell maintains a territory of roughly 500 square meters, traversing it in a weeks-long circuit |
| **Communal resting** | Tortoises gather in sunny clearings at midday, basking together in groups of 3-8 |
| **Seed dispersal** | After eating fruit, tortoises travel 50-100 meters before defecating, spreading seeds far from parent trees |
| **Fay interaction** | Coppershells respond to Fay presence by orienting toward them and remaining still -- a posture the Fay describe as "listening" |
| **Winter dormancy** | Coppershells enter a shallow burrow dormancy during winter, emerging in early spring |

---

## Ecological Relationships

| Partner | Relationship |
|---------|-------------|
| **Copperwood trees** | Mutualistic -- tortoises disperse seeds; trees provide food (fallen fruit) |
| **Threadweaver Spiders** | Spiders sometimes build small webs on Coppershell shells. The tortoises do not seem to notice. |
| **Meadow Drifters** | Drifters sometimes rest on Coppershell shells during low-wind periods |
| **Fay** | Deep symbiosis -- the Fay tend Coppershell populations and consider their health a proxy for forest health |

## Narrative Hooks

- A Coppershell with an unusually colored ring pattern -- one ring is not copper but blue-white. The ring dates to approximately Year Zero. What happened in Copperwood during the Convergence?
- A quest to find a specific ancient Coppershell whose shell plates record a season that contradicts the Fay's official history of a particular period
- Thornwhisper's garden includes a resident Coppershell named Patience. Patience is 180 years old and has never been in a hurry about anything.
