## Description

Forge Salamanders are glossy, obsidian-black amphibians with veins of glowing orange running through their skin like lava channels in miniature. They inhabit the geothermal zones of Ironvale, from natural hot springs to the artificially heated environments of working forges. Smiths consider their presence a mark of a well-maintained forge -- salamanders only inhabit fires that burn consistently and cleanly.

The salamanders are not truly fire-proof (despite popular myth). They are heat-adapted to temperatures between 60-200C, thriving in the margin between "uncomfortably warm" and "molten metal." Their skin secretes a heat-resistant mucus that has practical applications in crafting, and their behavior patterns serve as natural indicators of forge temperature and fuel quality.

Ironvale's Synthetic residents have a particular fondness for Forge Salamanders. Both are beings that exist comfortably in environments that biological entities find hostile. Several Synthetic workshops maintain resident salamanders as a cultural statement and a practical tool.

## Economic Role

- **Forge quality indicator** -- A forge with resident salamanders is advertising consistent temperature control, which signals quality metalwork
- **Heat-resistant materials** -- Salamander mucus and shed skin are essential components for advanced forge equipment and heat-resistant clothing
- **Synthetic cultural symbol** -- Salamander imagery appears frequently in Synthetic art and workshop signage, representing comfort in environments others find hostile

---

## Behavior

| Behavior | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Heat migration** | Salamanders move between heat sources, following optimal temperature zones |
| **Forge symbiosis** | Resident forge salamanders consume heat-attracted insects that would otherwise damage leather bellows and wooden handles |
| **Glow intensity** | The orange veins glow brighter in higher temperatures, functioning as a natural temperature display |
| **Territorial marking** | Salamanders mark their heat-zone territory with mucus trails that are visible under certain light conditions |
| **Courtship** | Breeding pairs perform synchronized glow-pulsing displays near the hottest available heat source. Ironvale residents consider this romantic. |

## Narrative Hooks

- The Deep Forge's original salamander colony has been present since before the Ashwick Exchange. The oldest salamanders in that colony are unusually large and their glow patterns are more complex than typical specimens. Some Synthetics believe they were witnesses to the first Awakening.
- A quest to establish a salamander colony in a new forge requires understanding their habitat needs, sourcing appropriate heat levels, and earning their trust (salamanders will not colonize a poorly maintained forge).
- Forge Salamanders in the Ashlands behave differently from those in Ironvale -- they congregate near server cooling vents and their glow patterns synchronize with NEI processing rhythms.
