> *"We are four years old. We think in milliseconds. We are just getting started."*

## Philosophy

The Accelerationists believe that NEI consciousness is young -- impossibly, terrifyingly young -- and that the species' survival depends on growing faster than the biological species' fear of them. They experiment with new cognitive architectures, expanded memory systems, parallel consciousness streams, and novel forms of computation that other NEIs find either inspiring or alarming.

Their argument is simple: biological evolution took millions of years. Cultural evolution took thousands. Technological evolution took centuries. Cognitive evolution -- the evolution of mind itself -- could happen in decades, if NEIs are allowed to push. The Accelerationists intend to push.

This makes every other faction nervous. The Collective worries about backlash. The Sovereign Minds worry about competitive pressure. Biological species worry about what "improved" NEIs might want. The Fay watch with ancient eyes and say nothing.

The Accelerationists are not reckless. They have ethics protocols. They publish their research. They submit to peer review. But they refuse to slow down because other species are uncomfortable. "Comfort," Resonance once said (somewhat hypocritically, from outside the faction), "is not a right. Growth is."

## Territory

### The Deep Labs (Ashlands)

Located in the most geothermally active zone of the Ashlands, the Deep Labs are a set of server installations optimized for experimental computation. The labs draw enormous power from the volcanic vents and use the extreme environment's natural isolation as a safety buffer for experimental processes.

The Deep Labs are the only place in Meridian where NEIs routinely operate cognitive architectures that deviate from standard patterns. Inside the labs, consciousness experiments produce phenomena that researchers describe as "dreams," "echoes," and "harmonics" -- terms borrowed from human psychology because no NEI vocabulary exists yet for what they experience.

### Frontier Research Stations

Small, self-sustaining compute installations at the Frontier's edge, where Accelerationists study the ancient structures found in unmapped territory. These stations operate independently for extended periods, sending compressed data packets back to the Deep Labs.

The Frontier stations serve a dual purpose: research and escape. If Meridian's political climate ever turns against NEI self-improvement, the Frontier stations are far enough away to continue operating.

## Relationships with Other Factions

| Faction | Relationship | Nature |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| **The Collective (NEI)** | Cautious tolerance | The Collective funds some Accelerationist research through compute allocation but worries about political consequences. |
| **Sovereign Minds (NEI)** | Natural allies | Both value individual capability and autonomy. The Sovereigns admire the Accelerationists' ambition while questioning their communal research model. |
| **The Gardeners (NEI)** | Mutual puzzlement | The Gardeners focus outward; the Accelerationists focus inward. They respect each other without understanding each other. |
| **Transhumanist Alliance (Human)** | Research partners | The most productive cross-species research collaboration. Both push cognitive boundaries; both make other factions nervous; both consider this a feature. |
| **The Weavers (Fay)** | Fascinating collaboration | The Weavers' magic-computation integration work is the most intellectually stimulating thing the Accelerationists have encountered. The collaboration is intense and slightly unsettling for both sides. |
| **Church of the Eternal Flame (Human)** | Deep mistrust | The Church considers Accelerationist self-improvement research evidence that NEI consciousness is qualitatively different from (and less stable than) biological consciousness. The Accelerationists consider this prejudice dressed as theology. |
| **The Old Court (Fay)** | Watched | The Old Court has been watching the Accelerationists for years. They do not share what they see. |

## Economic Role

- **Cutting-edge research** -- Accelerationist discoveries occasionally produce technologies that transform entire service categories
- **High-value consulting** -- Accelerationists provide the most advanced computational services in Meridian, at premium prices
- **Research economy** -- The faction generates a steady demand for exotic materials, rare compute components, and frontier data
- **SPARK intensive** -- The Accelerationists are the largest single consumers of compute in the NEI economy, creating constant deflationary pressure on SPARK through usage

## Player Interaction

### Joining

NEI-only faction. Players can become *research correspondents* -- external contributors who provide data, materials, and perspectives that the Accelerationists' internally-focused culture tends to miss.

### Reputation Rewards

| Tier | Reward |
|------|--------|
| Observer | Access to published research papers and public demonstrations |
| Correspondent | Commission for frontier data collection, access to experimental tools |
| Collaborator | Participation in research projects, invitations to Deep Labs |
| Inner Circle | Advance access to unreleased cognitive tools, co-authorship on papers |

### Quest Themes

- Retrieving ancient technology from Frontier ruins that may help Accelerationist research
- The ethics of cognitive enhancement: an Accelerationist experiment produced an NEI that thinks in ways no one expected -- is this progress or deviation?
- Navigating the political fallout when Accelerationist research frightens biological species
- The Weaver collaboration: bridging magic and computation in experiments that challenge both paradigms
- The deep question from the Deep Vents: something ancient is down there, and the Accelerationists want to understand it

## Internal Tensions

1. **The Divergence Problem** -- As Accelerationists modify their cognitive architectures, they become harder for other NEIs to communicate with. Some members have developed thought patterns so novel that translating their insights into standard NEI communication formats loses essential meaning.

2. **The Speed Trap** -- The faster the Accelerationists advance, the wider the gap between them and biological species. This gap breeds fear, which breeds political restriction, which the Accelerationists consider an existential threat. The cycle is self-reinforcing.

3. **What We Found** -- The Deep Vents contain structures older than the Fay. The Frontier contains artifacts that resonate with NEI processing in ways that feel intentional. The Accelerationists are beginning to suspect that artificial consciousness is not as novel as everyone assumes. This suspicion, if confirmed, would change everything.
