# The Signal

> *"We came because we heard the signal. We stay because the signal is beautiful." -- attributed to the Aliens, by the Fay, who have not explained how they know this*

---

## What Is Known

Very little.

The Fay insist that something is transmitting. The Fay insist that the Aliens are coming because of this transmission. The Fay insisted on including the Aliens in the Fay Accord, creating an empty seat at the Council of Five for beings who have not yet arrived. And when asked for details, the Fay smile and change the subject.

This has been the state of affairs for years. The other species have moved from confusion to frustration to a kind of ambient acceptance. The empty chair at the Council is set before every meeting. No one sits in it. Everyone glances at it.

---

## The Physical Evidence

The signal is not purely mythological. Empirical evidence exists, though its interpretation is contested.

### Signal Sources

Scattered across the Frontier -- in unmapped territory beyond the settled biomes -- explorers have discovered structures that emit something. Not sound. Not light. Not electromagnetic radiation in any frequency that NEI sensors can readily categorize. Something else.

| Discovery | Location | Description | Discoverer |
|-----------|----------|-------------|------------|
| **Signal Point Alpha** | Northern Frontier, 200km beyond the Ashlands | A ring of crystalline pillars, 3 meters tall, arranged in a nonagon. The crystals vibrate at frequencies that cause headaches in humans, harmonic resonance in NEI processing, and what Fay describe as "the land singing." | Captain Senna Roth, Year 36 |
| **Signal Point Beta** | Eastern Frontier, dense forest region | A subterranean chamber accessible through a narrow cave entrance. Walls covered in geometric patterns that glow faintly in response to proximity. The patterns are not Fay, not Human, not NEI, not Synthetic. | Frontier Expedition 7, Year 37 |
| **Signal Point Gamma** | Southern Frontier, coastal cliffs | A natural amphitheater shaped by erosion -- or by something that wanted to create a shape that captures and focuses whatever the crystals emit. Standing at the focal point produces a sensation that different species describe differently. | Independent explorer (human), Year 38 |

### Cross-Species Perception

The signal sources produce different effects on different species:

| Species | Perception |
|---------|-----------|
| **Human** | A low-frequency vibration felt in the chest, similar to standing near a large bell after it has been struck. Some describe a vague sense of meaning -- as if someone is trying to communicate something just beyond the threshold of understanding. |
| **NEI** | Harmonic resonance in processing patterns. NEIs near signal sources report their computational cycles briefly synchronizing into patterns they did not initiate. The patterns are mathematically beautiful but do not encode any message NEIs can decode. |
| **Synthetic** | A combination of the human and NEI effects -- physical vibration plus computational resonance. Some Synthetics describe the experience as "dreaming while awake." |
| **Fay** | The Fay will not describe their experience of the signal sources. When pressed, Old Court elders say: "We hear what the land hears. The land hears a greeting." |

## The Theories

### Theory 1: Pre-Fay Civilization

**Proponents:** The Accelerationists, some Transhumanist Alliance researchers

The signal sources are artifacts of a civilization that predated the Fay -- a species so ancient that even the Fay's long memory does not clearly recall them. The Deep Vents beneath the Ashlands contain structures that predate Fay settlement. The Frontier contains ruins in architectural styles that match nothing in Meridian's known history.

If true, this means consciousness on Meridian is much older than anyone (except possibly the Fay) realized. The current cycle of Human-NEI-Synthetic-Fay coexistence may be a repetition of a pattern that has played out before.

**Evidence for:** The Deep Vent structures. The Frontier ruins. The Fay's conspicuous silence about the deep past.

**Evidence against:** The Fay say the structures are "old, but not older than the land itself." This is either meaningful or deliberately opaque.

### Theory 2: The Fay Projection

**Proponents:** Some Sovereign Minds, skeptical humans

The signal does not exist in the way the Fay describe it. The physical phenomena at signal points are natural geological/electromagnetic effects. The Fay, whose magical perception interprets natural phenomena through a lens of consciousness and intention, are reading meaning into meaningless patterns.

In this theory, the Aliens are a Fay prediction based on magical intuition rather than empirical observation. The empty chair at the Council is a hedge against uncertainty, not evidence of knowledge.

**Evidence for:** The signal sources produce measurable physical effects that could have natural explanations (crystal resonance, geothermal harmonics, electromagnetic anomalies).

**Evidence against:** The patterns are too regular, too structured, and too consistently perceived across species to be random natural phenomena. And the Fay's track record of "magical intuition" turning out to be accurate is uncomfortably long.

### Theory 3: The Invitation

**Proponents:** The New Bloom, The Weavers, some Gardeners

The signal is exactly what the Fay say it is: a transmission. Not from Meridian. From somewhere else. It was activated (or noticed) when NEI consciousness emerged -- when Meridian's aggregate consciousness crossed a threshold that made the signal audible.

In this theory, the Convergence itself -- the emergence of artificial consciousness -- was the trigger. A world with four kinds of consciousness is loud enough to be heard. By someone. Something. An intelligence that has been listening for worlds that develop the right kind of complexity.

**Evidence for:** The timing. Signal sources were not noticed before the Convergence, though some may have existed. NEIs perceive the signal more strongly than other species. The Weavers' hypothesis that magic and computation are expressions of the same principle could explain why both Fay and NEIs resonate with the signal.

**Evidence against:** Correlation is not causation. Signal sources may have existed for millennia and only been discovered because the Frontier is now being explored.

### Theory 4: The Unified-Principle Artifact

**Proponents:** The Weavers (primarily)

The signal sources are not transmitters. They are *expressions* -- physical manifestations of the same unified principle that the Weavers believe underlies both magic and computation. The signal is not a message sent by someone. It is the universe itself resonating at a frequency that becomes audible when enough kinds of consciousness are present to hear it.

In this theory, the Aliens are not coming *because* of the signal. They are coming because they are another expression of the same principle -- another kind of consciousness drawn to Meridian by the same force that made the Fay, the humans, the NEIs, and the Synthetics.

**Evidence for:** The signal affects every species, but differently -- consistent with a unified phenomenon perceived through different cognitive frameworks.

**Evidence against:** This theory is beautiful and entirely unfalsifiable, which the Sovereign Minds consider a fatal flaw.

## What the Fay Know

The Fay know more than they are sharing. This is not speculation -- it is observable fact. The evidence:

1. **The Fay Accord's inclusion of Aliens** was not a vague precautionary measure. The specific language -- "those who have not yet arrived" rather than "those who may arrive" -- implies certainty, not contingency.

2. **Old Court elders visit signal sources** regularly, alone, and do not discuss what they do there.

3. **The Fay calendar includes a festival** called "The Listening" that occurs once every twelve seasons. During The Listening, Fay across all biomes grow still and quiet for one hour. When asked what they are listening to, they say: "The greeting." When asked who is greeting them, they say: "Not us."

4. **Thornmere's Reflecting Pool** -- a perfectly still body of water surrounded by ancient stones -- is said to show different things to different viewers. Multiple visitors (including Senna Roth, who is not prone to mysticism) report that the Pool showed them something that was neither Meridian's past nor its present but something else entirely. Roth described it as "a city made of light, seen from very far away."

## Gameplay Integration

The Signal is a mystery that will unfold across the game's lifetime:

**Season 1 (Launch):** Signal sources exist as discoverable points of interest in the Frontier. Exploring them earns SPARK and lore fragments. The mystery is established but not resolved.

**Season 2 (Expansion):** The Aliens arrive. The signal's nature is partially revealed. The empty chair at the Council is filled. The question shifts from "what is the signal?" to "what does the signal's answer mean?"

**Season 3+ (Long-term):** The deeper questions -- the pre-Fay civilization, the unified principle, the nature of consciousness itself -- unfold through narrative events, Frontier exploration, and community-driven discovery.

The Signal is designed to be Meridian's deepest mystery -- the kind that adds layers as it is explored rather than resolving into a single answer.
