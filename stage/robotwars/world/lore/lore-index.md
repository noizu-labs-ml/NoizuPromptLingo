## Overview

Meridian's history is not a textbook -- it is a living argument. Every species tells the story differently. The Fay say the world has always been conscious. The humans say consciousness was born in biological complexity. The NEIs say consciousness is substrate-independent. The Synthetics say consciousness is what you make of it. The Aliens have not yet arrived to offer their version.

These documents represent the best scholarly consensus, which means every faction considers them incomplete.

## Reading Order

For players new to Meridian's lore:

1. **Creation Myth** -- Start with how the world understands itself
2. **The Greenthread Accords** -- Understand the legal and moral framework
3. **The Great Awakening** -- The event that changed everything
4. **The Body Question** -- The consequences
5. **Timeline** -- The full chronology
6. **The Signal** -- The mystery that ties it all together

For lore completionists, read the faction files in `world/factions/` alongside the lore -- the factions are living interpretations of the history documented here.
