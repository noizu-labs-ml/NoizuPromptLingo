# Millhaven

> *"Where every road meets and every deal is struck."*

---

## Identity

Millhaven is the river town at Meridian's heart -- commercial, social, and political center. Built where the Bright River meets Copper Creek. The Commons (Meridian's capital district) occupies the central island, connected by seven bridges. The town radiates outward: Market Ring, Workshop Ring, Residential Ring, Garden Ring.

If Hearthfield is where you learn to play, Millhaven is where you learn to thrive.

---

## Ecology

Millhaven's ecology is urban-riparian. The Bright River and Copper Creek support fish populations, willow groves, and the Fay-maintained Garden Ring. Within the town, ecology is shaped by intentional cultivation rather than wild growth.

### Fauna

| Creature | Threat | Role | Notes |
|----------|--------|------|-------|
| River fish (dozens of species) | None | Fishing, cuisine | Accessible from bridges and riverbanks |
| Garden birds | None | Ambient | Attracted to the Garden Ring and window boxes |
| Market cats | None | Pest control, social | Semi-feral population maintained by shopkeepers |

## Points of Interest

| Location | Description | Gameplay Function |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **The Commons** | Central island: Hall of First Words, Council Chamber, species embassies, Great Market Square | Museum, governance, cross-species events |
| **Hall of First Words** | Museum: Ashwick Transcript, Greenthread Accords, Fay Accord. Free entry. | Lore discovery, cultural context |
| **The Great Market Square** | Meridian's largest marketplace. Permanent stalls, auction blocks, service terminals, public stage. | Primary trade hub, service economy |
| **The Seven Bridges** | Each architecturally distinct: Human (stone arch), NEI (crystal and light), Synthetic (steel cantilever), Fay (living wood), plus two Friendship Bridges | Navigation, cultural landmarks |
| **The Workshop Ring** | Circumferential district of specialty workshops | Advanced crafting, commissioning |
| **The Warm Current** | Famous tavern on river stilts. Glass floor sections. All-species cuisine. | Social hub, information, events |
| **Clearwater Mills** | Original water mills, now mixed functional/artistic use | Grain processing, artist studios |
| **The Garden Ring** | Fay-maintained parks, living architecture, Whispering Grove | Rest, Fay interaction, Copperwood news |

## Hazards

None within the city. The river can be dangerous during spring flood (minor traversal challenge on foot -- bridges are always safe).

---

## Seasonal Changes

| Season | Visual | Mechanical | Cultural |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Spring** | Flower markets, high river, new merchants | Spring Trade Fair (biggest market event) | New merchant arrivals |
| **Summer** | Boat races, floating lanterns, evening markets | Extended market hours, outdoor concerts | Festival of Tides |
| **Autumn** | River trees turn gold, political campaigning | Council elections, Harvest Market | Election season |
| **Winter** | Bridges lit with lanterns, frozen mill ponds | Indoor markets, ice skating | Winterlight Festival |

---

## Creature Encounters

Minimal -- Millhaven is an urban environment. Encounters are social and economic rather than ecological.

---

## Narrative Hooks

- The Great Market Square is where the service economy comes alive -- player's first marketplace listing
- Council meetings in The Commons drive the political narrative
- The Hall of First Words connects lore research to the Ashwick Exchange and the Accords
- Ash's Wandering Stall sometimes appears in Millhaven's market
- Cross-species festivals provide structured social gameplay

---

## Unique Mechanics

- **Central Marketplace** -- Best prices, widest selection, highest trade volume
- **Governance Center** -- Council meetings, elections, policy proposals
- **Information Hub** -- Real-time data on prices, events, faction standings
- **Social Hub** -- Highest NPC and player density; maximum social interaction opportunities
