# The Frontier

> *"Beyond the last map, past the final signpost, where the world is still deciding what to be."*

---

## Identity

Everything beyond the settled biomes. Infinite, procedurally generated wilderness that expands as explorers push outward. The only biome with genuine danger and the only biome without established governance. Contains fragments of all other biomes remixed: meadows giving way to volcanic rock, forests on coral, hot springs surrounded by bioluminescent moss. Meridian's subconscious.

## Unique Mechanics

- **Procedural Generation** -- Infinite expansion. Every exploration session reveals new territory. No two experiences identical.
- **Danger Level** -- Genuine risk. Resource competition, environmental hazards, territorial disputes. Death possible but not permanent (resource/reputation loss).
- **Land Claims** -- Stake territory, develop into settlements. Successful settlements become recognized biomes.
- **Discovery Economy** -- Mapping, documenting, cataloguing all generate SPARK directly.
- **Alien Contact Zone** -- Season 2: arrival point for Alien visitors. First-contact events.
- **No Pacts** -- The Wild Hunt has no authority here. The Fay Accord does not cover the Frontier. This is deliberate -- and terrifying to factions that define themselves by law.

## Narrative Hooks

- Captain Senna Roth's expedition quests teach frontier skills and reveal signal sources
- Echo Moth migration patterns map to signal source locations
- Ancient ruins predate even the Fay -- who built them?
- The empty Alien seat at the Council will be filled by someone who arrives through the Frontier
- Land claims create player-driven political dynamics as the Frontier becomes settled
