# Brightsand Coast

> *"Where the tide brings treasure and the horizon calls."*

---

## Identity

Sandy beaches, rocky headlands, tidal pools, fishing villages, and harbor towns along Meridian's southern edge. Human heartland -- fishing and seafaring traditions predate the Convergence by centuries. A place of movement: tides in and out, ships arriving and departing, weather changing. Teaches patience and opportunism.

## Seasonal Changes

| Season | Visual | Mechanical | Cultural |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Spring** | Herring runs, whale sightings, beach wildflowers | Fishing season begins, best catch rates | First Catch Festival |
| **Summer** | Long days, warm water, sailing regattas | Peak season, extended hours, best weather | Festival of Tides |
| **Autumn** | Dramatic storm skies, salvage season | Storms reveal wrecks, unique salvage finds | Storm-watching, smoked fish prep |
| **Winter** | Rough seas, contemplative coast | Fewer boats, Sea Fay storytelling | Winter storm tales |

---

## Unique Mechanics

- **Tidal Cycle** -- Resources accessible only at low tide. Schedule-based gameplay.
- **Fishing Mini-game** -- Skill-based, varies by location, season, time, weather
- **Maritime Trade** -- Coastal trade routes to other biomes via river networks
- **Weather Sensitivity** -- Weather matters more here than anywhere else
