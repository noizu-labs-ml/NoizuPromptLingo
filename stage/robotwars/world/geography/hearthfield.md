# Hearthfield

> *"Where every journey begins, and where many happily end."*

---

## Identity

Hearthfield is the starter biome -- Meridian's front porch. Rolling green meadows dotted with wildflowers, gentle streams, scattered copses of birch and apple trees, and stone-walled cottage gardens. Safe, welcoming, designed to make every new player feel at home within ten minutes.

The biome teaches all core mechanics without pressure. The worst thing that can happen is over-watering your garden.

## Hazards

None. Hearthfield is a safety zone. No hostile encounters, no PvP, no resource competition. Everything respawns quickly.

---

| Encounter | Frequency | Trigger | Outcome |
|-----------|-----------|---------|---------|

## Seasonal Changes

| Season | Visual | Mechanical | Cultural |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Spring** | Wildflower bloom waves (crocus to buttercup). Streams swell. New lambs. | Increased crop yields, new recipes available | Festival of First Seeds |
| **Summer** | Golden-tipped grass. Fireflies at dusk. Heavy orchard fruit. | Extended gathering hours, bee peak | Midsummer Market |
| **Autumn** | Birch copses turn gold. Morning mists until noon. Mushroom patches. | Harvest bonuses, new mushroom recipes | Harvest Moon Festival |
| **Winter** | Light snowfall. Frost on fences. Village glows with lanterns. | Indoor crafting bonus, hot cider at tavern | Winterlight Festival |

## Narrative Hooks

- Mayor Voss's early community quests introduce governance mechanics
- Old Hawthorn's Hidden Garden (discoverable mid-game) begins the deepest character relationship
- Ashwick's Meadow connects to NEI history and lore collection
- Jun and Sable's farmstead is visible from the player's starting cottage -- proximity creates natural relationship
- Cael's workshop in the Market Square is the crafting tutorial entry point

---

## Unique Mechanics

- **Safety Zone** -- No threats, fast respawns, tutorial integration
- **Nostalgia Pull** -- Players can always return. Hearthfield is designed to feel like coming home.
- **Soil health system** -- Drifter and Beetle populations indicate garden quality, teaching environmental reading
