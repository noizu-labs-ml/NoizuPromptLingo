# Ironvale

> *"Where the mountain gives, and the forge transforms."*

---

## Identity

Snow-capped peaks, deep valleys, mining settlements, and forge towns connected by cable cars and switchback roads. Birthplace of Synthetic consciousness. The Ironvale Deep Forge is where the Ashwick Exchange occurred. Settlements climb mountainsides in terraced layers. The sound of hammers rings across valleys, and at night, the forges glow amber against the snow.

## Hazards

| Hazard | Severity | Cause | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|-------|------------|
| Altitude exposure | Minor | Extended time at highest elevations | Altitude-appropriate equipment |
| Forge heat | Minor | Proximity to active forges without gear | Heat-resistant clothing |
| Cave instability | Moderate (deep caverns only) | Unmapped tunnels | Crystal Bat navigation, proper equipment |
| Winter isolation | Narrative | Heavy snow cuts off highest settlements | Cable car access, winter preparation |

## Seasonal Changes

| Season | Visual | Mechanical | Cultural |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Spring** | Snowmelt, alpine wildflower explosion | New mining areas accessible | Spring Forge Festival |
| **Summer** | Highest peaks accessible. Crystal-clear skies. | Extended cable car hours, peak accessibility | Festival of the Forge (Ashwick celebration) |
| **Autumn** | First snows on peaks. Alpine larch turns gold. | Forges burn hotter for winter prep | Harvest of ores |
| **Winter** | Deep snow, forge glow against white | Isolation creates intimacy; long-term crafting projects | Winter forge traditions |

## Unique Mechanics

- **Vertical Traversal** -- Cable cars, switchback paths, climbing routes. 3D movement.
- **Forge Mastery** -- Enhanced metalworking, unique Ironvale-only recipes
- **Synthetic Homeland Buff** -- Maintenance efficiency, exclusive components
- **Geothermal Power** -- Energy bonuses near vents for power-intensive crafting
